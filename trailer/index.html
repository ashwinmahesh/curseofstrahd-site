Curse of Strahd: step into the mists

Eighty-eight seconds from inside the game, every frame recorded in it, with the game's own sound and voices: turn-based fights on the 2024 D&D rules, a conversation voiced on both sides, making a hero and levelling up, interiors by candlelight, and the road to Castle Ravenloft. Strahd narrates, in his in-game voice.

Curse of Strahd is a free, single-player, turn-based tactical RPG for macOS and Windows that plays the whole campaign. It's an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.
