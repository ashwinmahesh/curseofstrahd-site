Release notes

What's new

Every version of the game, newest first. Download the latest, or update in one line.

1.0.9 October 15, 2026

An update for players on 1.0.3 through 1.0.8: the update command downloads only what changed.

Battles

  • Nearly every fight in the book has been retuned and tested against a well-played party. Ordinary fights are meant to be hard but fair, and boss fights harder still, every one of them tested to be won about 3 times in 8.
  • Fights no longer start with every enemy standing inside one fireball: groups start spread out across the battle map, and the boss fights too.
  • Lorghoth and Baba Lysaga are now proper boss fights.
  • Several bosses strike twice a round, and others fight more cleverly, spending the legendary actions they used to waste.
  • Creatures you cannot see vanish from the map for the hero whose turn it is, and foes follow the same rule.
  • Concentration checks and saves show after the attack animation and the damage number, not before.
  • The turn order scrolls when it is wider than its panel and follows whose turn it is. On a controller, LB+X and RB look along it.
  • Every creature is drawn as big as the squares it fills, and bosses drawn from a smaller creature's picture now have their own size.
  • Shapechangers look and sound the same way each time they change shape: werewolves, vampires' bat and mist forms, wild shapes and polymorphed creatures wear the beast's picture and change back.

Spells and rules

  • New spells from Xanathar's for Sorcerers, Warlocks and Wizards: Thunder Step and Far Step. Dimension Door can bring a companion along, picked from the screen.
  • Polymorph works on foes, Slow lets you pick its targets, and area spells aim from their centre.
  • Shield works against opportunity attacks. As a house rule, Reactions no longer count towards the turn's one spell slot, and free castings pay for them.
  • Hellish Rebuke always asks how to cast it when there is more than one way, even on Automatic.
  • Preserve Life asks how to share its pool of healing among the creatures it can reach, and shows the pool shrink as you spend it.
  • Spirit Guardians spares creatures that have joined your party. Large Form is greyed out, with the reason, when there is no room to grow.
  • Classes that change their spells on a Long Rest can change them any time outside a fight, with no limit.
  • Night attacks at camp now come before the Long Rest's benefits instead of after.
  • A new Experience option on the New Game difficulty page levels the party up from experience points instead of milestones. Milestones stays the default, and older saves stay on milestones.

Castle Ravenloft

  • The castle is inhabited. Its household and court walk the halls, each with a name and lines of their own, and the castle keeps the peace while you are a guest.
  • Small steps now lead up to the castle's raised blocks: tower stairs, the roof walk and the gatehouse.
  • The castle's music has no organ any more, and its tunes shuffle without repeating. A creepier track plays around the people who matter.

Music

  • Big battles play Dark Descent. The opening music is back to the Toccata and Fugue in D minor, and the Skeleton Waltz Theme plays in haunted places.
  • More tunes for the taverns, the castle and the ruins.

Voices

  • Rahadin and the castle household speak in new voices of their own, and 59 new Narrator lines are voiced.
  • Ezmerelda, Emil, Kiril, Ilya and eleven old townsfolk were recast and re-recorded. Companions at the camp fire speak in their own voices.
  • New voiced introductions for the castle, the bosses and the random road fights.

Pictures

  • Every cutscene now has at least two scenes, with 47 more pictures, and the first roc picture is redrawn at a believable scale.
  • The Lantern Girl has her own art and portrait, and every named Belview at the abbey has a portrait of their own.
  • Mouse's hut by the millrace is a thatched hut, and the werewolves' pen shows all five children.
  • Van Richten's Tower shows its stairs instead of burying them in the wall.

Items and shops

  • About 470 magic items are for sale at prices from the Sane Magical Prices guide. Diamonds are always in stock at Donavich, Father Lucian and Kasha Varo, and they offer Raise Dead services.
  • Powerful characters' hoards and stashes hold better magic items.
  • A skill check that reveals a hiding place holding an item no longer hands the item over. You take it from the hiding place, which glows until then.

Fixes

  • A dead or downed hero can no longer lead the party on the overworld, and the lead passes to the next hero who can act.
  • Revival fixes: bodies stay with the party, scrolls and the Rod of Resurrection revive outside fights, and Revivify past its window no longer wastes diamonds.
  • A fight that ends mid-beat no longer frees tokens still in use, and a paused game no longer holds a change of shape.
  • A duplicate character name at the graves is gone, and added foes no longer copy a unique character's name.

1.0.8 October 13, 2026

An update for players on 1.0.3 through 1.0.7: the update command downloads only what changed.

Battles

  • Fights across Barovia are retuned for the stronger heroes of the 2024 rules, so most are tougher: Death House, the Village of Barovia and the Old Svalich Road, the night visitors at camp, Yester Hill and the Wizard of Wines, and the named foes and bosses from the first roads to Castle Ravenloft.
  • Bosses spend the legendary actions they used to waste, and vampires get more of them in their lair.
  • Fights can no longer stall forever: a door with someone standing in it stays open, foes with hands open unlocked doors to reach you, and a foe that can't reach anyone for two turns leaves the fight.
  • Foes attack creatures they can't see but have located, at Disadvantage, and clever foes steer around dangerous ground they know about, such as Spike Growth.
  • An ally's square no longer counts as Difficult Terrain.

Combat

  • Area spells are drawn as an outline of their edge, round for a radius, and auras and emanations cover a round area. The paladin's aura shows on the floor again, without the glow.
  • Line spells such as Lightning Bolt aim at any angle, not only along rows, columns and diagonals. On a controller, the left stick points them.
  • When a hero has a Reaction to take, one prompt lists every reaction that fits, such as Protection or Sentinel, and you pick which to use.
  • Divine Smite's prompt calls out a Critical Hit, attack previews show the target's HP, and a hero with no attacks left is told so instead of "out of reach".

Spells and rules

  • Spells cast from a higher slot last longer, as the 2024 rules have it: Hex, Hunter's Mark, the Dominate spells, Bestow Curse, Major Image, Magic Circle and Mass Suggestion.
  • A Long Rest ends Concentration, and a saved game keeps a spell's time left.
  • A new warlock invocation, Grasp of Hadar (a house rule): each hit of your Warlock cantrip pulls a creature up to 10 ft closer. It and Repelling Blast are On/Off switches on the Warlock tab, one on at a time.
  • Spell Sniper's longer range, ignored cover and no Disadvantage in melee now show when you aim a spell.
  • Crossbow Expert's dual wielding works with magic hand crossbows.
  • Drinking a potion is a Bonus Action, as it always was in play, and the action text and glossary now say so.
  • Detect Thoughts and the four items that cast it are out of the game. Saves swap those items for others of the same rarity, and Mind Spike or Augury takes the spell's place wherever a class or feat granted it.

Camping and exploring

  • You can make camp wherever a place's own rest is risky, not only in the wild, indoors and in town too, with night visitors to match.
  • Short Rests work anywhere outside a fight, unless foes are in sight.
  • Guidance is offered before skill checks outside fights.
  • What a Search or Perception check turns up glows with an orange outline until you use it or leave.
  • The store's sell side has the inventory's filters and a toggle to show only what the merchant buys.
  • A reward's Boots of Levitation are now the 2024 Winged Boots, and a pair already given is swapped when the save loads.

The look of Barovia

  • Houses and walls between the camera and the party turn see-through, like the trees, instead of collapsing.
  • Walls on raised ground stand on it instead of floating above it, and wall-mounted things hang on real walls.
  • Landmarks show on the skyline from more places, such as the abbey from Krezk and Mount Ghakis from Vallaki.
  • Krezk's wall rings the whole village.
  • Figures meant to be lying down are shown lying down.
  • The party leader's footsteps fall in time with the walk.

Story, music and voices

  • Several story scenes make clearer why they matter.
  • The Wizard of Wines has cheerful music once its troubles are over: Kevin MacLeod's Lachaim and Bushwick Tarantella (CC BY 4.0).
  • The heroes each speak in one consistent voice again, and the new camp, night-visitor and story lines are voiced.

Fixes

  • A fight could fail to end after a guest had changed shape. It now ends.
  • Vine blights' Entangling Plants no longer restrains their own side.

1.0.7 October 12, 2026

An update for players on 1.0.3 through 1.0.6: the update command downloads only what changed.

Camping

  • A Long Rest in the wild makes camp right where you are, around a watch fire, with the Narrator to set the scene.
  • Something may come to the fire in the night.
  • Nights at camp bring dreams and night talk, all voiced, different in every region you can rest in.
  • Resting from the menu is fixed: a visit in the night waits until the rest screen closes instead of being lost, the morning keeps what it offers, and resting in an Instant Fortress or with a Rod of Security counts as a full Long Rest.

The look of Barovia

  • Each region has its own look in the Modern style, with its own atmosphere, plants and foliage. Classic is unchanged.
  • Each wild region has its own ground underfoot, on battle maps too, and its own trees and plants: autumn oaks, birches, willows, Scots pines, staked vines, heather and cattails.
  • Every indoor place, dungeon and lair has its own look too: its own light colour and flame tints, floors and wall surfaces, with wet and snowy floors indoors where they fit.
  • Painted landmarks on each region's horizon.
  • More rain and fog at the lake, in Vallaki, at Berez and at the winery.

Loot

  • Chests hold more magic items and potions.
  • Story fights and a place's own fights leave coins and a find, except fights with beasts and mindless undead.

Combat

  • The fights at the Wizard of Wines are tougher.

Fixes

  • The Narrator no longer repeats lines too often in a fight.
  • At the Wizard of Wines, a group that should start out of sight no longer stands in plain view.

1.0.6 October 12, 2026

An update for 1.0.3, 1.0.4 and 1.0.5 players: the update command downloads only what changed.

Exploring

  • Items the party comes by outside loot and shops are announced at the side of the screen, including what a skill check turns up.
  • The travel map's fog lifts over the land you've discovered, and clears once you know every place.
  • Fights on the road take place on battle grounds that match the land around them.

Guests

  • Guests travelling with the party talk about the quests and places along the way, and have a word after a won fight.
  • Guests cast their spells outside fights from the character sheet, and you can give them potions and use items on them.
  • A guest can be sent home from the party screen's Guests row, and guests who fall go back to their places.

Combat

  • An area spell's saving throws show in a panel at the top right, enemy rings are wider and brighter, and Slowed shows over a creature whose Speed was cut.
  • Speed comes back when a hold ends mid-turn: breaking free, escaping a grapple, or the creature holding you dying.
  • Enemies spread their attacks across the party instead of all going for one hero, and a few early boss fights were retuned.
  • Dark One's Own Luck asks before it's used.
  • The paladin's aura no longer draws a glow on the floor. Aura of Protection works as before.

Items

  • The Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location is out of the game, with six other items in its place, and random treasure spreads its uncommon items more evenly.

Voices

  • Nearly 200 new lines for guests, plus new narration for the road's battle grounds and the story fixes.

Fixes

  • The talk menu at camp no longer offers scenes meant for later in a companion's story.
  • Dozens of story fixes across Krezk, the abbey, the winery, the Svalich road, Van Richten's Tower, Berez, Mount Baratok, Castle Ravenloft, the Amber Temple and Tsolenka, with older saves updated where a fix needs it.
  • Ratatouille's name is spelled right everywhere, and older saves are updated when they load.

1.0.5 October 12, 2026

An update for 1.0.3 and 1.0.4 players: the update command downloads only what changed.

Learn to play

  • A hands-on tutorial for anyone new to D&D: moving, reading a character, skill checks, a conversation with a roll, items, resting, and a practice fight with attacks, Bonus Actions, reactions and a cantrip. It's offered when you start a new game, and you can replay it from Learn to play on the title screen.

Exploring

  • Skill checks in the world: right-click things to try Nature, Arcana, Acrobatics, History, Religion and more. Over 140 of them across Barovia, each with its own voiced narration. Hold Alt to see which skill something calls for.
  • Speak with Animals works on about twenty creatures, many of the dead answer Speak with Dead, and Speak with Plants lets some plants tell you what passed them last night.
  • The quest you're tracking marks its people on the minimap and the travel map, and journal cards say who to talk to.
  • The minimap turns with the camera.
  • Dozens of new and redrawn cutscene stills for the longer scenes.

Combat

  • Click beyond your reach and your hero walks as far as they can; click a foe in reach to walk up and attack. Every move takes the cheapest path with the fewest opportunity attacks.
  • Flanking from the 2024 rules, on or off in Settings. Heroes can share a square in fights, and nobody blocks your way outside them.
  • Click a portrait in the turn order to glide the camera to that creature. On a controller, hold LB and press X to step along the order.
  • Reaction spells let you pick the spell slot, Pact Magic included, and Divine Smite offers every slot level.
  • A readied action stays ready if you let a trigger pass, and you're asked again at the next one. A readied spell is aimed as it goes off.
  • Hex and Hunter's Mark move to a new target from the action bar once the first one drops.
  • Break free from webbing as an action.
  • The action bar hides to a slim strip with its button, H, or LB + Y, and comes back whenever you're asked something.
  • Find Steed works outside fights: the steed follows its paladin and joins fights, and casting it again swaps it.
  • A familiar travelling with its owner joins fights and takes its own turn.
  • Enemies go after a steed or familiar only when they can't reach a hero.
  • Low platforms and daises are easy steps: no climbing, and no high ground.
  • Repelling Blast's push has its own rule on the Reactions tab.
  • Cantrips that can replace an attack are cast that way from the Spells tab.
  • One of the harder early fights was rebalanced.

Spells

  • 55 spells that did nothing in this game are gone. A saved hero who knew one gets a spell of the same level in its place.
  • Prayer of Healing, Regenerate and Foresight can be cast outside fights, with their casting time passing. Animate Dead and Create Undead work while exploring, and the dead follow you for a day.
  • Suggestion and Mass Suggestion offer a choice of suggestions in fights, for creatures that can understand you.
  • Hail of Thorns is on the hotbar, and revival spells and Gentle Repose are cast from the character sheet.
  • Sculpt Spells: an Evoker picks who an Evocation spell spares.
  • Chromatic Orb: you choose where each leap goes.
  • Cloud's Jaunt lets you pick its square, and a square it can't reach costs nothing.
  • Spells that last 8 hours now last until your next Long Rest.
  • Dark One's Blessing works whenever anyone near you drops a foe, outright kills included.

Look and sound

  • Light ripples across jetties, banks and quays beside moving water.
  • Fern fronds frame the edges of wooded maps.
  • Fine dust drifts where lamps, candles, fires and window light fall.
  • Strahd's and Godrick's auras are back to their earlier look.
  • Horses are drawn at full size, with no more glitches.
  • The Wizard of Wines plays Eastern European folk music.
  • New voices for Father Lucian, Milivoj, Urwin Martikov, Davian Martikov, Matei the porter and Big Tobar, and re-recorded lines across Vallaki.
  • Ireena re-recorded, with fresh direction on every line.

Fixes

  • Several quests that could stall now finish, and a quest can't slip back to its first stage.
  • A conversation late in the story always has a way out, even if every check fails, and its last silent reply is now voiced.
  • Ireena's escort picks up correctly in saves from earlier versions.
  • A save made outside a fight no longer loads with a hero stuck in webbing, and resting clears such holds too.
  • Figures sharing a square no longer flicker.
  • In Vallaki, people stay where they stood when a conversation ends.
  • Hovering over a reflection no longer stutters.

1.0.4 October 9, 2026

The first update that comes as a patch: if you have 1.0.3, the update command downloads only what changed.

Strahd

  • A new look for Strahd, with new animations, a cape that moves even while he stands, and an aura of black fire.

Light and weather

  • Lamps, candles and fires flicker, and the light around them moves with them.
  • Lightning and thunder in the rain.
  • Characters reflect in water, puddles and polished floors.
  • Sunbeams and moonbeams outdoors, heat shimmer above fires and torches, and a wider, softer glow.
  • Footsteps kick up dust, snow and splashes.
  • The screen pulses when a fight starts and on heavy hits.
  • The wide edge blur is the default now, a little stronger.

Exploring and quests

  • Each place's name appears as you arrive, with the hour beneath it, and places fade in from a soft blur.
  • The camera eases in on a conversation and back out after.
  • Nine new side quests across Barovia, fully voiced, for parties from level 1 to level 9.
  • Clearer hints for several quests.

Items, spells and dice

  • Boon Companions' Bands and Sending Stones come in pairs.
  • Pipes of the Sewers, Dimensional Shackles, the Rod of Alertness and the Wand of Slumber work as the book has them.
  • Detect Evil and Good and Detect Poison and Disease work outside fights.
  • A second copy of a used-up manual can be studied.
  • The d20 sounds like resin on wood.

1.0.3 October 9, 2026

Updates

  • In-place updates: from this version on, updating downloads only what changed instead of the whole game. Run the one-line update command. This is the last full download for anyone already playing.
  • No more engine logo at launch: a dark screen until the title appears.

Quests

  • A new quest journal: Main and Other tabs, search, the details of each quest, and a Track button that the on-screen objective follows.
  • Stuck? Ask for a hint on any open quest.
  • Five new side quests across Barovia, fully voiced, for parties from level 3 to level 10.

Dice, spells and items

  • The emerald d20 has its own sounds, and now rolls for your heroes' saving throws in fights, death saves included.
  • Protection from Evil and Good and 26 other touch spells can be cast outside fights. Only the costly components a spell uses up are needed.
  • Nolzur's Marvelous Pigments paint real objects, worth up to 25 gp a pot.

Interface and fixes

  • The inventory opens at once, even the first time.
  • Liriel's portrait, redrawn again.
  • Take gold works after you've taken every item.

1.0.2 October 9, 2026

Combat

  • A new combat bar: icons on every action, costs at a glance, a row for your resources, and your chance to hit and the damage shown over the foe you're aiming at. Filters down the side sort your options, with spells split by level.
  • Examine: right-click any creature for its card.
  • Shared turns: heroes next to each other in the turn order act together, as in Baldur's Gate 3. Prefer one at a time? Change it under Settings → Party turns.
  • Every hero can Long Jump, and throws show their arc before you let go.
  • A tactical top-down camera on O, in fights and while exploring.
  • Enemies plan their turns without freezing the screen.

Dice and rules

  • An emerald 3D d20 rolls for conversation and overworld checks.
  • No Long Rest while enemies remain anywhere in the building, and 16 hours between Long Rests.
  • Honour runs can no longer be reloaded: one life, one save.

World, loot and quests

  • Walk Castle Ravenloft's gate wall, 30 feet up, and see the keep's new roof.
  • L-shaped buildings, and doorframes inside in the Modern look.
  • Every random encounter now pays a fitting reward, random loot is uncommon or better, and the finds along the roads have moved into the places of the main story.
  • New side quests, fully voiced.
  • The Last Egg, a new side quest high in the mountains for seasoned parties.

Interface and art

  • The inventory's paper doll: slots on a figure, a quiver and a focus slot, and the bag and stash as lists.
  • Thinner fog, and redrawn portraits, Liriel's among them.
  • Pick how you fight on a controller in Settings → Game: the action bar, or BG3-style action wheels. Both have a Tactical view for a top-down look at the battlefield.

Fixes

  • The party falling outside a fight no longer locks the game.
  • A move that's cut short now stops on a square it can stand on.
  • Large creatures no longer teleport into walls.
  • Cutscene pictures fade in instead of freezing the first time they show.
  • Fixed a rare error when a scene closed while a dice roll was still on screen.

1.0.1 October 9, 2026

  • The game has its own icon: Castle Ravenloft under the moon.
  • On macOS, a disk image: open it and drag the game into Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.
  • Cleaner dialogue busts, with the leftover white around many of them gone.
  • More voiced lines.
  • Fixed: some side-quest reward weapons and armour arrived as non-items, and one suit of armour dropped your Armor Class to 0.
  • Fixed: a character's first-meeting introduction replayed on every visit.

1.0.0 October 8, 2026

  • The first release: the whole Curse of Strahd campaign as a turn-based tactical RPG on the 2024 rules, for macOS and Windows.
