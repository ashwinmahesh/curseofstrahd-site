    Fennimore's Guide: The Tser Pool

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Old Svalich Road · level 4
    Given by
    Ambrose Fennimore
    Starts at
    The River Ivlis Crossroads
    Rewards
    A Gentleman's Compendium of Amiable Beasts, 10 gp

    Summary

    Ambrose Fennimore is writing the definitive guidebook to the valley and is not sure the Tser Pool camp's famous plum stew is really plum.

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Ambrose Fennimore stands by the signpost at The River Ivlis Crossroads from 7:00 to 19:00. The first time you talk to him he introduces himself and his guidebook, nine hundred pages so far, which gives everything in the valley a rating out of five stars. Ask "May we see the book?" He reads you a few entries, ending with "Tser Pool Camp: three stars. The plum stew is excellent." He has underlined "plum" twice, because his tongue says onion, hare and something much older than fruit. Choose "Can we find out about the stew?" He'll pay ten gold for the real recipe. The quest starts at Asked.

    Step by step

    1. Go to the Tser Pool camp. Travel west to the Tser Pool Encampment. Old Dorina stands at the iron pot over the coals beside the bonfire, at any hour.
    2. Ask Dorina about the pot. Choose "What is really in the pot?" She lists what went in today: plums, four onions, a hare Radu caught, salt, black bread and herbs. What was already waiting in the iron, she says, is another matter.
    3. Taste it, or ask gently. Either way gets you the truth:
      • "(Taste the stew.)" needs no roll. Under the plum, smoke and onion there's a heavier taste, older than anything grown this year.
      • [Persuasion DC 12] "(Ask her, gently, about the bottom of the pot.)" gets the story straight away. On a fail she snaps that you're like clerks for the devil up the hill, counting every onion. The option is then gone for good, but "(Taste the stew.)" is still there.

      Then she tells you. The pot has hung over her fire for sixty years and has never been emptied. Each morning's stew goes in on top of what's left of the last one. Her silver wedding spoon has been at the bottom since the night her husband was carried home. The quest moves to Known.

    4. Report to Fennimore. Go back to the crossroads signpost by day and choose "We found out what is in the stew." He's delighted. He rewrites the entry as "The stew is plum, onion, hare and sixty years. Ask for the bottom." and pays you 10 gp. The quest ends at Done.

    Choices and endings

    The good ending pays 10 gp, and none of your companions react. If the camp turns on you while the quest is still at Asked, Fennimore offers The Tser Pool camp has turned on us; we cannot get near Old Dorina's pot., which ends it at Unverified, a failure that pays nothing. Afterwards Fennimore stays by the signpost and rates the weather instead (one star). You meet him once more, much later. When every hero in your party has reached level 10, he's on the cliff road outside Castle Ravenloft, the Gates, writing up the castle from outside (two stars). Tell him "We're going in." and he puts you in his appendix: travelling companions, five stars, no notes.

    If you're stuck

    • Fennimore is only at the signpost from 7:00 to 19:00.
    • Dorina's "What is really in the pot?" only shows while the quest is at Asked, so talk to Fennimore first. Eating a bowl from her pot ("What's in the pot?") doesn't count.
    • If you asked Fennimore to visit the boy at Krezk (Dumitru's Visitor), he leaves the crossroads. You'll find him inside the gate of Krezk once it's open. He comes back to the signpost when that visit is done, and you can report to him then.
    • Old Dorina is only in the camp while the Vistani are at peace with you. If you've fought the camp or drawn steel on Madam Eva, she's gone.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Asked: Ambrose Fennimore, a travel writer, has rated the Tser Pool camp three stars and its plum stew excellent, but he isn't certain the stew is plum. He'll pay ten gold to know what is really in Old Dorina's pot.
    Hint: Ask Old Dorina at the stewpot in the Tser Pool camp what's really in her stew, and taste it.

    Known: Old Dorina's stew is plum, onion and hare, over a base that has never been emptied in sixty years. Her wedding spoon is somewhere at the bottom. Fennimore will want to hear it.
    Hint: Tell Ambrose Fennimore, near the signpost at the Old Svalich crossroads, what is in Old Dorina's stew.

    Done (the end: success): Fennimore has rewritten the Tser Pool entry: three stars, and the stew is plum, onion, hare and sixty years. Ask for the bottom. He has paid ten gold and is starting the next chapter at his signpost.

    Unverified (the end: failure): The Tser Pool camp turned on us before we could ask Old Dorina what is in the pot. Fennimore will print his guess, "plum?", paid nothing, and has noted that the camp is closed to inquiries.
