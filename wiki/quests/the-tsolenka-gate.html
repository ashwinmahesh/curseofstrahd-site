    The Tsolenka Gate

    The road to the Amber Temple crosses the Tsolenka Pass, through a guard tower whose portcullis has been down for centuries.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Tsolenka Pass · level 9
    Given by
    Sergeant Dorin Valcu
    Starts at
    The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    The Tsolenka Pass is on the travel map from the start. It's 6 hours up the old mountain road from Vallaki, or 4 hours along the high ridges from Mount Baratok, and the mountain roads have their usual chance fights (wolves, ogres, spiders, werewolves at night). The quest starts (stage Reached) when you read the notice nailed up by the portcullis, or when the sergeant of the watch challenges you inside the tower. It's pitched at about level 9.

    Before you go, it's worth a trip to Mordenkainen on Mount Baratok once he's himself again (The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok): "The gate in the Tsolenka Pass is held by dead men." gets you the watch's password. If you skip that, the watch log in the tower's captain's room has it too (see step 4).

    Step by step

    1. The cliff road. You arrive at the bottom of the cliff road.
      • Wayside shrine: Religion DC 14 to pray earns Heroic Inspiration.
      • Frozen traveller's pack: search DC 12 finds a Rope of Climbing.
      • The landing: edging out along the rocks over the gorge (Acrobatics DC 14, alone; a miss costs 2d6) reveals a roc feather caught on the rocks (search DC 12: Feather Token (Bird)).

      The landing is also where The Frozen Pilgrims and The Last Egg begin.

    2. The bridge, and the roc. The only way to the tower is across the stone bridge over the gorge and past the roc's shelf.
      • By day (6:00 to 19:00) the Roc of Mount Ghakis drops on anyone on the bridge: 160 hit points at level 8, 130 below (7200 XP). From level 9 it has 285 hit points and brings its mate, a second roc with 248 (14,400 XP). Its talons can seize one of you and hold on (escape DC 19).
      • By night it doesn't hunt, so the simple answer is to cross after dark.
      • Driving it off (or saving its chick in The Last Egg) ends the danger for good.
      • On the shelf beyond: Medicine DC 15 on the bones reveals a traveller the roc dropped (search DC 15: Wings of Flying). The roc's nest holds 140 gp, a chain shirt, a longsword, a Potion of Healing (Greater), a Weapon, +2 and an Ioun Stone (Agility).
      • History DC 15 on the carved faces over the gate tells you who the watch were.
    3. Into the tower. The low door to the left of the portcullis leads into The Tsolenka Guard Tower. Five grey soldiers stand their posts in the guardroom, and Sergeant Dorin Valcu steps out: "Halt. Watchword."

      Before you settle anything, ask "Heard anything interesting?" twice. The first time starts The Frozen Pilgrims, the second The Warm Snow. (If you forget, the watch log in the captain's room also starts The Warm Snow.)

      Then get past the watch:

      • "Cold hearth, shut door." This only appears once you know the word, from Mordenkainen or from the watch log in the captain's room. You can read the log before you answer him ("We'll go." ends the talk without settling anything): the captain's door, east off the guardroom, isn't locked. The watch lets you pass and stays at its posts.
      • Deception DC 15, "We're the relief. Your watch is over." Same result. A miss closes the option, and five grey hands go to five grey sword hilts, but nothing else happens.
      • Persuasion DC 15 or Religion DC 13. Tell him the crown is gone, or offer to say the words for his lads. Between them you get one attempt. Success releases the watch: they go to their rest one by one (Liriel +2, Godrick +2).
      • A cleric can offer the rites of the dead with no roll at all, for the same result.
      • "Stand aside, sergeant, or be made to." This means a fight (Godrick -1, Liriel -1): the sergeant, four Phantom Watchmen and ten more phantom warriors at level 8 or higher (10500 XP), and the sergeant, three watchmen and seven more below that. Phantom Warriors resist almost every kind of damage (acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder and weapons) and are immune to necrotic and poison, so radiant, force and psychic damage are what hurt them.

      Any of these moves the quest to Watch Passed.

    4. Loot the tower.
      • The barracks: a footlocker (20 gp, 20 crossbow bolts, a blanket).
      • The guardroom: a table of dice where Sleight of Hand DC 15 wins 40 gp, a hauberk that never rusted (Investigation DC 16: Armor, +2), and the watch roster. Deception DC 15 to sign the roster as the relief watch (one try; a miss costs 1d6 cold) unlocks the captain's strongbox and earns Heroic Inspiration.
      • The captain's room: the strongbox (lock DC 15; 90 gp, a potion of healing, and your Tarokka treasure if it's here) and the watch log on the desk. Reading the log tells you what the sergeant would:
        • the watchword, "cold hearth, shut door", which you can now say to him;
        • forty years of smoke on the north shoulder of Ghakis, which starts The Warm Snow if it hasn't started yet;
        • once the gate is open and the lowest-level hero in your party is level 10, this winter's lanterns on the goat path, which starts The Guide to the Temple if it hasn't started yet.
      • The gatehouse: the watch horn hidden behind the winch (search DC 14: Horn of Blasting).
    5. Raise the gate. The winch is in the gatehouse, the room at the north-west corner. Until the watch is passed, released or beaten, a grey hand closes over the crank. After that, "Haul on the winch." lifts the portcullis. The quest ends at Open, and the road up to the Amber Temple (3 hours) appears on your travel map.
    6. Through the gate. Behind the raised portcullis a curtain of green fire hangs across the passage. Walk into it and the first of you takes 4d10 fire (Dexterity DC 15 for half), and then it's gone. To put it out first, click it: Arcana DC 15, "Study the weave for the knot that holds it." (one try for the party), or cast Dispel Magic on it if anyone has it prepared. "Step back from it." always works.
      • The stone demons. Two stone demons stand against the wall either side of the gate. If you fought the watch, they wake as you step out of the tower: two Vrocks with 152 HP each, at any level (4,600 XP). Get past the watch any other way and they stay stone.

    Choices and endings

    • Open (success) is the only ending. How you got past the watch matters afterwards:
      • Passed (the watchword or the lie): the sergeant stays at his post and keeps logging. "Anything to report, sergeant?" carries on giving you The Frozen Pilgrims and The Warm Snow, and from level 10 he tells you about whoever else has gone up the road (The Guide to the Temple).
      • Released or fought: the watch is gone for good, and so is the sergeant. The watch log in the captain's room still starts The Warm Snow, and The Guide to the Temple once the gate is open and you're level 10.

    If you're stuck

    • Releasing or fighting the watch removes the sergeant. You lose his news, but not the quests: read the watch log in the captain's room for The Warm Snow and, with the gate open and from level 10, The Guide to the Temple.
    • No watchword option? Mordenkainen gives you the word once he's restored, and the watch log in the captain's room has it too. Read the log, then talk to the sergeant again.
    • Persuasion and Religion share one attempt, and the lie gets one try of its own. The cleric's rites and the fight always work.
    • The tower climbs higher. A stair niche in the guardroom's south wall leads up to the lookout, and its hatch to the roof. Neither holds anything you need.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Reached: High in the Tsolenka Pass, a guard tower straddles the road to the Amber Temple with its portcullis down. The watch is still on duty. The watch has been dead for a very long time.
    Hint: Talk to Sergeant Valcu in the Tsolenka guard tower: give the watch's word (the old man on Mount Baratok knows it), talk his watch into resting, or fight. Then haul the gate winch.

    Watch Passed: The Tsolenka watch no longer stands between us and the gate.
    Hint: Haul the gate winch in the gatehouse, west of the guardroom in the Tsolenka tower.

    Open (the end: success): The Tsolenka portcullis is up. The road climbs on into the cloud, toward the Amber Temple.

    ✦Every skill check in its conversations
    • Arcana DC 15: Study the weave for the knot that holds it.
    • Deception DC 15: We're the relief. Your watch is over.
    • Persuasion DC 15: Sergeant, the crown that gave you your orders is gone, and so are you.
    • Religion DC 13: Let us say the words for your lads, and stand the watch while they rest.
