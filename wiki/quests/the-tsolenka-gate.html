    The Tsolenka Gate

    ✦Side quest

    The road to the Amber Temple crosses the Tsolenka Pass, through a guard tower whose portcullis has been down for centuries.

    Region: The Tsolenka Pass (party level about 9)
    Given by: Sergeant Dorin Valcu
    Starts: A notice by the gate in The Tsolenka Pass (Under the tower); Sergeant Dorin Valcu in The Tsolenka Guard Tower (The guardroom)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    The Tsolenka Pass is on the travel map from the start. It's 6 hours up the old mountain road from Vallaki, or 4 hours along the high ridges from Mount Baratok, and the mountain roads have their usual chance fights (wolves, ogres, spiders, werewolves at night). The quest starts (stage Reached) when you read the notice nailed up by the portcullis, or when the sergeant of the watch challenges you inside the tower. It's pitched at about level 9.

    Before you go, it's worth a trip to Mordenkainen on Mount Baratok once he's himself again (The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok): "The gate in the Tsolenka Pass is held by dead men." gets you the watch's password.

    Step by step

    1. The cliff road. You arrive at the bottom of the cliff road.
      • Wayside shrine: Religion DC 14 to pray earns Heroic Inspiration.
      • Frozen traveller's pack: search DC 12 finds a Rope of Climbing.
      • The landing: edging out along the rocks over the gorge (Acrobatics DC 14, alone; a miss costs 2d6) reveals a roc feather caught on the rocks (search DC 12: Feather Token (Bird)).

      The landing is also where The Frozen Pilgrims and The Last Egg begin.

    2. The bridge, and the roc. The only way to the tower is across the stone bridge over the gorge and past the roc's shelf.
      • By day (6:00 to 19:00) the Roc of Mount Ghakis drops on anyone on the bridge: 190 hit points at level 9, 160 at level 8, 130 below (7200 XP). Its talons can seize one of you and hold on (escape DC 19).
      • By night it doesn't hunt, so the simple answer is to cross after dark.
      • Driving it off (or saving its chick in The Last Egg) ends the danger for good.
      • On the shelf beyond: Medicine DC 15 on the bones reveals a traveller the roc dropped (search DC 15: Wings of Flying). The roc's nest holds 140 gp, a chain shirt, a longsword, a potion of healing and a +2 Greatsword.
      • History DC 15 on the carved faces over the gate tells you who the watch were.
    3. Into the tower. The low door to the left of the portcullis leads into The Tsolenka Guard Tower. Five grey soldiers stand their posts in the guardroom, and Sergeant Dorin Valcu steps out: "Halt. Watchword."

      Before you settle anything, ask "Heard anything interesting?" twice. The first time starts The Frozen Pilgrims, the second The Warm Snow (see below for why it matters).

      Then get past the watch:

      • "Cold hearth, shut door." This only appears if Mordenkainen told you the word. The watch lets you pass and stays at its posts.
      • Deception DC 15, "We're the relief. Your watch is over." Same result. A miss closes the option, and five grey hands go to five grey sword hilts, but nothing else happens.
      • Persuasion DC 15 or Religion DC 13. Tell him the crown is gone, or offer to say the words for his lads. Between them you get one attempt. Success releases the watch: they go to their rest one by one (Liriel +2, Godrick +2).
      • A cleric can offer the rites of the dead with no roll at all, for the same result.
      • "Stand aside, sergeant, or be made to." This means a fight (Godrick -1, Liriel -1): the sergeant and four Phantom Watchmen at level 8 or higher (3500 XP), and the sergeant and three below that. Phantom Warriors resist almost every kind of damage (acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder and weapons) and are immune to necrotic and poison, so radiant, force and psychic damage are what hurt them.

      Any of these moves the quest to Watch Passed.

    4. Loot the tower.
      • The barracks: a footlocker (20 gp, 20 crossbow bolts, a blanket).
      • The guardroom: a table of dice where Sleight of Hand DC 15 wins 40 gp, a hauberk that never rusted (Investigation DC 16: +2 Chain Mail), and the watch roster. Deception DC 15 to sign the roster as the relief watch (one try; a miss costs 1d6 cold) unlocks the captain's strongbox and earns Heroic Inspiration.
      • The captain's room: the strongbox (lock DC 15; 90 gp, a potion of healing, and your Tarokka treasure if it's here) and the watch log. The log mentions the watchword, but reading it doesn't let you say it to the sergeant.
      • The gatehouse: the watch horn hidden behind the winch (search DC 14: Horn of Blasting).
    5. Raise the gate. The winch is in the gatehouse, the room at the north-west corner. Until the watch is passed, released or beaten, a grey hand closes over the crank. After that, "Haul on the winch." lifts the portcullis. The quest ends at Open, and the road up to the Amber Temple (3 hours) appears on your travel map.

    Choices and endings

    • Open (success) is the only ending. How you got past the watch matters afterwards:
      • Passed (the watchword or the lie): the sergeant stays at his post and keeps logging. "Anything to report, sergeant?" carries on giving you The Frozen Pilgrims and The Warm Snow, and from level 10 he tells you about whoever else has gone up the road (The Guide to the Temple).
      • Released or fought: the watch is gone for good, and so is the sergeant.

    If you're stuck

    • Releasing or fighting the watch removes the sergeant, and The Warm Snow can only be started by him. Ask "Heard anything interesting?" twice before you do either.
    • No watchword option? Only Mordenkainen gives you the word, and only once he's restored. If you asked him to join you before asking about the gate, use another way past.
    • Persuasion and Religion share one attempt, and the lie gets one try of its own. The cleric's rites and the fight always work.

    Journal stages

    Reached

    High in the Tsolenka Pass, a guard tower straddles the road to the Amber Temple with its portcullis down. The watch is still on duty. The watch has been dead for a very long time.

    • ☐Get past the watch
    • ☐Raise the gate from the winch room

    Hint: Talk to Sergeant Valcu in the Tsolenka guard tower: give the watch's word (the old man on Mount Baratok knows it), talk his watch into resting, or fight. Then haul the gate winch.

    Reached through: A notice by the gate in The Tsolenka Pass; Sergeant Dorin Valcu in The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    Watch Passed

    The Tsolenka watch no longer stands between us and the gate.

    • ☐Raise the gate from the winch room

    Hint: Haul the gate winch in the gatehouse, west of the guardroom in the Tsolenka tower.

    Reached through: Sergeant Dorin Valcu in The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    Open (ends: success)

    The Tsolenka portcullis is up. The road climbs on into the cloud, toward the Amber Temple.

    Reached through: The gate winch in The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    Fights

    Five grey soldiers draw five grey swords with one sound, the way a drill sergeant would want it. (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • The Tsolenka Guard Tower, when level ≥ 8: Sergeant Dorin Valcu (Phantom Warrior), 4 × Phantom Watchman (Phantom Warrior) (3500 XP)
    • The Tsolenka Guard Tower: Sergeant Dorin Valcu (Phantom Warrior), 3 × Phantom Watchman (Phantom Warrior) (2800 XP)

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Deception DC 15: We're the relief. Your watch is over.
    • Persuasion DC 15: Sergeant, the crown that gave you your orders is gone, and so are you.
    • Religion DC 13: Let us say the words for your lads, and stand the watch while they rest.

    Companion approval

    • Liriel Dawnsong: +2: You let the dead watch of Tsolenka stand down; -1: You fought your way through the dead watch of Tsolenka
    • Godrick Pendlebrook: +2: You let the dead watch of Tsolenka stand down; -1: You fought your way through the dead watch of Tsolenka
