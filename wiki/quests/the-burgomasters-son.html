    The Burgomaster's Son

    Ilya Krezkov, the burgomaster's last child, has been burning with fever for a month.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    Krezk · level 7
    Given by
    Anna Krezkova
    Starts at
    The Krezkovs' House
    Rewards
    Potion of Healing ×2, Amulet of Health, 40 gp, a level (milestone)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Ilya Krezkov, eight years old, has had a fever for a month, and his parents have already buried five children by the pool. The quest starts at Heard in any of these ways:

    • Go into The Krezkovs' House (the big house past the well inside Krezk) and talk to Anna Krezkova, who sits by Ilya's bed in the room upstairs.
    • Say to Dmitri Krezkov in the hall "You look like a man carrying something heavy".
    • Ask the Krezk Watchman "Heard anything interesting?" once the gate is open.
    • Give the watchman the priest's reason at the gate (a Cleric in the party). See The Gate of Krezk.
    • Up at the abbey, The Abbot offers to heal the boy in exchange for a wedding dress. That starts it straight at Abbot Offer.

    You need Krezk's gate open to reach the house.

    Step by step

    There are three cures. Any one of them will do.

    1. A cleric's prayer (the best). If you have a Cleric, choose "[Cleric] Let me pray over him." at Ilya's bedside. No roll. Ilya wakes up hungry and the quest ends at Healed. Anna gives you the 40 gp she saved for his gravestone and her mother's Amulet of Health, and the Cleric earns Heroic Inspiration. Liriel +3, Kip +2, Godrick +2, and Thistle, Wren and Ratatouille +1. The prayer stays on Anna's menu until Ilya is healed, so it also works after the pool water has made him frail.
    2. Water from the Pool of the White Sun. Walk north from the well square up the lane to The Pool of the White Sun, use the pool and choose "Fill flasks from the pool." (once only; you get 2 Holy Water). Back at the bedside, "Bathe his brow with water from the Pool of the White Sun." breaks the fever but not the sickness under it, and the quest moves to Frail (Kip +2, Liriel +1, Godrick +1). He'll live. After that a Cleric's prayer at the bedside or the Abbot's hands can finish the cure, as the journal's hint says. If you have a Cleric, you don't need the water at all.
    3. The Abbot's hands. At the abbey above Krezk, the Abbot offers to heal Ilya if you bring a wedding dress for his stitched bride (Abbot Offer). If you've heard about Ilya before the Abbot raises it, you can ask him yourself ("The burgomaster's son is dying. Will you help him?"), which leads to the same bargain. Anna will give you her own dress for this. Once you deliver it, the Abbot goes down at dawn and heals the boy in three of his endings: the wedding, his repentance, and the broken ending after the bride fight. He doesn't if you kill him or flee with Ireena. The whole climax is in The Abbot's Bride.

    At the bedside, Medicine DC 13 ("Let us look at him") only tells you what will work: a prayer with a real answer behind it, or a hand that has healed before.

    Choices and endings

    • Healed (success). Ilya plays at the well in the square by day, shouting his name down it, and wants to climb the wall. If the Abbot cured him, Ilya tells you about the angel with cold hands, and Dmitri admits he owes the Abbot and hates owing him. A healed son also wins Dmitri over at once in Clovin's Audience (no roll needed), and in Lighter Than It Should Be you can let Ilya meet his lost brother (frail works for that too). The epilogue remembers him.
    • Frail. The quest stays open. Ilya is out of bed but thin, and Anna carries him to the well each morning. If you never finish the cure, the epilogue still has a line for a frail Ilya.
    • Never cured. If you kill the Abbot or flee with Ireena before Ilya is cured, a Cleric's prayer can still heal him, whether or not you've used the pool water. Without a Cleric, the pool water can still save his life (Frail), but nothing else finishes the cure.

    If you're stuck

    • The pool lane is inside the wall, so you need the gate open to fetch the water. You can only fill flasks once.
    • A Cleric can finish the cure at any point. The prayer stays on Anna's menu until Ilya is healed, so you can bathe him first and still pray afterwards.
    • The Abbot only heals Ilya after the climax at the abbey, and only in the three endings where he lives: the wedding, his repentance, and the broken ending. If you plan to fight him, cure Ilya another way first.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Heard: Dmitri and Anna Krezkov have buried five children. Their sixth, Ilya, eight years old, has had a fever for a month that nothing in Krezk can break. Anna wants the Abbot's help. Dmitri would rather not owe the Abbot anything.
    Hint: The Abbot at the abbey above Krezk might cure Ilya, for a price; water from Krezk's Pool of the White Sun might help too.

    Abbot Offer: The Abbot of St. Markovia offered a bargain: bring him a wedding dress for his bride, and he will go down to Krezk and lay his hands on Ilya.
    Hint: Find the Abbot a wedding dress (ask in Krezk), or try water from the Pool of the White Sun, or a healer's prayer.

    Frail: Water from the Pool of the White Sun broke Ilya's fever. He will live, but he is very weak, and the sickness under the fever is still there.
    Hint: A healer's prayer, or the Abbot's hands, could finish it. Talk to Anna Krezkova or the Abbot.

    Healed (the end: success): Ilya Krezkov is well. He is out at the well shouting his own name down it, and threatening to climb the wall.
