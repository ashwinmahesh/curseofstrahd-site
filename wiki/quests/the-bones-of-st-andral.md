## Summary

Someone has stolen the bones of St. Andral from the crypt of Vallaki's church, and with them the only thing that kept the dead out of it.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

Several people in [Vallaki](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/vallaki.md) can tell you something has gone wrong at the church, and any of them puts the quest at **Rumored**:
- the gate watch, if you ask *"Heard anything interesting?"*: Father Lucian has stripped his altar;
- [Urwin Martikov](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/urwin-martikov.md) at the Blue Water Inn, asked the same: Milivoj is spending more on bread than he earns, and the coffin maker has had a night delivery of big crates;
- [A Big Dog](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/a-big-dog.md) sunning itself in the square, if someone can cast [Speak with Animals](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/spells/level-1-spells.md#speak-with-animals). It saw the gravedigger carry a sack of old bones to the coffin maker's back door, and it shows you a [Ring of Protection](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#ring-of-protection) in the mud by the stocks.

The quest proper starts with [Father Lucian](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/father-lucian.md) at [St. Andral's Church](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/st-andrals-church.md), in the churchyard north-east of the square. He's there at any hour except while the festival is running. The first time you meet him, he tells you the saint's bones were taken from the crypt three nights ago. While they lay there, nothing dead could enter the church, and only he and his gravedigger knew how the lid comes off. The quest moves to **Asked**.

### Step by step

1. **Promise Lucian.** Say *"We'll find the bones"* (Liriel +2, Godrick +1). The **Religion DC 12** question, offered only at this first meeting, tells you what made the bones holy.
   - Before you leave the church, read the church register: only Lucian and Milivoj know the way of the lid.
   - Go down the crypt stair and search the saint's sarcophagus (DC 12). Scrapes on the lid show it was opened by someone who knew the trick.
   - The vestry chest holds [Holy Water](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#holy-water) and six candles, but it's Lucian's.
   - Lucian will give you two Holy Water for a 25 gp donation, and the church sells potions and scrolls.
2. **Make Milivoj talk.** [Milivoj](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/milivoj.md) digs graves in St. Andral's churchyard by day (except during the festival). Once Lucian has told you about the theft, accuse him: *"The saint's bones. You know where they went."* He'll confess to any of these:
   - **Insight DC 12**: you spot stone-box dust under his nails.
   - **Intimidation DC 13** (Thistle +1, Liriel -1). If it fails, he turns hostile.
   - **Persuasion DC 13**.
   - With no roll: the lid scrapes, Henrik's confession if you went to Henrik first, the dog's story, or 20 gp to match what Henrik paid him (Kip +1).

   Each of the three checks gets one try only, so the other routes are your fallback.

   He sold the bones to Henrik the coffin maker for 20 gold, enough to feed his brothers and sister through the winter. The quest moves to **Milivoj Confessed**, and you choose:
   - *"We won't tell Father Lucian it was you"* (Wren +2, Kip +1, Liriel -1);
   - *"Father Lucian deserves to know"* (Liriel +1, Godrick +1, Wren -1);
   - give him 10 gp for the little ones (Kip +2, Godrick +1, Liriel +1, Wren +1).

   He warns you to go to Henrik's in daylight.
3. **Question Henrik.** [The Coffin Maker's Shop](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-coffin-makers-shop.md) is in the lower town, south-west of the square, and it's barred at night. [Henrik](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/henrik-van-der-voort.md) keeps his back to the storeroom door the whole time you talk. To make him crack, say *"Milivoj sold you the bones of St. Andral"* (after Milivoj has confessed), or use **Insight DC 11** (he's terrified of that door). Then:
   - **Persuasion DC 12**: he confesses either way. On a failure it just comes out in a whisper.
   - **Intimidation DC 12**: he confesses on a success. On a failure he screams, the things in the storeroom wake, and the storeroom fight (below) starts at once.
   - *"We'll see for ourselves"*: you skip the confession.

   If he confesses, the quest moves to **Henrik Confessed**. A driverless black carriage brought him crates, and then a voice in his head told him to buy the saint. What's in the crates sleeps by day and is waiting for the church to be "just a church". The bones are in the small crate by the back wall, wrapped in an altar cloth.

   While you're in the shop, his order book confirms the story. A Search can turn up a coffin with a false bottom (Investigation DC 14) hiding [Slippers of Spider Climbing](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#slippers-of-spider-climbing), and his cashbox (lock DC 12, his money) holds 20 gp.
   ![loc vallaki coffin maker 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-vallaki-coffin-maker-1.webp)
4. **Fight the spawn once, by day or by night.** Whichever you do first, the other fight never happens.
   - **The storeroom, by day.** Step through the storeroom door and the crate lids lift. The spawn start the fight surprised. At level 5 it's two [Vampire Spawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/vampire-spawn.md) and three [Ghoul](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/ghoul.md)s (4,200 XP, between moderate and hard for four level 5 characters). At level 4 it's one spawn and three [Swarms of Rats](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/swarm-of-rats.md) (1,950 XP); below that, one spawn and four ghouls (2,600 XP). Winning moves the quest to **Spawn Destroyed**. Destroying Strahd's spawn here also draws a little more of his attention.
   - **The vigil, by night.** After dark, tell Lucian *"We'll keep vigil here with you tonight"* (Liriel +2, Godrick +2, Thistle +1). (By day, *"Is the church in danger?"* gets you an invitation to come back at dusk.) He bars the doors and lights every candle, and the spawn come in through the windows, not surprised. At level 5 it's three spawn and five [Swarms of Bats](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/swarm-of-bats.md) (5,650 XP). At level 4 it's one spawn, five bat swarms and two [Giant Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-rat.md)s (2,100 XP); below that, two spawn and one swarm (3,650 XP). Winning moves the quest to **Vigil Kept**. The storeroom is empty after that: go back by day and take the bones from the small crate. There's no fight left there, and the journal's hint says only to go in by day and take them back.
   - Either way, vampire spawn have 90 HP and AC 16 and shrug off necrotic damage. Holy water deals radiant damage to them, so bring Lucian's.
   ![church vigil](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/church-vigil.webp)
5. **Bring St. Andral home.** Give the [Bones of St. Andral](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#bones-of-st-andral) to Lucian with *"We've brought St. Andral home"*. The quest ends at **Bones Returned**.
   - He gives you two Holy Water and his blessing (Liriel +5, Godrick +2; Kip, Thistle, Wren and Ratatouille +1).
   - This is Vallaki's milestone level, if the festival hasn't already given it.
   - If you shielded Milivoj, Lucian decides not to ask who took the bones. Otherwise he says he'll speak to Milivoj, and that hunger was not his sin.
   - If Ireena is with you, he offers her sanctuary on the spot.

### Choices and endings

- **The church is holy ground again.** Ireena can take sanctuary there ([Sanctuary](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/sanctuary.md)), and Strahd can only call to her from the churchyard gate.
- **The saint's niche opens.** In the crypt it holds a [Mace of Disruption](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#mace-of-disruption).
- **The altar blesses you.** **Religion DC 13** in prayer at the altar gives 1d8 temporary hit points.
- **At the festival.** If you stand with the town at [The Festival of the Blazing Sun](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-festival-of-the-blazing-sun.md), the crowd parts for Lucian because they know who brought the saint home.
- **The Keepers of the Feather.** With the bones home, Urwin will tell you about the Keepers after the festival, even in the Baron's town, or before it if you fed the raven on the road.
- **Henrik** sleeps again and offers you his wares, plus a free coffin if you ever need one.
- **Liriel and Hedda.** With Liriel in the party, the return is a step in [Carry the Dawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/carry-the-dawn.md). Hedda feels it too, if [Cold Prayers](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/cold-prayers.md) is under way.

### If you're stuck

- **Milivoj won't talk about the bones.** The accusation only appears after Lucian has told you about the theft. Milivoj is in the churchyard by day only.
- **The coffin maker's door is barred.** It's barred at night. Go by day, which is also when the spawn are asleep.
- **You can't start the vigil.** Lucian only offers it at night, and only before either spawn fight has happened.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **Rumored**: Vallaki says Father Lucian has stripped the altar at St. Andral's and won't say why, and that Milivoj the gravedigger has been spending more than he earns. Something has gone from the church that shouldn't have.
> *Hint:* Talk to Father Lucian at St. Andral's Church in Vallaki.
>
> **Asked**: Father Lucian of St. Andral's told us the saint's bones have been stolen from the crypt beneath his altar. While they lay there, the dead could not set foot in the church. Without them, he fears for his flock and for anyone sheltering under his roof.
> *Hint:* Find Milivoj the gravedigger in St. Andral's churchyard by day; he has been spending more than he earns.
>
> **Milivoj Confessed**: Milivoj the gravedigger admitted he dug up the bones and sold them to Henrik van der Voort, the coffin maker, for enough coin to feed his brothers and sister through the winter.
> *Hint:* Visit Henrik van der Voort at the coffin maker's shop in Vallaki and ask about the bones.
>
> **Henrik Confessed**: Henrik confessed: he bought the bones because something told him to. That something sleeps in crates in his storeroom, and it is waiting for a night when the church is undefended.
> *Hint:* Search the storeroom at the back of the coffin maker's shop by day, ready for a fight. Or keep vigil with Father Lucian at St. Andral's after dark.
>
> **Vigil Kept**: We held St. Andral's through a night of the undead. The church still stands, but its crypt is still empty.
> *Hint:* The bones are still in the coffin maker's storeroom. Go in by day and take them back.
>
> **Spawn Destroyed**: The things in Henrik's storeroom were vampire spawn, and they are destroyed. The saint's bones are somewhere among the crates.
> *Hint:* Take the bones back to Father Lucian at St. Andral's Church.
>
> **Bones Returned** (the end: success): St. Andral's bones are back in their crypt and Father Lucian has blessed them again. The church is holy ground once more.
