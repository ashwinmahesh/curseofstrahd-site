    The Order of the Silver Dragon

    ✦Side quest

    The dead knights of Argynvostholt still keep their watch for a dragon four hundred years gone. Their commander hates Strahd too much to let him die.

    Region: Argynvostholt (party level about 8)
    Given by: Sir Godfrey Gwilym
    Starts: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt (The courtyard); Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor (Sir Godfrey's room)
    Rewards: +1 Plate Armor, Holy Water ×2, 150 gp, a level (milestone)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Travel to Argynvostholt, which is on the map from the start: the knights' road runs north from Vallaki (5 hours) or up from Tser Pool (4½ hours). Sir Godfrey Gwilym waits on the broken front steps the first time you walk up the drive and speaks as you come near. That puts the quest at Met. Nothing has to happen first. After this first meeting he lives in his room on the upper floor.

    Come by day if you can. Once you've met Godfrey, the courtyard in front of the doors fills with phantom men-at-arms after dark: a fight with 4 Phantom Warriors at level 8, 3 at level 7, 2 below that. It happens once, and never after the Order's fate is settled.

    Step by step

    1. Meet Godfrey on the steps. He sends you to the commander in the great hall. Good questions: "Who are you? All of you?", "Why is the beacon dark?" (which starts The Dragon's Beacon), and, if Madam Eva's cards named him, "Madam Eva's cards named you, Sir Godfrey." (see step 4).
    2. Meet Vladimir. Go in through the front doors to the great hall. Vladimir Horngaard sits at the head of a long table of dead knights in a chair carved with folded wings, and he's there at any hour. Meeting him moves the quest to Commander. He won't help end Strahd: he wants Strahd alive and suffering forever, with the Order watching. Ask "Why keep the beacon dark?" and try Insight DC 13 ("That isn't the whole reason") to see the guilt under it. That opens a question for the dragon's echo later; failing costs nothing else. If the reading put a treasure "in the keeping of the dead knight who commands it", "You're keeping something that could hurt Strahd." gets him to admit it. Every ending below hands it over.
    3. Do the legwork in the chapel and upstairs. It opens the plain, roll-free routes:
      • In the chapel, at the west end of the ground floor, the echo of Argynvost answers from the frosted window. "Why can't your knights rest?", then "What did you ask of him, at the end?", gets the dragon's last words to Vladimir. "What oath did your knights swear?", or reading the oath stone by the chapel door, teaches you the Order's oath.
      • On Argynvostholt, Upper Floor, Godfrey's room is at the west end. The charcoal writing that covers his wall is a list of every knight's name and what they loved, with his own at the bottom; reading it counts as legwork too. Vladimir's bare room at the east end holds his duty roster and a strongbox (lock DC 18: 120 gp and a level 1 spell scroll).
    4. Help Godfrey remember (opens the muster). Once you've met Vladimir, ask Godfrey in his room "Your commander won't fight to end Strahd. Will you?" He admits he's lost the words of the oath. Give them back with any of:
      • the Order's oath ("Say the Order's oath to him");
      • his own wall ("Read him the bottom of his own wall", after you've read it);
      • the dragon's last words;
      • or Persuasion DC 15 ("You don't need the words. You remember that it mattered."). One try. If it fails he asks you to find the words.

      He remembers. The quest moves to Remembering, he becomes friendly, and he gives you his polished +1 Plate Armor (Godrick +4, Liriel +1). From then on "Is anything left in the Order's armory?" opens his shop. If the cards named him your ally, you can do this before asking him to join: joining through the oath or his wall later doesn't give you the plate.

    5. Choose the Order's fate (next section). Each of the three endings gives Argynvostholt's milestone (a level), once.

    Choices and endings

    • Ride (success): the Order marches with you. In Vladimir's menu, "Call the muster, Commander. Let the Order hear us." appears once Godfrey remembers or you know the dragon's last words. He strikes the table and every dead head turns. Then:
      • "Tell the knights what the dragon asked of Vladimir as he died." (last words, no roll). Vladimir breaks and leads the march himself (Godrick +3, Wren +2, Liriel +2).
      • Persuasion DC 14, "Sir Godfrey remembers the oath. Let him say it to them." On a success Vladimir leads. On a failure the Order rides anyway, but follows Godfrey, and Vladimir is left alone at an empty table.
      • Persuasion DC 18, "Your hate keeps him company...". One try. A failure returns you to his menu, where the other routes still work.
      • A trial of arms: "We'll settle this by the Order's own law. A trial of arms." (from his menu or the muster) starts Vladimir Duel: Vladimir (Vladimir Horngaard, AC 17, 195 HP) with a Revenant as his second at level 8, Vladimir alone at level 7, Vladimir at 140 HP below that. He regenerates 10 HP every turn unless he took fire or radiant damage since his last turn, so keep fire or radiant on him every round. When he goes down he yields and speaks as you approach. "Then the Order rides with us to the castle." gives you the Order with Godfrey leading. "We only want what you're keeping." (if the treasure is his) takes the treasure and nothing else. "Keep your knights for now." leaves it, and "By the Order's law, we command now." stays in his menu for later.

      On a ride the quest ends at Ride, The Dragon's Beacon moves to Waiting, and you get the milestone and whatever he was keeping. Afterwards the knights saddle horses that aren't there, hunt Strahd's creatures on the night roads, and salute you on the stair at the end of the game.

    • Rest (success): light the beacon. Kindle the dragon's fire at his bones in the mausoleum and carry it to the top of the tower; The Dragon's Beacon walks you through it. Vladimir climbs after you. The dragon's last words talk him down with no roll, Persuasion DC 17 does it with one, and otherwise you fight him there. When it burns, the knights lie down at the table at last and the quest ends at Rest, with big approval (Godrick +4, Liriel +3, everyone else +1). Lighting it after a ride also ends at Rest, with no fight, but the Order won't march.
    • Grudge (failure): swear Vladimir's oath. "What would the Order want from us? Name your terms.", then "We swear it." You promise to break Strahd and not end him. Vladimir pays 150 gp and 2 Holy Water from the Order's war chest, hands over what he was keeping, and you get the milestone. Godrick -4, Liriel -3, Wren -2, Kip -2. "No. When we reach him, he dies." turns him down instead (Thistle +1, Godrick +1). The oath has consequences:
      • Dead knights stand at the gate watching the castle from now on.
      • Lighting the beacon later breaks the oath, and Vladimir fights you at the cradle first.
      • At the very end, at Strahd's coffin, Vladimir comes down the stair to stop the killing blow. The dragon's last words make him stand aside; so does Persuasion DC 18, and failing it means a fight. Choosing to break the oath outright also means a fight.

    If you're stuck

    • The muster option isn't in Vladimir's menu. It needs Godfrey to remember the oath or you to know the dragon's last words, and the Order's fate must still be open.
    • Godfrey's "Will you?" question only appears after you've met Vladimir, and only while Godfrey is in his room. If he's travelling with you, tell him to wait for you at Argynvostholt and he goes back there.
    • The last words are free. Ask the echo in the chapel why the knights can't rest, then what he asked of Vladimir. Ask before you light the beacon, because the echo is gone afterwards.

    Journal stages

    Met

    At Argynvostholt, the burned house of the silver dragon Argynvost, his knights are still at their posts, dead and courteous. Sir Godfrey Gwilym met us on the steps and sent us to their commander in the great hall.

    • ☐Speak with the commander, Vladimir Horngaard

    Hint: Find the commander, Vladimir Horngaard, on Argynvostholt's ground floor; Sir Godfrey can tell you where.

    Reached through: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt; Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor

    Commander

    Vladimir Horngaard commands the dead Order. He hates Strahd more than anything left in the valley, and that is exactly why he won't help end him: death would let Strahd go. He wants him alive and imprisoned forever, and his knights kept at their posts to watch.

    • ☐Win the Order to the fight against Strahd
    • ☐Or give the knights their rest
    • ☐Or accept the commander's terms

    Hint: Help Sir Godfrey remember the Order's oath (the stone in the chapel, his own wall), or name terms with Vladimir, or challenge him by the Order's law.

    Reached through: Vladimir Horngaard in Argynvostholt, Ground Floor

    Remembering

    Sir Godfrey has been forgetting the oath the Order swore, and why it mattered. We helped him remember. He'll stand with us if we ask the commander to call the Order together and let the knights choose.

    • ☐Ask Vladimir to call the muster

    Hint: Ask Vladimir on Argynvostholt's ground floor to call the muster, and let Sir Godfrey say the oath.

    Reached through: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt; Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor

    Ride (ends: success)

    The Order of the Silver Dragon will march with us when we go up to Castle Ravenloft. They'll rest when he falls, and not before.

    Reached through: Vladimir Horngaard in Argynvostholt, Ground Floor

    Rest (ends: success)

    The beacon burns over Argynvostholt again, and the dead knights have laid down their watch at last.

    Reached through: The beacon's cradle in The Beacon Tower; Vladimir Horngaard in The Beacon Tower

    Grudge (ends: failure)

    We swore the commander's oath: when we reach Strahd, we break him and leave him alive in the prison he made. The Order will watch to see the oath kept, and it will not raise a sword to end him.

    Reached through: Vladimir Horngaard in Argynvostholt, Ground Floor

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Persuasion DC 15: You don't need the words. You remember that it mattered. That is the oath.
    • Insight DC 13: That isn't the whole reason.
    • Persuasion DC 14: Sir Godfrey remembers the oath. Let him say it to them.
    • Persuasion DC 18: Your hate keeps him company. Your knights deserve better than to be his audience.
