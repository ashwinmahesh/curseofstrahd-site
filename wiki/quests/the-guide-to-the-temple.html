    The Guide to the Temple

    ✦Side quest

    Since the Tsolenka gate opened, a man has led three parties up the goat path toward the Amber Temple and come down alone each time.

    Region: The Amber Temple (party level about 10)
    Given by: Sergeant Dorin Valcu
    Starts: Sergeant Dorin Valcu in The Tsolenka Guard Tower (The guardroom)
    Rewards: Rod of Alertness, 400 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Sergeant Dorin Valcu, the dead sergeant of the watch in The Tsolenka Guard Tower, starts this one, but only if you got past him the first time by being let through: with the watchword ("Cold hearth, shut door.") or the Deception DC 15 lie that you're the relief (see The Tsolenka Gate). If you released the watch to rest or fought your way through, the sergeant is gone and this quest can't start.

    Once the lowest-level hero in your party is level 10, talk to him again and ask Has anybody else gone up since we passed? His log shows three parties on the goat path round the tower this winter, with one lantern coming back down each time. Last night it was a man, a woman and a sledge with something small on it, coughing, led by someone who walks like he's never been cold. The quest starts (Asked), and a new road opens on the map from The Tsolenka Pass to the camp on the goat path (two hours).

    Step by step

    1. Travel to The Goat Path Camp. On a shelf of rock above the path, a fire burns beside a low tent. The Guide, a lean man in furs, bareheaded in the cold, offers you tea. Remus explains that their daughter has had lung fever since the autumn and the guide says the temple can cure her. Floarea says nothing and watches the tea.
    2. Find out what he's doing:
      • Medicine DC 14: (Look at the child on the sledge.) It isn't lung fever. Her pupils are pinpricks: someone has been giving her sleep-root every day. Floarea quietly admits he makes the girl's tea every night. This option stays if you fail, so you can try again.
      • Insight DC 15: (Watch the guide while Remus talks.) He watches the sledge like a drover the night before market. This only changes a line when the fight starts, and a failure is spent.
    3. Choose You've been putting something in her tea. (after the Medicine check), or Turn back. Take her home. Either way he stops pretending: something in the amber pays him a year of life for every family he brings to its door, and nobody turns back on his mountain.
    4. Fight The Guide (170 Hit Points here) with two of the people he led up before (Wraiths) and two of his own shadows (Shadows), 9,700 XP. He resists cold and necrotic damage, can turn two failed saves a day into successes, and his Whiteout hits up to three of you at once within 60 feet (Constitution DC 16, or 4d8 cold and blinded). His Amber Bolt hits hard at range, and he has Hold Person and Cone of Cold once a day each. Winning moves the quest to Fought.
    5. Talk to Floarea (or Remus) by the sledge. She pours the tea out onto the snow and throws the kettle after it. Without it the girl will wake in a day or two with only a cough. The quest is Done.

    Choices and endings

    • Done (success). In the guide's pack are a purse of other people's money (400 gp), a list of names with lines through them, and a Rod of Alertness. Liriel +3, Godrick +2, Kip +2, Wren +1. The family goes home.
    • There is no peaceful ending. Leaving them to their fire just leaves the quest open; every way of confronting the guide ends in the fight.

    If you're stuck

    • The sergeant has no new question. He only asks once you're level 10, and only if he is still at his post, which means you passed him with the watchword or the lie rather than releasing or fighting the watch.
    • No road to the camp. It only appears after the sergeant has told you about the goat path.

    Asked

    The dead sergeant at the Tsolenka watchtower has logged lanterns on the goat path round his tower: three parties this winter, and one lantern coming down each time. Last night a man, a woman, and a sledge with something small on it, coughing, and a guide in front who walks like he's never cold.

    • ☐Follow the goat path from the Tsolenka Pass toward the Amber Temple
    • ☐Find the party with the sledge

    Hint: Take the goat path from the Tsolenka Pass toward the Amber Temple, and find the camp with the sledge.

    Reached through: Sergeant Dorin Valcu in The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    Fought

    The guide had been keeping the child sick with sleep-root in her tea, to make her parents climb. Something in the amber paid him a year for every family. We stopped him.

    • ☐Talk to Floarea at the camp

    Hint: Talk to Floarea by the sledge at the camp on the goat path.

    Done (ends: success)

    Without the tea the little girl will wake in a day or two, with only a cough to carry home. Remus and Floarea are taking her down.

    Reached through: The Guide in The Goat Path Camp; Remus in The Goat Path Camp; 1 more
