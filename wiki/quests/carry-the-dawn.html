    Carry the Dawn

    ✦Side quest

    On the morning of her vows the Morninglord showed Liriel Dawnsong a valley where the sun had not risen in four hundred years, and told her to carry the dawn there.

    Region: Companions
    Starts: the mist behind you in The Old Svalich Road (The Svalich Woods)
    Rewards: Amulet of the Devout, +1, Holy Water ×2, a level (milestone)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This is Liriel Dawnsong's personal quest (the High Elf Cleric of the Light Domain). It starts by itself the first time you walk into the mist at the east end of The Old Svalich Road with Liriel in the party, right as the game begins. A custom hero in her place never gets it. Each later beat needs Liriel in the party at that moment, so keep her with you at St. Andral's Church, at the Abbey, at the Abbot's unveiling and in Castle Ravenloft.

    There's no level or approval gate on starting it. The Abbey of St. Markovia is a level 6–7 region, and the last step is in Castle Ravenloft.

    Step by step

    1. The silence (first Long Rest). After a Long Rest, "Liriel wants a word": she's kneeling at the edge of camp, praying into a silence. This talk is only offered while the quest is still at The Vision. Kneeling beside her is +1. "Maybe that's why you were sent. To be the next room" is +2, "He sent you here..." is +1, and "Do you want to stop?" is -1. The quest moves to The Silence. A later rest brings a second talk, about the little light in her bedroll; Insight DC 10 plays out the same pass or fail, and staying with her is +3. That talk doesn't come if she told Madam Eva she's afraid of the dark.
    2. Optional, Madam Eva. Liriel's truth (she's afraid of the dark) pays for the reading, for +3 and Heroic Inspiration. Spending the one question on "Will dawn ever come to this valley?" is also +3.
    3. A spark (optional). When you bring the Bones of St. Andral home to Father Lucian in Vallaki with Liriel present, she feels her god for one breath. The quest moves to A Spark, Liriel gains +5 (and the others +1 to +2), and Lucian gives you two Holy Water. If Vallaki's milestone level hasn't come yet, it comes now. See The Bones of St. Andral. You can skip this; the Abbot moves the quest on anyway.
    4. The angel. Go to The Abbey of St. Markovia above Krezk, and meet The Abbot in The Church of St. Markovia with Liriel beside you. On that first meeting she knows him for an angel of the Morninglord and kneels. The quest moves to The Angel.
    5. The fall. Ask him Clovin says you have work in the wards. What is it? with Liriel present. He tells you he's making Strahd a bride, Vasilka, and Liriel goes white. The quest moves to The Fall.
    6. Ask about the sun (do this). While the Abbot's fate is still open, his menu has Liriel's option Ask him whether he still remembers the sun. He remembers the snow going pink at dawn. Liriel gains +4 and Heroic Inspiration, and this opens her own way to save him at the unveiling.
    7. Settle the Abbot's fate. This runs through The Abbot's Bride: bring him a wedding dress and he wakes Vasilka. At the unveiling, Liriel's Remind him of the sun he told her about needs no roll, and he repents and unmakes his bride. That gives Liriel +5 and Godrick +1, and then the repentance's own approval on top: Liriel +5, Godrick +2, and Wren, Kip and Ratatoille +1 each. You also get two Potion of Healing, and Ilya is healed if he's still ill. Any other ending also moves Liriel's quest on:
      • The wedding ("It's your abbey...") costs Liriel -6, and the rest of the party loses approval too.
      • Stopping the wedding starts a fight with Vasilka (a Flesh Golem) and armed Belviews. Afterwards you can still bring the kneeling Abbot to repentance, leave him broken, or kill him.
      • "Kill him" is a fight against the Abbot himself, a Deva with magic resistance.
    8. Faith kept. At the next Long Rest after the Abbot's fate is settled, "Liriel wants a word". Her lines depend on what happened to him; either answer leads to her decision to carry the dawn herself. The quest moves to Faith Kept (+2).
    9. The dawn. In Castle Ravenloft, the Chapel, examine The altar step in front of the upside-down sunburst. It only appears with Liriel in the party at Faith Kept. Choose (Let her.) and she sings the morning hymn. On the third verse, real dawn comes through the windows. The quest ends at The Dawn (+8).

    Choices and endings

    There's one ending, The Dawn, and Liriel's approval shapes it:

    • Strained or worse (approval -25 or lower): at the altar step she can't hear her god, or the party either, and won't sing. The step stays there, so mend her approval and come back. Her Strained camp talk can give up to +10.
    • Otherwise: she sings the dawn and gains +8.
    • Close or better (25 or more): afterwards she takes each of your hands and gives away the sunburst from her first vows, an Amulet of the Devout, +1 that goes to whoever is speaking for the party. The whole party gains Heroic Inspiration.

    The Abbot's fate is the choice in this quest that changes the rest of the game (Krezk, Ilya, Vasilka, the wedding). The dawn itself isn't checked anywhere else in the files.

    If you're stuck

    • Bring Liriel to the Abbey. The Fall is only set in the Abbot's bride talk, and that talk can be had once. If you hear about Vasilka without Liriel in the party, nothing else moves her quest to The Fall, and her Faith Kept talk never comes. The same goes for attacking the Abbot before he's told you about the bride.
    • The Faith Kept talk needs two things: The Fall, and the Abbot's story over (wedding, repentance, left broken, or killed). Camp talks come one per Long Rest across the whole party, and a Strained or Doubtful talk of Liriel's plays before her quest talk.
    • Strahd's final room: if Madam Eva's reading put Strahd's final room in the chapel, walking into the nave starts the final battle, and the altar step has to wait until after it.

    Journal stages

    The Vision

    On the dawn of her vows, Liriel saw a valley in a ring of mountains, a castle on a spike of rock, and a sky that had not seen the sun in four hundred years. A voice like warmth on her face said: carry the dawn there. She left the next morning. She has never once doubted a vision.

    • ☐Carry the dawn to Barovia

    Hint: Take Liriel through the valley and rest with her; places where the Morninglord is still honoured matter to her.

    Reached through: the mist behind you in The Old Svalich Road

    The Silence

    In Barovia Liriel's prayers are still answered, but her god feels far away, as if heard through a wall. She has never prayed into a silence before. She says it's very loud.

    • ☐Find where the Morninglord's light still reaches in this valley

    Hint: Holy ground still answers in Barovia. Help restore St. Andral's Church in Vallaki, or visit the Abbey above Krezk, with Liriel in the party.

    A Spark

    When St. Andral's bones came home to his church in Vallaki, Liriel felt it: a warmth on her face, for the length of a breath, like a window opened in winter. The light reaches Barovia where somebody carries it.

    • ☐Keep carrying it

    Hint: Visit the Abbey of St. Markovia above Krezk with Liriel in the party and speak with the Abbot.

    Reached through: Father Lucian in St. Andral's Church

    The Angel

    The Abbot of St. Markovia is an angel of the Morninglord's own heaven, sent to keep this valley's faith. Liriel knew him at once, and wept for joy in his chapel.

    • ☐Learn what the angel is doing here

    Hint: Keep talking to the Abbot at the Abbey of St. Markovia with Liriel beside you, and ask what he is working on.

    Reached through: The Abbot in The Church of St. Markovia

    The Fall

    The Abbot is sewing the lord of Barovia a bride from Krezk's dead, and believes it is an act of love. Liriel did not shout at him. She asked him, very quietly, whether he still remembers the sun.

    • ☐Decide what becomes of the Abbot's work

    Hint: Decide the Abbot's fate, then take a Long Rest with Liriel in the party.

    Reached through: The Abbot in The Church of St. Markovia

    Faith Kept

    Heaven's own messenger fell here, and the dawn did not. Liriel has stopped waiting for the Morninglord to fix Barovia. She has decided that is what she is for.

    • ☐Bring the dawn to the castle

    Hint: In Castle Ravenloft, take Liriel to the chapel and the altar step.

    The Dawn (ends: success)

    At the defiled altar of Castle Ravenloft, Liriel sang the morning hymn the way the novices sing the sun up, and for the length of the hymn real dawn came through the chapel windows, gold on the dust. It went out when she stopped. It came, though. It came.

    Reached through: The altar step in Castle Ravenloft, the Chapel

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Religion DC 15: (Something about him is holy, and wrong. What is he?)
    • Insight DC 13: (Watch how he looks at Ireena.)
    • Insight DC 10: It isn't, is it?

    Companion approval

    • Liriel Dawnsong: +3: You told me I didn't choose to kneel to Strahd; +2: You promised he'd never get that close to me again; +10: You asked me to show you the way back; +6: You told me you'd mend it; -5: You told me you don't need my hymn; +6: You admitted we'd lost our way a little
    • Godrick Pendlebrook: +1: You let me remind the Abbot of the sun at the unveiling; +2: You brought St. Andral home to his church
    • Kip Smudgewick: +1: You brought St. Andral home to his church
    • Thistle: +1: You brought St. Andral home to his church
    • Wren Featherfoot: +1: You brought St. Andral home to his church
    • Ratatoille: +1: You brought St. Andral home to his church

    People

    The Abbot, Father Lucian

    Places
