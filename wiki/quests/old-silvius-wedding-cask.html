    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Asked: Old Silviu, the cooper at the Wizard of Wines, wants his daughter Dana's wedding cask carried to Vallaki: the last cask of the year Elvir was born. The wedding is at noon in the square, the day after next, and the road is bad.
    Hint: Talk to Old Silviu at the Wizard of Wines about the cask.

    Carrying: We are carrying Old Silviu's wedding cask to his daughter Dana in Vallaki's square. It should not be shaken, or tasted.
    Hint: Take the cask to Dana in the square at Vallaki.

    Done (the end: success): Dana had her father's cask at her wedding in Vallaki, and Master Costel made a speech about it.

    ✦Every skill check in its conversations
    • Athletics DC 12: (Pad the cask with straw and lash it down.)
    • Animal Handling DC 12: (Work out how to buy off a hungry wolf.)
    • Persuasion DC 12: (Have everyone swear off the bung.)
