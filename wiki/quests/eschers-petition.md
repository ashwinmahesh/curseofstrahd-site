## Summary

Every night for nine years Escher has slid the same petition under the count's door: let me see the sun. Now an answer has come back.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

[Escher](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/escher.md), one of Strahd's cast-off consorts and now a vampire spawn, is locked in his suite in [Castle Ravenloft, the Tower Rooms](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-tower-rooms.md). To get there, climb the high tower stairway from the main floor (or the tower stairs from the Court of the Count) into [Castle Ravenloft, the High Tower](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-high-tower.md). Fight past the stair's sentries on the upper turns (8 vampire spawn and 8 swarms of bats at level 10), then take the door off the high landing.

![loc castle ravenloft spires rooms 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-castle-ravenloft-spires-rooms-1.webp)

His door off the tower gallery is locked. Pick it (DC 16; forcing it with Athletics is harder), cast Knock, or talk him into opening it: Deception DC 15 ("Tell Escher the count has sent for him") or Persuasion DC 15 ("Coax Escher into opening his door"). Your party gets one try at each of those two. Stealth DC 14 lets you listen at the door first and hear how badly he wants to be somewhere Strahd would never look.

Once the lowest-level hero in your party is level 10, his menu has **What's that paper you keep turning over?** That starts the quest. Escher is in his suite at any hour, except at night while this quest is running, when he is up on the roof.

### Step by step

1. Ask about the paper. He shows you one word of the count's answer: Yes. Before you agree, try **Insight DC 15: (Watch how he holds the paper.)** A success makes him unfold the rest: "Bring your friends." He admits he hid that part. This matters later, because it stops you being surprised in the fight. A failure is spent, but you can still agree.
2. Choose **We'll come.** (or **We'll come anyway.** after the reveal). The quest moves to Asked; Thistle +1, Wren +1. **Not tonight.** leaves the paper in his pocket, and you can ask again another time.
3. Wait for night (from 19:00 until 6:00). From the tower gallery outside his suite, take the narrow door to the roof stair and climb the ladder to [Castle Ravenloft, the Roofs](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-roofs.md). You come up on the south tower roof, and Escher is waiting at the parapet in his good red coat. He speaks as you come near.
   ![loc castle ravenloft spires roofs 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-castle-ravenloft-spires-roofs-1.webp)
4. The rest of the count's answer drops off the keep roof: [Querreth](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/querreth.md), the devil bound to keep the castle's roofs, with two [Wraith](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/wraith.md)s, the "Ones Who Asked", hanging in its chains. Querreth has 570 HP here. If you never read the full note, your party starts the fight surprised, against a Querreth with 520 HP. Querreth is immune to fire and poison and resists cold, shrugs off spells with Magic Resistance, and can refuse two failed saves a day. Its chains reach 10 feet and grapple (escape DC 16), so don't bunch up near it. Winning moves the quest to Held.
5. Talk to Escher again on the roof while it's still night. You sit up with him until 6:00, and the sun comes over the mountains.
   ![sq escher dawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/sq-escher-dawn.webp)
6. Choose **(Stand with him while it comes up.)**, or try **Persuasion DC 15: Not this one. Wait for a dawn he isn't in.** If the Persuasion fails, he begs you to let him have it, and the sun comes up on him anyway.

If day breaks before you get back to him after the fight, Escher returns to his suite and says "Tonight, then." Go back up the next night.

### Choices and endings

- **Done (success).** Escher burns in the sunrise and doesn't step back. In the pocket of his coat you find a [Helm of Brilliance](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#helm-of-brilliance) and 250 gp. Liriel +3, Thistle +3, Wren +2, Godrick +1. His suite is empty from then on, so ask him anything you still want (about the Heart of Sorrow, the back stair to the ravens' roost or the clockwork jester) before the dawn.
- **Waited (success).** The Persuasion works: he laughs, backs into the shadow of the stair, and gives you the same Helm of Brilliance and 250 gp, to wear at the count's funeral. Wren +2, Godrick +1, Liriel +1, Ratatouille +1. He goes back to his suite and still talks to you there.

You get one helm and 250 gp either way, not both.

### If you're stuck

- **The paper option isn't there.** The lowest-level hero must be level 10, and the quest must not have started yet. If you threatened Escher into talking about the heart (Intimidation), he turns cold and only tells you to get out, so you can't ask about the paper. If you fought him, he is gone and so is this quest.
- **Escher isn't on the roof.** He is only there at night. By day he is back in his suite.
- **Getting surprised by Querreth.** Only the Insight check in step 1 prevents it, and you only get one try at that.
