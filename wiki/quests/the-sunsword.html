    The Sunsword

    Type
    Main quest
    Region
    The Old Svalich Road · level 4
    Given by
    Madam Eva

    Madam Eva's Tent
    Next
    The Destined Ally

    Summary

    A blade of sunlight that once belonged to the devil's brother, broken from its hilt and lost.

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This quest starts at the end of Madam Eva's reading in The Seer at Tser Pool. Her third card is "a blade. Not steel: daylight with an edge". It names the place where the hilt of the Sunsword lies, and the quest goes straight into your journal at "Foretold", with the place written into the entry.

    How the reading picks the place

    Madam Eva deals from the Tarokka's common deck: forty cards in four suits (Swords, Stars, Coins and Glyphs), one to nine plus a master of each. The deck is shuffled from your playthrough's seed, and the first three cards go to the Tome, the Holy Symbol and the Sunsword, in that order. So:

    • The reading is fixed for the whole playthrough. It's dealt once, the first time she reads, and reloading or asking her to "say the reading again" never changes it.
    • The three treasures are always in three different places. Each card names one place, and no two cards name the same one.
    • A card means the same place whichever treasure it lands on. The Avenger always means Vladimir's keeping at Argynvostholt, whether it's dealt for the Tome, the Symbol or the Sword.
    • The journal tells you where. The quest's journal entry and hint spell out the place in plain words (the "Where" column below), and Madam Eva's verses are on The Tarokka Reading.
    • Some places appear on the map because of the card. If the card points to the Abbey of St. Markovia, Yester Hill, the werewolf den, Van Richten's tower, Old Bonegrinder or the Amber Temple, that place shows up on your travel map straight away. The Amber Temple's road still needs the Tsolenka gate open.

    Fifteen of the forty places are inside Castle Ravenloft, so there's a good chance at least one treasure waits in the castle itself.

    Step by step

    1. Find your card. At the reading a notice names each card as it turns (for example "The Sunsword: Paladin"), and the Sunsword's journal entry ends with the place it named in plain words. Look the card up in the table below.
    2. Go there and do what the row says. Most places are a container to open: a chest, a sarcophagus, a sack, a drawer. Some are a conversation, a pool to wade into, or a fight you have to win, and several have a lock, a trap or a guard in the way.
    3. Take it. The moment the hilt is in your hands its picture plays and the quest is done.
    4. Attune to it. It's a sentient legendary weapon that needs attunement, and the blade only appears for its attuned bearer.

    Choices and endings

    The quest has one ending, "Found". What the Sunsword does for you:

    • A blade of sunlight. It fights as a +2 longsword (versatile, finesse) that deals radiant damage, plus an extra 1d8 radiant against undead. Its weapon mastery is Sap.
    • It sheds true sunlight while the blade is out: bright for 15 feet and dim for 15 more. A Bonus Action widens or narrows the bright light 5 feet at a time, from 10 up to 30 feet. Radiant damage and sunlight both stop Strahd's Regeneration, and if he's dropped to 0 hit points while he stands in sunlight he can't turn to mist and flee to his coffin.
    • It has a mind of its own. It's sentient and chaotic good, and its purpose is to see Strahd and every vampire destroyed.
    • It can wake him, and end him. In his tomb by day, "Draw the Sunsword over him" wakes Strahd from his coffin into a fight. Once he's beaten and lying in it, "End it with the Sunsword's light" destroys him.
    • Strahd notices. Carrying it adds 3 points to Strahd's attention. When you face him he says "And you carry my brother's blade", and at the coffin, "My brother's sword."

    If you're stuck

    • Listen for hints. Many places drop a hint while a treasure is still there: a narrator line about something catching the light, or someone nearby mentioning it. Davian Martikov's ravens report a cold light in the winery; Ilinca and Morgantha both mention something in Bonegrinder's oven; Zuleika knows about the bundle at Mother Night's feet; Ezmerelda mentions the strongbox in Van Richten's lantern room; Kasha at the Pool of the White Sun has "seen something strange in the water"; Baba Lysaga smells it under Marina's stone. Once it's taken, those hints stop.
    • Missionary card, and you unbound the scarecrows? Then there's no fight to loot. Examine the scarecrow with the stitched mouth and choose "(Cut the stitches.)": the treasure is sewn up inside it.
    • Mind Strahd's room. If the treasure is in the same room where the reading says Strahd waits (for example the throne room, the study, the treasury, the chapel or his tomb), walking in starts his final parley and battle. See Where the Devil Waits, and be ready before you go.
    • Owned containers (Izek's chest, Rictavio's wagon, Lady Wachter's desk, and Luvash's chest if you open it yourself) count as theft if someone sees you open them. Pick your moment.
