## Summary

Stanimir has bet his cask of plum wine that nobody can tell a better tale at his fire than he can.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **Challenged**: Stanimir has put his cask of plum wine on the log and says nobody has told a better tale at his fire than he has. Three rounds: a tale, a true tale of the valley, and the camp's vote. If we lose, we dance on Lavinia's stage.
> *Hint:* Talk to Stanimir at the bonfire in the Tser Pool camp and take up his challenge.
>
> **Done** (the end: success): We told our tales at Stanimir's fire, the camp banged its cups, and one way or another everybody laughed. Stanimir says it was the best night the camp has had in thirty years, and he'll be gracious about it for exactly one night.

> [!abstract]- Every skill check in its conversations
> - Performance DC 12: (Tell a tale with all the trimmings.)
> - Deception DC 12: (Tell a tale that is entirely made up, and swear it is true.)
> - History DC 12: (A true tale of the valley, from what you have read.)
> - Nature DC 12: (A true tale of the woods and what lives in them.)
