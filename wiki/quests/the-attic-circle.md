## Summary

The Baron's son Victor is trying to leave Barovia by magic, and it is not going well for the servants.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

[Lydia Petrovna](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/lydia-petrovna.md), the Baron's wife, sews bunting in her parlour on the east side of [The Burgomaster's Mansion](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-burgomasters-mansion.md), on the north side of [Vallaki](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/vallaki.md)'s square. She's there before the festival, and afterwards unless Lady Wachter won. Ask her *"How is your son?"* She tells you Victor hasn't come down from the attic in four days, and neither have the two maids who carried his trays up. That starts the quest at **Heard**. (**Insight DC 12** on her shows the fear under the smile, and nothing more.) You can also just climb up and meet Victor, which starts the quest at Found.

![loc vallaki burgomaster mansion 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-vallaki-burgomaster-mansion-1.webp)

### Step by step

1. **Climb to the attic.** The narrow stair goes up from a corner of the great hall.
   - Mind the floor. The faintly glowing chalk circle is a trap: if you step in it, you make a Constitution save (DC 14) or take 4d6 force damage. A Search finds it (DC 10) and it can be disarmed (DC 15). **Arcana DC 14** on the circle itself also shows you what it is.
   - Read *Victor's notes* to learn what happened to the maids, Grigor and Mirela.
   - Search near the circle (Investigation DC 15) for a loose board hiding a [Wand of the War Mage, +1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#wand-of-the-war-mage-1).
   - The old trunk holds [Fine Clothes](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#fine-clothes), four candles and 3 gp.
   - Downstairs in the Baron's study, Investigation DC 15 finds a [Necklace of Fireballs](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#necklace-of-fireballs) tucked behind the festival ledger. The Baron's strongbox (lock DC 16) holds 60 gp and a [Potion of Healing](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#potion-of-healing), though he'll complain about it.
2. **Talk to Victor.** The quest moves to **Found**. [Victor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/victor-vallakovich.md) is sixteen, furious, and means to leave Barovia through his circle. Ask him about the circle and the maids, then choose how to stop him:
   - **Show him the flaw.** Learn it first, with **Arcana DC 14**, *"(Study the sigils of the circle.)"* (it isn't a social check, so you can try again if it fails), or by letting a Wizard *"talk to him as one wizard to another"* (that Wizard earns Heroic Inspiration; Ratatouille +1). Then tell him *"The destination sigil is wrong. There's nowhere for it to take you."* The quest ends at **Persuaded**, and he gives you a [Spell Scroll (Level 1)](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#spell-scroll-level-1) (Ratatouille +2, Liriel +1).
   - **Persuasion DC 14**, *"You'll die in that circle, Victor. Stop."*: **Persuaded** (Liriel +1, Ratatouille +1). If it fails, he turns hostile and won't hear it again, so use another route.
   - *"(Scuff the chalk lines until the circle is ruined.)"*: **Broken**. The trap is gone, and Victor hates you (Liriel +1, Ratatouille -2).
   - **Tell his father.** Before the festival, say in the Baron's great hall *"Your son's attic circle has killed two of your servants"*: **Reported**. The Baron locks the book away in his desk.
   - *"Help him finish it"* ends nothing (Liriel -2, Godrick -1, Ratatouille -1) and leaves Victor in danger.
3. **If Madam Eva named Victor as your ally**, *"Madam Eva's cards named you, Victor. Come with us"* brings him into your party ([The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md)). If you broke his circle or he's hostile, he'll come only for **Persuasion DC 12** or ten gold "for chalk". He leaves the circle behind when he comes: if this quest was still at Heard or Found (or you'd helped him finish it), it ends at **Persuaded**, without the scroll.

### Choices and endings

- **Persuaded, Broken or Reported** all end the quest safely. Taking Victor into your party settles it too (step 3). Only showing him the flaw pays anything (the scroll).
- **Vanished** (a failure) happens only if Lady Wachter wins [The Festival of the Blazing Sun](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-festival-of-the-blazing-sun.md) while the circle is still unsettled: you never dealt with Victor, or you helped him finish it, and he isn't travelling with you. He steps into it the day his father falls. During the aftermath an attic window in the mansion flashes white, and Lydia screams his name. If Madam Eva had named him your ally, that's lost too.
- If Lady Wachter wins and Victor is safe, he and his mother shelter in St. Andral's Church. If Madam Eva named him, he can still join you from there.

### If you're stuck

- **Lady Wachter might win the festival.** Settle the circle before the festival, or take Victor with you if Madam Eva named him; her victory is the only thing that sets it off.
- **The Baron won't hear your report.** He only takes it in the great hall before the festival, and only while the quest is at Found.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **Heard**: Lady Lydia Petrovna says her son Victor hasn't come down from the attic in days, and two maids who carried up his meals have not come down at all.
> *Hint:* Climb to the attic of the burgomaster's mansion in Vallaki and talk to Victor.
>
> **Found**: Victor has drawn a teleportation circle on the attic floor, copied out of a stolen spellbook. He means to leave Barovia through it. Two servants already tried it for him.
> *Hint:* In the mansion attic, study the circle's sigils (Arcana) and show Victor what's wrong, talk him out of it, or scuff the chalk.
>
> **Persuaded** (the end: success): Victor agreed to leave the circle alone. He hasn't forgiven us, but he's alive to hold the grudge.
>
> **Broken** (the end: success): We scuffed the circle's runes past mending. Victor will need years to draw another, if he can find the nerve.
>
> **Reported** (the end: success): We told the Baron about his son's attic. Victor's spellbook is locked in his father's study, and Victor hates us very precisely.
>
> **Vanished** (the end: failure): Victor stepped into his circle the day his father fell. There was a flash and a smell of hot iron. There is no Victor now.
