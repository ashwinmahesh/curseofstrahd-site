    Exethanter's Notes

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Amber Temple · level 10
    Given by
    Exethanter
    Starts at
    The Amber Temple: the Library

    Summary

    Exethanter, the last Warden of the Amber Temple, has forgotten what he was doing this morning. His notes have come unpinned.

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    In the Amber Temple, take the library passage from The Amber Temple: Hall of the Faceless God into The Amber Temple: the Library. Exethanter sits in his study at the back, at any hour, pinned all over with notes. Ask him "There are so many notes pinned to you. What were you doing this morning?" He is almost certain he was busy. The pins work loose when he walks, five scraps have fallen into his lap, and he asks you to look them over. The quest starts at Asked.

    You can ask on your first visit. The question doesn't wait on a level, the wardens' word or Something with a Lid.

    Step by step

    1. Read the five scraps. They're the steps of one small job, out of order: take the copper kettle down to the well; set it on the hob and speak the small word of kindling; measure two spoons from the grey tin into the pot while it warms; stay by the hearth until it whistles; pour. Exethanter asks which comes first, then which comes next, and so on.
    2. Pick the first scrap. The answer is "The scrap about the well." If you'd rather have a clue, try [Investigation DC 12] "(Check which scrap was written first.)". A pass shows the well note is the palest (his ink-pot freezes in the night, so the earliest line is always the faintest) and puts it first for you. A fail shows nothing and you can roll again.
    3. Pick the kindling. "The scrap about the small word of kindling."
    4. Pick the tea. "The scrap about the grey tin." (The blue tin is cemetery dust.)
    5. Pick the wait. "The scrap about waiting for the whistle." The pouring scrap goes last by itself, and the quest moves to Done.

    A wrong pick costs nothing. He corrects you mildly ("Otherwise the kettle is quite empty, you see") and you choose again from the same step, as often as you like.

    Choices and endings

    • Done (success). The notes say he was making tea. He shuffles to the cold hearth, speaks a quiet word that smells of cedar, and the copper kettle gives a thin whistle. He pours everyone a cup: each member of the party gains Heroic Inspiration. Wren (+1), Liriel (+1) and Kip (+1) approve. There's no gold or item. Afterwards, ask "Is the kettle still warm?" He has pinned the five notes to his left cuff, in order, with two pins each.
    • Lost (failure). If you smash his phylactery (Something with a Lid) while the notes are still out of order, the five scraps slide off his lap onto the floor and he never picks them up. It doesn't matter where you smash it: "(Smash it on the floor.)" in his study, or "(Smash it.)" at the jar on the library shelf. Talk to him afterwards and he tells you to leave them on the stones.

    If you're stuck

    • He isn't in his study? Once Master Vosk has been dealt with, Exethanter goes down to the sanctum in The Amber Temple: the Vaults and waits there to ask about the wardens' last rite. Down there he only talks about the rite. Answer "Seal it. Nobody should ever bargain here again." or "Leave it open. We may need what's here." and he goes back to his study ("Not yet." leaves him waiting in the vaults).
    • Don't attack him. Choosing "(Attack the lich.)" starts a very hard fight, and there's no tea after it: the quest can never be finished.
    • Finish the notes before you decide about the phylactery. Smashing it first fails this quest.

    Rewards

    What your companions think

    • Liriel Dawnsong: +2 you smashed Exethanter's phylactery and let him go; +1 you put Exethanter's notes in order, and he made tea
    • Thistle: +1 you smashed Exethanter's phylactery and let him go
    • Wren Featherfoot: +1 you put Exethanter's notes in order, and he made tea
    • Kip Smudgewick: +1 you put Exethanter's notes in order, and he made tea

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Asked: Exethanter has forgotten what he was doing this morning. Five of the notes pinned to him have come loose, out of order, and he can't tell which goes where.
    Hint: Talk to Exethanter in the Amber Temple and ask about his notes.

    Done (the end: success): Put in order, the notes said what he was doing this morning: making tea. Exethanter made us a cup.

    Lost (the end: failure): We smashed Exethanter's phylactery and let him go before his loose notes were in order. The five scraps lie where they fell, and whatever he was doing this morning stays out of order for good.
