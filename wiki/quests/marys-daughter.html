    Mary's Daughter

    Mad Mary's daughter Gertruda, sixteen and never once allowed outside, slipped out of the house a few nights ago and hasn't come back.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Village of Barovia · level 3
    Given by
    Mad Mary
    Starts at
    Mad Mary's House
    Rewards
    Necklace of Prayer Beads

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Mad Mary rocks by a cold stove in the front room of Mad Mary's House, the shuttered townhouse on Mary's lane in the south-west of the village. She's there at any hour. Speaking to her puts the quest at Heard. So does asking Bildrath Cantemir for news about "The woman weeping down the lane."

    Step by step

    1. Hear Mary out. Ask who Gertruda is and how she disappeared: four nights ago, the window open, and a man singing old songs under it some nights. Then:
      • "We'll look for her." moves the quest to Promised. Mary turns friendly and gives you a message for her daughter (Godrick +2, Kip +2, Thistle +1, Liriel +1). That message matters later.
      • "We can't help you. I'm sorry." moves it to Refused (Godrick -2, Kip -2, Liriel -1). The offer to look stays open, so you can come back and promise after all.
      • [Insight DC 12] "Is she as mad as the village says?" and a Cleric's prayer are just for colour.
    2. Look at Gertruda's room. Her room is at the back of the house and the door isn't locked. Search the window (DC 12): the latch was opened from the inside. Tell Mary "Mary, her window latch was opened from the inside." and the quest moves to Window Clue. A one-try [Persuasion DC 12] "You kept her alive for sixteen years, Mary. That isn't a crime." makes her friendly (Kip +1, Liriel +1). The room also holds a well-thumbed book of tales and a chest with a bottle of Perfume.
    3. Find her in Castle Ravenloft. Gertruda isn't anywhere near the village. She's in the guest bedroom of Castle Ravenloft, the Rooms of Weeping, up the stairs from Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count. That's late-campaign ground, tuned for a party of about level 10, so this quest waits until then.
    4. Talk her into leaving. Gertruda thinks she's a guest in a fairy tale. Any one of these breaks the spell without a roll:
      • "Your mother isn't angry. She said the door is unlocked now. All three locks." This needs your promise to Mary (Kip +2, Godrick +1).
      • "You're frightened, Gertruda." This needs a successful [Insight DC 13] "(Look at her carefully.)" first.
      • "Anastrasya slept in this room once, in gowns like yours." This needs Anastrasya Karelova's story from the brides in the lounge along the corridor.
      • "The ledger downstairs has an account in your name." This needs you to have read the count's ledger in the accountant's office of the Court of the Count.
      • A Cleric's blessing.

      [Persuasion DC 15] "He doesn't love you. He collects." also works, but it's one try, and if it fails she rings for her host. That closes the Persuasion option with Rahadin in step 5.

      Then say "Come home, Gertruda. Stay close to us." She joins you as a guest and the quest moves to Found (Godrick +3, Kip +3, Liriel +2, Thistle +2, Wren +1, Ratatouille +1). "Not yet" leaves her waiting.

    5. Get past Rahadin. Unless you've already beaten him, Rahadin stands at the head of the grand stairs in the Court of the Count while Gertruda is with you.
      • [Persuasion DC 16] "A guest who can't leave is a prisoner..." (not offered if Gertruda warned him) or [Intimidation DC 17] "Look at us, Rahadin. You're one man." makes him step aside (Godrick +2, Thistle +1, Kip +1). A failed roll means a fight.
      • "Then we go through you." means a fight with Rahadin (795 HP, AC 18) and four Strahd's Animated Armor, one of them at 50 HP (15,100 XP).
      • "Gertruda, go back to your room." ends the quest at Lost.
    6. Bring her home. Walk Gertruda into Mary's house. Mary is on her feet before the door is open, and the quest ends at Home. Mary gives you her mother's Necklace of Prayer Beads.

    Choices and endings

    • Home (success): the Necklace of Prayer Beads. Afterwards Gertruda lives in her own room, Mary has stopped weeping, and the boards are off one window.
    • Lost (failure): you handed her back on the stairs. Everyone in the party hates it (Godrick -5, Kip -4, Liriel -3, Thistle -3, Wren -3, Ratatouille -2), and when you go back, Gertruda is in a new green gown, smiling at you like a stranger.
    • Beating Rahadin adds 2 to how closely Strahd watches you.

    If you're stuck

    • Rahadin only waits at the stairs while Gertruda is travelling with you. If he's already been beaten, nobody stops you.
    • If none of the no-roll options shows up, talk to the brides in the lounge (for Anastrasya's story) or read the ledger in the accountant's office downstairs before you risk the Persuasion roll.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Heard: Behind the barred door of a townhouse near the main road, Mad Mary weeps for her daughter Gertruda, who went out at night and never came home.
    Hint: Talk to Mad Mary in her house near the village's main road and let her tell you about Gertruda.

    Promised: We promised Mary we'd look for Gertruda. She's sixteen, dark-haired, and has never seen the valley she's lost in.
    Hint: Ask Mary to show you Gertruda's room; the window may tell you more. Gertruda herself is far from the village.

    Refused: We told Mary we couldn't look for her daughter. Her weeping followed us all the way down the lane.
    Hint: If you change your mind, Mary is still in her house near the main road.

    Window Clue: Gertruda's window latch was opened from the inside, and she took her good shoes. Nobody carried her off. She went out to meet someone.
    Hint: Gertruda went out to meet someone grand. Look for her in Castle Ravenloft, in the Rooms of Weeping.

    Found: In a yellow guest room in Castle Ravenloft we found Gertruda, the daughter of Mad Mary of the village of Barovia, dressed in borrowed gowns and waiting for the count. She wants to go home, and she's coming with us.
    Hint: Take Gertruda home to Mad Mary's house in the village of Barovia.

    Home (the end: success): Gertruda is home. Mary unlocked all three locks and is keeping them that way.

    Lost (the end: failure): We gave Gertruda back to the count's chamberlain at the top of the grand stairs. She went up alone.
