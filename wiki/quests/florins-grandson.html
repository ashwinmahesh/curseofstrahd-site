    Florin's Grandson

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Village of Barovia · level 3
    Given by
    Old Florin
    Starts at
    Village Church

    Summary

    Old Florin prays every day that his grandson, who went up the castle road for work two winters ago, doesn't come back.

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Old Florin prays in the nave of the Village Church in the Village of Barovia from 7:00 to 18:00, walking a slow round between the pews. Talk to him and he says his grandson went up the road to the castle two winters ago, and he prays the boy doesn't come back. Pick "Tell us about your grandson." Tudorel went to ask the steward for work, because the roof was leaking and a boy of sixteen eats like a horse. Florin knows what walks down from that hill, so he prays the boy stays wherever he fell.

    You have two options here:

    • [Insight DC 12] "(He's holding something back.)": on a pass, Florin admits he told the boy the castle pays in silver and never runs short of firewood, and he won't forgive himself for it. On a fail he keeps it to himself. Either way you go on, and the result changes nothing later.
    • "We'll look for him." goes on without a roll.

    Both start the quest at Asked.

    Step by step

    1. Go to Wachterhaus in Vallaki. Travel to Vallaki and go into Wachterhaus, Lady Wachter's house on the north side of the square. Tudorel is the young footman pouring tea in her parlour. He wears good grey-and-black wool, and on his collar is the castle's crest, a raven and crown. He knows what you're thinking, and asks you to keep your voices down in the hall.

    2. Tell him who sent you. Pick "We've come from Old Florin, in the Village of Barovia." He goes still. Every month he gives Old Kolya the carter a silver piece for his grandfather, wrapped in tea-paper with no name on it. If Florin knew whose coat he wore to earn it, he'd throw the coin in the well. Tudorel begs you not to say where you saw him. "Tell him I died clean in the ditch if you have to, or whatever you think will let him sleep." The quest moves to Found.
    3. Go back to Old Florin. At the church, pick "We found Tudorel." and choose what to tell him (see below). "Not yet." leaves without choosing, and you can come back.

    Choices and endings

    There are three endings. Each ends the quest at Done. There's no gold or item, and none of them needs a roll.

    • The whole truth: "Tell him the whole truth: Tudorel is alive, in the count's livery, and likes it." Florin sits very straight for a long moment. Better a grandson in a count's coat than in a count's cellar, he says. He's angry at the fool, and glad he has the mutton. Then he asks if anyone has a stub of pencil he could borrow. Godrick, Thistle and Liriel approve (+1 each).
    • The lie: "Tell him Tudorel died, at peace." Florin takes it like a blow he has braced for for two years, thanks you for telling him plainly, and asks you to light a candle by the door on your way out. Kip and Wren approve (+1 each), and Godrick disapproves (-1).
    • The half-truth: "Tell him Tudorel is well and warm, and don't say whose livery." Florin looks at you for a long time. "That is either a very good lie or a very careful truth," and he won't pry into which. Ratatouille and Liriel approve (+1 each).

    Afterwards the coin keeps coming. After the truth or the half-truth, Florin sits by the brazier turning a silver coin over: Old Kolya left it on his windowsill, and castle silver's paper always smells of good tea. Tudorel, if you visit again, thanks you for going.

    If you're stuck

    • Old Florin is only in the church from 7:00 to 18:00.
    • Tudorel isn't in the parlour while the Festival of the Blazing Sun is going on. Go and find him before the festival begins.
    • The quest starts in the Village of Barovia, but Tudorel is in Vallaki, a later region. The journal hint points the way: Lady Wachter's household keeps footmen in grey-and-black livery.
    • There's no fight, and no roll you need to pass.

    Rewards

    What your companions think

    • Godrick Pendlebrook: +1 you told Old Florin the truth about his grandson; -1 you told Old Florin his grandson was dead
    • Thistle: +1 you told Old Florin the truth about his grandson
    • Liriel Dawnsong: +1 you told Old Florin the truth about his grandson; +1 you told Old Florin his grandson was well and asked no more
    • Kip Smudgewick: +1 you told Old Florin his grandson was dead
    • Wren Featherfoot: +1 you told Old Florin his grandson was dead
    • Ratatouille: +1 you told Old Florin his grandson was well and asked no more

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Asked: Old Florin's grandson Tudorel went up the castle road to ask for work two winters ago and never came back. Florin says he prays the boy doesn't. A coin comes by the carter every month, with no name on it.
    Hint: Look for Tudorel among the servants of Vallaki: Lady Wachter's household keeps footmen in grey-and-black livery. If her house has fallen, he waits in the market square; if she rules the town, he is with her in the burgomaster's mansion.

    Found: Tudorel is alive: a footman in Lady Wachter's house in Vallaki, in the count's livery, warm and fed and ashamed. He sends his grandfather a coin a month by the carter. He asked us to tell Florin whatever will be kindest.
    Hint: Go back to Old Florin, praying in the church in the Village of Barovia, and tell him what became of his grandson.

    Done (the end: success): We told Old Florin what we chose to tell him about Tudorel. He took it the way he took it, and the coin still comes every month.
