    The Ravens' Ransom

    ✦Side quest

    Urwin and Danika Martikov's younger boy flew out of the inn's window after dark to look at the castle, and didn't come home.

    Region: Vallaki (party level about 5)
    Given by: Urwin Martikov
    Starts: Urwin Martikov in The Blue Water Inn (Taproom)
    Rewards: Elven Chain, 200 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This quest only opens once the Martikovs have shown you that they are the Keepers of the Feather. That happens when you speak to Urwin Martikov at The Blue Water Inn: he closes the shutters and the taproom fills with ravens. He only does this if your party has never struck down a raven and the town hasn't fallen to Lady Wachter, and one of these is true:

    • the town stood with neither the Baron nor Lady Wachter, or
    • the festival is over and St. Andral's bones are back in the church (The Bones of St. Andral), or
    • you fed the raven that walked beside you on the road and the bones are back.

    After that, the next time you ask Urwin "Heard anything interesting?", he leans in low across the bar instead. His younger boy Bray flew out of the window after dark on a dare to look at the castle. Now a raven that won't change back hangs in a silver cage in a dead pine on the hill trail above Lake Zarovich. It's a trap for whoever comes, and the Keepers can't be seen to go themselves. The quest opens at Asked. Urwin isn't behind the bar while the festival is running.

    Step by step

    1. Get to the cage. Go to Lake Zarovich: the Fishers' Landing, an hour from Vallaki, and take the path up from there to The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich. It's half an hour more, and the trail appears on your map once this quest has started. "A silver cage in a dead pine" hangs beside the trail. Danika Martikov has nothing to add in the kitchen except that she'll kill him, then feed him.

    2. Choose how to open it. The branches above the cage are full of something that isn't leaves.
      • Perception DC 13, "Look at the branches." This shows you bats by the hundred and two pale shapes in good coats with their eyes open. It's only information, and either result lets you carry on choosing.
      • Stealth DC 15, creep in and open the cage first. If it works, you have the raven in your hands before the fowlers drop down, and the fight starts normally. If it fails, a twig snaps and they surprise you.
      • Walk up and open it. That's what the trap is for, and you're surprised.
      • Back off. You can come back later.
    3. The fight (raven_trap).
      • Below level 6: The Count's Fowler (a Vampire Spawn at 90 HP) and two Swarm of Bats.
      • Level 6: the Fowler at 120 HP, a second fowler and three swarms.
      • Level 7 or higher: four swarms instead of three.

      Being surprised means the fowlers get their first round free, so the Stealth check is worth taking if you have a good sneak. The fowlers' claws grapple and their bite drains maximum HP from anyone they're holding. Stay close to each other, and pull grabbed friends free.

    4. Open the cage again after the fight. Winning doesn't bring Bray out by itself, and the journal still says Asked. Examine the cage again: the raven turns back into a skinny ten-year-old boy, and he keeps looking up the valley toward the castle.
    5. Check him before you take him home (optional). Insight DC 14 ("Watch where he keeps looking") shows two small marks on his wrist. The fowlers wanted to send him home so they could listen through him. Pass or fail, two cures then appear:
      • Cast Remove Curse, if someone can.
      • Religion DC 14: keep him in the daylight and pray over him. It takes six hours. If it fails nothing is lost, and you can try again.

      Or just pick "Take him home". Either way the journal moves to Home.

    6. Finish at the inn. If Bray is cured, ask Urwin "About Bray." If he isn't, Bray sits at the kitchen window watching the castle road, and Urwin won't pay until he's himself. Cure him at the inn the same way, with Remove Curse or Religion DC 14 (six hours, and you can retry). That finishes the quest on the spot. Urwin gives you the Elven Chain his grandfather flew in and 200 gp, and the quest ends at Done.

    Choices and endings

    There's one ending: Bray home and himself. You get the Elven Chain, 200 gp, and approval from Godrick Pendlebrook, Liriel Dawnsong and Thistle (+2 each) and Wren Featherfoot and Ratatoille (+1 each). Killing the fowlers adds a mark to Strahd's attention. The only real choice is where you cure Bray: on the trail, or later at the inn.

    If you're stuck

    • No ransom news from Urwin. You haven't seen the ravens in his taproom yet (see Getting the quest). If you struck a raven down on the road, the Keepers never open up to you, and this quest can't start.
    • No hill trail on the map. It appears once this quest has started, and you reach it from the fishers' landing.
    • The fowlers are dead but there's no Bray. Examine the cage again.
    • Urwin keeps saying "Sit with him". Bray still has the marks. Cure him at the inn with Remove Curse or the Religion check.

    Journal stages

    Asked

    Bray, Urwin and Danika's younger boy, flew out after dark to look at the castle and was caught. A silver cage hangs in a dead pine on the hill trail above Lake Zarovich with a raven in it that won't change back. It's a trap for whoever comes, and the Keepers can't be seen to.

    • ☐Find the cage on the hill trail above Lake Zarovich and bring Bray home

    Hint: Go to the hill trail above Lake Zarovich and find the silver cage in a dead pine. Look at the branches before you go close.

    Reached through: Urwin Martikov in The Blue Water Inn

    Home

    Bray is home at the Blue Water Inn.

    • ☐Make sure nothing is listening through him
    • ☐Tell Urwin

    Hint: Talk to Bray at the Blue Water Inn and watch where he keeps looking (Insight), then talk to Urwin.

    Reached through: A silver cage in a dead pine in The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich

    Done (ends: success)

    Bray is home and himself, and the castle still doesn't know about the Martikovs' cellar. Urwin gave us the mail his grandfather flew in, and money Danika told him not to argue about.

    Reached through: Urwin Martikov in The Blue Water Inn; Bray in The Blue Water Inn

    Fights

    The count's fowlers drop out of the dead pine, and the bats come down after them like a black sheet shaken… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when fowlers ready and level ≥ 7: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), A Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 4 × Swarm of Bats (3800 XP; surprise: party)
    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when fowlers ready and level ≥ 6: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), A Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 3 × Swarm of Bats (3750 XP; surprise: party)
    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when fowlers ready: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 2 × Swarm of Bats (1900 XP; surprise: party)
    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when not fowlers ready and level ≥ 7: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), A Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 4 × Swarm of Bats (3800 XP)
    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when not fowlers ready and level ≥ 6: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), A Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 3 × Swarm of Bats (3750 XP)
    • The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, when not fowlers ready: The Count's Fowler (Vampire Spawn), 2 × Swarm of Bats (1900 XP)
