## Summary

Boyar Vintila Crainic has waited two hundred and six years in Castle Ravenloft to present a petition about a boundary stone. It needs the household's four seals.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **Asked**: Boyar Crainic's petition needs the four seals of the household before it can be presented: the valet's, the secretary's, the verger's and the lamplighter's. Each will lend theirs for one small thing the wedding night stopped.
> *Hint:* Ask the ghost servants of Castle Ravenloft for their seals: Ghenadie, Anghel Daskal, Nicodim and Vlaicu.
>
> **Sealed**: The petition has all four seals. It has to be received by the Steward in the dining hall of Castle Ravenloft.
> *Hint:* Take the sealed petition to the Steward in the dining hall of Castle Ravenloft.
>
> **Received**: The Steward has received the Boyar's petition. Tell the Boyar.
> *Hint:* Tell Boyar Crainic, in the audience hall of Castle Ravenloft, that the Steward has received his petition.
>
> **Done** (the end: success): Boyar Crainic has been received after two hundred and six years. His boundary stone is under Lake Zarovich, and he is entirely satisfied.
