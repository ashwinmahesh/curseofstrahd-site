    Called by Name

    Big Tobar's son went into the trees after his dead mother's voice. Every night since, a voice from the gorge under Tser Falls has called his father in the boy's.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Old Svalich Road · level 4
    Given by
    Big Tobar
    Starts at
    Tser Pool Encampment
    Rewards
    Efficient Quiver, 100 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    The quest starts with Zora, who walks the track between the road and the fire at the Tser Pool Encampment. Ask her "Heard anything interesting?" and, among her other news, she tells you about Big Tobar's boy Nelu, who stood up from the fire three nights ago and walked off toward the falls after his dead mother's voice. That puts the quest in your journal.

    Then find Big Tobar, the huge man mending a wheel at the east side of the camp. He is there at any hour. Once you have heard Zora, he has a new option: "Zora says your son went after a voice." Without Zora's rumour that option never appears, so talk to her first.

    Step by step

    1. Hear Tobar out. He tells you about Nelu calling "Da, come down" out of the gorge. You can try Insight DC 12 to read whether he thinks the boy is alive (he doesn't, really); pass or fail, the conversation carries on the same way and he asks you to go to the falls after dark. When you agree, he adds that Nelu has been down there three days without bread and won't last three more. The clock starts as soon as he asks, whatever you answer: get Nelu off his ledge within three days, or the quest fails (see "Choices and endings"). The journal warns you while the time runs.
    2. Go to Tser Falls. Take the path north from the road and head for the cairn glade on the west side, where a new spot appears: "The edge of the gorge". At night (7 in the evening to 6 in the morning) the voices and the fight wait there (steps 3 and 4). By day there are no lights: Nelu is curled on his ledge twenty feet down, and you can bring him up at once and skip the fight (you still get its XP). Use Athletics DC 13 (rope off a pine), Survival DC 12 (an old goat path down the rock), or Misty Step or Spider Climb if someone knows the spell. A failed roll costs half an hour, and you can try again. Then skip to step 6.
    3. Face the voices. At the edge, the lights in the gorge call each of you by name in the voice of someone you've lost (your companions each hear their own), and under it all a boy's voice. The party's best makes a Wisdom DC 14 check. Pass and you hold your ground; fail and someone walks toward the edge, and the fight starts with your party surprised.
    4. Fight the wisps. It's Will-o'-Wisps and what follows them. If your lowest party member is below level 4: one wisp and two Bats. At level 4: two wisps and a Shadow, or one wisp, a Shadow and two Bats if someone walked toward the edge. At level 5 or higher: five wisps and a Shadow, or two Shadows if someone walked. The wisps have AC 19, resist slashing, piercing and bludgeoning (and acid, cold, fire and necrotic), are immune to lightning and poison, and can turn invisible. Each hit is 2d8+2 lightning, and one can finish off a party member at 0 hit points with Consume Life, so pick up anyone who drops at once. Force, radiant, psychic and thunder damage hit them at full strength.
    5. Bring Nelu up. Examine the edge of the gorge again. Nelu is alive on a ledge twenty feet down. Get him with Athletics DC 13 (rope off a pine), or with Misty Step or Spider Climb if someone knows the spell. A failed climb costs half an hour and a bad rope burn, but it isn't final: just try again. "Shout that you'll be back" lets you leave and return later.
    6. Take him home. Go back to the camp and talk to Big Tobar.

    While you're at the falls, it's worth searching around: an Arcana DC 13 check at the stone heaped with tokens reveals a Pearl of Power (search DC 13); Religion DC 12 at the cairn of names reveals a Necklace of Adaptation (search DC 13); climbing the claw-marked pine (Athletics DC 13, 1d6 damage on a fall) shows a satchel with Goggles of Night (search DC 14). The fallen trunk hides a Bag of Tricks (Rust) (Investigation DC 12), a woodcutter's stump has a Weapon, +1 (search DC 12), and a rain-soaked pack holds rope, torches, a Potion of Healing and 6 gp.

    Choices and endings

    There are three endings:

    • Home (success): when you bring Nelu back, Big Tobar gives you 100 gp and an Efficient Quiver, and Thistle (+2), Godrick (+2), Kip (+1) and Wren (+1) approve. From then on Nelu sits by the fire at the camp, and Zora's news changes to say the numbers match again.
    • Answered (failure): three days pass after Tobar asks you, and Nelu is still on the ledge. After that there's no fight at the falls: at night a new light hangs lowest in the gorge, calling for its Da in a boy's voice, and by day the ledge is empty. Tobar grieves at his wheels.
    • No Fire (failure): the camp has turned on you (see below), so Tobar is gone. A Nelu you've brought up waits at The Vistani Camp by the lake outside Vallaki's west gate, and talking to him there ends the quest, with no money and no quiver.

    If you're stuck

    • No option with Tobar? You need Zora's rumour first ("Heard anything interesting?").
    • Nothing at the edge of the gorge? It only appears after Tobar has asked you. The lights and the fight only come at night; by day you can climb down to Nelu instead.
    • Don't leave it too long. Nelu lasts three days from Tobar's ask. Once he's off the ledge, the clock stops, and Tobar will wait for you.
    • Stay off the lip of the falls at night unless you want a fight: stepping onto it after dark springs a Werewolf, a Dire Wolf, four Wolves and a Rat on you, with your party surprised. The gorge edge you want is in the cairn glade to the south-west, off the lip.
    • Don't fall out with the camp. If you fight Stanimir's people or threaten Madam Eva into turning them hostile, Big Tobar and everyone else at the fire is gone for good, and the quest can't end at Home.

    Fights

    • The lights come up out of the gorge like lanterns climbing a stair, and every one of them is still talking in…, in Tser Falls: Will-o'-Wisp, Shadow, Bat. Scales with your level.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Rumored: Zora says Big Tobar's son Nelu stood up from the fire three nights ago, said "that's Mama", and walked off toward the falls track. His mother has been dead six years.
    Hint: Find Big Tobar mending wheels at the Vistani camp at Tser Pool and ask about his son.

    Asked: Tobar went after Nelu and heard him calling out of the gorge under Tser Falls: "Da, I'm down here, come down." Nobody at the camp will go to the falls at night. Tobar asked us to.
    Hint: Go to Tser Falls after dark and look over the edge of the gorge.

    Voices: At the falls after dark there are lights in the gorge, and voices. They called each of us by name, in the voice of someone we've lost. Will-o'-wisps: they feed on grief, and they speak in whatever voice will bring you to the edge.
    Hint: At Tser Falls after dark, deal with the lights in the gorge, then search the gorge's edge for Nelu.

    Nelu Found: Nelu is alive, on a shelf of wet rock in the gorge, three days hungry. The lights had been calling his father in his voice.
    Hint: Take Nelu back to Big Tobar at the Tser Pool camp. If the camp has turned on you, take him to the Vistani camp by the lake outside Vallaki's west gate.

    Answered (the end: failure): We left Tser Falls to the dark too long. By the time we came, Nelu had answered the voices below.

    Home (the end: success): Nelu is home. Big Tobar dropped a wheel on his own foot and didn't notice. Nelu sits with his back to the falls now, and sings over anything that calls.

    No Fire (the end: failure): The Tser Pool camp has turned on us, and Big Tobar's fire is out. Nelu is alive, but we could not bring him home: he is with the Vistani by the lake outside Vallaki, and nobody will tell him why the road to the pool is shut.
