## Summary

Iancu's cousin Tsura rode the falls path with a letter three days ago. Her horse came home with web on its saddle.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

[Iancu](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/iancu.md) throws knives at a stump at the [Tser Pool Encampment](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/tser-pool-encampment.md) between 8 in the morning and 6 in the evening. Once everyone in your party is level 3 or higher, he has stopped throwing. Talk to him and he tells you his cousin [Tsura](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/tsura.md) rode out by the falls path three days ago with a letter in her coat, and her horse came home with web on the saddle. That starts the quest and puts [The Webbed Gully](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-webbed-gully.md) on your travel map, on the path between the crossroads and Tser Falls.

### Step by step

1. **Travel to the Webbed Gully** (from the River Ivlis crossroads or from Tser Falls).
   ![loc ivlis spider gully 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-ivlis-spider-gully-1.webp)
2. **Go to the long bundle of web on the ledge** at the back of the gully. Tsura is inside it, alive, with one eye open.
3. **Read the web first.** Try **Nature DC 12** ("Look at how the webs are strung"). On a success you see that every strand runs to the cracks in the walls, and the fight that follows starts normally. On a fail, or if you just cut, the spiders catch your party **surprised**. Either way, cutting her down starts the fight.
4. **Fight the spiders:** three [Giant Spiders](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-spider.md) (AC 14, venomous bite, a web that restrains on a failed DC 13 Dexterity save) and two swarms of spiderlings ([Swarm of Insects](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/swarm-of-insects.md), which resist weapon damage, so use spells or area attacks on them), plus a [Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/rat.md) if you read the web first. The spiders climb the walls, so ranged attackers earn their keep here; free webbed allies quickly.
5. **Talk to Tsura** at the ledge after the fight. The letter was hers, to her mother: she was running from a wedding she didn't want.

While you're there, the older bundles further up can be cut open with **Medicine DC 13** for 20 gp and an [Antitoxin](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/equipment.md#antitoxin) (1d4 poison damage on a fail).

### Choices and endings

Choose where Tsura goes. Either way she gives you the wedding rings, a pair of [Boon Companions’ Bands](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#boon-companions-bands), and **40 gp**, and Thistle (+2), Wren (+2), Godrick (+1) and Kip (+1) approve.

- **Back to the camp** to tell her mother to her face: Iancu later says she's back by the fire and nobody mentions the wedding.
- **On to [Krezk](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/krezk.md)**: Iancu later says she sent word from Krezk, where she mends nets for an old man, and her mother is packing to visit.

### If you're stuck

- **Iancu just throws knives?** Someone in the party is below level 3, or it's outside his hours.
- **Can't find the gully on the map?** It only appears once Iancu has told you about Tsura.
