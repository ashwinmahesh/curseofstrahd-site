## Summary

Ezmerelda d'Avenir keeps a book of the valley's killers with a price beside each name, and pays in a hunter's goods for proof that one is dead.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

You ask [Ezmerelda d'Avenir](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/ezmerelda-davenir.md) for work; she never offers it. Wherever she stands, her menu has "Have you any paying work?" (it shows while her trust is 0 or more). Choosing it, or clicking her while she travels with you, opens her own talk and starts the quest at **Heard**. The first time, she unwraps a battered book from its oilcloth: the valley's killers, a price beside each name. She pays for proof, not promises, in a hunter's goods, and she names three at a time.

She can be in four places:

- **[Van Richten's Tower](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/van-richtens-tower.md)**, at her camp on the shore by her painted wagon. She's here if Madam Eva's cards didn't name her as your ally, until she leaves for Vallaki (and again if she comes back). See [The Hunter's Tower](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-hunters-tower.md).
- **[The Abbey of St. Markovia](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-abbey-of-st-markovia.md)**, on a boulder at the top of the switchback, but only if the cards named her.
- **[The Blue Water Inn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-blue-water-inn.md)'s stable yard**, by [Rictavio](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/rictavio.md)'s wagon, after you've told her her teacher is there. She waits while he's still at the inn, and stays after their reunion until he leaves.
- **In your party.** Click her anywhere.

Her three chance meetings on the road (the Svalich road at night, the fields of Berez, below Argynvostholt) don't have the option.

### Step by step

1. **Open the ledger.** In her talk, choose "Let us see what work is in the ledger." She fills three slots, in this order:
   - names you've already killed and she hasn't paid for, in the book's order (the table below). Kills from before you ever met her count;
   - then open hunts in the region you're standing in, if your lowest-level hero is high enough for them;
   - then the hardest open hunts your level reaches, counting down from the Eye Below.

   With nothing killed, that's Dragan alone at level 3; the wagon and Dragan at level 4; Morgantha, the Vine Mother and the fowlers at level 5; Wintersplinter, Morgantha and the Vine Mother at level 6; Khazan, Kiril and the Grandsire at level 7; Baba Lysaga, Khazan and Kiril at level 8; and the Eye Below, Sarkhaza and Lysaga from level 9. Where you stand matters when she travels with you: on the Old Svalich Road at level 7 she names Dragan, the wagon and the Huntsman instead. Below level 3 with nothing killed, she says nothing in reach is worth your boots and closes the book.
2. **Ask about a name, if you like.** "Tell us about Dragan at Tser Falls." (and the same for each name) gets her briefing: where it is, and what to watch for. It's words only, and nothing starts a fight. "That's enough names for now." takes you back to her menu.
3. **Kill it.** Each name is a fight somewhere else in the valley, most of them inside another quest (see the table). Only the kill the story records counts.
4. **Claim the bounty.** Open the ledger again, wherever she is. A dead name now shows as a claim, such as "Dragan is dead at Tser Falls." or "We killed the tinker's wagon and freed Iosif." She strikes it out and pays. Then the ledger opens again with the next three names, so you can collect several payments in one visit. The first payment moves the quest to **Paying** and raises her trust by 1. The seventh moves it to **Half Struck**.
5. **The fourth and eighth names.** Whichever names they are, the fourth payment also brings her spare light crossbow, a [+1 Light Crossbow](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#weapon-1): you're no longer lucky beginners, she says. The eighth brings a dagger from her wagon, [Mage Breaker](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#mage-breaker): +1, and a concentrating creature it hurts has Disadvantage on the Concentration save.
6. **Strike the thirteenth.** When the last name is paid, she closes the book and the quest ends at **Ledger Struck**. The ledger option leaves her menu.

Every payment also carries healing, set by your lowest-level hero at the moment she pays:

- **Level 7 or 8:** 2 [Potion of Healing (Greater)](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#potion-of-healing-greater), or 3 for Lysaga, Sarkhaza and the Eye Below.

### Choices and endings

There's one ending, **Ledger Struck** (success), when all thirteen names are paid. She closes the book herself and, for once, isn't in a hurry. There's no deadline, no penalty and no companion approval either way. Each name's own quest carries what changes in the world; the ledger only pays.

Your choices elsewhere change which names she'll still brief as hunts:

- **Morgantha**, once you've bought the children back and left the coven standing.
- **Baba Lysaga**, once you've bargained with her or Strahd has broken her.
- **Kiril**, once he leads the pack.
- **The Grandsire**, once you've given Petru to him.

Only a kill strikes a name. Morgantha and Lysaga can still be killed after a bargain: "Your bargaining days are over." at the mill, or "We never said we'd leave you alive." (or "End it." by her broken hut) in Berez. Kiril can too, if you left the children in the den: go back, tell him "We've changed our minds about the children.", then "Then we'll take them." She pays for those kills like any other, briefed or not.

### If you're stuck

- **No "Have you any paying work?"** Her trust is below 0. Robbing her wagon (−2), a failed lie about her teacher (−1) and telling her Kiril leads the pack (−1) all lower it. Giving her the letter from the tower, telling her where Rudolph is, the reunion, and each chance meeting with her on the road all raise it.
- **Can't find her.** Once you tell her Rudolph is at the Blue Water Inn, she leaves the tower that night for the inn's stable yard. If he's left the inn before she gets there, she's back at her tower camp. The easy way round all of it is to have her join you ("Hunt with us." at trust 2, or at the abbey if the cards named her) and click her wherever you are.
- **A name she won't brief.** She only names what your lowest-level hero is ready for, three at a time, hardest first. Stand in that name's region with her in the party, or simply go and kill it: kills are always paid.
- **A kill she doesn't list.** Only the kills in the table count. Dragan is the werewolf that waits at the lip of Tser Falls at night, not the wolves that find your camp. Wintersplinter has to fall on the crown of Yester Hill. Khazan driven out of his body comes back; only destroying him for good counts.
- **Better healing.** The healing in a payment follows your level when she pays, not when you made the kill.
