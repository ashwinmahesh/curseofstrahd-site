    Grandda's Trail

    Thistle was raised in the deep woods by a trapper called Fen Burley. He followed a wolf the size of a pony into a wall of fog and never came back. She followed his trail blazes in.

    Side quest

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This is Thistle's personal quest (the Human Ranger). It starts by itself at the very beginning on The Old Svalich Road: when you look back into the mist at its east end, or, if you never do, when you reach the wolves in the narrows, with Thistle in the party. A custom hero in her place never gets it. The blazes and Fen Burley himself only appear while Thistle is in the party.

    There's no level or approval gate on starting it, but Fen is in the werewolves' den, a level 7 area. After a Long Rest, "Thistle wants a word" up a tree about her grandda; "We'll help" is +2.

    Step by step

    1. The first blaze. At The River Ivlis Crossroads, examine Notches in a dead tree by the signpost: three notches and a crow feather. Thistle presses a fresh feather into the cut. The quest moves to The Blazes (+2). Once you've been to Vallaki, this also puts The Hill Trail on your travel map.
    2. The second blaze. On The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, examine A blazed pine. It's cut left-handed, with RUN scratched under it, and it points up into the hills. The quest moves to The Wolf Road (+2), and the blaze puts The Werewolf Den on your travel map. While you're there, searching under a stump (DC 13) finds a hunting trap.
    3. Find the den. Travel to The Hills above Lake Zarovich and go down into The Werewolf Den. If you walk into the cave mouth before Zuleika Toranescu has vouched for you on the hillside (and before Emil is free), the guards attack: three Werewolf and a Dire Wolf, or four werewolves and two dire wolves at level 7, or five and two at level 8. Werewolves have Pack Tactics, so don't let two of them gang up on one hero. The Pack in the Hills covers the den itself.
    4. Grandda. Fen hangs in silver chains in the west alcove, beside Emil Toranescu. He's half wolf now. Talk to him and the quest moves to Found Him (+3).
    5. Decide what to do for him. His menu offers:
      • (Cast Remove Curse on him.) Someone in the party has to be able to cast Remove Curse right now; Liriel's level plan prepares it from level 6. This lifts the wolf out of him and gives the Fen Cured ending.
      • (Use the key on his chains.) This needs Emil's key. Examine A peg of keys in the caves first; the peg is gone once Emil is freed. This gives the Fen Freed ending.
      • Athletics DC 15 to tear his rings out of the rock, or Sleight of Hand DC 14 to pick his cuffs. Each gets one try (Fen tells you to find the key), and success gives the Fen Freed ending.
      • What can we do for you? Fen asks to be let out of the chains, or failing that for "the other thing", done quick. Thistle's option (Do the other thing, the way he taught her.) gives the Fen Rests ending.
      • We'll be back for you. This leaves him chained and waiting. He stays until you decide.
    6. Kiril's offer (in passing). Once Emil is free, Kiril Stoyanovich comes up the passage to answer Emil's challenge. The first time, with Thistle there, he offers her a place running with his pack, and she turns him down to his face. That earns Thistle Heroic Inspiration.
    7. Afterwards. At the next Long Rest, Thistle wants a word about how it ended.

    Optional, Madam Eva: Thistle's truth (she likes towns and fits nowhere) gives +3 and Heroic Inspiration, but then her camp talk about the jam cakes doesn't come. Spending the one question on "Is Grandda Fen alive?" is +3.

    Choices and endings

    All three endings count as success:

    • Fen Cured (Remove Curse, +10): the wolf goes out of him and leaves an old trapper, who says he'll wait for Thistle in Krezk. If Thistle is Close or better (approval 25 or more), he gives her his trapper's cloak, a Cloak of Elvenkind. If she's Strained or worse (-25 or lower), she stands between you and Fen and won't let the party's magic near him. The key and the two checks still work, and the spell comes back once her approval recovers.
    • Fen Freed (key, Athletics or Sleight of Hand, +5): he leaves on four legs for the high woods. "Don't follow my blazes this time. Make your own."
    • Fen Rests (mercy, +2): Thistle does it the way he taught her and buries him under a rowan.

    Fen doesn't appear anywhere after this, not even in Krezk. What changes is Thistle's camp talk afterwards: she braids feathers for him, answers his howl, or sits silent by the fire.

    If you're stuck

    • The den isn't on the map. Examining the lake blaze with Thistle (step 2) marks it. Other ways to hear where it is include the Krezk gate watchman's news, saving Bluto at Lake Zarovich: the Fishers' Landing, and the howls or the wolf on the ridge on the lake roads (Survival DC 13 for the howls).
    • Fen isn't there. He only appears with Thistle in the party, and he's gone once you've chosen his fate.
    • Remove Curse doesn't show. Nobody in the party can cast it right now, because no one knows it or there's no slot left. A Long Rest restores the slot.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    The Trail: Last spring a wolf the size of a pony came out of the Thornwood and took two of Fen Burley's hounds. Fen went after it, leaving his trail blazes the way he always did: three notches and a crow feather in the cut. The trail ran into a bank of fog that had not been there the day before. He didn't come back. Thistle followed the blazes.
    Hint: With Thistle in the party, look for Fen's blaze on a dead tree at the River Ivlis crossroads.

    The Blazes: Fen's blaze at the crossroads, a year weathered: three notches and a crow's feather gone to a quill. He came this way, alive and steady, following something big. Thistle put a fresh feather in the cut.
    Hint: Fen's next blaze is on the hill trail above Lake Zarovich (the Hill Trail on the map, past Vallaki). Take Thistle there.

    The Wolf Road: Another of Fen's blazes, on the trail above Lake Zarovich, cut with his left hand. He was hurt. The blaze points up into the hills where the wolves den, and below it, scratched in a hurry, a word Thistle has never seen him write: RUN.
    Hint: Follow the blaze into the hills: the werewolves' den above the lake's north shore. Take Thistle.

    Found Him: In the werewolves' den, chained in silver beside the pack's old leader: Fen Burley. Kiril's pack bit him beyond the mists and dragged him home, and he refused to hunt children for them, so they hung him up to wait for the moon to change his mind. It hasn't yet.
    Hint: Decide with Thistle what to do for Fen in the den's caves: lift the curse (Remove Curse), free him, or give him rest.

    Fen Cured (the end: success): The curse came out of Fen Burley like a splinter, and left an old man sitting in the straw, crying and laughing and patting Thistle's face to be sure of it. He'll wait for her in Krezk, where they have walls she can climb.

    Fen Freed (the end: success): Thistle broke Fen's chains, and he went out of the den on four legs with a sound that might have been a laugh. He'll keep to the high woods. 'Don't follow my blazes this time, lass. Make your own.'

    Fen Rests (the end: success): Fen asked Thistle to do it before the moon came, and she did, quickly, the way he taught her. She buried him under a rowan with a crow feather in the bark. She didn't speak for the rest of the day.
