    Sanctuary

    ✦Main quest

    Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.

    Region: Vallaki (party level about 5)
    Given by: Ireena Kolyana
    Starts: Town Guard in Vallaki (East gate)
    Rewards: a level (milestone)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Sanctuary begins at Vallaki's east gate the first time you arrive, as long as Ireena is travelling with you. Bringing her here is the end of Ireena's Escort. If she agreed in the Village of Barovia to come with you but isn't in your party yet, the gate adds her, and Ismark too if he joined the escort.

    The Town Guard stops you in the gateway. Whatever you say, he finishes with the town's rules, and the journal reads Arrived. If you pick "This is Ireena Kolyana, daughter of the late burgomaster of Barovia", the watch passes her name on to the Baron, and Ireena makes it clear she's here to hide, not to visit. The gate conversation only happens once, so if Ireena isn't with you the first time you walk in, this quest never starts.

    Step by step

    1. Take Ireena to St. Andral's. St. Andral's Church stands in the churchyard north-east of the square. Father Lucian kneels at the stripped altar; he's there at any hour except while the festival is running.
      • At your first meeting he tells you the saint's bones were stolen from the crypt three nights ago, which starts The Bones of St. Andral.
      • With Ireena beside you, he admits he can't promise her anything until the saint is home, and the quest moves to Church Unsafe.
      • Answer "We'll find the bones" (Liriel +2, Godrick +1). The Religion DC 12 question about the bones only adds lore, and it's only offered at this first meeting.
      • Ask "Could Ireena shelter here meanwhile?" and he sends you to the Blue Water Inn.

    2. Decide where she waits while you hunt for the bones.
      • Keep her with you. She stays a guest in your party and joins in conversations. That matters for Izek (see below).
      • Lodge her at the inn. At The Blue Water Inn, south of the square, ask Urwin Martikov at the bar "Can Ireena stay here for now?" (offered only while she's with you and the bones aren't back). She leaves your party for the room over the Martikovs' own, and the quest moves to At Inn. If Ismark is with you, choose whether he stays to guard her ("Ismark, stay with your sister") or comes with you. You'll find them in the guest rooms.

    3. Bring the saint home. Follow The Bones of St. Andral: Milivoj the gravedigger, then Henrik the coffin maker. Then either raid his storeroom by day or keep a night's vigil in the church. Finally, give the bones to Lucian ("We've brought St. Andral home"). The return also gives Vallaki's milestone level, if the festival hasn't given it already.
    4. Offer her sanctuary.
      • If Ireena is with you when you hand Lucian the bones, he offers her sanctuary on the spot. Later, with her in the party, "Ireena needs sanctuary" brings the same choice back.
        • "Stay here, Ireena. You'll be safe" leaves her at the church: Sanctuary (Godrick +1). If Ismark is with you, decide whether he stays with her or keeps travelling with you.
        • "Come with us. We'll keep you safer than walls" keeps her in your party: Travels On.
      • If she's at the inn, go and talk to her there; she's already had word from Lucian.
        • "Then go to St. Andral's. You'll be safest there" sends her to the church, and Ismark with her if he's guarding her: Sanctuary.
        • "Come with us again" brings her back into the party: Travels On.
      • Once she's at the church, you can still talk to her in the nave and ask her to "Come with us again" (Travels On). Ismark can be called back from the inn or the church with "Come with us again, Ismark".

    Choices and endings

    • Sanctuary. Ireena sleeps on holy ground at last. On some night from your fourth day in Barovia on (and only once you've seen Strahd watching you from the ridge), the church bell tolls by itself and Strahd calls to her from the churchyard gate. He can't come any further. Tell him to leave, ask what he's afraid of, or simply stand beside her; holy ground holds either way. If the Baron wins the festival and Izek is still alive and doesn't know the truth, Ireena tells you he stands at the church door every dusk.
    • Travels On. Ireena stays in your party as a guest. Walls didn't save her father, she says.
    • Leaving Vallaki. If Lady Wachter wins The Festival of the Blazing Sun, Ireena refuses to spend one more night in her town. Wherever she is in Vallaki (in your party, at the inn or at the church), she rejoins you and means to go on to Krezk and its abbey. Ismark comes back too if he'd stayed to guard her. This happens even if she had already taken sanctuary.
    • Izek. While Ireena is in your party or lodged in Vallaki (inn or church), Izek Strazni considers her within reach. If you back the Baron at the festival and Izek is alive and unaware that she's his sister, he asks for her as his reward (The Doll Collector). Walking through the square with her by day before the festival is also how Izek first sees her.
    • Who else is watching her. While she's in your party, Vallaki notices her. The Baron offers condolences and congratulates her on escaping "that dreary little village". Lady Wachter looks her over the way a jeweller looks at a stone. Outside the west gate, Arrigal offers her a place in the Vistani wagons, "where his carriages can't go". Insight DC 15 shows he'd carry her somewhere very quickly, and not where she'd want to go. There's no way to accept his offer, so it's a warning, not a choice.
    • This quest pays no gold. Where Ireena ends up (the church, the inn, your party, or on the road to Krezk) is remembered, and later chapters react to it.

    If you're stuck

    • The inn isn't safe forever. If Ireena is still lodged at the inn on some night from your fourth day in Barovia on (and you've seen Strahd on the ridge), arriving in Vallaki or at the inn after dark, or resting there through the night, can find her at an open window, entranced. Strahd stands outside it on nothing. Your ways to stop him:
      • raise the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, if you carry it;
      • have a Cleric or Paladin stand in the window;
      • speak a warding prayer (Religion DC 15);
      • call her back to herself (Persuasion DC 15, one try only);
      • go for him, which starts a short fight against Strahd and a Swarm of Bats. He leaves after two rounds, or sooner once he's hurt.

      Each failed prayer or plea lets her take a step toward him. Two steps, or choosing "Let her go", and she goes with him to the castle. The Sanctuary entry then stays open in your journal. Taking her into your party, or to the church once the bones are back, avoids the scene entirely.

    • Lucian won't take her in. Not until the bones are back in the crypt. Urwin's room is only offered before then; afterwards, take her to the church.
    • Lucian isn't in the church. While the festival is running, he's in the square.
    • The quest never appeared. It only starts if Ireena was with you the first time you came through the east gate.

    Journal stages

    Arrived

    We brought Ireena to Vallaki, as Ismark wanted. She means to ask for sanctuary at St. Andral's Church.

    • ☐Take Ireena to Father Lucian at St. Andral's

    Hint: Take Ireena to Father Lucian at St. Andral's Church in Vallaki.

    Reached through: Town Guard in Vallaki

    Church Unsafe

    Father Lucian cannot promise Ireena sanctuary: the bones that made his church holy ground are gone.

    • ☐Recover the bones of St. Andral
    • ☐Find Ireena somewhere to stay meanwhile

    Hint: Get St. Andral's bones back for Father Lucian; meanwhile Urwin Martikov at the Blue Water Inn can give Ireena a room.

    Reached through: Father Lucian in St. Andral's Church

    At Inn

    Ireena has taken a room at the Blue Water Inn while we deal with Vallaki's troubles. The Martikovs keep a close watch on their door.

    • ☐Find Ireena true sanctuary, or take her with us again

    Hint: Once St. Andral's bones are back, take Ireena to the church; or ask her to travel with you again. She's at the Blue Water Inn.

    Reached through: Urwin Martikov in The Blue Water Inn

    Sanctuary (ends: success)

    Ireena has taken sanctuary at St. Andral's Church, holy ground once more. For the first time since her father died, she slept through the night.

    Reached through: Ireena Kolyana in The Blue Water Inn; Father Lucian in St. Andral's Church

    Travels On (ends: success)

    Ireena chose to keep travelling with us. Walls didn't save her father, she says; we might do better.

    Reached through: one of Strahd's visits; Ireena Kolyana in St. Andral's Church; 1 more; Father Lucian in St. Andral's Church

    Leaving Vallaki (ends: success)

    Ireena won't stay in a town ruled by the devil's friends. She means to go on toward Krezk, with us.

    Reached through: Baron Vargas Vallakovich in Vallaki; Lady Wachter in Vallaki

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Religion DC 12: What made St. Andral's bones holy?
    • Insight DC 15: There's something he's not saying.
