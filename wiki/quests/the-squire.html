    The Squire

    ✦Side quest

    A Vallaki boy has run away to Argynvostholt to swear the dead Order's oath, and since the Order won't take the living, he means to wait on the squires' graves until he's dead.

    Region: Argynvostholt (party level about 8)
    Given by: Cosmin
    Starts: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt (The courtyard); Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor (Sir Godfrey's room)
    Rewards: Arrow-Catching Shield, 150 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    You may hear about the boy first in Vallaki. Ask the Town Guard at the gate "Heard anything interesting?" and he mentions the cooper's son, Cosmin, who ran north with a wooden sword to join the dead knights. That's only gossip and doesn't start the quest. The journal starts at Argynvostholt in one of two ways:

    • Ask Sir Godfrey Gwilym "Heard anything interesting?" (on the front steps the first time, in his room upstairs after that). The quest starts at Rumored.
    • Walk up to Cosmin himself. He sits wrapped in a horse blanket on the last of the small squires' graves in the south-west corner of the grounds, west of the drive. Meeting him moves it to Met.

    The quest is only open until the Order is put to rest: once the beacon is lit, Cosmin and Godfrey are gone.

    Step by step

    1. Talk to Cosmin. "Why do you want it so badly?" tells you his ancestor Ioan, the Order's last squire, is written down as "fled" in the Order's chronicle, and Cosmin wants one Vallaki name in the book that didn't run. Persuasion DC 17 ("Come home") doesn't change anything: pass or fail, he won't leave until Ioan's name is clean.
    2. Read the headstone he's sitting on. Investigation DC 13 finds small letters cut low on the back of the last stone: Ioan carved his brothers' headstones, then rode out with his lord's last word for the valley. If you miss it, look again. Knowing the dragon's last words from his echo in the chapel (The Dragon's Beacon) works just as well. Nearby, History DC 14 at the small headstones points you to something left on a squire's grave (Holy Symbol (Emblem), search DC 14).
    3. Ask Godfrey about Ioan. In Godfrey's room on Argynvostholt, Upper Floor, ask "Do you remember a squire called Ioan?" With the stone read or the last words known, he remembers sending Ioan down to the valley on his own horse, and how Vladimir wrote "fled" against his name. The quest moves to Truth. He tells you what a squire swears before the oath: a night's vigil at the graves, with the banner, against the men-at-arms who come out of the courtyard for anything living. Without the stone or the last words, he can't find the memory yet.
    4. Stand the vigil, after dark. Go back to Cosmin at night and choose "(Stand the vigil with him.)". The dead squires rise to watch, and you fight Squires Vigil on the graves:
      • level 9: the Gate Warden and 2 Knights Who Forgot (Revenants) with 8 Men-at-Arms (Phantom Warriors), 11,000 XP;
      • level 8: the same three knights with 4 Men-at-Arms, 8,200 XP;
      • level 7: the Warden, one Knight and 5 Men-at-Arms, 7,100 XP;
      • below that: the Warden at 90 HP with 4 Men-at-Arms.

      This is a hard fight. Rest first. Radiant damage is the answer to both kinds of enemy: the revenants regenerate 10 HP a turn unless fire or radiant hit them, and the phantom warriors halve weapon damage.

    5. Talk to Cosmin again once the last man-at-arms falls. Godfrey comes down with the Order's book, writes Ioan's name right and Cosmin's under it, and gives you Ioan's Arrow-Catching Shield and 150 gp. The quest is Done. Godrick +3, Liriel +2, Thistle, Wren and Ratatoille +1.

    Choices and endings

    There's one ending. Afterwards Cosmin stands at the gatehouse as the first living squire of the Order in four hundred years, and Godfrey's news mentions him.

    If you're stuck

    • Light the beacon last. If the Order is put to rest before you finish, Cosmin leaves the graves and the quest can't be completed.
    • Godfrey's Ioan question only appears in his own menu in his room, after you've met Cosmin. If he's travelling with you as your ally, tell him to "Wait for us at Argynvostholt" and ask him there.
    • The vigil option needs both the Truth stage and night.

    Journal stages

    Rumored

    A cooper's boy from Vallaki, Cosmin, has gone to Argynvostholt to join the dead knights, and Sir Godfrey says he sleeps on the squires' graves in the cold until the Order will take him.

    • ☐Find Cosmin on the squires' graves at Argynvostholt

    Hint: Find Cosmin on the squires' graves in Argynvostholt's grounds.

    Reached through: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt; Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor

    Met

    Cosmin wants the Order's oath to clear his ancestor Ioan, the Order's last squire, whom the chronicle calls fled. He won't go home a coward's grandson.

    • ☐Find out what really happened to Ioan

    Hint: Ask Sir Godfrey at Argynvostholt about the squire Ioan. The headstone Cosmin sits on may jog his memory (Investigation).

    Reached through: Cosmin in Argynvostholt

    Truth

    Ioan didn't flee. Sir Godfrey sent him on his own horse with the dragon's last words for the valley, after Ioan had cut his brothers' headstones. A squire's first oath was the vigil: a night at the squires' graves with the banner, and the men-at-arms will come out of the courtyard for the living.

    • ☐Stand the vigil with Cosmin at the squires' graves, after dark

    Hint: Go back to Cosmin on the squires' graves after dark and stand the vigil with him.

    Reached through: Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt; Sir Godfrey Gwilym in Argynvostholt, Upper Floor

    Done (ends: success)

    Cosmin stood the vigil, the dead squires touched his banner, and Sir Godfrey wrote him into the Order's book under Ioan's corrected name. The first living squire of the Order in four hundred years.

    Reached through: Cosmin in Argynvostholt

    Fights

    The men-at-arms come out of the courtyard in their grey ranks, still fighting the last battle, and turn… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • Argynvostholt, when level ≥ 9: The Gate Warden (Revenant), 2 × A Knight Who Forgot (Revenant), 8 × Man-at-Arms (Phantom Warrior) (11000 XP)
    • Argynvostholt, when level ≥ 8: The Gate Warden (Revenant), 2 × A Knight Who Forgot (Revenant), 4 × Man-at-Arms (Phantom Warrior) (8200 XP)
    • Argynvostholt, when level ≥ 7: The Gate Warden (Revenant), A Knight Who Forgot (Revenant), 5 × Man-at-Arms (Phantom Warrior) (7100 XP)
    • Argynvostholt: The Gate Warden (Revenant), 4 × Man-at-Arms (Phantom Warrior) (4600 XP)

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Persuasion DC 17: Come home. Your mother's at the north gate every night.
    • Investigation DC 13: (Look at the last headstone, the one he's sitting on.)

    • Wren Featherfoot: +1: You stood the squire's vigil with Cosmin
    • Ratatoille: +1: You stood the squire's vigil with Cosmin
