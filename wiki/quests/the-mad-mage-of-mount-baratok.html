    The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok

    ✦Side quest

    A powerful wizard lives alone on Mount Baratok with his memory gone. Someone took it on purpose.

    Region: Mount Baratok (party level about 9)
    Given by: The Mad Mage
    Starts: The Mad Mage in The Mad Mage's Hut (The hut); Mordenkainen in The Mad Mage's Hut (The hut)
    Rewards: Spell Scroll (Level 1) ×2, Ring of Spell Storing

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Mount Baratok is on the travel map from the start. It's 5 hours from Vallaki, 2 hours up from Van Richten's Tower and 4 hours from the Tsolenka Pass, and the mountain roads can throw dire wolves, ogres, giant spiders or (at night) werewolves at you. Climb the goat track to the stone hut in the frost garden on the east side. Inside The Mad Mage's Hut, The Mad Mage looks up from his dragonchess board and speaks first, asking whether you're real. That starts the quest. The hut is a safe place to rest.

    Step by step

    1. Talk to him. He doesn't know his own name ("I am... Osric. No. Osric was a goat."), only that he went down to the castle with an excellent plan and that a little old woman with a jar said "hush now".
      • Insight DC 14: every time a word slips away, his eyes go to the cairn on the summit. A miss tells you nothing, and the cairn is there whether you spot this or not.
      • A wizard in the party can name the spell he keeps muttering, Mordenkainen's: it hurts him (Ratatoille +2, and Heroic Inspiration for the wizard).
      • Dragonchess: playing him wins Ratatoille and Wren +1.
      • The lightning question: from level 8, ask about the lightning on the glass to start The Forgotten Storm.
      • The cards: if Madam Eva named him as your ally, he cheerfully agrees to come and then forgets who you are mid-sentence. He can't join until he's himself again.
    2. Give him back his mind. Any one of these works.
      • The spellbook in the cairn. Climb to the summit in the north-west corner of the mountain. The cairn is ringed by his own ward, a thunder trap (spot DC 15, disarm DC 15, or Dexterity DC 15 against 4d6 thunder). Instead of disarming it, you can try Arcana DC 16 on the cairn to unpick it (one try; a miss costs 2d8 lightning). "Unstack the stones." finds his spellbook and two Spell Scroll (Level 1), and the quest moves to Book Found. Back in the hut, "Show him the spellbook from the cairn." gives him his name, then "Read him the last page." brings him back. No rolls needed.
      • The witch's jar. Baba Lysaga in The Ruins of Berez keeps his memory in a jar on her shelf. You get it either by winning Intimidation DC 15 when you blackmail her, or by taking the labelled jar from her shelf once she's dead or broken (The Witch of Berez). The quest moves to Jar Found. "Uncork the witch's jar for him." brings him back.
      • Greater Restoration. If the party is level 9 or higher and has a cleric, druid or bard, that character can cast Greater Restoration on him from the menu.
      • Talk him back. Arcana DC 16, one try only. A miss fills the hut with a short, furious snowfall and the option is gone.

    3. Mordenkainen. Restored, he remembers coming to Barovia to kill Strahd, losing, and having the witch bottle his mind. The quest moves to Restored, and he gives you the Ring of Spell Storing he's worn for forty years without knowing why (Ratatoille +5; Kip, Liriel and Godrick +1). Before you settle what he does next, use his new menu:
      • "The gate in the Tsolenka Pass is held by dead men." gets you the watchword, "cold hearth, shut door", the easy way through The Tsolenka Gate.
      • "Heard anything interesting?" starts The Mage's Lost Pages. Later answers lead to The Name Over the Door and The Drowned Library.
      • From level 8, ask about the lightning on the glass if you haven't yet.
      • Ask what he knows about Strahd and about the Amber Temple.
    4. What he does next.
      • If Madam Eva named him: "Madam Eva's cards named you. Will you come?" He joins you as a guest, The Destined Ally is found, and this quest ends at Joined.
      • Otherwise: "Will you help us against him?" He stays on his mountain to rebuild his spells and promises to come when you go up to the castle. This quest ends at Promised, and he stays in his hut to talk to.

    Worth a look while you're on the mountain:

    • Ring of glass on the trail: a wand fused into it (search DC 15: Wand of Lightning Bolts).
    • Summit: a stone that isn't part of the cairn (search DC 16: Ioun Stone (Intellect)), and a cleft in the rock with 15 gp and a potion of healing.
    • Frost garden: a page blown into the snow (search DC 13: Spell Scroll (Cone of Cold)).
    • Inside the hut (Investigation DC 17 each): a chess piece that isn't a piece (Figurine of Wondrous Power (Serpentine Owl)) and one book on the shelf that isn't blank (Tome of Understanding). His travelling satchel holds rations and a component pouch.

    Choices and endings

    • Joined (success, only if he's your Tarokka ally): Mordenkainen travels and fights beside you as a guest.
    • Promised (success): he stays home, mending, and has promised to come to the castle.
    • Either way, a restored Mordenkainen is what the epilogue remembers: one morning he vanishes from his mountain, leaving a thank-you note that's slightly too pleased with itself.

    If you're stuck

    • Once he joins you, his hut conversations are over. He leaves the hut, and a guest can't be talked to, so you can't get the Tsolenka watchword, start or finish The Mage's Lost Pages, hand in The Forgotten Storm, or hear his news about Khazan. Do all of that before you say "Will you come?". The promise ending has no such cost.
    • The Greater Restoration options only appear once every party member is level 9.
    • If you tried the Arcana talk and failed, use the book, the jar or the spell.

    Journal stages

    Met

    An old wizard lives in a stone hut on Mount Baratok, playing dragonchess against nobody. He doesn't know his own name, and the weather does whatever he forgets he told it to.

    • ☐Find a way to give him back his memory

    Hint: Look for what was taken from the old man: a warded cairn on Mount Baratok's summit, and Baba Lysaga's jars in Berez.

    Reached through: The Mad Mage in The Mad Mage's Hut; Mordenkainen in The Mad Mage's Hut

    Book Found

    Under a warded cairn on the summit of Mount Baratok we found a spellbook in a strong, impatient hand, hidden by someone who didn't want it found, not even by himself.

    • ☐Show the spellbook to the old man in the hut

    Hint: Show the spellbook to the old man in the hut on Mount Baratok. Baba Lysaga's jars in Berez hold the rest.

    Reached through: A cairn on the summit in Mount Baratok

    Jar Found

    Among Baba Lysaga's jars was one labelled in her scrawl: the wizard on the mountain, his remembering. Something silver moves inside it.

    • ☐Take the jar to the hut on Mount Baratok

    Hint: Take the jar to the old man in the hut on Mount Baratok.

    Reached through: A jar with a label in Baba Lysaga's Hut; Baba Lysaga in Baba Lysaga's Hut

    Restored

    He remembers. His name is Mordenkainen. He came to Barovia to destroy Strahd, lost, and had his memory bottled by the witch of Berez.

    • ☐Ask him what he knows

    Hint: Ask Mordenkainen in his hut what he knows.

    Reached through: The Mad Mage in The Mad Mage's Hut; Mordenkainen in The Mad Mage's Hut

    Joined (ends: success)

    Mordenkainen walks with us now. Madam Eva's cards called him the broken one. He is mending.

    Reached through: The Mad Mage in The Mad Mage's Hut; Mordenkainen in The Mad Mage's Hut

    Promised (ends: success)

    Mordenkainen is staying on his mountain to rebuild his spells. When we go up to the castle, he says, he will come.

    Reached through: The Mad Mage in The Mad Mage's Hut; Mordenkainen in The Mad Mage's Hut

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Arcana DC 14: What made that cloud?
    • Insight DC 14: Watch where his eyes go when he loses a word.
    • Arcana DC 16: Talk him back to himself, one spell at a time.

    Companion approval

    • Liriel Dawnsong: -2: You blackmailed Baba Lysaga over Marina's murder; +1: You gave Mordenkainen back his name
    • Godrick Pendlebrook: -2: You blackmailed Baba Lysaga over Marina's murder; +1: You gave Mordenkainen back his name
    • Kip Smudgewick: -1: You blackmailed Baba Lysaga over Marina's murder; +1: You gave Mordenkainen back his name
    • Ratatoille: +2: You named Mordenkainen's own spell to him; +1: You sat down to a game of dragonchess with a mad old man; +5: You gave Mordenkainen back his name
    • Wren Featherfoot: +1: You sat down to a game of dragonchess with a mad old man

    People

    Baba Lysaga, The Mad Mage, Mordenkainen

    Places

    Baba Lysaga's Hut, Mount Baratok, The Mad Mage's Hut
