## Summary

Of the five children brought home from the werewolves' den, the smallest came home different, and something big circles the farms below Krezk's wall.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

You first have to free the five stolen children from the werewolves' den (see [The Children in the Pen](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-children-in-the-pen.md)). Once you have, the smallest of them, [Little Petru](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/little-petru.md), is in the goat pens on the west side of [Krezk](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/krezk.md), day and night. Any of these starts the quest:

- Ask the [Krezk Watchman](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/krezk-watchman.md) at the gate *"Heard anything interesting?"*. He tells you Petru sleeps with the goats and that something bigger than a wolf has been circling the farms (**Rumored**).
- If you can speak with animals, any goat in the pens tells you the boy who sleeps there smells more of wolf every night (**Rumored**).
- Just talk to Petru (**Asked**).

### Step by step

1. **Talk to Petru.** *"What happened to you in the den?"*: the moon came for him before you rescued them, and the Grandsire, the pack's eldest, licked his face and said the runt was his. He'll come for Petru at the full moon, which is very close.
2. **Look at him closely, then decide whether to cure him.** *Insight DC 13* ("Look at him the way a goat would") shows that he's a wolf nearly all the way through. Whether you pass or fail, the cure options appear afterwards. To lift the curse, someone who can cast [Remove Curse](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/spells/level-3-spells.md#remove-curse) uses it on him, or you read a [Spell Scroll (Remove Curse)](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/spell-scrolls.md#level-3-scrolls) over him. Far beyond the wall, something howls at the loss. Curing him is optional: the fight and the reward are the same either way, and the Grandsire is angrier if you did. Only the cure makes him a boy for good, though.
3. **Wait with him for the moon.** Come back at night and choose *"(Wait with him by the pens for the moon.)"*. [The Grandsire](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/the-grandsire.md), a wolf the size of a cart horse, comes over the wall into the square with the old wolves of his pack and demands the runt. Choose *"(Stand in front of the boy.)"* to fight, or *"(Hear what it wants.)"*: he offers the pack's word to take no more Krezk children in exchange for Petru. You can still say *"No."* and fight.

![sq grandsire](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/sq-grandsire.webp)

4. **Fight the Grandsire** (**Grandsire**) in Krezk's square:
   - level 9: [The Grandsire](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/the-grandsire.md) (AC 16, 310 HP), 3 Old Wolves of the Pack ([Werewolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/werewolf.md)s) and 2 [Dire Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/dire-wolf.md)s;
   - level 8: the Grandsire at 330 HP, 2 werewolves and 2 dire wolves (6,800 XP);
   - level 7: the Grandsire at 180 HP, 2 werewolves and a dire wolf;
   - below that: the Grandsire at 140 HP with one werewolf and one dire wolf.

   His bite knocks you prone, and everything here has Pack Tactics, so keep your fighters back to back rather than strung out alone. His howl frightens everyone within 60 feet (Wisdom save DC 15). He turns two failed saves a day into successes, and between your turns he snaps or lopes away. Kill the smaller wolves first to take away his advantage.
5. **Talk to Petru again** after the fight. The quest ends at **Done**.

### Choices and endings

- **Done (you fought).** Petru's mother thanks you without words, and Dmitri pays Krezk's bounty for a child-taking wolf: 300 gp and the [Iron Bands of Bilarro](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#iron-bands-of-bilarro) that have hung in the watch house for generations. Godrick +2, Liriel +2, Thistle +2, Wren +1, Ratatouille +1. Afterwards the watchman reports that Petru sleeps in a bed and nothing circles the farms. Petru himself tells you how it went: if you cured him, he's a boy all month now; if you didn't, the curse is still on him, and his mother locks him in the pen with the goats on full-moon nights.
- **Given (the quest fails).** *"(Give Petru to the Grandsire.)"* He walks off into the dark with the old wolf and runs on all fours before they reach the trees. The pack keeps its word: no more children are taken from Krezk's farms. There's no reward. Godrick -3, Liriel -3, Thistle -2, Wren -2, Ratatouille -2.

### If you're stuck

- **Petru only appears after the den's children are free.**
- **The wait option needs night**, and only appears while the quest is at Asked (talk to Petru at least once first).
