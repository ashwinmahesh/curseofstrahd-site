## Summary

Argynvost gave his hoard away: every Midwinter the Order left a silver coin on every doorstep in the valley. The last winter's tithe never went out.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

This quest opens at level 9 (every character in the party has to be level 9). It starts at [Argynvostholt](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/argynvostholt.md) in either of two ways, and both put it at **Asked**:

- Ask [Sir Godfrey Gwilym](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/sir-godfrey-gwilym.md) in his room on [Argynvostholt, Upper Floor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/argynvostholt-upper-floor.md) *"A dragon's house. Where did he keep his hoard?"* He explains the Dragon's Tithe: the dragon gave his silver away, a coin on every doorstep, every Midwinter. The last tithe was chested in the undercroft the night the house fell, and its keeper went down to it and never came back up.
- Or examine the draught under the servants' table in the servants' hall, at the east side of [Argynvostholt, Ground Floor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/argynvostholt-ground-floor.md). This way works even once the knights are at rest and Godfrey is gone.

### Step by step

1. **Go down to the undercroft.** Once you've been told about the tithe, a stair opens under the servants' table and leads down into [The Undercroft](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-undercroft.md). At the far end of the vault, [The Gilded Knight](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/the-gilded-knight.md) sits on the tithe chest, grown over with four hundred years of silver, counting. He challenges you as you come close.
2. **Talk to him.** Your options:
   - *"Godfrey sent us. It's time it went down to the valley."* He refuses: the valley belongs to the count now, and he won't let the count's people have the dragon's silver.
   - *Insight DC 15* ("Watch his hands on the chest") shows a squire's shield cut nearly in two at his feet. Somebody tried to carry the tithe down the hill that night, and he stopped them. A failed Insight can't be tried again, but it costs you nothing.
   - *"Let them go home."* is only offered if you know the dragon's last words: from his echo in the chapel, or by clearing Ioan's name with Godfrey in [The Squire](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-squire.md) (reading Ioan's headstone alone, as when Cosmin goes home after the beacon, doesn't count). It reaches him. He wants the silver off him but can't let go, so you still have to fight, but **he starts at 140 HP with three harnesses instead of 710 HP with twelve**. Use it if you can.
   - *"Then we'll carry it down ourselves."* starts the fight at full strength.
3. **The fight.** The Gilded Knight ([The Gilded Knight](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/the-gilded-knight.md), AC 19) rises with twelve An Escort's Harness ([Animated Armor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/animated-armor.md)), or three if the dragon's last words reached him. It's the same fight at every level. He swings twice with a silvered longsword, can paralyse one of you with his Covetous Glare (Wisdom save DC 16), turns two failed saves a day into successes, and between your turns gets two extra moves, either a sword stroke or a step. He regenerates 10 HP every turn unless fire or radiant damage hit him, so put fire or radiant on him every round, and save your best save-or-suffer spells until his Legendary Resistance is spent. Winning moves the quest to **Fought**.

![gilded rises](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/gilded-rises.webp)

4. **Open the tithe chest.** Use it once he's gone. In what's left of his armour you find a [Golden Idol of Good Fortunes](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#golden-idol-of-good-fortunes), and then you decide what happens to the silver.

### Choices and endings

- **Delivered: send it down to the valley, as the dragon meant.** You get 250 gp. Godrick +3, Kip +3, Liriel +2, Wren +2, Thistle +1. If the knights still walk, Godfrey has them carry it up and ride it out at Midwinter. If they're at rest, you carry it to the villages yourselves. Either way, weeks later you see old dragon coins on strings around children's necks.
- **Kept.** You get 1,200 gp. Godrick -3, Kip -3, Liriel -2, Wren -1, Ratatouille +1.
- *"(Leave it for now.)"* lets you come back and decide later.

### If you're stuck

- **No option to ask about the hoard?** Godfrey only asks once everyone in the party is level 9, and only from his own room (not while he travels with you). The draught under the servants' table works too.
- **The stair down isn't there** until Godfrey or the draught has told you about the tithe.
