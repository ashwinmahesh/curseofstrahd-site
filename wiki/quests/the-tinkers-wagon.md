## Summary

A Vistani tinker sells trinkets from a green wagon at the crossroads, and the travellers who buy from him don't come back down the road.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

Two ways in:

- **[Zora](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/zora.md)** at the [Tser Pool Encampment](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/tser-pool-encampment.md): ask "Heard anything interesting?". At the end of her news she mentions Iosif, a tinker who used to be one of theirs, selling trinkets from a green wagon at the crossroads. Four travellers who bought from him never came past the camp again, and the wagon gets fatter.
- **Iosif himself.** [Iosif the Tinker](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/iosif-the-tinker.md) sits on the board of a green wagon by the signpost at [The River Ivlis Crossroads](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-river-ivlis-crossroads.md) from 8 in the morning to 6 in the evening. Talking to him starts the quest even if you never heard Zora.

### Step by step

1. **Go to the crossroads by day** and talk to Iosif. He's selling pots, a doll from the Durst house and a music box that never stops, five gold each, and he never gets down off the board.
   ![loc svalich crossroads 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-svalich-crossroads-1.webp)
2. **Work out what's wrong.** Two checks, one try each:
   - **Insight DC 13** (watch him while he talks): he keeps glancing at his left foot and mouths "don't".
   - **Perception DC 14** (look at the wagon, not the man): the wheels are one piece with the axle, a shaft twitches, and a grey, wet chain runs from his ankle into the boards.

   A failure shows you nothing. Pass either one and a new option appears: **(Go for the chain at his ankle.)**

   There's also a way that needs no roll: ask him "Why don't you ever get down off the board?" He laughs and says he'll never leave the wagon, and something under the paint shivers. If neither check has passed, a new option then appears: **(Pull the blanket off his knees.)** So two failed checks don't leave you stuck.
3. **Go for the chain (or pull the blanket off).** Either way you pull the blanket off, the chain has grown into his skin, and the wagon stands up off its wheels.
4. **Fight the Tinker's Wagon.** [The Tinker's Wagon](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/the-tinkers-wagon.md) is a Huge mimic: AC 14, 110 HP below level 5, 220 at level 5 and 450 from level 6, immune to acid. Each turn it lashes with two sticky pseudopods (reach 10 ft, grapples, escape DC 15) and bites; on a 5–6 it sprays a 20-ft cone of trinkets (DC 14 Dexterity save, 3d6 and Restrained); it shakes off one failed save a day and lashes up to twice more between your turns. With it come "trinkets", [Mimics](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/mimic.md): one below level 6, five at level 6 and up. Spread out so the cone can't catch everyone, and keep someone free to pull grappled allies loose.
5. **Talk to Iosif again.** After the fight he's in the road at the crossroads at any hour. The chain drops off on its own.

### Choices and endings

There's one ending. Iosif tells you to open the wagon's belly, and inside are **300 gp** of travellers' money and a [Gem of Seeing](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#gem-of-seeing). Thistle (+2), Godrick (+2), Liriel (+1) and Kip (+1) approve. Iosif goes back to the Tser Pool camp, where he sits on the ground now (chairs have legs), and Zora counts him back in.

### If you're stuck

- **No wagon at the crossroads?** Iosif and his wagon are only there between 8 in the morning and 6 in the evening.
- **No option to go for the chain?** It only appears after a passed Insight or Perception check. If both failed, ask him why he never gets down off the board, then choose "(Pull the blanket off his knees.)".
- This is a boss fight, harder than the region's level-4 average, and it grows with your level. Below level 5 it's weaker (110 HP, one trinket), but still a boss; from level 6 it's 450 HP with five trinkets.
