    The Cook's Guests

    ✦Side quest

    Below Castle Ravenloft's kitchen, the cook keeps his Master's guests fresh in the cells.

    Region: Castle Ravenloft (party level about 10)
    Given by: Cyrus Belview
    Starts: Cyrus Belview in Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen (The kitchen)
    Rewards: Ration ×3, Ioun Stone (Sustenance)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Take the servants' stairs down from the servery behind the main floor's dining hall into Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen. Cyrus Belview, the castle's mongrelfolk cook, is stirring his cauldron in the kitchen and speaks as soon as you come near, at any hour. He is making soup for the guests in the cells downstairs, who must be kept fresh for the Master's table. That starts the quest (Heard).

    Step by step

    1. Talk to Cyrus first. Nothing here costs you anything, and several answers help later:
      • Who are the guests downstairs? He tells you about the crying girl with soot on her hands, and the holy brother who never eats his soup. That second detail gives you an easy way to unmask the brother later.
      • Where does your Master eat? The table of bones below, and its green lamp. Turning the furnace's water wheel puts the lamp out.
      • What's kept in the wine cellar? The wine merchant he bricked up in the last rack at the back.
      • We've met your family, at the abbey above Krezk (if you met the Belviews or Clovin at the The Abbey of St. Markovia). He turns friendly and gives you 3 Rations and an Ioun Stone (Sustenance). Saying Clovin sends his love earns Kip +1 and Wren +1.
    2. Go down to the cells. Follow the guards' run east to the door studded with nails, the jailers' stair, and take the stairs down to Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon. Walk east through the dungeon hall and down the passage to the cells. Or use the lift: in the winch room, set the brake on the winch (Investigation DC 13 first shows its catch has been filed half through, and you wedge it), and the cage drops you right beside the north cells.
    3. Find Nadia. Nadia Boteanu, a lamplighter from Vallaki, is chained by the ankle in the first cell of the north row. The cell gates are barred from outside, not locked. Talking to her moves the quest to Found. Ask her about the hall at the end of the passage to hear about the table of bones.
    4. Get her chain off. Three ways, and the two rolls can be tried again:
      • The jailers' keys. They hang on the key board in the jailers' guardroom, off the dungeon hall. Walking in wakes 3 Helmed Horrors (3,300 XP).
      • Sleight of Hand DC 13 to work the pin loose. A failure costs ten minutes.
      • Athletics DC 15 to wrench the ring out of the wall. A failure costs ten minutes and makes something upstairs stop to listen.

      With the chain off the quest moves to Freed (Thistle +2, Godrick +1, Kip +1).

    5. Get Cyrus's help. Nadia's only safe way out is Cyrus's scrap barrel, which goes down the cliff path at first light. Once you have met Nadia, go back to the kitchen and ask The girl in the cells. Help us get her out. He agrees if you:
      • remind him Clovin would help her (only after you gave him his family's news);
      • hold out 30 gp (80 gp after a failed Persuasion);
      • pass Persuasion DC 14 ("Nobody upstairs counts the guests"). A failure is spent and raises his price to 80 gp;
      • pass Intimidation DC 13 ("you'll be in your own pot"; Godrick -1). A failure is a disaster: he screams for the watch. If you haven't already beaten the dead watch in the guardroom (2 Wights, 2 helmed horrors and 4 Strahd Zombies, 4,400 XP), they fight you in the kitchen. Either way Cyrus turns hostile and will never help.
    6. Send her out. Back in her cell, choose The cook will smuggle you out at dawn in his scrap barrel. She hugs you and goes up toward the kitchen: Escaped.

    You can do steps 4 to 6 in a different order. If Cyrus isn't helping yet, tell Nadia to shut yourself in here and hide (Hidden), get his help, and come back for her.

    The other prisoners:

    • Brother Anatol, in the first cell of the south row, says he is a monk from St. Andral's. He is a vampire spawn, and his shackle was never locked. Unmask him with any of these: ask how long he's been here and then point out that his breath doesn't fog; Insight DC 15 (spent if it fails); Religion DC 12 to pray the dawn prayer with him (one try; on a success he gets it wrong); mention Father Lucian if you have met him; mention the cook's soup; or have a cleric or paladin raise a holy symbol. Unmasked, he fights with two more spawn off the ceiling (5,400 XP), or you can leave him sitting in his cell. Don't free him: "We're getting you out" starts the same fight with your party surprised.
    • Doru, in the second cell of the north row, is only here if you let him out of the undercroft in the Village of Barovia. He begs for an end. We could end this, Doru gives him rest (Liriel +2, Thistle +1, Godrick +1). Striking his chains off makes the hunger win, and he attacks (a vampire spawn, 1,800 XP; Kip -2, Liriel -1).

    Worth grabbing: a loose stone in the south cells (search DC 14, a potion of healing), the satchel in the open cell (a potion of healing and 12 gp, but it's bait over a 40-foot pit: spot DC 15), and the jailers' chest (manacles, a hooded lantern, a holy symbol, 26 gp).

    Choices and endings

    • Escaped (success). Nadia goes down the cliff path in the scrap barrel, and next time Cyrus mentions that the barrel was very heavy and the crows were disappointed.
    • Caught (failure). If you send her up the servants' stair alone and tell her Yes. Go now, the next time you pass the foot of the servants' stair her shawl is lying folded on the bottom step. Looking at it closes the quest.
    • Hidden. She waits in her cell for as long as you like. Come back and send her out with Cyrus's barrel, or up the stair.

    None of these pay anything themselves. The rations and the Ioun Stone come from giving Cyrus his family's news. Attacking Cyrus is a trivial fight (a Mongrelfolk, 50 XP) but costs Ratatouille -3, Godrick -2 and Kip -1, and it closes the barrel for good.

    If you're stuck

    • No "help us get her out" option. You have to meet Nadia before you can ask Cyrus. If he is hostile (a failed Intimidation) or dead, he can't help, and the only ways out for Nadia are the stair (which fails) or waiting in her cell.
    • Don't send her up the stair alone. That ending is the failure, and it's final.

    Journal stages

    Heard

    Cyrus Belview, the castle's cook, makes soup for his Master's guests in the cells below. He says they must be kept fresh for the table.

    • ☐Find the guests in the dungeon

    Hint: Find the cells in Castle Ravenloft's dungeon, below the larders.

    Reached through: Cyrus Belview in Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen

    Found

    Nadia Boteanu, a lamplighter from Vallaki, is chained by the ankle in a cell under the castle. She got into a carriage with no driver.

    • ☐Get her chain off
    • ☐Find her a way out of the castle

    Hint: Get Nadia's chain off in her cell, then find her a way out of the castle.

    Reached through: Nadia Boteanu in Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon

    Freed

    Nadia's chain is off. Now she needs a way out of a castle where every corridor belongs to him.

    • ☐Find her a way out of the castle

    Hint: Find Nadia a way out of the castle, or have her hide in her cell until you're done.

    Reached through: Nadia Boteanu in Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon

    Hidden

    Nadia is hiding in her cell with the gate shut, waiting for us to finish with the lord of the castle.

    • ☐Come back for Nadia

    Hint: Come back to Nadia's cell in the castle dungeon when it's safe.

    Reached through: Nadia Boteanu in Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon

    Escaped (ends: success)

    Nadia went down the cliff path at first light in the cook's scrap barrel. The crows were disappointed. She'll be lighting Vallaki's lamps again by winter.

    Reached through: Nadia Boteanu in Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon

    Caught (ends: failure)

    We sent Nadia up the stair alone. Her shawl was waiting on the bottom step, folded neatly.

    Reached through: a folded shawl on the bottom step in Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen

    Fights

    The chain falls. For a breath he is only grateful. Then the hunger reaches you first.

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon: Doru (Vampire Spawn) (1800 XP)

    He laughs, and two more shapes unfold from the vault above the passage.

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon: Brother Anatol (Vampire Spawn), 2 × Vampire Spawn (5400 XP)

    His hand closes on the nearest throat, and two more shapes drop from the vault above the passage.

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon: Brother Anatol (Vampire Spawn), 2 × Vampire Spawn (5400 XP; surprise: party)

    Cyrus's shriek goes down the guards' run like a bell. Boots answer it, and some of them are not boots.

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen, when not larders watch beaten: Sergeant of the Watch (Wight), Wight, 2 × Helmed Horror, 4 × Strahd Zombie (4400 XP)

    Cyrus snatches up a cleaver in one hand and a ladle in the other, weeping.

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Larders of Ill Omen: Cyrus Belview (Mongrelfolk) (50 XP)

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Sleight of Hand DC 13: (Work the shackle's pin loose.)
    • Athletics DC 15: (Wrench the ring out of the wall.)
    • Insight DC 15: (Watch him while he talks.)
    • Religion DC 12: Pray the dawn prayer with us, Brother.
    • Persuasion DC 14: Nobody upstairs counts the guests. One fewer won't spoil anything.
    • Intimidation DC 13: Help her, or you'll be in your own pot.
