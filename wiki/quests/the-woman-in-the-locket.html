    The Woman in the Locket

    ✦Side quest

    Tamsin Tealeaf lifted a silver locket from a coach with no driver and no horses he could hear. The woman painted inside it has called his name in his dreams ever since.

    Region: Companions
    Starts: the mist behind you in The Old Svalich Road (The Svalich Woods)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This is Tamsin Tealeaf's personal quest (the Halfling Rogue, a Thief, with the Criminal background). Tamsin is one of the first four heroes, and they left the roster on 2026-10-06, so a new game can't pick him. The quest only runs in a save from before then with Tamsin in the party. A custom hero never answers to his name, and every locket option is Tamsin's own. Companion approval doesn't apply to him, so nothing here is gated by it.

    It starts the first time you walk into the mist at the east end of The Old Svalich Road with Tamsin in the party. The decision comes at the Pool of the White Sun in Krezk (level 6–7) or in Castle Ravenloft.

    Step by step

    1. Her face. At the Burgomaster's Mansion, pick Tamsin's Show her the locket with Ireena Kolyana. The painted woman has Ireena's face. The quest moves to Her Face, and later Strahd remarks at the burial that the little thief carries her portrait.
    2. The inscription. After a Long Rest, "Tamsin wants a word". He finally picks the back of the locket: "For T., from S. Every morning, at the water." The quest moves to The Inscription.
    3. Her name. Either one works:
      • Spend Madam Eva's question on Who is the woman in the locket? She says Tatyana.
      • Bring Tamsin and Ireena to The Pool of the White Sun in Krezk after Ireena has seen the locket. When Sergei's reflection appears beside her, Tamsin makes the connection.

      The quest moves to Her Name.

    4. The coach (optional). When Henrik van der Voort confesses about the crates and the driverless black carriage at The Coffin Maker's Shop in Vallaki (part of The Bones of St. Andral), Tamsin recognises the coach he robbed. After that, either a Long Rest talk or Arrigal at The Vistani Camp (Ask about the black carriage that drives itself) tells him Strahd left the locket out for whatever thief came along. The quest moves to The Coach.
    5. The pool. At the Pool of the White Sun, with the quest at Her Name or later, pick (Take out the locket.). Sergei von Zarovich's reflection appears beside Tamsin's. Choose:
      • Drop the locket into the pool: Given to Sergei.
      • Put the locket in Ireena's hand (only if she's travelling with you): Given to Ireena.
      • Close it, and keep it: Kept, and the decision moves to the castle.

    6. The castle. In the study of Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count, examine Candles under her portrait. If Tamsin still has the locket (kept, or never decided), Strahd is suddenly in the chair by the fire, unless he's lying in his coffin or already destroyed:
      • (Put the locket in his hand.) Given to Strahd.
      • (Drop the locket into the fire.) Burned.
      • Not yet. He'll ask again; come back to the candles.

      If the locket went to Sergei, Strahd only remarks on it. If it went to Ireena and she's with you, she shows it to him herself.

    Choices and endings

    • Given to Sergei and Given to Ireena (success): the dreams stop. At the next Long Rest Tamsin sleeps through the night for once.
    • Burned (success): Strahd, for once, has nothing to say.
    • Given to Strahd (failure): he thanks Tamsin and means it. That's the worst part.

    Optional, Madam Eva: Tamsin's truth is that somebody put the locket where his hand would find it.

    If you're stuck

    • Arrigal's option needs Henrik first. Arrigal only knows about the coach once Henrik has confessed with Tamsin in the party.
    • The pool option needs Her Name. It won't show before then.
    • Strahd isn't in the chair. He doesn't come while he's in his coffin or after he's destroyed. Tamsin keeps the locket, and the candles wait.

    Journal stages

    The Dreams

    Three months ago Tamsin lifted a silver locket from a nobleman's coach. Inside is a tiny portrait of a red-haired woman he has never met, and since that night she has called his name in his dreams from somewhere cold. He followed the dreams the way other people follow roads, and they brought him here.

    • ☐Find out who the woman in the locket is

    Hint: With Tamsin in the party, show the locket to Ireena at the burgomaster's mansion, or ask Madam Eva at Tser Pool.

    Reached through: the mist behind you in The Old Svalich Road

    Her Face

    The woman in the locket has Ireena Kolyana's face: the same eyes, the same red hair, in a gown a few centuries out of fashion.

    • ☐Find out who she was

    Hint: Ask Madam Eva at Tser Pool about the locket, or rest with Tamsin and let him look at it again.

    Reached through: Ireena Kolyana in Burgomaster's Mansion

    Her Name

    Her name was Tatyana. Strahd loved her; she loved his brother Sergei, and on their wedding day she went off the castle wall rather than be his. She keeps being born again in this valley, and Ireena is the latest.

    • ☐Find out who put the locket where Tamsin's hand would find it

    Hint: Take a Long Rest with Tamsin in the party, or ask Arrigal at the Vistani camp outside Vallaki about a carriage that drives itself.

    Reached through: The Pool of the White Sun in The Pool of the White Sun; Madam Eva in Madam Eva's Tent

    The Inscription

    Behind the portrait, worn almost smooth: 'For T., from S. Every morning, at the water.' An S, but not the one on Ilse's letter. This one was written by someone who loved her and expected to be loved back.

    • ☐Find the water Sergei meant

    Hint: The water is the Pool of the White Sun in Krezk. Take Tamsin there, and ask Arrigal about the coach.

    The Coach

    The coach Tamsin robbed was Strahd's own black carriage, the one that goes about Barovia with nobody driving it. Last spring it was sent out through the mists, empty, the door on the latch and the locket on the seat. A thief would come, its master said. A thief always comes. Tamsin carried Tatyana's face home for him, exactly as planned.

    • ☐Decide what becomes of the locket

    Hint: Take Tamsin to the Pool of the White Sun in Krezk and decide what becomes of the locket.

    Reached through: Arrigal in The Vistani Camp

    Given To Sergei (ends: success)

    At the Pool of the White Sun, Tamsin gave the locket back to the one who gave it first. Sergei's hand closed round it under the water, and the dreams stopped. Whatever Strahd meant the locket to do, it is out of his reach now, and the thief kept nothing for himself.

    Reached through: The Pool of the White Sun in The Pool of the White Sun

    Given To Ireena (ends: success)

    Tamsin put the locket in Ireena's hand. She looked at her own face, three hundred years old, and closed it gently, and said she'd keep it until she knew whose it ought to be. The dreams stopped. She has started remembering things.

    Reached through: The Pool of the White Sun in The Pool of the White Sun

    Kept

    Tamsin kept the locket. He says it's because he's a thief. He has also stopped opening it, and never lets it out of his hand at night.

    • ☐Decide what becomes of the locket before the castle decides for you

    Hint: Decide what becomes of the locket before the castle does; in Castle Ravenloft, look for the candles under her portrait.

    Reached through: The Pool of the White Sun in The Pool of the White Sun

    Given To Strahd (ends: failure)

    In his own hall, Strahd held out his hand, and Tamsin put the locket in it. Strahd looked at the face inside for a long time and said thank you, as if he meant it, which was the worst part.

    Reached through: Candles under her portrait in Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count

    Burned (ends: success)

    In the devil's own hall, with the devil watching, Tamsin dropped the locket into the fire. Strahd said nothing at all. It is the only time anyone has seen him with nothing to say.

    Reached through: Candles under her portrait in Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Insight DC 14: (Watch her face. What does she really want?)
    • Medicine DC 12: Look at what the scarf is hiding.
