    The Destined Ally

    ✦Main quest

    Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.

    Region: The Old Svalich Road (party level about 4)
    Given by: Madam Eva
    Starts: Madam Eva in Madam Eva's Tent (Madam Eva's tent)
    Rewards: Holy Water ×2, Hat of Disguise, a level (milestone)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This quest starts at the end of Madam Eva's reading in The Seer at Tser Pool. Her fourth card is "a friend. One who will stand beside you when the others run". The card is dealt from the Tarokka's high deck of fourteen cards, shuffled from your playthrough's seed: the first high card is your ally, and the second is the room where Strahd waits (Where the Devil Waits). So your ally's card and Strahd's card are never the same card. The reading never changes during a playthrough, the journal entry names your ally and where to look, and the verses are on The Tarokka Reading.

    An ally who joins travels as a guest: they follow you, appear in the party frames and fight on your side under your control. When they join, the quest moves to "Found". If you lose them for good, it moves to "Lost" and you go on without them.

    Step by step

    Find your card below and follow its route. In most cases you talk to the ally and pick the option that begins "Madam Eva's cards named you…". It only appears when they really are your ally.

    Artifact: Rictavio. He's in The Blue Water Inn (one of his two spots there is by day only). With this card he stays in Vallaki even if Lady Wachter takes the town.

    1. Choose "Madam Eva's cards named a man like you." He drops the showman act at once, admits he's Rudolph van Richten, and gives you two flasks of holy water and his Hat of Disguise.
    2. Then choose "Madam Eva's cards named you. Will you hunt with us?" He joins.
    3. If you helped Lady Wachter take Vallaki, he refuses to stand with you:
      • Persuasion DC 15 ("We made a bad bargain") wins him back.
      • If it fails, he goes home to his tower on Lake Baratok, and Van Richten's Tower appears on your map. Get in with the name Khazan (see The Hunter's Tower) and ask again ("The cards still name you"). He joins at once if you've freed the stolen children from the werewolf den, brought the children home from Old Bonegrinder, or lit Argynvostholt's beacon. Otherwise you get one more Persuasion DC 15; if that fails too, come back after one of those deeds.
      • "Then go" loses him for good.

    Beast: Emil Toranescu. He's in The Werewolf Den, chained in silver in the west alcove. The den appears on your map because of the card. See The Pack in the Hills.

    1. Get in. Either Zuleika, waiting at the den in the hills, vouches for you, or you fight the guards at the cave mouth.
    2. Free him with the key (Zuleika has one; Kiril keeps the other on a peg in his lair north of the great cave), or tear the rings out with Athletics DC 15, or pick the cuffs with Sleight of Hand DC 14. Each roll is one try. He joins as a guest and howls his challenge.
    3. When Kiril arrives, choose "We stand with Emil" and win the fight. Below level 7 it's Kiril with two werewolves and a dire wolf; at level 7 he brings three werewolves and two dire wolves; at level 8, four werewolves and two dire wolves. If you've already beaten the den's guards, there's one werewolf fewer at levels 7 and 8.
    4. Afterwards Zuleika takes the pack, and Emil comes with you because the cards said so.
    5. Standing aside, handing Emil back to Kiril, or turning on both of them ("Neither of you leads anything") loses him.

    Broken One: Mordenkainen. He lives in The Mad Mage's Hut on Mount Baratok. As the Mad Mage he says yes to the cards and then forgets you mid-sentence, so you must give him his mind back first (The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok). Any one of these works:

    • Uncork the memory jar from Baba Lysaga's shelf in Berez. You can take it from her hut, or demand it as part of her bargain (Intimidation DC 15).
    • Cast Greater Restoration with a Cleric, Druid or Bard, once your whole party is level 9.
    • Unstack the warded cairn on the summit to find his spellbook. Show it to him, then read him its last page.
    • Arcana DC 16: talk him back one spell at a time (one try).

    Restored, he gives you a Ring of Spell Storing. Then choose "Madam Eva's cards named you. Will you come?" and he joins.

    Darklord: nobody. The cards gave you no ally. The quest ends straight away at Alone, and you face Strahd with your own party.

    Donjon: Victor Vallakovich. He's in the attic of The Burgomaster's Mansion with his teleportation circle (The Attic Circle).

    1. Choose "Madam Eva's cards named you, Victor. Come with us." He comes gladly, unless you scuffed his circle or he has turned hostile toward you.
    2. If he sulks, either Persuasion DC 12 ("Out of this house… isn't that what you wanted?") or ten gold "for chalk" brings him round. If the roll fails, the chalk money still works.
    3. If Lady Wachter wins Vallaki at the festival (The Festival of the Blazing Sun) while his circle is still intact, he vanishes through it, and the ally is lost. If you'd broken his circle, talked him out of it or reported it, he's sheltering at St. Andral's with his mother instead, and still comes when asked.

    Executioner: Ismark Kolyanovich. There's no special scene. Ismark joins when you take Ireena out of the village and accept his offer ("Glad to have you, Ismark") in Ireena's Escort. Later, if he stayed behind guarding Ireena, you can ask "Come with us again, Ismark" at the Blue Water Inn, St. Andral's Church, or the Krezkovs' house in Krezk.

    Ghost: Sir Godfrey Gwilym. He's at Argynvostholt: in the grounds the first time, then on the upper floor. See The Order of the Silver Dragon.

    1. Choose "Madam Eva's cards named you, Sir Godfrey." If he already remembers his oath, he joins at once.
    2. If not, say the oath with him (once you've read it in the chapel), read him the bottom of his wall of names, or tell him you'll give him something new to remember. All three work, with no roll.
    3. If you light the dragon's beacon (The Dragon's Beacon) before he has joined, he climbs the tower and asks you to choose:
      • "Ride with us, Sir Godfrey": he joins, and Godrick approves (+2).
      • "Rest": the ally is lost, and Liriel (+2) and Godrick (+1) approve.

    Horseman: Kolyan Indirovich. The late burgomaster must be raised from the dead.

    1. His grave appears in the village churchyard once he's buried (The Burgomaster's Burial).
    2. At the grave you need a Cleric or Bard in the party, everyone at level 9, and 500 gp for the diamonds.
    3. Raised, he either rides with you at once or waits on his grave until you come back for him.

    Innocent: Parriwimple. Bildrath's big, gentle nephew is in Bildrath's Mercantile. Choose "Madam Eva's cards named you, Parriwimple. Come with us." He joins, no roll.

    Marionette: Pidlwick II. The clockwork jester comes down the stair to meet you in Castle Ravenloft, the High Tower.

    1. Choose "Madam Eva's cards showed us a clockwork jester", then "Come with us, Pidlwick" (Godrick +1, Wren +1).
    2. "We won't take you" loses him.
    3. You can also bring him his wind-up key from the castle's ravens' roost (The Jester's Key). He gives you a Figurine of Wondrous Power (Ebony Fly) and asks to come along.

    Mists: Ezmerelda d'Avenir. With this card she waits on a boulder at the top of the switchback below The Abbey of St. Markovia, watching it through a spyglass. The abbey shows on your map because of the card. Answer her challenge any way you like, then choose "We're going after the devil in the castle. Hunt with us." She joins.

    Raven: Davian Martikov. At The Wizard of Wines, choose "Madam Eva's cards named you."

    • He won't leave while the druids are in his house. Clear them out of the winery and the press house, then tell him "The druids are out of your house" (The Wizard of Wines).
    • Ask again and he joins.

    Seer: Kasimir Velikov. The dusk elf sits mending a net by a shuttered wagon at The Vistani Camp outside Vallaki. Choose "Madam Eva's cards named you as our ally." He joins, no roll.

    Tempter: Arabelle. She has been taken from her father's wagon (The Missing Girl).

    1. Her father Luvash at the Vistani camp outside Vallaki asks you to find her. The Vistana at the fire saw Bluto the fisherman carry a moving sack to the lake.
    2. Catch Bluto wading out with the sack. Talk him down (Persuasion DC 13), scare him (Intimidation DC 13) or grab it (Athletics DC 12).
    3. If those fail, he throws her in, and someone must dive: Athletics DC 13, then Perception DC 12. If both fail, she drowns.
    4. Once she's safe there are two ways to bring her along:
      • Take her to Luvash. She tells him she's going with you, and you choose "We'll keep her safe, Luvash".
      • Once Bluto's business at Lake Zarovich: the Fishers' Landing is settled, find her in a boat drifting off the end of the jetty. Haul it in (Athletics DC 12; even a failure gets her in) or throw her a line, then "We promise. Come with us."
    5. If you send her home instead, you can still collect her from the camp later.

    Choices and endings

    • Found: the ally walks with you to the end and fights at your side against Strahd.
    • Lost: you lose Rictavio by telling him to go, Emil by standing aside, betraying him or turning on the pack, Victor if he vanishes through his circle, Godfrey by letting him rest under the beacon's light, and Pidlwick by refusing him. The quest ends as a failure, and you face Strahd without them.
    • Some allies pay you on the way in: Rictavio's holy water and Hat of Disguise, Mordenkainen's Ring of Spell Storing, Pidlwick's ebony fly. Luvash gives 50 gp and a Cloak of the Manta Ray for Arabelle's rescue. If she joins you in that same conversation, he hands them over the next time you talk to him.

    If you're stuck

    • The "cards named you" line isn't there? It only appears for the one ally your reading named, and for some only once something else is done: Mordenkainen restored, Davian's house cleared, Kolyan raised.
    • Ismark, Kolyan and Parriwimple: the quest moves to Found the moment the one the cards named travels with you, whichever scene brought them in.
    • Darklord: the quest ends at once at Alone: the cards named no one, and you face Strahd with the friends you have.
    • Arabelle drowned? The journal won't say so for this quest, but she's gone for good.

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Persuasion DC 17: Hating him isn't fighting him. It's keeping him company. Let them rest.
    • Athletics DC 12: Wade out and haul the boat in.
    • Insight DC 13: What is he grieving for?
    • Insight DC 15: The bluster is a costume. What's under it?
    • Persuasion DC 15: We're hunting the same thing. Let's stop pretending.
    • Persuasion DC 15: We made a bad bargain. Help us make it right.
    • Persuasion DC 14: Then go down and tell her that yourself, while there's still someone to tell.
    • Persuasion DC 15: We've learned. Come and watch us prove it.
    • Medicine DC 13: You're holding your arm strangely. Let me see it.
    • Insight DC 14: It isn't Adrian you're angry with.
