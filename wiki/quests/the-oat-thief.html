    The Oat Thief

    ✦Side quest

    Somebody is stealing four sacks of oats a week from Bildrath's back room, and the lock is never touched.

    Region: The Village of Barovia (party level about 3)
    Given by: Bildrath Cantemir
    Starts: Bildrath Cantemir in Bildrath's Mercantile (Shop floor)
    Rewards: Bag of Tricks (Tan)

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    In Bildrath's Mercantile (open 6:00 to 19:00), ask Bildrath Cantemir "Heard anything interesting?" and then about "Your stock." Four sacks of oats go missing every week, the lock is never touched, and Parriwimple is a very poor liar. The quest starts at Rumored.

    Step by step

    1. Catch Parriwimple on the north path. Between 22:00 and 4:00, Parriwimple creeps up the north path at the north-east corner of the village, past the churchyard, with sacks of oats on his shoulders. Every answer gets the truth out of him. If you befriended him earlier, "It's all right. You can tell us." works. Otherwise use [Persuasion DC 10] "We won't tell your uncle. Who are they for?" (one try), or say "Those are Bildrath's oats." The oats are for a woman called Agafia and her three small children, who mustn't ever eat meat. The quest moves to Asked, and a new exit opens: "Up the north path into the pines".
    2. Go up to the hollow. The path leads to The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow. Agafia meets you at the hut door with a knife. Ask "Who are you hiding from?" or "Why only oats?" She ran from the werewolf den when Kiril Stoyanovich began taking children for the count's army, and her own three were born to the wolf. A howl comes up the path.

    3. Read the ground, then hold the door. [Survival DC 12] "(Look at the ground round the hut.)" spots the trail of spilled oats from Parriwimple's holed sack, with huge paw prints over it. You catch the hunters unawares (they're surprised) and one wolf is left behind down the path. A failed roll, or "(Stand in front of the door.)", means a straight fight.
      • Trail spotted: at level 3 or higher, A Pack Hunter (Werewolf) and 2 × Wolf (800 XP); below level 3, the werewolf alone (700 XP).
      • Trail missed: at level 3 or higher, the werewolf and three wolves (850 XP); below level 3, the werewolf and one wolf (750 XP).

      The werewolf has 71 HP and AC 15, and its bite can curse a humanoid (DC 12 Constitution), so keep your weakest characters off it. Winning moves the quest to Defended.

    4. Talk to Agafia. She gives you her grandmother's Bag of Tricks (Tan): reach in, throw what you find, and a small beast gets up and fights for you till morning. The quest ends at Done (Liriel +2, Godrick +2, Thistle +2, Wren +1).

    Choices and endings

    There's one ending. Agafia and her children stay in the hollow, Parriwimple keeps carrying oats, and Bildrath, if you ask him for news afterwards, has decided the missing sacks are charity.

    If you're stuck

    • Parriwimple is only on the path from 22:00 to 4:00, and only while the quest is at Rumored or Asked. The north path exit stays shut until he has told you about Agafia.
    • If Parriwimple is travelling with you as Madam Eva's chosen ally, he isn't on the north path at night, and nothing else opens the way to the hollow.

    Journal stages

    Rumored

    Bildrath is short four sacks of oats a week, and the lock on his back room is never touched. He says Parriwimple is a very poor liar.

    • ☐Watch the north path at night

    Hint: Watch the north path at the village's north edge after dark and see who's carrying the oats.

    Reached through: Bildrath Cantemir in Bildrath's Mercantile

    Asked

    Parriwimple carries the oats up the north path at night, to a woman called Agafia who is hiding in the woods with three small children. They mustn't ever have meat, she says.

    • ☐Go up the north path to the hollow in the pines

    Hint: Take the north path at the village's north edge up into the pines, to the hollow.

    Reached through: Parriwimple in Village of Barovia

    Defended

    Agafia ran from the werewolf den with her children, who were born to the wolf. The pack's hunters followed the oats up the path to her door, and we stood in it.

    • ☐Speak to Agafia

    Hint: Talk to Agafia at the hollow up the north path.

    Done (ends: success)

    Agafia and her children are safe in the hollow, for now, on oats, and Parriwimple still carries them up. Bildrath has decided it's charity. Don't repeat that.

    Reached through: Agafia in The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow

    Fights

    You saw them coming up the oats, and where they'd come from. They didn't see you seeing, and one of the… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow, when oat trail seen and level ≥ 3: A Pack Hunter (Werewolf), 2 × Wolf (800 XP; surprise: enemies)
    • The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow, when oat trail seen: A Pack Hunter (Werewolf) (700 XP; surprise: enemies)
    • The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow, when level ≥ 3: A Pack Hunter (Werewolf), 3 × Wolf (850 XP)
    • The Charcoal-Burners' Hollow: A Pack Hunter (Werewolf), Wolf (750 XP)
