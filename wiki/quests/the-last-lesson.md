## Summary

Wren Featherfoot's old master, a dusk elf called Sorrel, died before Wren's training was finished, and left him one errand: a sealed letter for a brother named Kasimir, in the valley where the sun does not rise.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

This is **Wren Featherfoot**'s personal quest (the Halfling Monk, Warrior of the Open Hand). It starts by itself at the very beginning on [The Old Svalich Road](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-old-svalich-road.md): when you look back into the mist at its east end, or, if you never do, when you reach the wolves in the narrows, with Wren in the party. A custom hero in his place never gets it, and every beat needs Wren in the party.

There's no level or approval gate on starting it. Kasimir is just outside Vallaki, a level 4–5 region. Patrina is in the Amber Temple (level 9–10), and Rahadin is in Castle Ravenloft. After a Long Rest, "Wren wants a word" from a handstand about Master Sorrel (+1).

### Step by step

1. **Deliver the letter.** Go to [The Vistani Camp](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-vistani-camp.md) outside [Vallaki](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/vallaki.md)'s west gate. [Kasimir Velikov](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/kasimir-velikov.md) sits by a shuttered wagon, mending a net. Pick Wren's **Give him Master Sorrel's letter**. Kasimir reads it aloud: Sorrel was his younger brother, and the letter says "Let her rest, brother." The quest moves to *The Brother* (+5).
   ![loc vallaki vistani camp 1](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-vallaki-vistani-camp-1.webp)
2. **Hear about Patrina.** While you're there, either **(Sit with him a while, and say nothing.)** (+2 Wren, +1 Liriel) or pass **Insight DC 13**. Kasimir then tells you about his sister. This matters: it puts the Amber Temple on your travel map, and it lets you name Patrina to her face later. A Long Rest after this brings Wren's talk about the letter; "He was right" is +3.
3. **Patrina.** In [The Amber Temple: Hall of the Faceless God](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-amber-temple-hall-of-the-faceless-god.md), [Patrina Velikovna](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/patrina-velikovna.md)'s ghost is turning crumbling pages. How her story ends is up to you ([The Wailing Hall](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-wailing-hall.md)):
   - **With Kasimir travelling with you.** He only joins if Madam Eva's cards named him your ally. Wren's option **(Have Kasimir read her the last line of Sorrel's letter.)** gives +3, and she rests. Don't tell Kasimir about the vestige in the vaults; that costs Wren -3.
   - **Without him.** Name her ("You're Patrina. Kasimir's sister."), then pick "Your brother grieves for you" and pass **Persuasion DC 15** ("He's been too afraid to come...", +2 Wren). If that fails it's gone, and she drives you out or you fight. Or pass **Religion DC 16** ("Let them lie, and rest", +2 Liriel, +1 Wren); a failure there doesn't close it.
   - **Fight her.** She's a [Banshee](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/banshee.md) with 110 HP, with eleven [Specter](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/specter.md)s and seven more banshees. Each banshee's once-a-day wail hits everyone within 30 feet who isn't undead or a construct.
   - **Kasimir's bargain.** He can buy her back from [Tarakamedes](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/tarakamedes.md). That's the failure ending of Patrina's own quest, but it still counts for Wren.
4. **Let her rest.** At the next Long Rest after Patrina's story is over (at rest, destroyed or restored), "Wren wants a word". He doesn't feel finished. The quest moves to *Let Her Rest*. This talk only comes if you haven't already met Rahadin as Wren (step 5).
5. **The chamberlain.** In Castle Ravenloft, find [Rahadin](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/rahadin.md): first in the long hall of [Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-court-of-the-count.md), and after that in his room in [Castle Ravenloft, the Rooms of Weeping](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-rooms-of-weeping.md). Pick Wren's **Tell him who your teacher was**, which is available from *The Brother* onward and stays until the quest ends. Rahadin calmly admits killing the Velikovs' mother, and one of the voices screaming around him calls Sorrel's name. The quest moves to *The Chamberlain*.
   ![rahadin voices](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/rahadin-voices.webp)
6. **Choose how it ends.**
   - **(Tell him what Sorrel thought of him.)** Wren pities him and walks away. That's the *Learned* ending (+8).
   - **(Go for his throat.)** That's the *Unlearned* ending (-4), and a fight with Rahadin: AC 18, three scimitar cuts a turn, and an aura of screams. In the long hall he has 325 HP and three of [Strahd's Animated Armor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/strahds-animated-armor.md) (270, 270 and 215 HP), or 795 HP and four armours if Gertruda travels with you; in his room he has 265 HP and three armours of 220 HP each. Anyone within 10 feet of him at the start of his turn makes a DC 16 Wisdom save or takes 3d10 psychic, so keep your casters back.
   - **(Pull Wren back.)** This gives +3 and takes you back to Rahadin's menu. Nothing is lost: Wren's **Tell him who your teacher was** is still there, then or on a later visit, and picking it again skips the introduction ("Wren steps out in front of the chamberlain again") and goes straight to these three choices.

Optional, [Madam Eva](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/madam-eva.md): Wren's truth (he isn't finished) gives +3 and Heroic Inspiration. Spending the one question on "Where is Kasimir?" is +3.

### Choices and endings

- **Learned (success, +8):** "Leave my sight." If Wren is **Close or better** (approval 25 or more), he stops on the landing afterwards and says the last lesson out loud ("You don't get finished on your own"), and he gains Heroic Inspiration. If he's **Strained or worse** (-25 or lower), the pity option doesn't appear at all, and he won't take the party's way. Mend his approval before you go to Rahadin; his Strained camp talk can give up to +10.
- **Unlearned (failure, -4):** you fight Rahadin then and there.

Either way, Strahd greets Wren at dinner as "the little monk" whose teacher's manners unsettled Rahadin, if Wren has met him by then.

### If you're stuck

- **You pulled Wren back.** That's fine. His option with Rahadin stays until the quest ends, so go back to Rahadin (step 5 says where he is) and pick it again. If the pity option was missing because Wren was Strained, pull him back, mend his approval at camp, and then return.
- **Kasimir's reading needs Kasimir as an ally.** Kasimir only travels with you if Madam Eva's cards named him. Without him, use the Persuasion or Religion routes to give Patrina rest.
- **The Let Her Rest talk is skipped if you meet Rahadin first.** That's harmless; the quest simply goes on from *The Chamberlain*.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **The Letter**: Master Sorrel took Wren in off the street after Wren tried to pick his pocket, and taught him a lesson a day for fourteen years. He died last winter before the last one, and left Wren a sealed letter in a hand that shook: for my brother Kasimir, in the valley where the sun does not rise. Wren hasn't opened it.
> *Hint:* With Wren in the party, find Kasimir, a dusk elf at the Vistani camp outside Vallaki's west gate.
>
> **The Brother**: Kasimir Velikov is a dusk elf mending nets at the Vistani camp outside Vallaki, and Sorrel was his younger brother, the one who went over the mountains before the mists closed. Kasimir read the letter aloud: 'You stayed, and I ran, and neither of us saved her. Let her rest, brother. Don't go to the temple. Whatever answers there will want more than either of us has.' Their sister was Patrina, who went to Strahd, and their own people stoned her for it.
> *Hint:* Help Kasimir let his sister rest: she haunts the Amber Temple's Hall of the Faceless God. Take Wren.
>
> **Let Her Rest**: Patrina Velikovna is at peace, or gone. Sorrel's letter got where it was going. Wren says he doesn't feel any different. He keeps stopping in doorways.
> *Hint:* Face Rahadin, Strahd's chamberlain, in Castle Ravenloft, with Wren in the party.
>
> **The Chamberlain**: Strahd's chamberlain Rahadin is a dusk elf too. The screaming that follows him is the voices of everyone he ever killed for his master, and Sorrel's mother is one of them. Sorrel never once said his name.
> *Hint:* Face Rahadin in Castle Ravenloft's Court of the Count with Wren, and choose how it ends.
>
> **Learned** (the end: success): Wren told Rahadin that Master Sorrel never hated him, only pitied him, and that Wren did too. For a moment every voice around the chamberlain went quiet. Wren walked away from him on his own two feet. It was the last lesson; Sorrel had left it in the letter after all.
>
> **Unlearned** (the end: failure): Wren went for Rahadin's throat with every lesson he had, and none of them were Sorrel's. Whatever happens now, Wren knows which lesson he skipped.
