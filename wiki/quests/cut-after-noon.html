    Cut After Noon

    ✦Side quest

    The woodcutters of Barovia cut what they need by noon and go home. Grigore's son stayed late, and brought something back.

    Region: The Village of Barovia (party level about 3)
    Given by: Grigore
    Starts: Arik in Blood of the Vine (Behind the bar); Bildrath Cantemir in Bildrath's Mercantile (Shop floor); Petre in Village of Barovia (Village square); Vasile in Village of Barovia (Village square)
    Rewards: Wraps of Unarmed Power, +1, 40 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Ask almost anyone in the village "Heard anything interesting?" and you hear about Grigore's lad. Vasile and Petre gossip in the square from 10:00 to 17:00. Arik behind the bar of Blood of the Vine gives it as his first rumour, and Bildrath Cantemir has it under his news ("Grigore the woodcutter"). Any of these puts the quest at Rumored.

    Grigore himself hauls wood along the west road by his yard from 8:00 to 17:00 (not in a storm). Ask "We heard about your lad.", or "Why the hurry?" and then "Somebody stayed, didn't they?", and he tells you about Luca: the quest moves to Asked.

    Step by step

    1. Find out what the wood is. Any one of these works, and each can be tried again if it fails:
      • The pale logs on Grigore's woodpile: [Nature DC 13] "Split one open and look at the grain." or [Arcana DC 15] "Is there magic in it?"
      • Luca, asleep on a pallet in the yard: [Medicine DC 13] "Open his hand and look." finds the splinter working its way in.
      • Bildrath's storeroom: "The wood you bought from Grigore. Show us.", then [Nature DC 13]. This works even before you've talked to Grigore.

      The quest moves to Seedwood.

    2. Get Bildrath's load (needed to burn it). Once you know it's seedwood, ask Bildrath about the wood again and say "That's blight-wood. Give it to us, and we'll burn it with the rest." He writes it off, and Parriwimple carries it to Grigore's yard. The shop is shut from 19:00 to 6:00.
    3. Deal with the wood. Talk to Grigore by day. You have two choices:
      • Burn it by day. This needs Bildrath's load. "We've got Bildrath's load. Burn the lot, now, while it's light." Grigore balks at burning his winter's firewood. Win him over with [Persuasion DC 12] "It's the winter or your son, Grigore." (one try) or by pressing 10 gp into his hand. The burning logs split and blights crawl out: at level 4 or higher, 2 × Vine Blight, 4 × Needle Blight and 2 × Twig Blight (450 XP); at level 3, one vine, three needle and four twig blights (350 XP); below that, two needle and three twig blights (175 XP). The quest moves to Burned.
      • Wait for dusk. "Then we sit with Luca till dark, and see what comes for him." Time passes to 19:00, Bildrath's logs roll up the street, and the woodpile stands up. At level 4 or higher it's The Woodpile (Shambling Mound) with 2 × Vine Blight and 4 × Needle Blight (2,200 XP); at level 3, The Woodpile (85 HP) with four twig and two needle blights (2,000 XP); below level 3, one vine, three needle and three twig blights (325 XP). The quest moves to Stood Up. At level 3 and up this is a much harder fight than burning. The mound is healed by lightning and resists fire and cold, so hit it with anything else.
    4. Draw the splinter. After either fight, Grigore comes to you in his yard at any hour. [Medicine DC 13] "Draw the splinter out, slowly, root and all." gets it out clean. A caster with Lesser Restoration does the same, and "(Just pull it out.)" works but leaves Luca a scar. Luca wakes, and the quest ends at Luca Woke.
    5. Collect. Grigore offers you the 40 gp Bildrath paid him. Take it, or say "Keep it. Buy Luca a hat for the noon sun." (Godrick +2, Wren +2, Liriel +1). Then go back to Bildrath and ask "About that firewood." He buys your silence with a pair of Wraps of Unarmed Power, +1, plus 10% off his prices if you hadn't already haggled him down (Kip +1).

    Choices and endings

    Burning the wood and fighting it at dusk both end with Luca awake. The difference is the fight: burning by day keeps it small. Luca tells you about a big man in a bear's hide who planted the late trees in spring, singing that "the hill wants the valley back". Unless the druids' tree on Yester Hill has already burned, this puts Yester Hill on your travel map.

    If you're stuck

    • Grigore is only on the west road from 8:00 to 17:00, and not in storms. After the fight he finds you whenever you're near his yard.
    • If burning isn't on offer, you don't have Bildrath's load yet: burning Grigore's pile alone isn't enough.
    • Bildrath only hands the wood over once you know it's seedwood.

    Journal stages

    Rumored

    Vasile and Petre say Grigore the woodcutter's lad, Luca, went out for the last load two days ago, came home after dark, said nothing and went to bed. He hasn't got up since.

    • ☐Ask Grigore about his son; he hauls wood on the west road by day

    Hint: Find Grigore the woodcutter hauling wood by his yard on the village's west road, and ask about Luca.

    Reached through: Arik in Blood of the Vine; Bildrath Cantemir in Bildrath's Mercantile; Petre in Village of Barovia; Vasile in Village of Barovia

    Asked

    Luca stayed in the woods by the west road to cut a bit more after noon, which his father has told him a hundred times not to do. He came home late with a load of pale wood and has slept ever since. Grigore sold the load to Bildrath for the price of the medicine he couldn't afford.

    • ☐Look at the wood Luca cut: Grigore's woodpile on the west road, or Bildrath's back room

    Hint: Look at the pale logs on Grigore's woodpile on the west road, or the load Luca sold to Bildrath's back room.

    Reached through: Grigore in Village of Barovia

    Seedwood

    The late wood isn't firewood. The sap runs red and the grain runs the wrong way: it is seedwood, the kind that grows up into blights. Luca has a splinter of it in his palm, working its way in. The wood wants him.

    • ☐Burn every stick of the late wood by day, Bildrath's load too
    • ☐Or wait in Grigore's yard for whatever comes at dusk

    Hint: Burn all the late wood by day, Grigore's pile and Bildrath's load, or wait in Grigore's yard at dusk.

    Reached through: Pale logs on the woodpile in Village of Barovia; Luca in Village of Barovia; Grigore in Village of Barovia; Bildrath Cantemir in Bildrath's Mercantile

    Burned

    We burned the late wood in Grigore's yard. It screamed in the fire, and what crawled out of the ashes we finished with steel.

    • ☐Get the splinter out of Luca's hand

    Hint: Talk to Grigore in his yard on the village's west road, and get the splinter out of Luca's hand (Medicine helps).

    Stood Up

    At dusk the late wood walked. Bildrath's load rolled up the street on its own, and Grigore's woodpile stood up as one thing and reached for the boy. We cut it down in the yard.

    • ☐Get the splinter out of Luca's hand

    Hint: Talk to Grigore in his yard on the village's west road, and get the splinter out of Luca's hand (Medicine helps).

    Luca Woke (ends: success)

    Luca is awake. The late trees by the west road grew where a man in a bear's hide planted seeds in the spring, singing to them. He told Luca the hill wants the valley back. The hill must be Yester Hill, west of the Wizard of Wines.

    Reached through: Grigore in Village of Barovia

    Fights

    The logs split in the fire, and things crawl out of the splits: pale, thorned, too many legs, and very angry… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • Village of Barovia, when level ≥ 4: 2 × Vine Blight, 4 × Needle Blight, 2 × Twig Blight (450 XP)
    • Village of Barovia, when level ≥ 3: Vine Blight, 3 × Needle Blight, 4 × Twig Blight (350 XP)
    • Village of Barovia: 2 × Needle Blight, 3 × Twig Blight (175 XP)

    The woodpile stands up. It is taller than Grigore's house, logs for ribs and roots for fingers, and the… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • Village of Barovia, when level ≥ 4: The Woodpile (Shambling Mound), 2 × Vine Blight, 4 × Needle Blight (2200 XP)
    • Village of Barovia, when level ≥ 3: The Woodpile (Shambling Mound), 4 × Twig Blight, 2 × Needle Blight (2000 XP)
    • Village of Barovia: Vine Blight, 3 × Needle Blight, 3 × Twig Blight (325 XP)

    • Persuasion DC 18: A loyal customer is worth more than one sale. (one try)
    • Intimidation DC 18: Lower the prices. (one try)
    • Deception DC 15: We've no idea what you're talking about. (If he doesn't believe you, you're thrown out.)
    • Intimidation DC 14: Call your nephew off, or else. (If he doesn't back down, you're thrown out.)
    • Nature DC 13: Split one open and look at the grain.
    • Arcana DC 15: Is there magic in it?
    • Medicine DC 13: Open his hand and look.
