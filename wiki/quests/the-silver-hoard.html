    The Silver Hoard

    Argynvost gave his hoard away: every Midwinter the Order left a silver coin on every doorstep in the valley. The last winter's tithe never went out.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    Argynvostholt · level 8
    Given by
    Sir Godfrey Gwilym
    Starts at
    Argynvostholt
    Rewards
    Golden Idol of Good Fortunes, 250 gp or 1200 gp, depending on how it goes

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    This quest opens at level 9 (every character in the party has to be level 9). It starts at Argynvostholt in either of two ways, and both put it at Asked:

    • Ask Sir Godfrey Gwilym in his room on Argynvostholt, Upper Floor "A dragon's house. Where did he keep his hoard?" He explains the Dragon's Tithe: the dragon gave his silver away, a coin on every doorstep, every Midwinter. The last tithe was chested in the undercroft the night the house fell, and its keeper went down to it and never came back up.
    • Or examine the draught under the servants' table in the servants' hall, at the east side of Argynvostholt, Ground Floor. This way works even once the knights are at rest and Godfrey is gone.

    Step by step

    1. Go down to the undercroft. Once you've been told about the tithe, a stair opens under the servants' table and leads down into The Undercroft. At the far end of the vault, The Gilded Knight sits on the tithe chest, grown over with four hundred years of silver, counting. He challenges you as you come close.
    2. Talk to him. Your options:
      • "Godfrey sent us. It's time it went down to the valley." He refuses: the valley belongs to the count now, and he won't let the count's people have the dragon's silver.
      • Insight DC 15 ("Watch his hands on the chest") shows a squire's shield cut nearly in two at his feet. Somebody tried to carry the tithe down the hill that night, and he stopped them. A failed Insight can't be tried again, but it costs you nothing.
      • "Let them go home." is only offered if you know the dragon's last words: from his echo in the chapel, or by clearing Ioan's name with Godfrey in The Squire (reading Ioan's headstone alone, as when Cosmin goes home after the beacon, doesn't count). It reaches him. He wants the silver off him but can't let go, so you still have to fight, but he starts at 140 HP with three harnesses instead of 178 HP with twelve. Use it if you can.
      • "Then we'll carry it down ourselves." starts the fight at full strength.
    3. The fight. The Gilded Knight (The Gilded Knight, AC 19) rises with twelve An Escort's Harness (Animated Armor), or three if the dragon's last words reached him. It's the same fight at every level. He swings twice with a silvered longsword, can paralyse one of you with his Covetous Glare (Wisdom save DC 16), turns two failed saves a day into successes, and gets two extra moves a round, at the end of a hero's turn, either a sword stroke or a step. He regenerates 10 HP every turn unless fire or radiant damage hit him, so put fire or radiant on him every round, and save your best save-or-suffer spells until his Legendary Resistance is spent. Winning moves the quest to Fought.

    1. Open the tithe chest. Use it once he's gone. In what's left of his armour you find a Golden Idol of Good Fortunes, and then you decide what happens to the silver.

    Choices and endings

    • Delivered: send it down to the valley, as the dragon meant. You get 250 gp. Godrick +3, Kip +3, Liriel +2, Wren +2, Thistle +1. If the knights still walk, Godfrey has them carry it up and ride it out at Midwinter. If they're at rest, you carry it to the villages yourselves. Either way, weeks later you see old dragon coins on strings around children's necks.
    • Kept. You get 1,200 gp. Godrick -3, Kip -3, Liriel -2, Wren -1, Ratatouille +1.
    • "(Leave it for now.)" lets you come back and decide later.

    If you're stuck

    • No option to ask about the hoard? Godfrey only asks once everyone in the party is level 9, and only from his own room (not while he travels with you). The draught under the servants' table works too.
    • The stair down isn't there until Godfrey or the draught has told you about the tithe.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Asked: Every Midwinter the Order of the Silver Dragon carried the dragon's hoard down the valley and left a coin on every doorstep with a fire behind it: the Dragon's Tithe. The last winter's tithe was chested in Argynvostholt's undercroft the night the house fell, and the knight who kept it went down to it and never came up again.
    Hint: Take the stair under the servants' table in Argynvostholt's hall down to the undercroft, and talk to the knight on the tithe chest.

    Fought: The tithe-keeper had been sitting on the dragon's silver for four hundred years, and it had grown over him. We took it off him. The tithe chest is ours to open.
    Hint: Open the tithe chest at the far end of Argynvostholt's undercroft.

    Delivered (the end: success): We sent the dragon's last tithe down to the valley, four hundred years late: a silver coin on every doorstep with a fire behind it. Children wear them on string now.

    Kept (the end: success): We kept the dragon's last tithe for ourselves. It's very heavy, and it never seems to get any lighter.
