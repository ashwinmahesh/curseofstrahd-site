    Rosemary for Kolyan

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    The Village of Barovia · level 3
    Given by
    Ireena Kolyana
    Starts at
    The Krezkovs' House

    Summary

    Ireena asked us to lay a sprig of pressed rosemary on her father's grave in the Village of Barovia.

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Ireena asks this of you once her father is in the ground (The Burgomaster's Burial) and she's sheltering somewhere without you: at The Blue Water Inn or St. Andral's Church in Vallaki, or at The Krezkovs' House in Krezk. Check in on her: "How are you holding up under the Martikovs' roof?" at the inn, "How are you holding up here at the church?" at St. Andral's, or "How are they treating you behind Krezk's wall?" in Krezk. When the check-in reaches its menu, pick "There is something we wanted to speak with you about." and then "You have something in your hand. What do you need from us?"

    She pressed a sprig of rosemary in her father's ledger the night they buried him, and she can't walk that road herself. "Give us the rosemary. We'll lay it on his grave." starts the quest at Given. The sprig isn't an item in your pack; the journal remembers it. "Keep it for now. We're not heading that way yet." leaves it with her, and she asks again at a later check-in.

    Step by step

    1. Lay it on the grave. Go to the Village of Barovia and examine The burgomaster's grave in the churchyard behind the church, where you buried him. You wedge the sprig against the marker at the head of the plot, and the quest moves to Laid. There's no roll and no fight at the grave.
      • If Ismark stayed home in the village when Ireena left (he isn't travelling with you, guarding his sister, or in Krezk), he comes down the path with a spade and is glad to have something of her hand at the grave.
      • If you questioned Kolyan with Speak with Dead earlier, his gruff voice seems to rise from the earth once more, telling you to see that his girl eats a hot meal.
    2. Tell Ireena how it looks. Check in on her again, wherever she is (the inn, the church, Krezk, or on the road if she has joined you since), and pick "There is something we wanted to speak with you about.", then "We went by the churchyard and laid the rosemary for your father." She asks how his grave looks:
      • "Raw earth, a wooden marker, and old wildflowers gone brown." The plain truth, which is what she asked for and what her father would have wanted.
      • "It looked peaceful. Someone had left fresh flowers all across it." She sees straight through it ("You're terrible liars"), but thanks you for the kindness.
      • "Ismark was there at the graveside when we laid it down.", only if he came. She's glad he was there.

      Whichever you choose, she then tells you something about her father she has never told anyone. The quest ends at Done.

    Choices and endings

    • Done. Your answer only changes her reply; all three end the same way. Ireena's trust in you rises by 1, and everyone in the party who lacks it earns Heroic Inspiration for carrying a sprig to a grave for her.
    • Alive. If Kolyan is raised from his grave before you lay the sprig (only possible when Madam Eva names him as your ally, and it takes a cleric or bard of 9th level and 500 gp of diamonds), she has nothing left to send. At your next check-in, "There is something we wanted to speak with you about." has her say that a man walking about again has little use for grave herbs, and she keeps the rosemary. The quest ends at Alive, with no trust or inspiration.

    If you're stuck

    • She never asks. The burial has to be done, Kolyan must still be in his grave, and she has to be lodged at the inn, the church or the Krezkovs' house. She doesn't ask while she travels with you, or while she's back in her father's house after you declined to take her. Even then, the request only comes inside a check-in, after "There is something we wanted to speak with you about."
    • The grave shows nothing new. It only takes the sprig while the quest is at Given. Before that, or after it's laid, examining it gives the usual scene at Kolyan's grave.
    • No "We went by the churchyard" option. The quest has to be at Laid, and it only appears inside a check-in, after "There is something we wanted to speak with you about."

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Given: Ireena gave us a sprig of rosemary from her father's ledger and asked us to lay it on his grave in the village churchyard. She wants us to tell her plainly how the place is keeping.
    Hint: Kolyan Indirovich's grave is in the churchyard of the Village of Barovia.

    Laid: We laid Ireena's sprig of rosemary on Kolyan Indirovich's grave in the village churchyard. We should go back to Ireena and tell her how the grave looks.
    Hint: Speak with Ireena at the Blue Water Inn or St. Andral's in Vallaki, or in Krezk.

    Done (the end: success): We told Ireena how her father's grave is keeping, and she told us a story about him she had never told anyone else.

    Alive (the end: success): Kolyan Indirovich was raised from his grave before we could lay the rosemary on the earth, so Ireena kept the sprig for herself.
