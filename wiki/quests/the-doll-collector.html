    The Doll Collector

    Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    Vallaki · level 5
    Given by
    Izek Strazni
    Starts at
    Vallaki
    Rewards
    Signal Whistle, 1 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, stands in Vallaki's town square by day until the festival begins. If Ireena is in your party, he walks straight up to her the first time he sees her, and the quest starts at Noticed. You can answer him three ways:

    • "Her business is her own": he watches her walk away.
    • Intimidation DC 14, stepping between them: he backs off, "not in front of the town" (Godrick +2, Thistle +1). If it fails, he turns hostile and marks you.
    • "Her name is Ireena": he tells her he's seen her every night since he was nine.

    If Ireena isn't with you, the journal starts later, at Dolls Found, when Blinsky tells you about the dolls (step 1).

    Step by step

    1. Find the dolls. Blinsky Toys is on market row, west of the square, and is open by day only.
      • If Ireena is with you, Blinsky recognises "the doll lady" the moment she walks in, and the quest moves to Dolls Found.
      • Without her, read the order book on his counter (eleven red-haired dolls for "I.S."), or examine the chest of dolls in Izek's room at The Burgomaster's Mansion. Then ask Blinsky "Tell us about Izek's dolls" to reach Dolls Found.
      • While you're in the shop, try Performance DC 12, "Try to make him laugh". Success earns a Signal Whistle (Wren +2); failure costs you a gold piece to his monkey.

    2. Hear his story. Find Izek in the square by day and ask "What happened to your arm?". When he was nine, wolves came out of the woods past the lake and took his little sister and his arm, and he woke up with the arm he has now. "We've heard about your dolls" gets his side of it: she's real, and when he finds her he'll keep her safe. The Insight DC 13 option only adds colour.
    3. Work out who she is. You need to have heard his story, seen the dolls and met Ireena. Then either:
      • with Izek in the square, try Insight DC 12, "(Put the pieces together.)". Success moves the quest to Truth Guessed. A failure can't be retried.
      • or, while Ireena is lodged at the Blue Water Inn or at St. Andral's, ask her "Ireena, what do you remember from before Kolyan found you?". She remembers trees, the cold, and a boy shouting a name. That's Truth Guessed with no roll, and she would rather he knew.

      If the Insight fails while Ireena is travelling with you, leave her at the inn (before the bones come home) or the church (after), then ask her.

    4. Tell him. Choose "Izek, we know who the red-haired woman is", in the square by day before the festival or in his room at night. Then:
      • "Let Ireena tell him herself", if she's in your party: no roll needed.
      • Persuasion DC 14, "Make him hear it". If it fails, he calls you a liar, turns hostile, and will only hear it from her.
      • "We'll bring her to you", if she isn't with you.

      Once he believes it, the quest ends at Truth Told. He won't touch her, won't let anyone else touch her, and won't break any more heads for the Baron.

    5. Or confront him at night. His room is off the mansion's foyer, and after dark he sits on his cot turning a doll in the candlelight. You can tell him you know about the dolls, warn him off with Intimidation DC 16 (success: he tells you to get out; failure: he fights), or draw steel.
      • The fight is the Izek Confrontation: at level 5, Izek (112 HP) and two sergeants of the watch (Warrior Veterans), 3,200 XP. That's between a moderate and a hard fight for four level 5 characters. At level 4 it's Izek and one sergeant (2,500 XP, harder than hard), and below that Izek alone (1,800 XP).
      • He swings his battleaxe twice a turn, and can swap a swing for a 60 ft bolt of fire. While he holds his axe he parries one melee hit a round (+3 AC against it), so spells and ranged attacks land more surely.
      • Winning ends the quest at Izek Slain. His chest holds a Potion of Giant Strength (Hill Giant) and a Potion of Giant Strength (Fire Giant), though taking them counts as theft.

    ✧Speak with Dead: Kolyan

    If you've heard Izek Strazni's story of his lost sister, casting Speak with Dead on the burgomaster (at his coffin in the mansion, or his grave in the village churchyard) proves it: Kolyan found Ireena on the lake road the winter the wolves took Izek's arm. The journal moves to the truth being guessed, and you can tell Izek who she is.

    Choices and endings

    • Truth Told. This is the kindest ending, and the one that most helps you at the festival. If you side with Lady Wachter or the town, Izek stays out of the festival fight, where he would otherwise be the hardest enemy. If the Baron wins, he makes no claim on Ireena. Telling him on the dais after the Baron's triumph also earns Wren +2, Liriel +2 and Godrick +1.
    • After the truth. Izek leaves the Baron, but he doesn't vanish. Ireena's news when you check in on her points you to him: he's on The Hill Trail above Lake Zarovich, digging with his bare claws under the pines where he lost her. Tell him "Ireena asked after you." and he says he'll go to her father's grave. From the day after you find him, he stands at the burgomaster's grave in the Village of Barovia. There, "Go and sit by Ireena's door. Keep watch over her." sends him to guard her wherever she shelters (the Blue Water Inn, St. Andral's, the Krezkovs' house or her father's mansion), and "Ireena is with us. Walk with us.", if she's in your party, has him follow her as a guest who fights beside you. While he guards her door, Strahd's visit to her window gets a new answer, "Let Izek take the window" (see Sanctuary). Three scenes between brother and sister then play in order, at her door ("Is there something you two haven't said?"), at the grave if she's with you, or by the camp fire: the first doll Blinsky carved, their parents, and the name he shouted through the trees, which she can keep beside her own or leave. Each one raises her trust, and nothing rolls.
    • Izek Slain. He can die in his room, or in the duel after the Baron's triumph. He also dies automatically if you side with Lady Wachter or the town at the festival and he fights you there for the Baron.
    • Spurned. If you back the Baron and Ireena is with you or lodged in Vallaki, Izek asks for her as his reward after the festival. Persuasion DC 14 makes the Baron refuse him. This doesn't end the quest: Izek waits and watches. In the Baron's town he stands in the square by day, but you can only talk it over with him in his room at night, where you can still tell him the truth, or fight him. If he met her in the square before the festival and she's lodged at the inn or the church, he carries her off to his room on the first night after it (see the next point).
    • Izek takes Ireena. While he doesn't know the truth and she's lodged at the inn or the church, he comes for her (Sanctuary has the nights and who brings the news). At night you find them both in his room, and his choices change: "Ireena, are you hurt?"; "Izek, we know who the red-haired woman is.", once you've worked it out, then Persuasion DC 14 "Make him hear it." or "Let Ireena tell him herself." with no roll (either ends the quest at Truth Told, and he lets her go; if the Persuasion fails, Ireena offers to tell him herself); Intimidation DC 16 "Let her walk out, Izek, or you won't see another festival." (he lets her go and stays hostile; a failure means the fight); or "(Draw steel.) This ends tonight." (she slips out to wait on the stairs, then the Izek Confrontation). By day, while he's out, she's alone in the room and "Come on. We're getting you out of here now." walks her out. If nobody comes within two days, she breaks his nose with a candlestick and walks out on her own. Afterwards his room at night shows how it ended, and the usual choices come back.
    • If Ireena has taken sanctuary at St. Andral's in the Baron's town, Izek stands at the church door every dusk.
    • Later, if you've heard Izek's story (or Ireena is with you), Emil Toranescu in the werewolf den has something to add about that night by the lake. Once Izek knows the truth, you can pass it on to him: "Emil saw that night. Castle wolves, and a rider in black."

    If you're stuck

    • Izek isn't in the square. He leaves it when the festival begins. At night he's in his room, and after the festival he stays in town only if the Baron won. Once he knows the truth he leaves Vallaki: look on the hill trail above Lake Zarovich, then at the burgomaster's grave.
    • There's no "Put the pieces together" option. You need his story (ask about his arm), the dolls (Blinsky's order book, Izek's chest, or Blinsky meeting Ireena), and to have met Ireena.
    • You can't ask Ireena what she remembers. She can only be asked once she's lodged at the inn or the church, not while she travels with you.
    • The toyshop is shut. It's only open by day.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Noticed: Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, looked at Ireena as if he had seen her face before, many times.
    Hint: Ask Blinsky the toymaker in Vallaki who orders his dolls, or watch Izek when Ireena is near.

    Dolls Found: Izek pays Blinsky the toymaker to carve doll after doll of one red-haired woman, and keeps them in a chest at the foot of his bed. They all have Ireena's face.
    Hint: Ask Ireena what she remembers from before Kolyan found her, or press Izek about his past (Insight).

    Truth Guessed: Izek lost a little sister in the woods when he was a boy, the night the wolves took his arm. Ireena was found wandering at about that age, with no memory of where she came from. They are brother and sister.
    Hint: Decide whether to tell Izek what you suspect. He is in Vallaki by day and in his room at the Baron's mansion at night.

    Truth Told (the end: success): We told Izek who Ireena is. The Baron's fist went quiet as a struck bell. He will not lay a hand on her now, nor, it seems, on anyone else for the Baron. He walked out of the Baron's service that same day, and nobody has seen him in the square since.

    Izek Slain (the end: success): Izek Strazni is dead. Whatever he dreamed of died with him.

    Spurned: The Baron refused Izek his reward. Izek obeyed, for now, and still watches Ireena whenever she crosses the square.
    Hint: Izek still watches Ireena in Vallaki. Telling him what you know may change things.
