    Lady Wachter's Book Club

    Lady Fiona Wachter wants the Baron's chair, and she is not shy about who she would rule for.

    Type
    Side quest
    Region
    Vallaki · level 5
    Given by
    Lady Wachter
    Starts at
    Wachterhaus

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    The rumour reaches you from all over Vallaki, and any of these puts the quest at Rumored:

    • the Baron's "Who would wish your festival ill?" in his great hall;
    • the gate watch's "Heard anything interesting?", while the book club still meets;
    • Urwin Martikov's "Heard anything interesting?" at the Blue Water Inn;
    • simply calling on Lady Wachter herself.

    She receives in the parlour of Wachterhaus, up Wachter lane north-west of the square, at any hour, except while the festival is running.

    Step by step

    1. Take tea. She pours for everyone without asking how you take it. Ask about the book club or the Baron to hear her talk.
      • Insight DC 14, "What does she actually want?", shows you a patient, faithful woman who wants the Baron's chair. A failure tells you nothing, and you can't try it again.
      • Investigation DC 13, asking about the locked door upstairs and the scratching, makes her tell you about her daughter Stella. That starts The Cat in the Window. A failure only gets you "mice", and since it isn't a social check you can ask again.
    2. Hear her offer. "You didn't invite us here for the tea" moves the quest to Offer. She means to step up when the Baron's festival fails, and she wants you standing behind her.
      • "You'd rule it for Strahd" names it plainly (Godrick +1, Liriel +1).
      • "We'll stand with you", now or later as "About your offer. We're with you", moves this quest to Joined and the festival to Wachter Side (Liriel -3, Godrick -3, Kip -2, Thistle, Wren and Ratatouille -1).
      • Once you've joined, she gives her orders: soak the wicker sun after midnight, keep Izek off her on the day, and don't kill Izek before the festival.
    3. Find the cellar. Go through the hall into her study.
      • The desk drawer (lock DC 14) holds 45 gp and Perfume. It's hers, and she'll mention next time that she knows.
      • A Search near the desk can turn up a hidden drawer (Investigation DC 15): Eyes of Charming.
      • A Search beside the bookcase that reaches 13 finds a catch behind the third shelf, and the same roll finds the panel itself, a secret door with the same DC 13. Stella, once you've freed her, tells you the same trick.
      • The panel opens on a stair down to a cellar made into a chapel, with a black-draped shrine to the lord of Ravenloft. Read A letter on the shrine: Strahd's own letter, thanking her for her loyalty and signed "S." That letter is your evidence.
      • Religion DC 15 at the shrine reads its dedication and reveals the cult's tithe hidden underneath it: a Rod of Rulership. A Search beside the shrine (DC 15) finds the tithe too.
    4. The book club, by night. By day the cellar is empty. If you go down after dark while you're not pledged to her (and before the festival's fight), the book club is waiting at the shrine. They're in plain view as you come down the stair, so you can strike first. Nikolai and Karl Wachter (Toughs) and the circle:
      • at level 5: six Cultists, three hired swords and two more Warrior Veterans, and "something from the shrine", a Shadow (3,950 XP, a moderate fight for four level 5 characters);
      • at level 4: four cultists, one hired sword and two more Toughs (1,200 XP, low);
      • below level 4: five cultists and one hired sword (1,025 XP).

      The shadow shrugs off most elements but radiant damage hurts it badly. The cultists have about 9 HP each.

      Winning moves the quest to Cult Broken. From then on Lady Wachter sits alone in black and won't take a pledge, and at the festival only her hired swords come for the Baron.

    5. Decide who sees the letter.
      • The Baron: "We found this in Lady Wachter's cellar", offered unless you're pledged to her. The quest moves to Evidence Found, and he denounces her at the festival.
      • Father Lucian: "We found a letter in Lady Wachter's cellar", offered only while you're pledged to the town. The quest moves to Evidence Found, and he reads it to the square.
      • Nobody: if the town wins the festival, you can still read it out yourself.
      • Telling Lady Wachter you've read it changes nothing; she isn't ashamed of her correspondents.
    6. The festival. Lady Wachter makes her move at The Festival of the Blazing Sun, and which side you stand on decides how this quest ends.

    Choices and endings

    • Coup Crushed (you stood with the Baron and won the coup fight). She goes into the stocks in the square.
      • There, Insight DC 12 shows she's calmly waiting for nightfall and a visitor.
      • In his great hall, the Baron will exile her instead for Persuasion DC 15 or 100 gp for a "Festival of Mercy" (Liriel +2, Wren +2, Godrick +1, Kip +1).
    • Coup Won (you stood with her and won the loyalists fight). She rules from the Baron's great hall and pays you 150 gp. Ask her what Vallaki's future is: an open gate for black carriages, and bows for the castle's guests. Insight DC 13 shows what she promised the castle.
    • Exposed (you stood with the town). After the fight she offers to take the Baron's place. If the Baron or Lucian has the letter, it's read to the crowd at once. Otherwise you can read it yourself, or make her betray herself with Persuasion DC 15. The council locks her in the lockup.
    • Slipped Away (you stood with the town, but nobody read the letter out, and the Persuasion failed or you chose to let her talk). She walks home free to Wachterhaus, and the quest ends there as a success; she has only a sour word for you if you call on her.
    • Reading the letter also gives you an argument for the Abbot at The Abbey of St. Markovia much later (Persuasion DC 12).

    If you're stuck

    • No panel behind the bookcase. Stand right by the bookcase and Search again: a Search finds things within 15 feet, and a total of 13 turns up both the catch and the panel.
    • No book club at night. The cellar fight never happens while you're pledged to Lady Wachter, and the cellar is empty by day. If all you want is the letter, go by day.
    • She isn't at home. She's in the square while the festival runs. Afterwards she's only at Wachterhaus if she slipped away.
    • She won't take your pledge. She turns cold once her cellar circle is broken.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Rumored: Lady Fiona Wachter's book club meets late and reads nothing. The Baron is sure she is plotting against him. He may even be right.
    Hint: Pay Lady Wachter a call at Wachterhaus in Vallaki.

    Offer: Lady Wachter told us plainly that she means to unseat the Baron at the festival, and that she would like our help. She would like it very much.
    Hint: Pledge to Lady Wachter at Wachterhaus, or look into her cellar's reading circle first.

    Evidence Found: Beneath Wachterhaus we found a shrine to the lord of Barovia and a letter in his hand, thanking Lady Wachter for her loyalty. Whoever reads it will know what she is.
    Hint: The letter has been read. Go to the Festival of the Blazing Sun in Vallaki's square and see what Lady Wachter does.

    Cult Broken: We broke up the midnight circle in Lady Wachter's cellar. Her sons and her hired swords are finished, whatever she still plans.
    Hint: Go to the Festival of the Blazing Sun in Vallaki's square and see what Lady Wachter still tries.

    Joined: We pledged ourselves to Lady Wachter's cause. She wants the Baron's wicker sun to fail, and us beside her when it does.
    Hint: Ask the festival herald in Vallaki's square to begin, and stand with Lady Wachter when she speaks.

    Coup Crushed (the end: success): Lady Wachter's coup failed at the festival. She has been dragged to the Baron's stocks to wait for his justice.

    Coup Won (the end: success): Lady Wachter's coup succeeded. She sits in the burgomaster's chair, and Vallaki belongs to her and, through her, to someone else.

    Exposed (the end: success): Lady Wachter's devotion to the lord of Barovia was read out before the whole town. The new council has locked her in the Baron's lockup.

    Slipped Away (the end: success): Lady Wachter lost her chance at the festival, but nobody could prove what she is. She has gone home to Wachterhaus to wait for another.

    ✦Every skill check in its conversations
    • Persuasion DC 15: Ask her, in front of everyone, whose hand she means to rule with.
    • Insight DC 13: Read him. What's under the ribbons?
    • Persuasion DC 15: Let Lady Wachter out of the stocks. Exile her instead.
    • Insight DC 14: What does she actually want?
    • Investigation DC 13: (Ask about the locked door upstairs, and the scratching.)
    • Insight DC 12: What is she waiting for?
    • Insight DC 13: What did she promise the castle?
    • Insight DC 15: There's something he's not saying.
