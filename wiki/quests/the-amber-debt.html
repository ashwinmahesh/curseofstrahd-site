    The Amber Debt

    ✦Side quest

    Exethanter has an old note pinned under all the others, in his own hand: DO NOT GO BELOW THE VAULT. YOU SEALED IT. YOU DO NOT REMEMBER WHY. THAT IS WHY.

    Region: The Amber Temple (party level about 10)
    Given by: Exethanter
    Starts: Exethanter in The Amber Temple: the Library (The warden's study)
    Rewards: Spellguard Shield, 700 gp

    Walkthrough

    Getting the quest

    Once the lowest-level hero in your party is level 10, Exethanter's menu in The Amber Temple: the Library has There's an older note pinned under the others. It is in his own hand, brown with age: DO NOT GO BELOW THE VAULT. YOU SEALED IT. YOU DO NOT REMEMBER WHY. THAT IS WHY. He mentions that the vault floor has been warm lately (Rumored).

    You can also skip him: from level 10 a crack weeping amber appears in the vault floor, and opening it starts the quest at Found.

    Step by step

    1. Go down to The Amber Temple: the Vaults. At the far east end of the hall of vestiges, near the cracked, silent sarcophagus, is a crack weeping warm amber. Something deep in it blinks.
    2. Athletics DC 15: (Lever the slab up.) A failure still gets it open; it just takes half an hour. The quest moves to Found, and a stair goes down through soft amber.
    3. Go down to Under the Vaults. Stepping into the hall of sleepers wakes the Eye's dreams: a sleeper in amber (an Amber Golem at 130 Hit Points) and four Dream-Gazers.
    4. In the wardens' archive, read the wardens' book on the lectern (Read). Choose all three entries:
      • (Read about the mirrors.) The Eye's small dreams hate their own kind; the wardens turned great mirrors to face its chamber.
      • (Read about the singing.) The lullaby that puts it back to sleep, if you sing it to the end. This unlocks the song.
      • (Read the last entry.) Exethanter gave the Eye his memory to keep it asleep. Every time anyone helps him remember, it wakes a little more.
    5. In the hall of sleepers, use A tall mirror, turned to the wall and (Turn the mirrors round to face the chamber.) No roll, ten minutes. With the mirrors turned, no dream-gazer joins the Eye's fight, and the Eye loses its lair.
    6. Walk into the Eye's chamber at the north end. The Eye Below, a beholder as wide as a cart, floats out of its broken block and speaks as you come near.
    7. Performance DC 15: (Sing the wardens' lullaby, all the way to the end.) (only if you read about the singing; one try). On a success it sinks into the amber to its middle, and the fight starts with it at 135 Hit Points instead of 180, and surprised. On a failure, or if you choose (Draw your weapons.), it fights at full strength.
    8. Fight The Eye Below (CR 13, 10,000 XP). Its great central eye is still clouded, but its ten small eyes fire rays out to 120 feet, all against DC 16 saves: paralysis, fear, amber that holds you fast, 6d8 necrotic and 7d8 force among them. It also takes three legendary actions a round and can turn three failed saves a day into successes. The paralysis ray is the worst of them. A paralyzed hero repeats the save at the end of each of their turns, so keep them healed until it wears off. Winning moves the quest to Slain.
    9. Use A heap of things behind the broken block. Among a thousand years of the wardens' rings, crooks and travellers' purses are 700 gp and a Spellguard Shield the wardens made to stand against the Eye. The quest is Done.

    Choices and endings

    • Done (success). 700 gp and the Spellguard Shield. Godrick +2, Liriel +2, Thistle +2, Ratatouille +2, Wren +1.
    • Afterwards, tell Exethanter The thing under the vault is dead. He burns the old note, and for a moment almost remembers a spell (Ratatouille +1, Liriel +1).

    If you're stuck

    • No crack in the vault floor. It only appears once the lowest-level hero is level 10, or once Exethanter has shown you his old note.
    • No lullaby option. You have to read about the singing in the wardens' book in the archive first.
    • The fight ended but the quest is at Slain. Search the heap behind the broken block to finish it.

    Journal stages

    Rumored

    Under all the notes on Exethanter's sleeve there's an old one in his own hand: DO NOT GO BELOW THE VAULT. YOU SEALED IT. YOU DO NOT REMEMBER WHY. THAT IS WHY. He says the vault floor has been warm lately.

    • ☐Look at the vault floor

    Hint: Look at the floor of the Amber Temple's vaults for what Exethanter sealed.

    Reached through: Exethanter in The Amber Temple: the Library

    Found

    A crack in the vault floor weeps warm amber, and something deep in it blinked. Under the slab a stair goes down.

    • ☐Go down under the vaults

    Hint: Go down the stair under the vault floor.

    Reached through: A crack weeping amber in The Amber Temple: the Vaults

    Read

    The wardens' day-book: a thing all eyes came up from under the mountain while they built the temple. They couldn't kill it; they sang it to sleep in amber, and when the song stopped holding it Exethanter gave it his memory. Every time he remembers anything, it wakes a little more.

    • ☐Optional: turn the hall's mirrors toward its chamber
    • ☐Optional: learn the wardens' lullaby
    • ☐Put out the Eye Below

    Hint: Under the vaults, use the wardens' book: turn the mirrors, learn their lullaby, then put out the Eye Below.

    Reached through: The wardens' book in Under the Vaults

    Slain

    The Eye Below is dead in its broken amber.

    • ☐Look through what it collected

    Hint: Search the heap of things the Eye collected, under the vaults.

    Done (ends: success)

    The Eye Below is dead, and the things its dreams fetched it for a thousand years are ours, among them a shield the wardens made against it. Exethanter can forget it in peace now, or remember. Either way it won't matter.

    • ☐Optional: tell Exethanter

    Reached through: A heap of things in Under the Vaults

    Fights

    The Eye Below sinks back into the amber to its middle as the last note of the lullaby goes, and wakes too… (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)

    • Under the Vaults, when deep lullaby sung and deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below (10000 XP; surprise: enemies)
    • Under the Vaults, when deep lullaby sung and not deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below, Dream-Gazer (10100 XP; lair; surprise: enemies)
    • Under the Vaults, when not deep lullaby sung and deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below (10000 XP)
    • Under the Vaults: The Eye Below, Dream-Gazer (10100 XP; lair)

    Skill checks in conversation

    • Athletics DC 15: (Lever the slab up.)
    • Performance DC 15: (Sing the wardens' lullaby, all the way to the end.)

    Companion approval

    • Ratatoille: +1: You told Exethanter the thing he sealed is dead; +2: You put out the Eye Below
    • Liriel Dawnsong: +1: You told Exethanter the thing he sealed is dead; +2: You put out the Eye Below
    • Godrick Pendlebrook: +2: You put out the Eye Below
    • Thistle: +2: You put out the Eye Below
    • Wren Featherfoot: +1: You put out the Eye Below
