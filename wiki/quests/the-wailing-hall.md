## Summary

In the frozen reading hall of the Amber Temple, a dead woman weeps over a book that will not stay whole.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

In the Amber Temple, climb from the doors into [The Amber Temple: Hall of the Faceless God](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-amber-temple-hall-of-the-faceless-god.md) and go west into the hall of echoes. At the one desk not furred with frost sits [Patrina Velikovna](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/patrina-velikovna.md), a dusk elf ghost scarred by stones, turning pages that crumble at her touch. She speaks as you come near, and the quest starts (Heard). See [The Amber Temple](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-amber-temple.md) for how to get up the mountain.

![patrina alone](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/patrina-alone.webp)

It helps a great deal to have met her brother first. [Kasimir Velikov](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/kasimir-velikov.md) mends nets at [The Vistani Camp](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-vistani-camp.md) outside Vallaki. Pass **Insight DC 13** on what he is grieving for, or simply **sit with him a while** (an hour, no roll; Wren +2, Liriel +1), and he tells you about his sister Patrina, stoned by their own people while he stood at the back. He travels with you if Madam Eva's cards named him as your ally. Otherwise, once he has told you about her, ask him at his wagon **Come with us to the temple, Kasimir.**: it's offered from level 8, or once you've been to the Tsolenka Pass, and he comes as a guest.

### Step by step

**Without Kasimir in the party:**

1. Talk to her. **Who are you?** gets her story. Insight DC 14 there shows it's the crowd she can't forgive, and one face at the back of it.
2. If Kasimir told you about her, say **You're Patrina. Kasimir's sister.** The quest moves to Named, and she tells you her brother stood at the back and watched.
3. Lay her to rest one of two ways:
   - **Your brother grieves for you. He's sorry.** (after naming her), then **Persuasion DC 15: He's been too afraid to come. That's the only thing he's ever been, and he knows it.** She calls that very nearly brave, and rests (Wren +2). If the roll fails it is spent: she starts to wail, and you must **back away, quickly** or fight. **He'd bring you back, if he could.** only gets a scornful no.
   - **Religion DC 16: Your bones lie under the castle. Let them lie, and rest.** This doesn't need her name, and if it fails she just tells you not to preach, so you can try again. On a success she rests (Liriel +2, Wren +1).

**With Kasimir in the party:**

1. The first time you talk to her with him along, he stops in the archway and faces his sister. This plays whether or not you named her on an earlier visit without him. The quest moves to Named.
   ![patrina kasimir](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/patrina-kasimir.webp)
2. She tells him to say it properly. Every choice here ends with her at rest:
   - **(Let him speak for himself.)** "I let them, Patrina. Because I was afraid of being you."
   - **Persuasion DC 13: (Help Kasimir find the words.)** On a success he says it in full. On a failure he shakes his head and finds his own words anyway.
   - With Wren in the party, after Wren has given Kasimir Master Sorrel's letter ([The Last Lesson](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-last-lesson.md)), have Kasimir **read her the last line of Sorrel's letter** (Wren +3).
   - **Kasimir. In the vaults below, there's a vestige that could bring her back.** She forbids it (Wren -3, Liriel -2, Kip -2), and Kasimir speaks for himself.

**Kasimir's bargain.** While Kasimir is with you and Patrina isn't yet at rest, he can step up to [Tarakamedes](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/tarakamedes.md), the dragon in the grave, in the vaults' hall of vestiges. It offers to give her back bones, voice and breath for every year he has left but one.
- **Let him choose.** He pays. Patrina is restored.
- **Persuasion DC 15: She went to the castle the first time, Kasimir. She'll go again.** On a success he steps back (Wren +4, Liriel +2, Kip +1). **On a failure, he makes the bargain.**
- **You heard her. She doesn't want to be bought back.** (only if you've met Patrina). No roll, and he steps back with the same approval.

**The fight.** **(Draw steel.)**, or staying after a failed Persuasion, brings Patrina (a [Banshee](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/banshee.md) with 110 Hit Points), eleven frozen students ([Specter](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/specter.md)s) and seven more banshees, 11,000 XP: just past High for four level 9 heroes, a little under High at level 10. Once a day each banshee's Deathly Wail hits everyone within 30 feet (Constitution DC 13), and a hero who fails it at 25 Hit Points or fewer drops to 0. Keep your hurt heroes out of that range.

### Choices and endings

- **At rest (success).** She closes the book, and it doesn't crumble. She asks you to tell her brother she is done waiting, and fades like breath from a window. If you destroy Strahd, the epilogue tells you her bones lie quiet under the castle. If Kasimir was there to say it himself, his own slide takes the place of hers: he leaves Barovia lighter than he came (or, if Strahd wins, his peace with her ghost is the only peace he finds).
- **Restored (failure).** Kasimir's hair goes ash-grey, the wailing stops, and bare footprints lead out of the temple and down toward the castle. She didn't wait for him. Wren -3, Liriel -2, Kip -2. In the epilogue he spends his last year at her side. If he walks to [Castle Ravenloft, the Gates](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/castle-ravenloft-the-gates.md) with you, he won't go in after her: at the chasm's edge (or at Strahd's table, if you came by the black carriage) he leaves the party and sits on a stone by the road below the drawbridge, counting his last year. You can talk to him there and still buy his scrolls.
- **Destroyed (success).** Her spirit is gone, and the reading hall is only cold.

If Kasimir stayed at the camp, go back and tell him how it ended: **Kasimir, we have returned from the temple with word of Patrina.** after she found rest, or **Kasimir, sit with us - we must speak of Patrina.** after she was destroyed. What he says, and how he greets you afterwards, follows the ending.

### If you're stuck

- **No way to name her.** Only Kasimir's story in the Vistani camp, or Kasimir himself, gives you her name. Without it, the prayer (Religion DC 16) is the peaceful way.
- **You named her before Kasimir joined you.** That's fine: his meeting with her still plays the next time you talk to her with him along. While he travels with you the "Your brother grieves" option is hidden; in his scene he says it to her himself.
- **Mind the failed Persuasion at Tarakamedes.** It doesn't just fail: it lets Kasimir make the bargain. "You heard her" is the safe choice once you've met Patrina.
