    Summary

    Radu has a handsome dun gelding called Dinte that bites everyone, and he will pay fifteen gold to anyone who finds it a home.

    Journal

    ❝Every journal entry and hint

    Offered: Radu's dun gelding Dinte bites everyone who comes within reach, and Radu has been unable to sell him. He'll pay fifteen gold to whoever finds Dinte a home.
    Hint: Look over Dinte in the horse pen at the Tser Pool camp, then find someone who wants a horse: Costica and Ambrose Fennimore are on the Old Svalich Road near the crossroads.

    Buyer Found: Dinte has a home, and a buyer who wasn't bitten, or was. Radu owes us fifteen gold and is going to find that very hard to believe.
    Hint: Tell Radu at the horse pen in the Tser Pool camp that Dinte has a home.

    Sold (the end: success): Dinte has a home. Radu is stunned and delighted, has paid his fifteen gold, and says he will tell this story for the rest of his life, and in it we are very tall.
