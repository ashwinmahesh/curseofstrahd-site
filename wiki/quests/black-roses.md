## Summary

Last winter Ignat the lamplighter's daughter Catalina was found smiling and white at the lake gate, and black roses came with a card. This morning there were black roses on the chandler's step.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

There are two ways in, and both end at Ignat the lamplighter ([Ignat the Lamplighter](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/ignat-the-lamplighter.md)).

- **Urwin's rumour.** In the taproom of [The Blue Water Inn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-blue-water-inn.md), ask [Urwin Martikov](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/urwin-martikov.md) "Heard anything interesting?". Among his news he mentions black roses on [Pyotr Petrov](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/pyotr-petrov.md)'s step, and the quest opens at *Rumored*. Urwin won't talk while the festival is running, and if you backed Lady Wachter he won't give you more than a bed, so in either case go straight to Ignat.
- **Ignat himself.** Ignat only comes out after dark, from 19:00 to 06:00, walking his round of [Vallaki](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/vallaki.md)'s lamps: Wachter lane, the edge of St. Andral's churchyard, the east gate and the main street. If you've heard the rumour, tell him "There were black roses on the chandler's step this morning." If you haven't, ask "Who are the lamps really for?", hear about his daughter [Catalina](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/catalina.md), then ask "Have there been roses since?". Either way the quest moves to *Asked*.

![loc vallaki overview](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/shots/loc-vallaki-overview.webp)

### Step by step

1. **Hear Ignat out.** He asks you to watch the Petrovs' house tonight and not to hurt whatever comes. Before you agree, you can look at him properly with **Insight DC 13** or notice the scarf with **Medicine DC 12**. Pass either one and he shows you the bite marks: Catalina comes to his window every night and he lets her feed (journal *Fed*). This is optional, and it adds a line to his thanks at the end. A failed Insight is gone for good. A failed Medicine isn't: pick "Not tonight", talk to him again and you can try it again. Once you say "We'll watch the house tonight", the chance to ask is gone.
2. **Watch the Petrovs' house after dark.** It's at the far end of the lower town, the quarter by the west gate, and its shutters are nailed shut. Go there between 19:00 and 06:00. Catalina walks up and speaks first when your leader gets within eight squares of her. The journal moves to *Vigil*. She only comes once, so decide here.
3. **Talk to Catalina.** Your options:
   - **"Where do you sleep, Catalina?"** costs nothing, so always ask it first. She tells you she's under the stone with the lamb in St. Andral's churchyard, next to Irina and Daria. This puts the grave on the map.
   - **Persuasion DC 14**, "Go home, Catalina." If it works she drops her roses on the step and climbs away over the roofs to her grave, and you learn where it is. If it fails, she calls the other two girls and the street fight starts. A failed Persuasion can't be tried again.
   - **"Let her go home to her father."** This ends the quest the bad way (see below).
   - **"Attack."** This starts the street fight.
4. **The street fight (`roses_street`)** happens in the lower town at night. At level 6 or higher you face Catalina, two more [Vampire Spawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/vampire-spawn.md) (Irina and Daria) and a [Swarm of Rats](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/swarm-of-rats.md). At level 5 it's Catalina, Irina, Daria, two [Swarms of Bats](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/swarm-of-bats.md) and a rat swarm. At level 4 it's Catalina and Irina, with a [Bat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/bat.md) and a [Giant Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-rat.md). Below that, Catalina comes with three bat swarms. Each girl is a vampire spawn with 90 HP. Their claws grapple, and the bite that drains maximum HP only works on someone who is grappled, restrained or incapacitated. Free a grabbed friend before you do anything else.
5. **Or go to the grave.** Once you know where Catalina sleeps, a "Three small graves" spot appears in St. Andral's churchyard, on the town map at the east end of Vallaki, beside the church door.
   - **By day (06:00–19:00):** "Dig them up now, while it's light" is an **Athletics DC 12** check, and you can retry it if you fail. If it passes, the coffins come up at once. If it fails, the dig still works but takes two hours. The girls wake surprised: at level 6 or higher it's Catalina, Irina and Daria plus one more [Vampire Spawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/vampire-spawn.md); at level 5 it's the three girls with three bat swarms; below that, only Catalina and Irina, with two [Ghouls](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/ghoul.md). Every spawn has 90 HP. Start digging before 17:00. If the two slow hours carry you past 19:00, you get the night version of the fight instead.
   - **By night:** "Wait here for them" gets you the fight with nobody surprised. At level 6 or higher that's Catalina, Irina, Daria and one more spawn; at level 5, Catalina, Irina and eight bat swarms; below that, Catalina and Irina, with a giant rat and a [Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/rat.md).
6. **Tell Ignat, after dark.** Winning either fight moves the journal to *Slain*, but the quest isn't over yet. Find Ignat on his lamp round between 19:00 and 06:00 and talk to him. He thanks you, the quest moves to *Ended*, and he gives you Catalina's mother's ring, the [Ring of Resistance (Necrotic)](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#ring-of-resistance-necrotic).
7. **Collect from Pyotr by day.** Pyotr Petrov stands outside his house in the lower town from 08:00 to 18:00, but not in a storm. Once the girls are destroyed or Ignat is dead, he pays you 200 gp for Mila's life. You can do this before or after seeing Ignat.

### Choices and endings

- **Catalina laid to rest (*Ended*).** You destroy the three girls, in the street or at their graves, and then tell Ignat. You get the Ring of Resistance (Necrotic) from Ignat and 200 gp from Pyotr. Liriel Dawnsong and Godrick Pendlebrook approve (+2), and so does Thistle (+1). Destroying the spawn adds a mark to Strahd's attention. Ignat keeps lighting the town's lamps, all but the one outside his own window, and Urwin's later gossip mentions that he sleeps at night now.
- **Ignat lost (*Ilie Lost*).** You let Catalina go home. Time skips to 07:00, and the watch finds Ignat dead at the lake gate. He's gone from the streets for good, so there's no ring. Pyotr still pays 200 gp because Mila lived. Godrick and Liriel disapprove (-2), and so does Thistle (-1). If you asked Catalina where she sleeps before you let her go, the graves stay on the map and you can still fight the girls there, but the journal stays at *Ilie Lost* and nobody is left to give you the ring.

### If you're stuck

- **The journal says *Slain* and nothing more happens.** The quest only ends when you talk to Ignat on his evening round, 19:00 to 06:00. If the fight ended near dawn, wait for the next evening.
- **Catalina won't appear.** She only comes at night, and only after you've promised Ignat to watch the house. After your first meeting she never comes back.
- **No graves in the churchyard.** They only show once Catalina has told you where she sleeps (by asking her, or by persuading her home) or once the girls are destroyed. Ask her before you pick "Attack".
- **Ignat isn't around in the daytime.** Look for him after dark.

## Journal

> [!quote]- Every journal entry and hint
> **Rumored**: Urwin at the Blue Water Inn says there were black roses on Pyotr Petrov the chandler's step this morning, and that Ignat the lamplighter went white when he saw them.
> *Hint:* Ignat the lamplighter walks Vallaki's streets after dark, lighting the lamps. Find him and ask about the roses.
>
> **Asked**: Ignat has counted. Every girl taken in Vallaki this year had black roses on her step the day before, not after. Tonight's roses are on the Petrovs' step, and their daughter Mila was Catalina's best friend. Ignat asked us to watch the house tonight, and not to hurt whatever comes if we can help it.
> *Hint:* Wait outside the Petrovs' house in Vallaki's lower town after dark, and see who comes to the window.
>
> **Fed**: Ignat wears a scarf in summer. Under it are bite marks, old and new. Catalina is the visitor: she comes to his window every night, and he lets her in and lets her feed, so that she won't go to anyone else. It isn't enough for her any more.
> *Hint:* Wait outside the Petrovs' house in Vallaki's lower town after dark, and see who comes to the window.
>
> **Vigil**: Catalina came to Mila's window with an armful of black roses, "like the gentleman does". She is fourteen, and dead, and hungry, and she still talks like a girl inviting a friend to play.
> *Hint:* By day, look for Catalina's grave in St. Andral's churchyard. Or face her by night, when she comes to the windows.
>
> **Slain**: Catalina is at rest, and the girls she made lie with her. Her father still lights the lamps every night, and doesn't know yet.
> *Hint:* Find Ignat on his lamp rounds through Vallaki's streets, 7 in the evening to 6 in the morning, and tell him. Pyotr Petrov the chandler, in the lower town by day, will want to know too.
>
> **Ended** (the end: success): Catalina is at rest, and so are the two girls she brought with her. In her coffin was her mother's ring, bought from a Vistana to keep the night out. Ignat has put out one of his lamps, the one outside his own window.
>
> **Ilie Lost** (the end: success): We let Catalina go home to her father. In the morning the watch found Ignat at the lake gate, sitting against the post, smiling, white as milk. Mila Petrova is alive. Nobody lights the lamps on his street now.
