## Summary

The dead burgomaster's wolfhound howls every night at the mansion's study wall, not at the wolves.

## Walkthrough

### Getting the quest

Ask [Bildrath Cantemir](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/bildrath-cantemir.md) for news about "The dog at the burgomaster's house.", or just walk up to [Lupu](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/people/lupu.md), the big grey wolfhound in the Burgomaster's yard outside the mansion. He's there at any hour: lying on the step by day, howling at the study wall at night. Either one puts the quest at **Rumored**.

### Step by step

1. **Win Lupu over.** [Animal Handling DC 12] "(Crouch down and let him come to you.)" If he stays wary, try again later. When he warms to you he takes your sleeve and leads you round to the study window, and the quest moves to **Asked**. With [Speak with Animals](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/spells/level-1-spells.md#speak-with-animals) (cast on the spot, or already running) he tells you himself: his boy Sergiu is in the wall.
2. **Find the cold corner.** Inside the [Burgomaster's Mansion](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/burgomasters-mansion.md) (the door opens once you've answered Ismark or Ireena has let you in), examine the cold corner of the study. It's there as soon as the quest is in your journal, even at Rumored. New plaster covers hurried brickwork with garlic in the cracks, and something scratches on the other side.
   - [Investigation DC 12] "(Look for anything pushed in among the bricks.)" finds Kolyan's note. His first son, Sergiu, let the thing at the window in during the siege winter and came back hungry, and his father walled him up. You can try again if you miss it.
3. **Seal it or open it.**
   - **Seal it:** [Religion DC 12] "(Bless the bricks and pack fresh garlic into every crack.)" can be retried if the words don't take. The scratching stops, Lupu stops howling, and the burgomaster's desk drawer gives up a purse of 60 gp. The quest ends at **Sealed** (Liriel +1, Godrick +1). No fight.
   - **Open it:** [Athletics DC 11] "(Break the bricks open.)" opens the wall whether you pass or fail; failing only costs some skinned knuckles. Lupu bursts in and joins you as a guest for the fight. At level 3 or higher it's The Tenant ([Ghast](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/ghast.md), 55 HP) and 5 × A Siege-Winter Servant ([Ghoul](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/ghoul.md), 35 HP each) (1,450 XP); at level 2, the Tenant (70 HP), two ghoul servants (45 HP each), a servant who is a [Zombie](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/zombie.md), and a [Giant Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-rat.md) (925 XP); at level 1, the Tenant (24 HP) and three ghoul servants (1,050 XP). Their claws can paralyze (DC 10 Constitution; elves shrug off the ghouls' but not the ghast's), and standing next to the ghast can poison you, so keep your front line's Constitution saves in mind and don't let one paralyzed hero get swarmed.
4. **After the fight, examine the cold corner again.** This is easy to miss. Lupu goes down into the little room, then leaves you and lies on the mansion step. On a shelf you find a shirt of fine rings Kolyan bought his son: [Mithral Armor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/magic-items.md#mithral-armor), with 60 gp. The quest ends at **Done** (Godrick +2, Thistle +2, Liriel +1).

### Choices and endings

Both endings count as success. Sealing is safe and pays 60 gp. Opening the wall means a fight, and pays the Mithral Armor and 60 gp. You get 60 gp either way, not both purses. Afterwards Lupu sleeps on the mansion step like an ordinary dog.
