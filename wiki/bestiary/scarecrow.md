> **Hit Points:** 27 (6d8)
> **Speed:** 30 ft

## Stat block

A straw figure brought to malevolent life: frightening claws and a stare that roots victims in place. Burns easily.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |

- **Vulnerable:** fire
- **Immune:** poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** understands its creator's languages but can't speak

### Actions

- **Fearsome Claw.** A raking claw that leaves the target Frightened until the end of the scarecrow's next turn.
- **Terrifying Glare.** DC 11 Wis save by a creature within 30 ft or Frightened and Paralyzed until the end of the scarecrow's next turn.
