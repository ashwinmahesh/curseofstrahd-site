Strahd's own war armor, walking on its own: a lightning-charged greatsword and bolts from range.

- **Skills:** Perception +3
- **Resistant:** cold, fire
- **Immune:** lightning, poison
- **Condition immunities:** blinded, charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** understands Common but can't speak

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Greatsword attacks, or Shocking Bolt twice.
- **Greatsword.** A heavy sword that crackles with lightning.
- **Shocking Bolt.** Hurls a bolt of lightning up to 60 ft.
