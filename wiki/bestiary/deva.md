## Stat block

A radiant warrior angel: two holy mace blows, healing magic for allies, and resistance to magic.

- **Saving throws:** WIS +9, CHA +9
- **Skills:** Insight +9, Perception +9
- **Resistant:** radiant
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft; passive Perception 19
- **Languages:** all

### Traits

- **Exalted Restoration.** If it dies away from its home plane, its body vanishes and it re-forms there with all its Hit Points.
- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Holy Mace attacks.
- **Holy Mace.** A mace blow carrying a burst of radiant power.
- **Spellcasting.** It casts with Charisma (save DC 17), needing no Material components. At will: Detect Evil and Good, and Shapechange limited to Beast or Humanoid forms, without Concentration and without the spell's Temporary Hit Points. 1/day each: Commune, Raise Dead.

### Bonus actions

- **Divine Aid.** Uses the same spellcasting ability as its Spellcasting action. The two uses cover all three spells.
