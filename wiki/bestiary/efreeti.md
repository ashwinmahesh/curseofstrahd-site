## Stat block

A towering genie of fire, cruel and proud.

- **Saving throws:** WIS +6, CHA +8
- **Immune:** fire
- **Senses:** darkvision 120 ft; passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** primordial

### Traits

- **Elemental Restoration.** Slain away from the Elemental Plane of Fire, it reforms there days later.
- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Wishes.** May know Wish, granted only to others who ask; three castings, then a year's rest.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Three attacks, Heated Blade or Hurl Flame in any mix.
- **Heated Blade.** A blade glowing with heat.
- **Hurl Flame.** A ball of fire flung up to 120 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** Charisma, DC 16: Detect Magic and Elementalism at will; Wall of Fire (at 7th level), Invisibility, Plane Shift and more once a day.
