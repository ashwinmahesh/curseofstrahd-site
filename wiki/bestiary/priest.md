### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two attacks with Mace or Radiant Flame in any mix.
- **Mace.** A mace blow with holy radiance.
- **Radiant Flame.** Hurls holy fire up to 60 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** Wisdom is its spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13). At will: Light, Thaumaturgy. 1/day: Spirit Guardians.

### Bonus actions

- **Divine Aid.** Uses Wisdom as for its Spellcasting action; the three uses cover all four spells.
