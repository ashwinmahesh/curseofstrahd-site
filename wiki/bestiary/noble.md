## Stat block

Prefers talk to steel; duels with a rapier and parries, but yields or flees when hurt.

- **Skills:** Deception +5, Insight +4, Persuasion +5
- **Languages:** Common, two other languages

### Actions

- **Rapier.** Thrusts with a rapier.

### Reactions

- **Parry.** Trigger: the noble is hit by a melee attack roll while it holds a weapon. Response: the noble gains a +2 bonus to its AC against that one attack, which can make the attack miss.
