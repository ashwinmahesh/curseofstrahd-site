## Stat block

A howling whirl of wind that batters foes with thunder.

- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, lightning, piercing, slashing
- **Immune:** poison, thunder
- **Condition immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** primordial

### Traits

- **Air Form.** Can enter and stay in a creature's space, and slip through gaps an inch wide.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Thunderous Slams.
- **Thunderous Slam.** A blast of compressed air.
- **Whirlwind.** Recharge 4-6. A Medium or smaller creature in its space: DC 13 Str save or 4d10+2 Thunder, flung 20 ft and Prone (half damage only on a success).
