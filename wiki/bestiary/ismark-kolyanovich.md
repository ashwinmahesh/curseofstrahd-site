### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Three Greatsword attacks, or two Heavy Crossbow attacks and one Greatsword.
- **Greatsword.** The burgomaster's son cleaves with a greatsword.
- **Heavy Crossbow.** Fires a heavy crossbow bolt.

### Reactions

- **Parry.** Trigger: Ismark is hit by a melee attack roll while he holds a weapon. Response: he gains a +2 bonus to his AC against that one attack, which can make the attack miss.
