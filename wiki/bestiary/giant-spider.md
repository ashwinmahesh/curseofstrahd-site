## Stat block

A horse-sized hunting spider: climbs walls, binds prey in webs and bites with venom.

| 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 2 (-4) | 11 (+0) | 4 (-3) |

- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +7
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 14

### Traits

- **Spider Climb.** Climbs difficult surfaces, even upside down on ceilings, with no ability check.
- **Web Walker.** Webs don't slow it, and it feels anything touching its webs.

### Actions

- **Bite.** A venomous bite.
- **Web.** The webbing is an object on the battlefield: AC 10, 5 Hit Points, Vulnerability to Fire, Immunity to Bludgeoning, Poison and Psychic damage. Breaking it frees the creature.
