> **Type:** Medium undead
> **Alignment:** neutral evil
> **Challenge:** 5 (1800 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 13
> **Hit Points:** 67 (9d8+27)
> **Speed:** 5 ft, fly 60 ft

## Stat block

A shadow of hatred that passes through walls, drains life with a touch and raises its victims as specters.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | 67 (9d8+27) | 5 ft, fly 60 ft | 5 | 1800 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) |

- **Resistant:** acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, piercing, slashing
- **Immune:** necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common plus two other languages

### Traits

- **Incorporeal Movement.** Moves through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain; 5 (1d10) Force if it ends a turn inside one.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while in sunlight.

### Actions

- **Life Drain.** Necrotic touch that also lowers the target's Hit Point maximum by the damage taken.
- **Create Specter.** Text only for the engine.
