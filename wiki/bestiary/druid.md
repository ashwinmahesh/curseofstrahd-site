## Stat block

A wild-eyed druid of Yester Hill: hurls flame from range, then a Shillelagh staff, with roots and thunder to spare.

- **Skills:** Medicine +5, Nature +3, Perception +5
- **Languages:** Common, Druidic, Sylvan

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two attacks, Vine Staff or Verdant Wisp in any mix.
- **Vine Staff.** A staff wound with thorny vines.
- **Verdant Wisp.** A mote of green light flung up to 90 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** It casts with Wisdom (save DC 13). At will: Druidcraft, Speak with Animals. 2/day each: Entangle, Thunderwave. 1/day each: Animal Messenger, Longstrider, Moonbeam.
