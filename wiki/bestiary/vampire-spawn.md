## Stat block

Grabs a victim with its claws, bites to heal itself, and disengages freely; shuns sunlight.

- **Saving throws:** DEX +6, WIS +3

- **Resistant:** necrotic
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Common, one other language

### Traits

- **Spider Climb.** The vampire can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down along ceilings, without making an ability check.
- **Vampire Weakness.** The vampire has these weaknesses. Forbiddance: it can't enter a residence unless an occupant invites it in. Running Water: it takes 20 Acid damage if it ends its turn in running water. Stake to the Heart: it is destroyed if a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into its heart while it has the Incapacitated condition. Sunlight: it takes 20 Radiant damage if it starts its turn in sunlight, and while in sunlight it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Claw attacks and uses Bite.
- **Claw.** Hit: 8 (2d4 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 13), held by one of the vampire's two claws.
- **Bite.** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 14, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or has the Grappled, Incapacitated or Restrained condition. Failure: 5 (1d4 + 3) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by the Necrotic damage it took, and the vampire regains that many Hit Points.

### Bonus actions

- **Deathless Agility.** The vampire takes the Dash action or the Disengage action.
