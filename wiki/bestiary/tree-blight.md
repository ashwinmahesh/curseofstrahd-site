## Stat block

A dead-looking tree that walks: long branches batter, roots grab and crush, and its maw bites anyone it holds.

- **Condition immunities:** deafened
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common and Druidic (understands but can't speak)

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Branch attacks and Grasping Root.
- **Branch.** A sweeping branch with 15 ft reach.
- **Grasping Root.** DC 17 Str save by a Large or smaller creature within 15 ft: pulled 10 ft closer and held by a root (escape DC 16), crushed 2d6+6 at the start of each of its turns.

### Bonus actions

- **Gnash.** A creature it holds: DC 17 Dex save or 3d8+6 Piercing, half on a success.
