## Stat block

An armoured warrior: two greatsword strokes or crossbow bolts with a radiant edge, and a parry for melee hits.

- **Saving throws:** CON +4, WIS +2
- **Condition immunities:** frightened
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two attacks with Greatsword or Heavy Crossbow.
- **Greatsword.** A greatsword stroke with a flash of radiant zeal.
- **Heavy Crossbow.** A heavy crossbow bolt with a flash of radiant zeal.

### Reactions

- **Parry.** Reaction: +2 AC against one melee attack that would hit it, while holding a weapon.
