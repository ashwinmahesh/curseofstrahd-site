## Stat block

A jackal-headed yugoloth scholar that hoards secrets and sells them dear.

- **Saving throws:** DEX +5, CON +6, INT +9, WIS +7
- **Skills:** Arcana +9, Deception +7, Insight +7, Perception +7
- **Resistant:** cold, fire, lightning
- **Immune:** acid, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, poisoned
- **Senses:** truesight 120 ft; passive Perception 17
- **Languages:** all

### Traits

- **Fiendish Restoration.** Slain away from Gehenna, it melts into ichor and re-forms there at once.
- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Soul Tome.** Carries a magic tome (AC 17, 35 HP, healing fully each turn) that powers its Banishing Claw.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Three Fiendish Bursts; one can be a Banishing Claw.
- **Banishing Claw.** A claw that can drag a creature into the pages of its Soul Tome (DC 17 Cha save each turn to escape; three failures bind it).
- **Fiendish Burst.** A blast of fiendish power, close up or up to 120 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** Intelligence, DC 17: Alter Self, Detect Magic and other small magics at will; Dimension Door, Mind Blank and two divinations once a day.

### Bonus actions

- **Teleport.** Teleports up to 30 ft to a space it can see.

### Reactions

- **Counterspell.** Casts Counterspell when a spell is cast in range.
