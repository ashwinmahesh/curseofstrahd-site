## Stat block

A patchwork of beast and human from the abbey's breeding: bites, claws and a stolen dagger, and a voice that mimics.

- **Languages:** Common

### Traits

- **Extraordinary Feature.** Each mongrelfolk has one of these features, rolled on a d20 or picked: 1-3 Amphibious (it breathes air and water); 4-9 Darkvision 60 ft.; 10 Flight (wings and a 40 ft. fly speed); 11-15 Keen Hearing and Smell (Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks using either sense); 16-17 Spider Climb (climbs sheer surfaces and ceilings without checks); 18-19 Standing Leap (jumps 20 feet long and 10 feet high, with or without a run); 20 Two-Headed (Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on saves against being Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Stunned or knocked Unconscious).
- **Mimicry.** The mongrelfolk can imitate any sound it has heard, voices included. A listener who succeeds on a DC 12 Wisdom (Insight) check knows it is an imitation.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** A Bite, and a Claw or Dagger attack.
- **Bite.** Bites with mismatched teeth.
- **Claw.** Scratches with an animal's claw.
- **Dagger.** Melee or ranged attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 1) Piercing damage.
