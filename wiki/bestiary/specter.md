> **Type:** Medium undead

> **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 12
> **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
> **Speed:** 30 ft, fly 50 ft

## Stat block

Drifts through walls to strike from unexpected angles, draining Hit Point maximums; shrugs off weapons.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Resistant:** acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, piercing, slashing, thunder
- **Immune:** necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common (understands but can't speak), one other language (understands but can't speak)

### Traits

- **Incorporeal Movement.** The specter can move through other creatures and through objects, treating them as Difficult Terrain. If it ends its turn inside an object, it takes 5 (1d10) Force damage.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** While the specter is in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.

### Actions

- **Life Drain.** Hit: 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by the amount of damage it took.
