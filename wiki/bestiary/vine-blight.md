> **Type:** Medium plant
> **Alignment:** neutral evil
> **Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 12
> **Hit Points:** 19 (3d8+6)
> **Speed:** 20 ft

## Stat block

A slow heap of creepers that crushes a victim from 10 ft away and makes the ground itself grab at feet.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 5 (-3) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

- **Skills:** Stealth +1
- **Condition immunities:** deafened
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common

### Actions

- **Constricting Vine.** Lashes out 10 ft and grips a Large or smaller target (escape DC 12), crushing it 1d8 at the start of each of its turns.
- **Entangling Plants.** Recharge 5-6. Casts Entangle (Con, DC 12).
