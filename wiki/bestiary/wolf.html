    Traits

    • Pack Tactics. The wolf has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature when at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of that creature and that ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.

    Actions

    • Bite. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Piercing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
