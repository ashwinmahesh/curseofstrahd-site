## Stat block

The archmage who built the tower on the tarn, before the count was born. He became a lich the cheap way: not with souls but with his own name, cut over his door so that everyone who comes in says it. Courteous, curious and very old, he collects the names of his visitors. His touch freezes, his bursts tear, and his undercroft whispers your name back to you.

### Traits

- **Legendary Resistance (5/Day).** When Khazan fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead, five times a day.
- **Turn Resistance.** Khazan has Advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns Undead.
- **Multi-Concentration.** Khazan can maintain Concentration on two spells at once.
- **Remembered.** Khazan's phylactery is his own name, cut into the lintel of his tower and spoken by everyone who opens the door. While it stands, a body destroyed comes apart into dust that goes back into the name, and he forms again below the tower. Unmake the name, and the next body is the last.

### Actions

- **Paralyzing Touch.** Melee Spell Attack Roll: +15, reach 5 ft. Hit: 15 (3d6 + 5) Cold damage, and the target has the Paralyzed condition until the start of Khazan's next turn.
- **Eldritch Burst.** Ranged Spell Attack Roll: +15, range 120 ft. Hit: 31 (4d12 + 5) Force damage.
- **Spellcasting.** Khazan is a 20th-level spellcaster using Intelligence (spell save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). At will: Detect Magic, Dispel Magic, Fireball (5th level), Lightning Bolt (5th level), Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Shield, Counterspell, Misty Step and Sleet Storm. 4th level (3 slots): Blight, Ice Storm, Phantasmal Killer. 5th (3 slots): Cloudkill, Hold Monster, Wall of Force. 6th (3 slots): Chain Lightning, Circle of Death, Disintegrate. 7th (2 slots): Finger of Death, Plane Shift. 8th (2 slots): Maze, Mind Blank. 9th (2 slots): Power Word Kill, Time Stop, Meteor Swarm, Prismatic Wall.

### Bonus actions

- **Misty Step.** Khazan teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.

### Reactions

- **Protective Magic.** Khazan casts Counterspell or Shield in response to that spell's trigger.

### Lair actions

- **Every Name He Knows.** Every book in the undercroft whispers the name of up to two creatures Khazan can see. Each makes a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. Failure: 7 (2d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the start of Khazan's next turn.
- **The Lake Comes Through.** The cold of the lake overhead comes down through the stone onto up to two creatures Khazan can see. Each makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) Cold damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
