## Stat block

A seasoned soldier: shoots while foes close, then cleaves twice a turn and parries melee hits.

- **Skills:** Athletics +5, Perception +2
- **Languages:** Common, one other language

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Greatsword attacks or two Heavy Crossbow attacks.
- **Greatsword.** Cleaves with a greatsword.
- **Heavy Crossbow.** Fires a heavy crossbow bolt.

### Reactions

- **Parry.** Trigger: the warrior is hit by a melee attack roll while it holds a weapon. Response: the warrior gains a +2 bonus to its AC against that one attack, which can make the attack miss.
