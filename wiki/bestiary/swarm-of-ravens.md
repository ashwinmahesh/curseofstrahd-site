- **Skills:** Perception +5
- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned

### Traits

- **Swarm.** The swarm can move into and stay in another creature's space, and other creatures can enter its space. It can pass through any opening wide enough for one Tiny raven. The swarm can't regain Hit Points and can't gain Temporary Hit Points.

### Actions

- **Beaks.** Hit: 5 (1d6+2) Piercing damage, or only 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied.
- **Cacophony.** Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 10, one creature in the swarm's space. Failure: the target has the Deafened condition until the start of the swarm's next turn, and while Deafened this way it also has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.
