## Stat block

A lean, smiling mountain guide in furs who goes bareheaded in the snow. Something in the Amber Temple pays him a year of life for every family he brings to its door, and he has brought a great many.

### Traits

- **Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** When the Guide fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead, twice a day.
- **The Mountain's Own.** Long ago the Guide took a gift from something sealed in the Amber Temple: he is never cold, never lost on the mountain, and every family he brings to the temple's door buys him another year. He was born before the count came over the mountains.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** The Guide makes one Ice Axe attack and one Amber Bolt attack.
- **Ice Axe.** Melee Attack Roll: +8, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) Cold damage.
- **Amber Bolt.** Ranged Attack Roll: +8, range 120 ft. Hit: 18 (4d8) Necrotic damage.
- **Whiteout.** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 16, up to three creatures the Guide can see within 60 feet. Failure: 18 (4d8) Cold damage, and the target has the Blinded condition until the end of the Guide's next turn. Success: Half damage only.
- **Spellcasting.** The Guide casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16): once a day each, Hold Person and Cone of Cold.

### Reactions

- **Protective Magic.** The Guide casts Counterspell or Shield in response to that spell's trigger (twice a day).
