### Actions

- **Mace.** Strikes with a mace for bludgeoning plus radiant damage.
- **Radiant Flame.** Hurls a bolt of holy fire up to 60 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** The priest casts one of these spells, using Wisdom as its spellcasting ability. At will: Light, Thaumaturgy.

### Bonus actions

- **Divine Aid.** Once per day, the priest casts one of Bless, Healing Word or Sanctuary, using Wisdom as its spellcasting ability as for its Spellcasting action. The one use covers all three spells.
