> **Hit Points:** 27 (5d8+5)
> **Speed:** 40 ft

## Stat block

Hides in darkness, slips through cracks and drains Strength; weak to radiant damage and sunlight.

- **Skills:** Stealth +6
- **Vulnerable:** radiant
- **Resistant:** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder
- **Immune:** necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10

### Traits

- **Amorphous.** The shadow can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch, and doing so costs it no extra movement.
- **Sunlight Weakness.** While the shadow is in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on D20 Tests.

### Actions

- **Draining Swipe.** Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) Necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score is reduced by 1d4. If this brings the score to 0, the target dies. A Humanoid killed by this attack rises as a new Shadow 1d4 hours later.

### Bonus actions

- **Shadow Stealth.** If the shadow is in Dim Light or Darkness, it can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action.
