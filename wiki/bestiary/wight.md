> **Speed:** 30 ft

## Stat block

An undead warrior with sword and bow that drains the life of anyone it holds close; hates sunlight.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 82 (11d8+33) | 30 ft | 3 | 700 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) |

- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Resistant:** necrotic
- **Immune:** poison
- **Condition immunities:** exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** Common plus one other language

### Traits

- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while in sunlight.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two attacks with Necrotic Sword or Necrotic Bow in any mix; one can be Life Drain instead.
- **Necrotic Sword.** A sword cut chilled with necrotic energy.
- **Necrotic Bow.** An arrow chilled with necrotic energy.
- **Life Drain.** A Humanoid killed this way rises 24 hours later as a Zombie under the wight's control.
