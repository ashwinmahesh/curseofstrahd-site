### Traits

- **False Appearance.** As long as the figure in the frame doesn't move, nothing marks it out from a normal painting.

### Actions

- **Innate Spellcasting.** The portrait casts spells using Intelligence (spell save DC 12), needing no material components. 3/day each: Counterspell, Crown of Madness, Hypnotic Pattern, Telekinesis.
