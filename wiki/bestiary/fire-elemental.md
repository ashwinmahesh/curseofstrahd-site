## Stat block

A living blaze that sets what it touches on fire.

- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Immune:** fire, poison
- **Condition immunities:** exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained, unconscious
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** primordial

### Traits

- **Fire Aura.** At the end of its turns, everything within 10 ft takes 1d10 Fire and starts burning.
- **Fire Form.** Can enter creatures' spaces; the first time each turn it does, that creature takes 1d10 Fire and starts burning.
- **Illumination.** Sheds Bright Light 30 ft and Dim Light 30 ft more.
- **Water Susceptibility.** Takes 1d6 Cold for every 5 ft it moves through water or every gallon splashed on it.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Burn attacks.
- **Burn.** A searing touch that sets the target burning.
