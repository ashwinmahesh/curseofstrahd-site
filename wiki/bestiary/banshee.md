## Stat block

A wailing spirit: a withering touch, a face that terrifies, and a cry that can kill outright.

- **Saving throws:** WIS +2
- **Resistant:** acid, bludgeoning, fire, lightning, piercing, slashing, thunder
- **Immune:** cold, necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish

### Traits

- **Detect Life.** Senses the direction of living creatures (not Constructs or Undead) within a mile.
- **Incorporeal Movement.** Moves through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain; 5 (1d10) Force if it ends a turn inside one.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Corrupting Touches and Horrify.
- **Corrupting Touch.** A withering touch.
- **Horrify.** DC 13 Wis save by a creature within 60 ft that sees it, or Frightened until the banshee's next turn; success gives a day's immunity.
- **Deathly Wail.** Once a day: everyone within 30 ft but Constructs and Undead makes a DC 13 Con save; a failure drops one at 25 HP or fewer to 0, else 3d6 Psychic.
