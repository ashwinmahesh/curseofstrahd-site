- **Languages:** Common and three other languages

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes three Arcane Burst attacks.
- **Arcane Burst (Melee).** A crackle of force at arm's length.
- **Arcane Burst (Ranged).** A bolt of force up to 120 ft.
- **Spellcasting.** Intelligence is its spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14). At will: Detect Magic, Light, Mage Armor, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation. 2/day each: Fireball, Invisibility. 1/day each: Cone of Cold, Fly.

### Bonus actions

- **Misty Step.** Three times a day: teleports up to 30 ft with Misty Step.

### Reactions

- **Protective Magic.** Text only for the engine.
