### Traits

- **Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls against a target that has a non-Incapacitated ally of this creature within 5 ft.
- **Running Leap.** Long jumps up to 25 ft with a 10-ft run-up.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Rend attacks; one can be a Roar.
- **Rend.** Claws and fangs.
- **Roar.** DC 11 Wis save by a creature within 15 ft or Frightened until the lion's next turn.
