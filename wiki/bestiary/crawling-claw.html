    Crawling Claw

    Tiny undead, neutral evil

    A severed hand that scuttles up walls and goes for the throat; harmless alone, nasty in numbers.

    ACHPSpeedCRXP
    122 (1d4)20 ft, climb 20 ft010
    STRDEXCONINTWISCHA
    13 (+1)14 (+2)11 (+0)5 (-3)10 (+0)4 (-3)
    • Immune: poison
    • Condition immunities: charmed, exhaustion, poisoned
    • Senses: blindsight 30 ft; passive Perception 10
    • Languages: understands Common but can't speak

    Traits

    • Turn Immunity. The claw is immune to effects that turn Undead.

    Actions

    • Claw. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning or Slashing damage, as the claw likes. The engine always deals Bludgeoning.

    Where you meet it

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count: The rug lifts off the floor like a sheet in the wind and comes for you, and from the ledger shelves something…
    • Castle Ravenloft, the Dungeon: A grey figure at the rack looks up, pleased. Hands scuttle out of every corner, and the drain begins to rise.
