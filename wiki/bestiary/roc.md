A bird the size of a ship: a tearing beak and talons that snatch prey away.

- **Skills:** Perception +8

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Beak attacks; one can be a Talons attack.
- **Beak.** A tearing beak strike with 10 ft reach.
- **Talons.** Seizes a Huge or smaller target in both talons (escape DC 19), Restrained while held.

### Bonus actions

- **Swoop.** Recharge 5-6. With a creature in its talons, climbs half its Fly Speed without Opportunity Attacks and drops it.
