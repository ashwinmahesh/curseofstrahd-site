    The bones of the count's vanguard marshal and his warhorse, grown together over four hundred years of holding the valley's gate: a long blade cold with the grave, a parry, and a charge between your turns.

    • Legendary Resistance (2/Day). When the Bone Marshal fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead, twice a day.
