## Stat block

A hired bruiser who gangs up with allies for Advantage and beats one target down with a mace.

- **Languages:** Common

### Traits

- **Pack Tactics.** The tough has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature when at least one of the tough's allies is within 5 feet of that creature and that ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.

### Actions

- **Mace.** Clubs a target with a mace.
- **Heavy Crossbow.** Fires a heavy crossbow bolt.
