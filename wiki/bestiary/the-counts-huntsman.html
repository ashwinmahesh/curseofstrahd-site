    The Count's Huntsman

    Type
    Medium undead
    Alignment
    lawful evil
    Challenge
    9 (5000 XP)
    Armor Class
    17

    165 (22d8+66)
    Speed

    Stat block

    The master of the count's hounds, four hundred years dead: a long grey man in a hunting coat gone black with weather, with a horn, a boar spear and a quiver of black arrows. He runs down whoever the count gives him, and every quarry he runs down becomes one of his hounds.

    Traits

    • Legendary Resistance (2/Day). When the Huntsman fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead, twice a day.
    • The Rules of the Hunt. The Huntsman sounds his horn on three nights before he runs a quarry down, and the hunt ends under the crossroads gallows at moonrise. A quarry who stands there and beats him in a fair fight ends the hunt for good: the count's own rule, made because he thought it could never be kept.

    Actions

    • Multiattack. The Huntsman makes two attacks, using Hunting Spear or Black Arrow in any combination.
    • Hunting Spear. Hit: 10 (1d10 + 5) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.
    • Black Arrow. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.
    • Horn of the Hunt. The Huntsman sounds his horn. Each creature within 60 feet of him that isn't an Undead or a Beast makes a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. Failure: the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the Huntsman's next turn.

    Where you meet it

    • The River Ivlis Crossroads: The Count's Huntsman · The Huntsman sets the pack on you with one note of the horn, and the hounds come up the road in silence.
