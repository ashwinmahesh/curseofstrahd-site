### Traits

- **Deathly Choir.** Anyone within 10 feet of Rahadin who isn't shielded by Mind Blank hears, inside their head, the screams of the many he has killed. As a Bonus Action he can make each creature hearing them attempt a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw; on a failure it takes 16 (3d10) Psychic damage. The engine does this at the start of his turn, to his enemies only.
- **Fey Ancestry.** Rahadin has Advantage on saving throws against the Charmed condition, and magic can't put him to sleep.
- **Mask of the Wild.** Rahadin can attempt to hide even when he is only lightly obscured by foliage, heavy rain, falling snow, mist or similar natural cover.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Rahadin makes three Scimitar attacks, or throws two Poisonous Darts. The engine runs the three swings; at range he throws one dart.
- **Scimitar.** A quick, precise cut.
- **Poisonous Dart.** Throws a poisoned dart up to 60 ft.
- **Innate Spellcasting.** Rahadin casts spells using Wisdom (spell save DC 15), needing no components. 3/day each: Misty Step, Phantasmal Killer.
