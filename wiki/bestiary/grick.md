## Stat block

Ambushes from walls and ceilings, grabbing a victim with its tentacles and pecking it with its beak.

- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 12

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes one Beak attack and one Tentacles attack.
- **Beak.** Stabs a target with its hooked beak.
- **Tentacles.** Hit: 7 (1d10 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is Medium or smaller, all four tentacles seize it and it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 12).
