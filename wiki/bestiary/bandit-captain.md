## Stat block

Leads from the front, mixing pistol shots and scimitar cuts, parrying blows; bandits flee if it falls.

- **Saving throws:** STR +4, DEX +5, WIS +2
- **Skills:** Athletics +4, Deception +4
- **Languages:** Common, Thieves' Cant

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two attacks with Scimitar and Pistol in any combination.
- **Scimitar.** Slashes with a scimitar.
- **Pistol.** Fires a pistol.

### Reactions

- **Parry.** Trigger: the bandit is hit by a melee attack roll while it holds a weapon. Response: the bandit gains a +2 bonus to its AC against that one attack, which can make the attack miss.
