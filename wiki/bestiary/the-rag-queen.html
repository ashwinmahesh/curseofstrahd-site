    The Rag Queen

    Type
    Huge construct
    Alignment
    chaotic evil

    Speed

    Stat block

    Baba Lysaga's masterpiece: a scarecrow twice the height of a man, stitched from the wedding dresses of every drowned bride of Berez, with a mouth full of their rings. Her claws frighten, her glare freezes, the field stitches her back together, and only fire stops it.

    • Senses: darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 12

    Traits

    • Legendary Resistance (2/Day). When the Rag Queen fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead, twice a day.
    • Restitching. The Rag Queen regains 15 Hit Points at the start of each of her turns: the field's straw and the brides' thread stitch her back together. If she took Fire damage since the start of her last turn, this trait doesn't work on that turn.

    Actions

    • Multiattack. The Rag Queen makes two Fearsome Claw attacks.
    • Fearsome Claw. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6) Slashing damage, and the target has the Frightened condition until the end of the Rag Queen's next turn.
    • Terrifying Glare. One creature the Rag Queen can see within 30 feet makes a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. Failure: the target has the Frightened and Paralyzed conditions until the end of the Rag Queen's next turn.
