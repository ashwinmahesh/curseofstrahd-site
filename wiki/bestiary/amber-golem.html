    Traits

    • Immutable Form. Effects that would change its shape don't work on it.
    • Magic Resistance. Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

    Actions

    • Multiattack. Two attacks, Slam or Force Bolt in any mix.
    • Slam. A crushing amber fist humming with force.
    • Force Bolt. A bolt of force up to 120 ft.

    Bonus actions

    • Slow. Recharge 5-6. Casts Slow (Con, DC 17) as a Bonus Action.
