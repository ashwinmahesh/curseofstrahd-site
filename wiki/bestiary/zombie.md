> **Type:** Medium undead
> **Alignment:** neutral evil
> **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 8
> **Hit Points:** 15 (2d8+6)
> **Speed:** 20 ft

## Stat block

Shambles toward the closest living thing and slams it; hard to put down without radiant or crits.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 15 (2d8+6) | 20 ft | 1/4 | 50 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 6 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 6 (-2) | 5 (-3) |

- **Saving throws:** WIS +0
- **Immune:** poison
- **Condition immunities:** exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** Common (understands but can't speak), one other language (understands but can't speak)

### Traits

- **Undead Fortitude.** When damage drops the zombie to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 plus the damage taken. On a success it drops to 1 Hit Point instead. It can't make this save if the damage was Radiant or came from a Critical Hit.

### Actions

- **Slam.** Clubs a target with a rotting fist.
