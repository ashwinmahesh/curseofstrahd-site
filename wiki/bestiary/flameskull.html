    Traits

    • Illumination. Sheds Bright Light 15 ft and Dim Light 15 ft more.
    • Magic Resistance. Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
    • Undead Restoration. Destroyed, it re-forms in an hour unless Holy Water or Dispel Evil and Good is used on its remains.

    • Multiattack. Two Fire Rays.
    • Fire Ray. A jet of green fire, close up or up to 60 ft.
    • Spellcasting. Intelligence, DC 13: Mage Hand at will; Magic Missile (2nd level) twice a day; Fireball once.
