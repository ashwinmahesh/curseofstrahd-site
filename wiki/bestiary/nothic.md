### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Two Claw attacks.
- **Claw.** Long hooked claws.
- **Rotting Gaze.** DC 13 Con save by a creature within 120 ft it can see: 5d6 Necrotic, half on a success.

### Bonus actions

- **Weird Insight.** Recharge 6. A creature within 120 ft makes a DC 14 Wis save or the nothic learns one of its secrets.
