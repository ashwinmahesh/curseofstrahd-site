## Stat block

A fine carpet that lies still until someone steps on it, then rolls them up and squeezes.

- **Immune:** poison, psychic
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, deafened, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 6

### Traits

- **Damage Transfer.** While the rug is grappling a creature, it takes only half the damage dealt to it (rounded down), and the creature it holds takes the other half.

### Actions

- **Smother.** Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. A Medium or smaller target is Grappled (escape DC 13); until it gets free it is also Blinded and Restrained, can't breathe, and takes 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The rug can hold only one creature at a time.
