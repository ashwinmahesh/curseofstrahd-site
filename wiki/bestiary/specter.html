    Traits

    • Incorporeal Movement. The specter can move through other creatures and through objects, treating them as Difficult Terrain. If it ends its turn inside an object, it takes 5 (1d10) Force damage.
    • Sunlight Sensitivity. While the specter is in sunlight, it has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls.

    Actions

    • Life Drain. Hit: 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its Hit Point maximum is reduced by the amount of damage it took.
