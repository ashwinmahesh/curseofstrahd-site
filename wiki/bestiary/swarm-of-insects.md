## Stat block

A crawling, biting cloud of insects that pours over a victim; resists weapon damage.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 19 (3d8+6) | 20 ft, fly 20 ft | 1/2 | 100 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (-4) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 1 (-5) | 7 (-2) | 1 (-5) |

- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned
- **Senses:** blindsight 10 ft; passive Perception 8

### Traits

- **Swarm.** Shares spaces with other creatures and fits through insect-sized gaps; can't heal or gain Temporary Hit Points.

### Actions

- **Bites.** Many small bites; the damage drops once the swarm is Bloodied.
