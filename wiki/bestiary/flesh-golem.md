## Stat block

A stitched-together brute: two shocking slams, healed by lightning, hampered by fire, and liable to rage when hurt.

- **Immune:** lightning, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** understands Common and one other language but can't speak

### Traits

- **Aversion to Fire.** After taking Fire damage it has Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the end of its next turn.
- **Berserk.** Starting a turn Bloodied, it rolls a d6; on a 6 it attacks the nearest creature (or an object) every turn until it is no longer Bloodied.
- **Immutable Form.** Effects that would change its shape don't work on it.
- **Lightning Absorption.** Lightning damage heals it instead: it regains Hit Points equal to the Lightning damage dealt.
- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Slam attacks.
- **Slam.** A heavy blow that also shocks.
