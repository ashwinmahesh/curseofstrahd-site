    The Tinker's Wagon

    Huge monstrosity, neutral

    A tinker's green painted wagon on the Old Svalich Road that is a mimic the size of a wagon, and has been for years. It keeps the tinker alive at the front to sell its bait, and comes for whoever buys. Its lashes stick, its tailboard bites, and it spits its trinkets at you.

    Traits

    • Legendary Resistance (1/Day). When the Wagon fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead, once a day.
    • False Appearance. While the Wagon is motionless, it is indistinguishable from an ordinary tinker's wagon, painted green, with pots on the sides and a man at the front selling things.

    Actions

    • Multiattack. The Wagon makes two Pseudopod attacks and one Bite attack.
    • Pseudopod. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) Acid damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15). The Wagon can hold two creatures this way.
    • Bite. Hit: 14 (2d8 + 5) Piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) Acid damage.
    • Spill the Trinkets. Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 14, each creature in a 20-foot Cone. Failure: 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage, and the target has the Restrained condition until the end of the Wagon's next turn. Success: Half damage only.

    Where you meet it

    • The River Ivlis Crossroads: The green wagon stands up off its wheels, and two of the trinkets on its hooks open their own small mouths.
