## Stat block

An empty, flying suit of plate that fights with a longsword and shrugs off most magic.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

- **Skills:** Perception +4
- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Immune:** force, necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, stunned
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** understands its creator's languages but can't speak

### Traits

- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
- **Spell Immunity.** The horror is immune to three spells picked by whoever made it. Fireball, Heat Metal and Lightning Bolt are the usual choices.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Longsword attacks.
- **Longsword.** Cuts with a longsword.
