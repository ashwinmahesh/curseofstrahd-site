> **Type:** Large beast
> **Alignment:** unaligned
> **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 12
> **Hit Points:** 11 (2d10)
> **Speed:** 5 ft, fly 30 ft

## Stat block

A shrieking cloud that envelops a creature and bites; resists weapon damage, weakens once Bloodied.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 11 (2d10) | 5 ft, fly 30 ft | 1/4 | 50 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 (-3) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 2 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 4 (-3) |

- **Resistant:** bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 11

### Traits

- **Swarm.** The swarm can move into and stay in another creature's space, and other creatures can enter its space. It can pass through any opening wide enough for one Tiny bat. The swarm can't regain Hit Points and can't gain Temporary Hit Points.

### Actions

- **Bites.** Hit: 5 (2d4) Piercing damage, or only 2 (1d4) Piercing damage if the swarm is Bloodied.
