> **Type:** Large beast
> **Alignment:** unaligned
> **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 14
> **Hit Points:** 22 (3d10+6)
> **Speed:** 50 ft

## Stat block

A huge, fast wolf that hunts with its pack, dragging prey Prone so its packmates strike easily.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | 22 (3d10+6) | 50 ft | 1 | 200 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

- **Skills:** Perception +5, Stealth +4
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 15

### Traits

- **Pack Tactics.** The wolf has Advantage on an attack roll against a creature when at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of that creature and that ally doesn't have the Incapacitated condition.

### Actions

- **Bite.** Hit: 8 (1d10 + 3) Piercing damage. If the target is Large or smaller, it has the Prone condition.
