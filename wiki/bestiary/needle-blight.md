> **Type:** Medium plant
> **Alignment:** neutral evil
> **Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 12
> **Hit Points:** 16 (3d8+3)
> **Speed:** 30 ft

## Stat block

A hunched figure of conifer needles that sprays needles from the trees and rakes anyone who closes in.

- **Condition immunities:** deafened
- **Senses:** blindsight 60 ft; passive Perception 9
- **Languages:** Common (understands but can't speak)

### Actions

- **Claw.** Needle-tipped claws.
- **Needles.** A spray of needles up to 60 ft.
