## Stat block

The ghost of a fallen knight, still fighting: two spectral sword strokes, and it walks through walls.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |

- **Resistant:** acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, piercing, slashing, thunder
- **Immune:** necrotic, poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** the languages it knew in life

### Traits

- **Ethereal Sight.** Sees 60 ft into the Ethereal Plane from the Material Plane.
- **Incorporeal Movement.** Moves through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain; 5 (1d10) Force if it ends a turn inside one.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Spectral Longsword attacks.
- **Spectral Longsword.** A ghostly blade that cuts with force.
