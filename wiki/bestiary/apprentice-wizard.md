### Actions

- **Dagger.** Melee or ranged attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 2 (1d4) Piercing damage.
- **Fire Bolt.** Ranged spell attack: +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d10) Fire damage. A flammable object that nobody is wearing or carrying catches fire.
- **Spellcasting.** The apprentice casts spells as a 1st-level wizard, using Intelligence (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). Cantrips (at will): Fire Bolt, Mending, Prestidigitation. 1st level (2 slots): Burning Hands, Disguise Self, Shield.
