> **Speed:** 30 ft

## Stat block

Wades into melee so its stench sickens foes, paralyzing one with claws then biting the helpless.

- **Saving throws:** WIS +2
- **Resistant:** necrotic
- **Immune:** poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common

### Traits

- **Stench.** Any creature that starts its turn in the 5-foot Emanation around the ghast makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failure it has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. On a success it is immune to this ghast's Stench for 24 hours.

### Actions

- **Bite.** Bites for piercing plus necrotic damage.
- **Claw.** Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature that isn't Undead, it makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failure it has the Paralyzed condition until the end of its next turn.
