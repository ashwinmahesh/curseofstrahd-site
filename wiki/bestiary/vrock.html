    Traits

    • Demonic Restoration. If it dies outside the Abyss, its body dissolves into ichor and it revives with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Abyss (not built: here it stays dead).
    • Magic Resistance. Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

    Actions

    • Multiattack. Two Shred attacks.
    • Shred. Beak and talons together: 2d6+3 Piercing plus 3d6 Poison.
    • Spores. Recharge 6. A cloud of spores: each creature within 20 ft, DC 15 Con save or Poisoned, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns (Holy Water on it ends the effect early; the 1d10 Poison at the start of its turns isn't tracked).
    • Stunning Screech. 1/Day. Each creature within 20 ft: DC 15 Con save or 3d6 Thunder and Stunned until the end of the vrock's next turn.
