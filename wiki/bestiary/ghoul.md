> [!infobox]
> ![ghoul](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/portraits/ghoul.webp)
> **Type:** Medium undead
> **Alignment:** chaotic evil
> **Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)
> **Armor Class:** 12
> **Hit Points:** 22 (5d8)
> **Speed:** 30 ft

## Stat block

Hunts in groups: claws to paralyze a victim, then the pack bites the helpless target twice a turn.

| AC | HP | Speed | CR | XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 22 (5d8) | 30 ft | 1 | 200 |

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

- **Immune:** poison
- **Condition immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft; passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Makes two Bite attacks.
- **Bite.** Bites for piercing plus necrotic damage.
- **Claw.** Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage. If the target is a creature that is neither Undead nor an elf, it makes a DC 10 Constitution saving throw. On a failure it has the Paralyzed condition until the end of its next turn.
