    About

    An archer of the castle's watch, three centuries dead, walking the inside of the gatehouse wall with an empty quiver and his fingers still crooked for a bowstring. The castle's archers shot the count on the night of the wedding; he has kept them on their rounds ever since.

    Where to find them

    • Castle Ravenloft, the Gates, The front courtyard, walking a round
