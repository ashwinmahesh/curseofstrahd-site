    Sorin

    ✦The First Krezkov

    Dmitri and Anna Krezkov's first son, carried up to the abbey with the fever at nine and 'improved' there for twenty years: goat-legged, too big, with a boy's eyes and a Belview's gift for other people's voices.

    Region: Krezk
    Starting attitude: indifferent
    Pronouns: he/him
    In a fight: Mongrelfolk

    Where to find them

    • The Krezkovs' House, The hall (when sorin brought and not sorin fate)
    • The Krezkovs' House, The hall (when sorin fate is “home”)
    • The Pool of the White Sun, Krezk's graves (when night and sorin vigil and not sorin seen)
    • The Pool of the White Sun, Krezk's graves (when sorin known and not sorin brought and (sorin pursuers beaten or abbot fate))
    • The Pool of the White Sun, Krezk's graves (when sorin fate is “pool”)
