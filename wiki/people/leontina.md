## About

The Baron's cook: brisk, proud, sixty, in a spotless apron. She has made four hundred and thirteen sun-shaped dishes for his festivals, every one of them round, and wants to make one that isn't. She trained in a great kitchen beyond the mists, which nobody in Vallaki believes.
