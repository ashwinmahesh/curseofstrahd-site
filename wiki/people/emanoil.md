## About

A young Vallaki clerk in black he can't afford, who writes long bad poems to the castle and sleeps in a coffin from Henrik's because he wants to be chosen and bitten by the lord of the valley. He is secretly afraid of the dark and sleeps with a candle burning in the coffin.
