## About

A Vallaki woman who led eight pilgrims up the Tsolenka Pass forty years ago, after a winter that killed their children, to ask the Amber Temple never to be cold again. The amber said yes. She has been frozen in the gorge wall since, with a lamp in her hands, alive.
