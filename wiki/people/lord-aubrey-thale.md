## About

A gentleman traveller who came through the mists in his own carriage a hundred and ninety years ago, dined with the count and stayed the night. He has been leaving in the morning ever since: a vampire spawn in a velvet coat gone grey with dust, who drifts about the great entry greeting guests as if the house were his.
