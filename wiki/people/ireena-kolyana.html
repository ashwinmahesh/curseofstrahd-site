    Ireena Kolyana

    ✦Daughter of the Burgomaster

    The burgomaster's adopted daughter, red-haired, proud and twice bitten by Strahd. She won't leave until her father is buried properly.

    Region: The Village of Barovia
    Starting attitude: indifferent
    Pronouns: she/her
    In a fight: Noble
    Can travel with the party as a guest.

    Where to find them

    • The Abbey of St. Markovia, The courtyard (when ireena waits in courtyard and not ireena with the party)
    • Burgomaster's Mansion, Parlor (when not burial procession or ireena escort is “later” or ireena escort is “declined”)
    • The Krezkovs' House, The hall (when ireena in krezk and not ireena with the party)
    • The Blue Water Inn, Guest rooms (when ireena sanctuary is “inn” and not ireena with the party)
    • St. Andral's Church, Nave (when ireena sanctuary is “st andrals” and not ireena with the party)
    • Village of Barovia, Churchyard (when burial procession and not burgomaster buried)
    • Village of Barovia, Churchyard (when burgomaster buried and not ireena escort)
