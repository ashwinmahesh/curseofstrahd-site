## About

A child-sized clockwork jester in faded motley, built long ago to amuse the children of the castle in place of their jester, Pidlwick. He cannot speak; he mimes and chalks words on a little slate. He wanders the high tower stair, lonely, with a secret about how the first Pidlwick died. The Tarokka can name him as the party's ally.
