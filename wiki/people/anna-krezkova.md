## About

Dmitri's wife, small and unbending, who has sat at her son Ilya's bedside for a month singing so he knows she's there. She would climb the mountain to the Abbot on her knees if Dmitri let her, and she keeps a wedding dress in her chest for a daughter she no longer has.
