## About

A travel writer from beyond the mists who has spent eleven years in the valley writing the definitive guidebook, nine hundred pages so far, rating everything in stars and a short comment. Pompous in a mild way, kind, observant and a little lost; he has never found the way home and has decided that the book is the point.
