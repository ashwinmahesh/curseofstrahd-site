## About

A vestige sealed in a sarcophagus of amber in the breached vault off the Amber Temple's vault stair: the evasive. Still able to speak and to bargain. An empty-looking block; only at the corner of the eye, a slight figure in a hooded cloak slipping toward whichever side is not watched.
