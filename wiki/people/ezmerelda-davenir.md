## About

A Vistana who left the wagons to hunt the things that hunt people, trained by Rudolph van Richten and now looking for him. Rapier, silver, a crossbow, a long dark coat, and an oak-and-steel brace where a werewolf took her leg below the knee. The Tarokka can name her as the party's ally.
