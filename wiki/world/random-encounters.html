    Random Encounters

    Travelling between places on the map can be interrupted. Each road has a chance by day and by night, and a weighted table of what comes.

    Amber Temple Road

    The ice stair between the Tsolenka Pass and the Amber Temple: whiteouts, a dead expedition, gargoyles off the cliffs, and the frozen dead who climb at night.

    Chance per journey: 25% by day, 45% by night.

    Vineyard Road

    The lake road west of Vallaki and the barrow track to Yester Hill: hedgerows, moor and thin woods.     Night visitors at camp

    When a night at camp in a risky place is interrupted, one of these comes out of the dark, picked by the region's land and your party's level (Resting and Saving). They're meant to be hard.

    Forest

    The Old Svalich Road, Into the Mists, The Village of Barovia, Old Bonegrinder, The Werewolf Den, The Wizard of Wines, Van Richten's Tower, Yester Hill, Argynvostholt

    Party levelWhat comesXP
    1–3Eyes in the dark beyond the fire, low to the ground, and more of them every time you count. 4 × Wolf200
    4–6Something big moves between the trees, then something bigger. The Count's hounds have found your fire. Dire Wolf, 5 × Wolf, Rat460
    7–11Shapes walk upright out of the pines, sniffing at the smoke, and the wolves behind them grin. 2 × Werewolf, 3 × Dire Wolf, 2 × Wolf, Giant Rat2125
