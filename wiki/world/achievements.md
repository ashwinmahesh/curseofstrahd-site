# Achievements

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/achievements.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

| Achievement | How |
|---|---|
| First Blood | Win a fight in the story. |
| Fortune Favours | Land a Critical Hit. |
| Deadly Aim | Land 25 Critical Hits in one playthrough. |
| Cursed Dice | Roll 20 natural 1s in one playthrough. |
| Hunter of the Night | Defeat 100 foes in one playthrough. |
| Wolfsbane | Defeat 25 wolves, dire wolves and werewolves in one playthrough. |
| Rest in Peace | Put 50 undead to rest in one playthrough. |
| Overwhelming Force | Deal 50 damage or more with one blow. |
| Not a Scratch | Win a fight against four foes or more without a hero taking damage. |
| Last One Standing | Win a fight with only one hero still on their feet. |
| Not Today | Roll a natural 20 on a Death Saving Throw. |
| Burgomaster's Purse | Hold 1,000 gp at once. |
| None Left Behind | Reach an ending without losing a hero for good. |
| Proving Grounds | Win a Skirmish. |
| Against All Odds | Win a Skirmish rated beyond High. |
| Dress Rehearsal | Defeat Strahd in a Skirmish. |

Reaching each ending is an achievement too.
