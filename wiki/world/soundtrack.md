# Soundtrack

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/soundtrack.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

The music follows where you are: each place has a mood, and a mood plays its pieces one after another, every one before any repeats. A place's own mood comes first, then its region's (taverns, inns and camps keep their own), then the mood of the kind of place it is. Fights switch to fight music, which swells to a harder piece when a boss is on the field or someone is Bloodied.
