## Dark Gift feats

| Feat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Aberrant Anatomy | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Hold your breath for an hour, Expertise in Perception and Blindsight 15 feet; a natural 1 can leave you Stunned. |
| Echoing Soul | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Two skills, a language and an Expertise you can move each Long Rest; a natural 1 can leave you Incapacitated and slow. |
| Gathered Whispers | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Message, a free Augury and a Reaction that adds your Proficiency Bonus to AC; a natural 1 can drown you in whispers. |
| Living Shadow | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Your shadow works a Mage Hand and lengthens your reach; a natural 1 lets it take you over for a moment. |
| Mist Walker | Ravenloft Dark Gift. The Mists carry you between the Domains of Dread and pull you 15 feet clear of harm; they won't let you sit still. |
| Second Skin | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Alter Self, free once per Long Rest and without Concentration; a catalyst can force the change on you. |
| Symbiotic Being | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Something lives in you: a skill, a language and Hit Point Dice to shore up failed saves; on a natural 1 it may take the reins. |
| Touch of Death | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Chill Touch that ignores Necrotic Resistance; death has a firmer grip on you. |
| Watchers | Ravenloft Dark Gift. Unseen watchers lend you Beast Sense, Speak with Animals and sharp eyes; a natural 1 lets them unnerve you. |
