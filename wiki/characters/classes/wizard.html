    Wizard

    A scholar of arcane magic with the widest spell list, a spellbook that grows every level and rituals for free.

    • Hit die: d6
    • Primary ability: INT
    • Saving throws: INT, WIS
    • Skills: choose 2 from Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Religion
    • Weapons: simple
    • Spellcasting: INT, full caster (spell list)

    Features by level

    • 1: Spellcasting. Cast Wizard spells with Intelligence from a spellbook that starts with six level 1 spells.
    • 1: Ritual Adept. Cast any Ritual spell in your spellbook as a Ritual without preparing it.
    • 1: Arcane Recovery. Once per Long Rest, after a Short Rest, recover slots totalling half your Wizard level (round up).
    • 2: Scholar. Expertise in one of Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature or Religion that you're proficient in.
    • 3: Wizard Subclass. Choose a Wizard subclass.
    • 4: Ability Score Improvement. Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat you qualify for.
    • 5: Memorize Spell. After a Short Rest, swap one prepared spell for another in your spellbook.
    • 6: Subclass Feature. Gain a feature from your Wizard subclass.
    • 8: Ability Score Improvement. Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat you qualify for.
    • 10: Subclass Feature. Gain a feature from your Wizard subclass.
    • 12: Ability Score Improvement. Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat you qualify for.
    • 14: Subclass Feature. Gain a feature from your Wizard subclass.
    • 16: Ability Score Improvement. Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat you qualify for.
    • 18: Spell Mastery. Cast a chosen level 1 and level 2 spell at their lowest level without a slot.
    • 19: Epic Boon. Gain an Epic Boon feat or another feat you qualify for.
    • 20: Signature Spells. Two level 3 spells always prepared, each cast once per rest without a slot.

    Bladesinger

    An elven tradition joins wizardry, swordplay, and dance.

    • 3: Bladesong. Bonus Action while wearing no armor or Shield: Bladesong for 1 minute. It ends if Incapacitated, you don armor or a Shield, or you use two hands for a weapon attack; end it freely. Uses equal Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) per Long Rest; Arcane Recovery restores one. While active, add Intelligence modifier to AC (minimum +1), gain 10 feet of Speed and Advantage on Acrobatics, use Intelligence for proficient weapon attack and damage rolls, and add Intelligence modifier to Concentration saves.
    • 3: Training in War and Song. Gain proficiency with Melee Martial weapons without Heavy or Two-Handed, and choose one listed skill.
    • 3: Weapon Focus. A proficient Melee weapon may serve as your Wizard Spellcasting Focus.
    • 6: Extra Attack. Two attacks per Attack action.
    • 6: Cantrip Substitution. One attack in your Attack action may be replaced by a Wizard cantrip with an action casting time.
    • 10: Song of Defense. Reaction when taking damage with Bladesong active: spend a spell slot to reduce the damage by five times its level.
    • 14: Song of Victory. After casting a spell with an action casting time, you may make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
