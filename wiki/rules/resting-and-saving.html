    Resting and Saving

    Resting (R)

    • A Short Rest takes an hour and can be taken anywhere outside a fight, dungeons and places where nobody can sleep included. It's refused while foes who are awake are in plain view, or after you ran from a fight on this map, and its card says why.
    • A Long Rest can't start within 16 hours of the last one finishing, so two in a row can't clear Exhaustion twice in one night. It ends Concentration, so Hex and the like don't last the night.
    • You can't Long Rest inside a dungeon or building while enemies remain in it, on any of its floors. Towns, inns, camps and the open road are fine, and so are magic items that make a safe place to sleep. Leomund's Tiny Hut and Guards and Wards lift the rule: with either, you can Long Rest in a building that still has enemies in it.
    • Castle Ravenloft is rested in floor by floor: once the floor you're on (the ground floor, the court, the tower, the larders or the catacombs) is clear of enemies, you can Long Rest there. A night in the castle breaks one time in three, and what comes out of the dark is the castle's own: vampires and their kind.
    • Each place says whether resting there is safe or risky (it's on the place's page here). A Long Rest in a risky place has a 1 in 6 chance of being interrupted, except on Story difficulty (Difficulty). Hallow, Forbiddance, Private Sanctum or Nondetection make a rest safe.
    • An inn room booked for the night makes the next Long Rest there better: temporary Hit Points equal to each hero's level, or twice that and one more level of Exhaustion gone in the best room (Merchants and Services).
    • Making camp. Wherever a place's own rest is risky, the Long Rest button says Make camp: the wild and the road, and also town streets, houses, churches, manors and dungeons. The party pitches a fire, a stewpot and bedrolls where it stands (and a tent, out of doors), or at the nearest spot with room for them, and everyone gathers round: the party, your benched companions and any guests, with Ratatouille at the pot. Safe places (the inns, the Krezkovs' house, the Mad Mage's hut, Van Richten's tower, the Wizard of Wines, Tser Pool and the Pool of the White Sun), a room booked for the night, the tutorial and the magic items' rests use the rest screen instead.
    • At camp, a speech bubble marks anyone with something to say; walk up to them to start the talk. These camp talks carry the heroes' personal stories (Companion Approval), and each plays once.
    • The camp panel offers Sleep (8 hours), the Long Rest. The night follows the usual rules (16 hours since the last, the place's risk, wards and Alarm).
    • Night visitors. If a night in a risky place is interrupted, something comes out of the dark 25 to 40 ft from the fire, from more than one side, before the night has done the party any good. What comes depends on where you are (a town's or an indoor place's own visitors, else the region's land: forest, marsh, mountain or castle) and your level, and these fights are meant to be hard (Random Encounters lists them). Companions waiting at camp keep out of it. Win, and the party sleeps on for the hours left plus an hour for each fight, then wakes with the full Long Rest. If foes are still waiting in the building, or there's nothing to fight, the night ends with only a Short Rest's benefits. A visit of Strahd's that may come to blows works the same way.
    • A full night ends with the night's narration, the region's dream and the morning. Strahd may come in the night, as after any Long Rest.
    • In the morning the panel offers Change prepared spells (you can also change them any time outside a fight, from the character sheet) and Break camp, which packs everything away. Each companion has one word in the evening and one after the night.
    • Resting in a safe place uses the rest screen. For an hour after a Long Rest, opening it again still offers the companions' words and the change of prepared spells. Strahd's night visit waits until you close the rest screen. Daern's Instant Fortress and the Rod of Security give the same full Long Rest.
    • Mage Armor can be set to cast itself as each Long Rest ends (How Magic Works).

    Saving

    • F5 quicksaves and F9 loads the quicksave. Save Game in the pause menu (Esc) writes a new slot or overwrites one of yours, with a note of up to 60 letters.
    • The game autosaves on arriving somewhere new, and saves the start of each fight's round so you can retry it.
    • Load a Save lists every save newest first, with a picture of where you were. It can sort by place or by day.
    • Chapters: the Load page's second tab starts a game at the beginning of any chapter of the story, with the heroes at that chapter's level and gear.
    • Backups: the third tab keeps copies made when the game updates.
    • Loading a game you saved more than 30 minutes ago opens with a short "Previously in Barovia" recap from the Narrator.
    • An Honour run keeps a single save that the game writes for you (Difficulty).
