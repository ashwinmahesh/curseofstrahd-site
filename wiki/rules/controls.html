    Mouse

    • Left-click the ground to walk there; left-click a person, door or thing to use it.
    • Right-click for a menu of everything you can do with what's under the cursor.
    • In a fight, click a square to move (a far click walks as far as it can) or a foe to attack.

    Tips from the loading screens

    • Q and E turn the camera.
    • Tab changes who leads the party. In a conversation, it changes who speaks for you.
    • 1 to 4 pick a party member.
    • F5 makes a quick save, and F9 loads it.
    • Right-click anything in the world to see everything you can do with it.
    • In a conversation, H shows everything that has been said so far.
    • A failed check can sometimes be saved: Heroic Inspiration rerolls the d20.
    • A failed Persuasion, Intimidation, Deception, Performance or Insight attempt can't be tried twice. Choose who speaks with care.
    • In a fight, Space ends your turn and T picks the next target.
    • Barovia keeps no secrets from its lord. Strahd may visit when you least expect him.
    • Esc opens the menu, with Settings and your saves.
    • F1 shows every control.
    • M opens the travel map.
