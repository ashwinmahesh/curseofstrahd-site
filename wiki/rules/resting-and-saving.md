# Resting and Saving

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/rules/resting-and-saving.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

#### Resting (R)

- A **Short Rest** takes an hour and can be taken anywhere.
- A **Long Rest** can't start within 16 hours of the last one finishing, so two in a row can't clear Exhaustion or the Raise Dead penalty twice in one night.
- You can't Long Rest inside a dungeon or building while enemies remain in it, on any of its floors. Towns, inns, camps and the open road are fine, and so are magic items that make a safe place to sleep. Leomund's Tiny Hut and Guards and Wards lift the rule: with either, you can Long Rest in a building that still has enemies in it.
- Each place says whether resting there is safe or risky (it's on the place's page here). A Long Rest in a risky place has a 1 in 6 chance of being interrupted, except on Story difficulty ([Difficulty](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/rules/difficulty.md)). Hallow, Forbiddance, Private Sanctum or Nondetection make a rest safe.
- An inn room booked for the night makes the next Long Rest there better: temporary Hit Points equal to each hero's level, or twice that and one more level of Exhaustion gone in the best room ([Merchants and Services](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/merchants.md)).
- After a Long Rest, a companion may ask for a word at camp. These camp talks carry the heroes' personal stories ([Companion Approval](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/characters/companion-approval.md)); each plays once.
- Mage Armor can be set to cast itself as each Long Rest ends ([How Magic Works](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/rules/how-magic-works.md)).

#### Saving

- **F5** quicksaves and **F9** loads the quicksave. Save Game in the pause menu (**Esc**) writes a new slot or overwrites one of yours, with a note of up to 60 letters.
- The game autosaves on arriving somewhere new, and saves the start of each fight's round so you can retry it.
- Load a Save lists every save newest first, with a picture of where you were. It can sort by place or by day.
- **Chapters:** the Load page's second tab starts a game at the beginning of any chapter of the story, with the heroes at that chapter's level and gear.
- **Backups:** the third tab keeps copies made when the game updates.
- Loading a game you saved more than 30 minutes ago opens with a short "Previously in Barovia" recap from the Narrator.
- An Honour run keeps a single save that the game writes for you ([Difficulty](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/rules/difficulty.md)).
