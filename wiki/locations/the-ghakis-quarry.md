## Overview

The count's quarry on the south face of Mount Ghakis, cut last spring: raw stone terraces, abandoned tools, a wagon with a glowing crate, and mountain folk chained to a ring in the floor (The Mountain's Heart, docs/story/side_quests.md).

A raw bite out of the mountain's south face, cut in terraces. The stone is fresh and pale where it was cut and the dust hasn't settled, because the ground keeps moving. A wagon stands in the middle with a crate on it, glowing at the seams.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

## Fights

- *The quarry floor sits up, and then stands up, three times a man's height, with a hole in its chest.*, in [The Ghakis Quarry](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-ghakis-quarry.md): [Grandfather Stone](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/grandfather-stone.md) (395 HP). Starts from a conversation. Part of [The Mountain's Heart](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-mountains-heart.md).
