## Overview

A rain-slick walk round the outside of the Rooms of Weeping, a thousand feet above the valley, between the keep's wall and a low parapet. Strahd's empty armour keeps the watch. A window lets onto the count's bedroom.

Freezing rain on a narrow stone walkway, high above the gulf of mist.

**Resting:** No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not out here, in the rain, with the armour walking.
