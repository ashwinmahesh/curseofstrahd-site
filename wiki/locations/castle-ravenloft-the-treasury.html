    Castle Ravenloft, the Treasury

    A vault between the floors, reached by a narrow stair behind a portrait in the King's Hall. The count's hoard lies in heaps on the floor, centuries of tithes he has never needed to spend.

    Castle Ravenloft, the Court of the Count

    Overview

    A low vault at the foot of a narrow stair. Coin lies on the floor in drifts, with chests and caskets sunk in it like rocks in snow.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not on his gold. Not with the stair the only way out.
