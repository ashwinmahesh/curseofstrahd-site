    Overview

    Under the great hall: a long gallery of ten talking sarcophagi, a door of glowing amber over a vault heaped with the offerings of the desperate, and at the bottom of everything the sanctum where a prince of the west once made his bargain with the thing whose sarcophagus is now empty.

    The amber in the walls glows here, faintly, like honey held up to a candle. It is the only warm colour for a mile.

    Resting: no Nobody sleeps down here. The voices in the amber know your names now, and they take turns.
