## Overview

A steep, pine-dark mountain above Van Richten's lake, with frost where frost shouldn't be, a ring of glass where lightning lands every evening, and a stone hut whose owner has forgotten who he is.

The goat track comes up from the south through switchbacks of pine. The hut stands in a yard to the east; the summit, with its cairn, rises to the north-west; a lookout to the west shows the lake below.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [A Seat at the Table](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/a-seat-at-the-table.md): Ratatouille learned magic so that people would sit with him. He crossed the mists to apprentice himself to the Wizard of Wines, a master of…
- [The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md): Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
- [The Drowned Library](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-drowned-library.md): Something whispers up the old well on the shore of Lake Baratok, from the archmage Khazan's library under the lake: don't come down.
- [The Forgotten Storm](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-forgotten-storm.md): Every evening lightning lands on the ring of glass on Mount Baratok. The mage told it something once, and it's still doing it.
- [The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-mad-mage-of-mount-baratok.md): A powerful wizard lives alone on Mount Baratok with his memory gone. Someone took it on purpose.
- [The Mage's Lost Pages](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-mages-lost-pages.md): Mordenkainen's memory came back with pages missing: letters in a hand he knows better than his own, and no name with it.
- [The Name Over the Door](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-name-over-the-door.md): Mordenkainen says the archmage who built the tower on the tarn never died: he moved downstairs, and he's waking.
