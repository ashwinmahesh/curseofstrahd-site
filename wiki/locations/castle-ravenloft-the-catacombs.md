## Overview

The crypts under the castle where the house of von Zarovich keeps its dead in rows: generals, servants, a conjurer, a jester, a champion watched by stone, and the black horse's stall. Stairs lead up to the castle and down to the oldest tombs.

Aisles of crypts run away into the dark in straight lines, each behind an iron gate with a name over it. The air is cold and very still.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
