    Under Mount Ghakis

    ✦Mount Ghakis · party level about 10

    The count's caves under the north shoulder of Mount Ghakis: the keepers' post, their larder, the anchor of a great chain, a gallery of fire-cracked rock and, at the bottom, the dragon chained on her hoard (The Warm Snow, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    Resting: no Every few minutes the floor shivers with her breathing, and the heat never lets up. Nobody sleeps in here.
    Light: dark

    A tunnel into the mountain, warm as a kitchen and getting warmer. The rock sweats. Every so often the whole floor trembles, very slightly, in and out, like a sleeper's chest.
