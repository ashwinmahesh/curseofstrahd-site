## Overview

The roof: a square of flat adamantine inside a toothed parapet, thirty feet above the ground, with the trapdoor in one corner.

**Resting:** No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. The wind on the battlement would take the blankets and the sleepers in them. The floors below are the place to sleep.
