    The Mad Mage's Hut

    A stone hut colder inside than out: a dragonchess board with frost on the pieces, a mirror turned to the wall, shelves of blank books, and an old man in a magnificent ruin of a robe.

    Overview

    One room with a sleeping corner and a study divided off by a half wall. The hearth has never been lit; the cold doesn't seem to bother him.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • A Seat at the Table: Ratatouille learned magic so that people would sit with him. He crossed the mists to apprentice himself to the Wizard of Wines, a master of…
    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Drowned Library: Something whispers up the old well on the shore of Lake Baratok, from the archmage Khazan's library under the lake: don't come down.
    • The Forgotten Storm: Every evening lightning lands on the ring of glass on Mount Baratok. The mage told it something once, and it's still doing it.
    • The Mad Mage of Mount Baratok: A powerful wizard lives alone on Mount Baratok with his memory gone. Someone took it on purpose.
    • The Mage's Lost Pages: Mordenkainen's memory came back with pages missing: letters in a hand he knows better than his own, and no name with it.
    • The Name Over the Door: Mordenkainen says the archmage who built the tower on the tarn never died: he moved downstairs, and he's waking.
