## Overview

The top floor of the dragon's house: the trapped hallway between the knights' quarters, the dragon's audience hall with its throne turned to the windows, the round table where four dead knights wait for orders, the emptied vault, the stained-glass gallery, the dragon's bare bedroom, and the stair to the roof.

A cold hallway at the head of the stair, shut doors on either side. North, a long passage with the sky showing through its far end, and off it the great doors of a hall built for wings.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
