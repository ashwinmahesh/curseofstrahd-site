    The Hills above Lake Zarovich

    A pine-dark slope in the hills above Lake Zarovich, climbing to a rock face with a cave in it. The ground around the cave mouth is bare from the traffic of many feet, not all of them on two legs.

    Overview

    A cold stream, boulders, pines scored with claw marks, a heap of bones politely out of sight of the trail, and a cave mouth breathing out warm air that smells of wet dog and old meat.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
