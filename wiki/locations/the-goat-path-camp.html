    Overview

    A shelf of rock above the goat path round the Tsolenka watchtower, out of the wind, where the guide makes camp with the families he leads up to the Amber Temple (The Guide to the Temple, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    A shelf of rock above the goat path, out of the worst of the wind. A fire in a ring of stones, a low tent, a sledge drawn up to the warmth, and the mountain going up into the cloud behind.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.
