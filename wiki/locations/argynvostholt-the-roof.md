## Overview

The roof of the dragon's house: the leads between low parapets, the corner turrets, a stone dragon gargoyle the wind talks through, the old ballista and the broken one turned on the tower, the hole over the passage below, and the beacon tower's second door.

Wet slates between low parapets, open to the sky. The beacon tower rises at the north-east corner, with a door on the roof.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
