    The Burgomaster's Mansion

    Baron Vallakovich's mansion: a hall hung with festival banners, a dining room laid for guests who never stay, Izek's bare room off the hall, and in the attic the Baron's son and his circle.

    Overview

    Bunting on every banister, portraits of the Baron in every pose a man can strike. A narrow stair climbs from the hall to the attic, where the air smells of chalk and something burnt.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. The Baron's guest beds have been re-stuffed for the festival, and Lady Lydia will not have them slept in before then. Or after.

    Quests and lore

    • Florin's Grandson: Old Florin prays every day that his grandson, who went up the castle road for work two winters ago, doesn't come back.
    • Lady Wachter's Book Club: Lady Fiona Wachter wants the Baron's chair, and she is not shy about who she would rule for.
    • Sanctuary: Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.
    • The Attic Circle: The Baron's son Victor is trying to leave Barovia by magic, and it is not going well for the servants.
    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Doll Collector: Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.
    • The Festival of the Blazing Sun: Baron Vallakovich holds a festival every week to prove Vallaki is happy. This one, with its great wicker sun, will decide who rules the…
    • The Sun-Cake: The Baron wants a cake shaped like the sun for the festival, and his cook, who has made four hundred and thirteen round things, wants to…
    ❝The festival ledger

    The Baron's festival accounts, in a round, hopeful hand. Festival of the First Snow: a success. Festival of the Late Harvest: a great success. Festival of the Returning Swallows (no swallows): a success. Beside each, a second column headed MALCONTENTS, and names. The column is longer than the first.

    ❝Victor's notes

    Pages of chalk diagrams, crossed out and redrawn. 'Third attempt. Grigor stepped in at my word. Flash, heat, nothing. Not transported. Not anywhere.' Below, smaller: 'Fourth attempt: Mirela. The same.' At the bottom, underlined twice: 'The fault is in the destination sigil. Or there is nowhere to go.'
