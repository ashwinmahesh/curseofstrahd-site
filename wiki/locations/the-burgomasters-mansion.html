    The Burgomaster's Mansion

    ✦Vallaki · party level about 5

    Baron Vallakovich's mansion: a hall hung with festival banners, a dining room laid for guests who never stay, Izek's bare room off the hall, and in the attic the Baron's son and his circle.

    Resting: no The Baron's guest beds have been re-stuffed for the festival, and Lady Lydia will not have them slept in before then. Or after.
    Light: dim

    Bunting on every banister, portraits of the Baron in every pose a man can strike. A narrow stair climbs from the hall to the attic, where the air smells of chalk and something burnt.

    Things to read

    ❝The festival ledger

    The Baron's festival accounts, in a round, hopeful hand. Festival of the First Snow: a success. Festival of the Late Harvest: a great success. Festival of the Returning Swallows (no swallows): a success. Beside each, a second column headed MALCONTENTS, and names. The column is longer than the first.

    ❝Victor's notes

    Pages of chalk diagrams, crossed out and redrawn. 'Third attempt. Grigor stepped in at my word. Flash, heat, nothing. Not transported. Not anywhere.' Below, smaller: 'Fourth attempt: Mirela. The same.' At the bottom, underlined twice: 'The fault is in the destination sigil. Or there is nowhere to go.'
