    Castle Ravenloft, Strahd's Tomb

    ✦Castle Ravenloft · party level about 10

    The tomb at the bottom of the castle where Strahd von Zarovich keeps his coffin and sleeps away the day. The only place in the valley where he can be ended for good.

    Resting: no Not within reach of that coffin. Nobody here could close their eyes.
    Light: dark

    A square vault at the foot of a long black stair. In the middle, on a stepped dais, a plain wooden coffin on trestles, and the smell of turned earth.
