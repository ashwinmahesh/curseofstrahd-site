## Overview

A Vistani camp beside a dark pool fed by the Tser Falls: painted wagons around a fire, horses, music, wine, and the great patched tent where Madam Eva reads the Tarokka.

The Old Svalich Road runs past a clearing on the edge of the Svalich Woods. In it, painted wagons stand in a loose ring around a bonfire, horses doze in a rail pen by the water, and a tent the size of a small house glows from inside.

**Resting:** Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [Fennimore's Guide: The Tser Pool](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/fennimores-guide-the-tser-pool.md): Ambrose Fennimore is writing the definitive guidebook to the valley and is not sure the Tser Pool camp's famous plum stew is really plum.
- [Radu's Unsellable Horse](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/radus-unsellable-horse.md): Radu has a handsome dun gelding called Dinte that bites everyone, and he will pay fifteen gold to anyone who finds it a home.
- [Stanimir's Fire](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/stanimirs-fire.md): Stanimir has bet his cask of plum wine that nobody can tell a better tale at his fire than he can.
- [The Dancing Bear](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-dancing-bear.md): Lavinia's bear Bujor, who has danced on the Tser Pool camp's stage for nine years, broke his chain in the night and went up into the woods.

- [The Last Muster](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-last-muster.md): Every night, riders made of bones go east along the Old Svalich Road to the Gates of Barovia, and none of them comes back.
- [The Seer at Tser Pool](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-seer-at-tser-pool.md): The Vistani say an old seer at Tser Pool reads the Tarokka for strangers, and that she has been expecting us.

- [The Tinker's Wagon](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-tinkers-wagon.md): A Vistani tinker sells trinkets from a green wagon at the crossroads, and the travellers who buy from him don't come back down the road.
