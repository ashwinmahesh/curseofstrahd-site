    Castle Ravenloft, the Ravens' Roost

    The long space under the castle's roofs where hundreds of ravens roost on the beams. They hide bright things in their nests, and they watch everything.

    Overview

    Under the roof the dark is full of small sounds: feathers settling, claws shifting on wood, a hundred birds deciding what you are.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

    Getting here

    Ways in
