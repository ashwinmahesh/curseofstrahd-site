## Overview

A walled town on the shore of Lake Zarovich, kept cheerful by order of its Baron: a sharpened palisade, a church whose saint has gone missing, an inn of ravens, and a square where a great wicker sun waits to be burned.

A palisade of sharpened logs rings the town. The Old Svalich Road comes in at the east gate and leaves by the west, past a Vistani camp outside the walls. The burgomaster's mansion, Wachterhaus and St. Andral's Church face the square from the north; the Blue Water Inn, the coffin maker and the lockup stand to the south.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [Florin's Grandson](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/florins-grandson.md): Old Florin prays every day that his grandson, who went up the castle road for work two winters ago, doesn't come back.
- [Ireena's Escort](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/ireenas-escort.md): Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
- [Lady Wachter's Book Club](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/lady-wachters-book-club.md): Lady Fiona Wachter wants the Baron's chair, and she is not shy about who she would rule for.
- [Old Silviu's Wedding Cask](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/old-silvius-wedding-cask.md): Old Silviu wants the last cask of the year Elvir was born carried to Vallaki for his daughter Dana's wedding.
- [One Lantern Too Many](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/one-lantern-too-many.md): Every festival week the children of Vallaki carry paper lanterns round the square for the Baron. Thirty-one children. Thirty-two lanterns.
- [Ribbons](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/ribbons.md): Daciana gives out yellow festival ribbons in Vallaki's square and writes down everyone who won't wear one. It isn't her who wants to know.
- [Sanctuary](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/sanctuary.md): Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.
- [The Attic Circle](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-attic-circle.md): The Baron's son Victor is trying to leave Barovia by magic, and it is not going well for the servants.
- [The Bones of St. Andral](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-bones-of-st-andral.md): Someone has stolen the bones of St. Andral from the crypt of Vallaki's church, and with them the only thing that kept the dead out of it.
- [The Cat in the Window](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-cat-in-the-window.md): Lady Wachter keeps her daughter Stella locked in a bedroom at Wachterhaus. Stella believes she is a cat. Nobody in Vallaki believes she's…
- [The Count's Portraitist](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-counts-portraitist.md): Haralamb, who paints the Baron's wicker sun, was fetched to the castle and kept eleven nights painting a woman from the count's memory. He…
- [The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md): Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
- [The Doll Collector](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-doll-collector.md): Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.
- [The Festival of the Blazing Sun](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-festival-of-the-blazing-sun.md): Baron Vallakovich holds a festival every week to prove Vallaki is happy. This one, with its great wicker sun, will decide who rules the…
