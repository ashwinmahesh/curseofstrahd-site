## Overview

The top of Yester Hill inside a ring of standing stones: a black, twisted tree that drinks what it is given, a fire, and a great earthen effigy of the lord of the valley, lying on its back with a stone in its heart.

The crown of the hill is flat and bare inside its ring of stones. In the middle stands a tree with bark like a burnt hand and roots that go into the ground the way fingers go into a wound. Beside it lies a man made of earth and timber, forty feet long, cloaked, with a face you have seen before.

**Resting:** No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Nobody sleeps in reach of those roots. Nobody who wants to wake up.
