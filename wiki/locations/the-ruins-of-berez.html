    The Ruins of Berez

    ✦Berez · party level about 9

    A village the Luna drowned: broken walls standing in reeds and black water, a white statue on the only dry mound, and in the middle, on a stump wider than a house, a hut that breathes.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    The marsh south of the Luna river, where Berez used to be. The track from the road comes in from the south-east along an old causeway. Marina's monument stands on its mound in the west; Baba Lysaga's hut squats on its giant stump in the east, ringed by scarecrows.

    Things to read

    ❝A swollen diary

    The last pages of the diary of Ivo Dragan, burgomaster of Berez, the ink run with damp. He writes that the lord of the castle has been calling on Marina, the candlemaker's girl, with gifts nobody dares refuse. He hid her in the chapel, under the bell, and sat up with her. In the morning she was dead in her blankets, not a mark on her, only a scratch over the heart like a bramble's. The last entry is one line: The river is coming up the street, and the bell is ringing on its own.
