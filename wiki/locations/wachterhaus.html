    Wachterhaus

    Lady Fiona Wachter's townhouse: a hall of family portraits, a parlour set for a book club, a locked bedroom that something scratches at from inside, and, behind the study bookcase, a stair down.

    Overview

    Polished floors, good candles, and the hush of a house where servants are trained not to hear things.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not under this roof. Somewhere above you, slow and patient, something is scratching at a door.
