## Overview

A shelf of wind-scoured snow high above the Tsolenka Pass, where a temple's doors are cut into the mountain between two colossi of amber, one split and dead, one watching. Inside: a frozen vestibule and the wardens' two guardrooms.

The air is thin enough to make the head swim. Below, the whole valley lies under its lid of cloud.

**Resting:** no Not out here. The wind takes the warmth out of you faster than sleep could put it back, and the colossus never stops watching.
