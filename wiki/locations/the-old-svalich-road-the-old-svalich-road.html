    The Old Svalich Road

    An open stretch of the Old Svalich Road between ditches and stone field walls: where the road's random fights and meetings play out.

    Overview

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Fights

    • The lanterns drop into the mud, still burning. The mourners come on without them., in The Old Svalich Road: 5 × Mourner (Strahd Zombie), 2 × Pallbearer (Strahd Zombie), 2 × Mourner in Black (Wight). Starts from a conversation.

    At camp here

    A Long Rest here makes camp, and 1 night in 6 is interrupted (never on Story difficulty). Then the forest night visitors for your level come out of the dark (Random Encounters):

    Party levelWhat comesXP
    1–34 × Wolf200
    4–6Dire Wolf, 5 × Wolf, Rat460
    7–112 × Werewolf, 3 × Dire Wolf, 2 × Wolf, Giant Rat2125

    On the road

    Random encounters met on Krezk Road, Svalich Road can be fought here. What comes, and how likely each is, is on Random Encounters.
