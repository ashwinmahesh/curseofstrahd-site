## Overview

The tower's upper floor: a bare lookout under the roof where the watch kept an eye on the road, with a spyglass at the slit window and a hatch to the roof.

The stair comes up in the south-east corner. The lookout runs the width of the tower, with slit windows on the road side and a hatch in the ceiling that leads to the roof. Ours, not the book's: the book gives the tower an upper floor and little in it.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
