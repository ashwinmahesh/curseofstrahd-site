    Overview

    What the Amber Temple's wardens sealed under their own vaults: a stair down through the weeping floor, the hall of sleepers in their amber, the wardens' archive and, at the bottom, the chamber of the Eye Below (The Amber Debt, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    Under the vault floor the amber isn't set. It weeps out of the walls like resin from a cut pine, warm, and slow, and it smells of honey and of something that has been asleep for a very long time.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Every time you shut your eyes, you feel it looking at you through them. Nobody sleeps down here.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • Something blinks inside the nearest block of amber. Then the amber blinks back, everywhere, and the little…, in Under the Vaults: A Sleeper Woken (Amber Golem, 130 HP), 10 × Dream-Gazer, A Sleeper Woken (Amber Golem).
    • The Eye Below sinks back into the amber to its middle as the last note of the lullaby goes, and wakes too…, in Under the Vaults: The Eye Below, Dream-Gazer. Scales with your level. Part of The Amber Debt.
    ✦By party level (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)
    • Under the Vaults, when deep lullaby sung and deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below (335 HP) (10000 XP; surprise: enemies)
    • Under the Vaults, when deep lullaby sung and not deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below (100 HP), Dream-Gazer (10100 XP; lair; surprise: enemies)
    • Under the Vaults, when not deep lullaby sung and deep mirrors turned: The Eye Below (135 HP) (10000 XP)
    • Under the Vaults: The Eye Below (335 HP), Dream-Gazer (10100 XP; lair)
