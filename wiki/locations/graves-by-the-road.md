## Overview

A burial ground climbs the slope beside the road behind a sagging fence: rows of leaning headstones, raw mounds, an open grave and a crypt on the top terrace. A random fight on the valley's roads may play out here.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 4 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

## Fights

No fights of its own.

### At camp here

A Long Rest here makes camp, and 1 night in 6 is interrupted (never on Story difficulty). Then the forest night visitors for your level come out of the dark ([Random Encounters](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md)):

| Party level | What comes | XP |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 4 × [Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/wolf.md) | 200 |
| 4–6 | [Dire Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/dire-wolf.md), 5 × [Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/wolf.md), [Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/rat.md) | 460 |
| 7–11 | 2 × [Werewolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/werewolf.md), 3 × [Dire Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/dire-wolf.md), 2 × [Wolf](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/wolf.md), [Giant Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-rat.md) | 2125 |

### On the road

Random encounters met on [Argynvostholt Road](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md#argynvostholt-road), [Krezk Road](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md#krezk-road), [Svalich Road](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md#svalich-road), [Svalich Woods](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md#svalich-woods) can be fought here. What comes, and how likely each is, is on [Random Encounters](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md).
