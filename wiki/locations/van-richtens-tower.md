## Overview

A tower of black stone on an island in Lake Baratok, a cold tarn under Mount Baratok. A long-dead archmage built it; Rudolph van Richten keeps it now, and his door answers to the builder's name. A Vistani wagon is parked on the shore.

Cold, still water; a half-drowned stone causeway out to the island; a round tower with one lit window and no handle on its door. On the shore, a painted wagon and a campfire.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md): Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
- [The Drowned Library](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-drowned-library.md): Something whispers up the old well on the shore of Lake Baratok, from the archmage Khazan's library under the lake: don't come down.
- [The Hunter's Ledger](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-hunters-ledger.md): Ezmerelda d'Avenir keeps a book of the valley's killers with a price beside each name, paying gold for the easier names and kit from her…
- [The Hunter's Tower](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-hunters-tower.md): A dead archmage's tower on an island in Lake Baratok, kept now by Rudolph van Richten and warded against one person in particular.
- [The Name Over the Door](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-name-over-the-door.md): Mordenkainen says the archmage who built the tower on the tarn never died: he moved downstairs, and he's waking.
- [The Pack in the Hills](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-pack-in-the-hills.md): A pack of werewolves dens in the hills above Lake Zarovich. Its leader serves the castle, and its old leader hangs in chains for refusing…
