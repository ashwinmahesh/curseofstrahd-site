    Overview

    The abbey's church: a nave of plain pews, an altar on its step under a great gilded sunburst, the Abbot's bare cell on one side and the sacristy on the other. Where the Abbot works, prays and means to wake his bride.

    Resting: no Not here. Not under that sunburst, with him lighting candles in the dark at the end of the nave.

    Quests and lore

    • Carry the Dawn: On the morning of her vows the Morninglord showed Liriel Dawnsong a valley where the sun had not risen in four hundred years, and told her…
    • The Abbot's Bride: The Abbot of St. Markovia is stitching a bride for the lord of Barovia from Krezk's dead, and believes a wife will cure him.
    • The Burgomaster's Son: Ilya Krezkov, the burgomaster's last child, has been burning with fever for a month.
    • The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind: A holy symbol of great power against the undead, lost long ago in the valley.
    • The Sunsword: A blade of sunlight that once belonged to the devil's brother, broken from its hilt and lost.
    • The Tome of Strahd: A history of the devil, written in his own hand. Madam Eva says it holds the knowledge of his past.
    ❝A ledger of improvements

    A long ledger in a beautiful, even hand, every entry dated. 'Yevgeni B., a hound's patience, for his temper; took well.' 'Old Marta B., wings, for her lightness of spirit; did not take. Wards.' 'Little Ilsa B., an owl's eyes, for the dark; took.' The last pages are a different list: 'Left hand, from the grave of a seamstress. Throat, from a singer. Eyes, two, from sisters, to be matched.' At the bottom: 'Something white.'
