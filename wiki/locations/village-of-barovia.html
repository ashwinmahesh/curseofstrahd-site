    Village of Barovia

    A shuttered village in a mist-choked valley: muddy streets, boarded houses, a tavern with almost no wine, a mansion under siege, and a church on the hill where something screams at night.

    Overview

    The Old Svalich Road runs through the village from the gates and the mists in the east to the far hills in the west, past doors nailed shut. A big house stands behind an iron fence to the north-west, a church on its rise to the north-east, and a tall, handsome house behind a rusted fence at the south-east edge.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Count Off: Ilse Varga's mercenary company marched into the mists a year ago at the invitation of someone who signed himself S. She was meant to march…
    • Cut After Noon: The woodcutters of Barovia cut what they need by noon and go home. Grigore's son stayed late, and brought something back.
    • Ireena's Escort: Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
    • Six Feet: Old Mihail the sexton digs graves before anybody has died. There's always somebody by the end of the week.
    • The Burgomaster's Burial: Kolyan Indirovich, burgomaster of the village of Barovia, died three nights ago. His children want him buried in the churchyard, and no…
    • The Burgomaster's Hound: The dead burgomaster's wolfhound howls every night at the mansion's study wall, not at the wolves.
    • The Doll Collector: Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.
    • The Fourth Sister: Once Morgantha is dead, a girl sells plain pastries on the miller's lane below Old Bonegrinder, and says her Granny is coming for her…
    • The Goatherd's Count: Goodwife Petra's grandson Erno counts one more goat every evening on the high pasture than he owns.
    • The Last Muster: Every night, riders made of bones go east along the Old Svalich Road to the Gates of Barovia, and none of them comes back.
    • The Last Traveller: A young man in rags and beautiful boots sits at the edge of the mists on the Old Svalich Road, asking what day it is.
    • The Nursemaid's Grave: Old Mihail the gravedigger says a girl in a nursemaid's cap stands at the churchyard wall by night and won't come in. Nobody ever brought…
    • The Oat Thief: Somebody is stealing four sacks of oats a week from Bildrath's back room, and the lock is never touched.
    • The Polite Caller: Widow Ileana's husband Teodor died three winters ago and still knocks at her shutter every night. He has never once asked to be let in.
    • The Priest's Son: Screams rise from beneath the village church. Father Donavich's son Doru came back from Castle Ravenloft as something else, and his father…
    ❝Notice board

    By order of the burgomaster, Kolyan Indirovich: no one is to be abroad after dusk. Doors barred, shutters closed, lamps out by the second bell. Do not answer a knock you were not expecting, whoever it claims to be. Do not go to the castle. Do not go to the mists. Do not go into the house at the end of the lane. Below, in another hand, smaller: he is not coming back to read this.
