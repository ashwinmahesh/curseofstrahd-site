    Overview

    A walled town on the shore of Lake Zarovich, kept cheerful by order of its Baron: a sharpened palisade, a church whose saint has gone missing, an inn of ravens, and a square where a great wicker sun waits to be burned.

    A palisade of sharpened logs rings the town. The Old Svalich Road comes in at the east gate and leaves by the west, past a Vistani camp outside the walls. The burgomaster's mansion, Wachterhaus and St. Andral's Church face the square from the north; the Blue Water Inn, the coffin maker and the lockup stand to the south.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Black Roses: Last winter Ignat the lamplighter's daughter Catalina was found smiling and white at the lake gate, and black roses came with a card. This…
    • Count Off: Ilse Varga's mercenary company marched into the mists a year ago at the invitation of someone who signed himself S. She was meant to march…
    • Emanoil Wants to Be Bitten: A Vallaki clerk who sleeps in a coffin writes long poems to the castle and wants to be chosen by the lord of the valley.
    • Ireena's Escort: Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
    • Lady Wachter's Book Club: Lady Fiona Wachter wants the Baron's chair, and she is not shy about who she would rule for.
    • Old Silviu's Wedding Cask: Old Silviu wants the last cask of the year Elvir was born carried to Vallaki for his daughter Dana's wedding.
    • One Lantern Too Many: Every festival week the children of Vallaki carry paper lanterns round the square for the Baron. Thirty-one children. Thirty-two lanterns.
    • Ribbons: Daciana gives out yellow festival ribbons in Vallaki's square and writes down everyone who won't wear one. It isn't her who wants to know.
    • Sanctuary: Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.
    • The Attic Circle: The Baron's son Victor is trying to leave Barovia by magic, and it is not going well for the servants.
    • The Bones of St. Andral: Someone has stolen the bones of St. Andral from the crypt of Vallaki's church, and with them the only thing that kept the dead out of it.
    • The Cat in the Window: Lady Wachter keeps her daughter Stella locked in a bedroom at Wachterhaus. Stella believes she is a cat. Nobody in Vallaki believes she's…
    • The Count's Portraitist: Haralamb, who paints the Baron's wicker sun, was fetched to the castle and kept eleven nights painting a woman from the count's memory. He…
    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Doll Collector: Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.
    • The Festival of the Blazing Sun: Baron Vallakovich holds a festival every week to prove Vallaki is happy. This one, with its great wicker sun, will decide who rules the…
    • The Missing Girl: Arabelle, the young daughter of the Vistani headman Luvash, vanished from the camp outside Vallaki in the night.
    • The Sun-Cake: The Baron wants a cake shaped like the sun for the festival, and his cook, who has made four hundred and thirteen round things, wants to…
    • The Toymaker's Masterpiece: Gadof Blinsky, Vallaki's toymaker, has wanted all his life to see the clockwork jester his master's master made for the count's children.
    ❝Festival notice

    BY ORDER OF THE BARON VARGAS VALLAKOVICH, BURGOMASTER: The FESTIVAL OF THE BLAZING SUN will be held in the town square at noon. Every citizen will attend. Every citizen will be glad. A great sun of wicker will be lit to remind the sky what it is for. Frowning, muttering and remarks about the weather will be noted by the watch. ALL WILL BE WELL.

    ❝Decree on the fence (when not festival over)

    DECREE OF THE BURGOMASTER, the ninth this month: Malcontents found spreading gloom shall spend one day in the stocks, or two if they spread it there. Lamps out at the second bell. The gates close at dusk. Gossip about the castle is gloom. Gossip about the weather is gloom. Gossip about the Baron is treason.

    ❝Decree on the fence (when vallaki backed is “baron”)

    DECREE OF THE BURGOMASTER: The traitor Fiona Wachter has been taken in the act and set in the stocks for the town's instruction. Her house is forfeit to the festival fund. The next festival will be held on the seventh day, and it will be the best yet. ALL IS WELL, as was foretold.

    ❝Proclamation on the fence (when vallaki backed is “wachter”)

    PROCLAMATION OF THE BURGOMISTRESS, LADY FIONA WACHTER: There will be no more festivals. Citizens may frown as they please. The curfew stands. The town gives thanks to its rightful lord for its deliverance, and will show him every courtesy when he calls.

    ❝Council notice (when vallaki backed is “neither”)

    NOTICE: Until the town chooses otherwise, Vallaki is kept by a council of its householders, with Father Lucian Petrovich and Urwin Martikov to speak for it. The stocks are firewood. The curfew stands, because the night has not changed its mind. Bring grievances to the church steps, sober.
