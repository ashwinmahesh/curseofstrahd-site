    Overview

    Khazan's library, cut into the rock under Lake Baratok and reached by an old well-shaft on the shore: rows of shelves gone soft with damp, half of it flooded black, and the great catalogue desk that was never a desk (The Drowned Library, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    A long low hall under the lake, shelf after shelf of swollen books, the far half standing in still black water. The air is wet paper and cold stone, and in the middle of it all, a great catalogue desk of lacquered oak with a hundred little drawers.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 8 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • The catalogue desk opens all its drawers at once, and every one of them has teeth. Round the room, two…, in The Drowned Library: The Index (390 HP), 2 × A Reading Chair (Mimic). Starts from a conversation. Part of The Drowned Library.

    At camp here

    A Long Rest here makes camp, and 1 night in 6 is interrupted (never on Story difficulty). Then the indoors night visitors for your level come out of the dark (Random Encounters):
