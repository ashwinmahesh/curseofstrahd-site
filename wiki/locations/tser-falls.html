    Tser Falls

    Where the River Ivlis throws itself off a cliff in a long white fall. A shelf of rock juts over the drop; Barovians leave tokens there for the drowned and the fallen.

    Overview

    A footpath climbs north through the pines to the lip of a gorge. The river goes over the edge in one long white rope of water, and the roar fills everything.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Fights

    • Something has been sitting at the edge of the trees, listening to the water with you. It stands up on two…, in Tser Falls: Werewolf, Dire Wolf, 4 × Wolf, Rat.
    • The lights come up out of the gorge like lanterns climbing a stair, and every one of them is still talking in…, in Tser Falls: Will-o'-Wisp, Shadow, Bat. Scales with your level. Part of Called by Name.
