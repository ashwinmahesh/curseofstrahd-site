    Madam Eva's Tent

    A tent of patched and painted canvas, hung with charms and thick with incense, where an old woman reads the Tarokka at a low table by candlelight.

    Region

    Overview

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Madam Eva does not keep a guest bed, and you would not sleep well in it if she did. The camp outside is safer.

    What to find

    #WhereWhatHow
    3Madam Eva's painted chestPotion of Clairvoyanceopen it

    Tarokka treasure spots. If the reading hid one of the treasures here, it's in: Tser Pool Eva Tent (eva_card_chest)

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 4 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Quests and lore

    • Cold Prayers: Hedda Ironvow dreamed of a dying sun and a silver raven, and left her chapel before dawn to follow the dream. Since she crossed the mist,…
    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind: A holy symbol of great power against the undead, lost long ago in the valley.
    • The Seer at Tser Pool: The Vistani say an old seer at Tser Pool reads the Tarokka for strangers, and that she has been expecting us.
    • The Sunsword: A blade of sunlight that once belonged to the devil's brother, broken from its hilt and lost.
    • The Tome of Strahd: A history of the devil, written in his own hand. Madam Eva says it holds the knowledge of his past.

    • Where the Devil Waits: Madam Eva's last card showed where in his castle Strahd will be when the end comes.
