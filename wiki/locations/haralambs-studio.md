## Overview

Haralamb the painter's house on Vallaki's east row: a front room of pots and rags, and up the back a long studio full of portraits of one woman, and the great canvas the count gave him to copy from (The Count's Portraitist, docs/story/side_quests.md).

A painter's house that smells of linseed and something under it, like old flowers. Every canvas in it is turned to the wall, except the ones that aren't, and those all have the same face.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 5 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

## Fights

- *The great canvas leans out of its frame on its own, and the portraits round the walls come with it, frames…*, in [Haralamb's Studio](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/haralambs-studio.md): [The Count's Likeness](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/the-counts-likeness.md) (495 HP), 7 × A Copy ([Guardian Portrait](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/guardian-portrait.md), 40 HP). Starts from a conversation. Part of [The Count's Portraitist](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-counts-portraitist.md).

### At camp here

A Long Rest here makes camp, and 1 night in 6 is interrupted (never on Story difficulty). Then the indoors night visitors for your level come out of the dark ([Random Encounters](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md)):

| Party level | What comes | XP |

| 1–3 | 4 × [Giant Rat](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/bestiary/giant-rat.md) | 100 |
