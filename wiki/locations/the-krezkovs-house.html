    Overview

    The burgomaster's house: a long hall with a hearth and a table older than the village, the room where Ilya lies burning with fever, Dmitri and Anna's bedroom, and a small room that was their eldest son's.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Clovin's Audience: Clovin Belview, the abbey's errand-runner, has written jokes for forty years and wants to tell them once to people who could choose not to…
    • Ireena's Escort: Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
    • Lighter Than It Should Be: Somebody leaves carved toys on the five Krezkov graves at the Pool of the White Sun every night. Nobody in Krezk carves like that.
    • Rosemary for Kolyan: Ireena asked us to lay a sprig of pressed rosemary on her father's grave in the Village of Barovia.
    • Sanctuary: Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.
    • The Abbot's Bride: The Abbot of St. Markovia is stitching a bride for the lord of Barovia from Krezk's dead, and believes a wife will cure him.
    • The Burgomaster's Burial: Kolyan Indirovich, burgomaster of the village of Barovia, died three nights ago. His children want him buried in the churchyard, and no…
    • The Burgomaster's Son: Ilya Krezkov, the burgomaster's last child, has been burning with fever for a month.
    • What He Wants With Me: Ireena wants to know the truth about the woman whose face she wears, from the vampire's own book and from any monument made in her likeness.
    ❝The burgomaster's ledger

    The village ledger of Krezk, in Dmitri's square hand. Births, deaths, goats, the wine cart each autumn and what it cost. The deaths outnumber the births by two pages. Five names on the last page are in a different ink and underlined: Pyotr, Mila, Sofia, Anya, Lev. The sixth line has been ruled and left empty, and the ruling has been gone over many times.
