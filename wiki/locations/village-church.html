    Village Church

    ✦The Village of Barovia · party level about 3

    A failing church of the Morninglord: cracked windows, cold candles, a priest praying at the altar, and a barred door to the undercroft where his son screams for blood.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim

    The Morninglord's church, one of the few doors in the village still left unbarred. Below the nave, behind a barred door, lies the undercroft.

    Things to read

    ❝Burial register

    The village burial register, kept in a careful hand that grows less careful toward the end. The last entries are a widow, an infant and a woodcutter, each with the words 'buried in daylight'. On the newest line someone has written DORU, son of Donavich, then scored it out so hard the nib tore the page. Under it, smaller: not yet. Not yet. Not yet.
