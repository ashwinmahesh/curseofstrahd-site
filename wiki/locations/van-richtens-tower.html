    Overview

    A tower of black stone on an island in Lake Baratok, a cold tarn under Mount Baratok. A long-dead archmage built it; Rudolph van Richten keeps it now, and his door answers to the builder's name. A Vistani wagon is parked on the shore.

    Cold, still water; a half-drowned stone causeway out to the island; a round tower with one lit window and no handle on its door. On the shore, a painted wagon and a campfire.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Drowned Library: Something whispers up the old well on the shore of Lake Baratok, from the archmage Khazan's library under the lake: don't come down.
    • The Hunter's Ledger: Ezmerelda d'Avenir keeps a book of the valley's killers with a price beside each name, paying gold for the easier names and kit from her…
    • The Hunter's Tower: A dead archmage's tower on an island in Lake Baratok, kept now by Rudolph van Richten and warded against one person in particular.
    • The Name Over the Door: Mordenkainen says the archmage who built the tower on the tarn never died: he moved downstairs, and he's waking.
    • The Pack in the Hills: A pack of werewolves dens in the hills above Lake Zarovich. Its leader serves the castle, and its old leader hangs in chains for refusing…
    ❝A note pinned to a board by the wagon

    Rudolph. The old lock still opens to the name of the wizard who built this place: KHAZAN. I found it under the moss in an hour. Your new ward opens to nothing, and it bites me. It bites only me. You keyed it to me. I am sitting on your doorstep until you come out and explain that to my face. E.
