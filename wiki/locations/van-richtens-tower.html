    Van Richten's Tower

    ✦Van Richten's Tower · party level about 8

    A tower of black stone on an island in Lake Baratok, a cold tarn under Mount Baratok. A long-dead archmage built it; Rudolph van Richten keeps it now, and his door answers to the builder's name. A Vistani wagon is parked on the shore.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    Cold, still water; a half-drowned stone causeway out to the island; a round tower with one lit window and no handle on its door. On the shore, a painted wagon and a campfire.
