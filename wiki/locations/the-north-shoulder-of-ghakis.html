    The North Shoulder of Ghakis

    ✦Mount Ghakis · party level about 10

    A shelf of steaming black rock high on Mount Ghakis, where the snow won't lie, and an iron gate in the mountain with the count's crest on it (The Warm Snow, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    Slush, then mud, then bare black rock that steams in the cold. At the top of the shelf, an iron gate set into the mountain, and warm air breathing out round it, slowly, in and out.
