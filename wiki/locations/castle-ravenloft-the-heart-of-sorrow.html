    Castle Ravenloft, the Heart of Sorrow

    ✦Castle Ravenloft · party level about 10

    The round room at the very top of the high tower, with windows on the whole valley. In the middle, over a low dais, hangs a crystal heart the size of a man, beating slowly. It takes Strahd's wounds before he does.

    Resting: no Nobody sleeps beside that heartbeat.
    Light: dim

    A round room at the top of the world, windows on every side. In the middle hangs a heart of red crystal as big as a man, and it beats.
