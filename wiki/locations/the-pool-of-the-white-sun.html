    Overview

    A round spring-fed pool at the north end of Krezk that never freezes, ringed by Krezk's graves, with an open pavilion shrine to the White Sun beside it. The sun lingers in its water a breath longer than in the sky.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Lighter Than It Should Be: Somebody leaves carved toys on the five Krezkov graves at the Pool of the White Sun every night. Nobody in Krezk carves like that.
    • The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind: A holy symbol of great power against the undead, lost long ago in the valley.
    • The Pool of the White Sun: At the pool in Krezk, Ireena saw a young man in the water who knew her by another name.
    • The Sunsword: A blade of sunlight that once belonged to the devil's brother, broken from its hilt and lost.
    • The Tome of Strahd: A history of the devil, written in his own hand. Madam Eva says it holds the knowledge of his past.
