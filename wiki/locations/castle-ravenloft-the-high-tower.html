    Overview

    The high tower of Castle Ravenloft: a stair that winds up the inside of the tallest tower around an open well, from the main floor past the count's floor to the high landing, where one door leads to the tower rooms and one last stair to the Heart of Sorrow.

    A stair without a rail climbs the inside of a tower so tall its top is lost in the dark. In the middle there is nothing at all: a well of black air going straight down.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Doll Collector: Izek Strazni, the Baron's enforcer, has dreamed of a red-haired woman all his life, and has had her face carved over and over.
    • The Heart of Sorrow: At the top of Castle Ravenloft's high tower hangs a crystal heart that takes Strahd's wounds before he does.
    • The Jester's Key: Pidlwick II, the clockwork jester of the high tower, is running down: the ravens took his wind-up key.
    • The Toymaker's Masterpiece: Gadof Blinsky, Vallaki's toymaker, has wanted all his life to see the clockwork jester his master's master made for the count's children.
    • The Unnamed Crypt: One crypt in Castle Ravenloft's catacombs has no name on it, and something in it holds the count's doors.
