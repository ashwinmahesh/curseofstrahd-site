## Overview

A clearing up the deer path from the Tser Pool camp, where the stream comes down out of the Svalich Woods: pines, a jumble of rocks with an old bear den under them, and hunters' traps in the leaves (The Dancing Bear, docs/story/side_quests.md).

A clearing in the pines where the stream comes down over stones. Under a heap of mossy rocks on the far side there's a dark hollow that smells of animal, and the leaves round it have been scraped up into a bed.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 4 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.
