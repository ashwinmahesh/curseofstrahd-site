## Overview

A way that was never meant for guests, cut into the thickness of the keep's walls behind the study fireplace: a false treasury, a hall of webs and a belfry, ending in a secret stair down to the count's vault.

Soot, old webs and bare stone. The passage runs on in the dark between the walls of the keep, and whoever built it did not expect to be followed.

**Resting:** No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not here, with a bell overhead and the webs all round.
