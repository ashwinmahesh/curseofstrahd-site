    The Webbed Gully

    A gully off the path from the crossroads to Tser Falls, where a stream cuts down through the rock and giant spiders have strung the walls with web (The Spiders' Gully, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    Overview

    A gully off the falls path, where a stream comes down over black rock. Web hangs across it in sheets, grey with dust and old rain, and in the cracks high in the walls something shifts its weight.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 4 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • Things come out of the cracks in the gully walls on long legs, very quickly, down their own threads., in The Webbed Gully: Giant Spider, Swarm of Insects. Scales with your level. Part of The Spiders' Gully.

    • The Spiders' Gully: Iancu's cousin Tsura rode the falls path with a letter three days ago. Her horse came home with web on its saddle.
