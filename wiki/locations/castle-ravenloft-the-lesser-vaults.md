## Overview

An older range of crypts under the catacombs, where the house buried the people it did not honour: servants, guildsmen, saints nobody visited, a warlord with his captains, a bride in webs. A stair leads up to the catacombs' lower gallery, and one crypt hides a niche that opens on the count's own tomb.

The stair ends in a long low passage of older stone with crypts on both sides. Some of the gates are polished. Some have been very carefully locked from the inside.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
