## Overview

Under the larders: a vaulted hall, the jailers' post, two rows of cells and their prisoners, the torture chamber, a workroom where bones are made into furniture, the green furnace and its machinery, and the hall of bones, where the lord of the castle entertains.

Cold stone, dripping vaults and the sound of chains settling. Far off, a furnace breathes.

**Resting:** no Something in the cells is always awake down here, and it listens for breathing that slows.
