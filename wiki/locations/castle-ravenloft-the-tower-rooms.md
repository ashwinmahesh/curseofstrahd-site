## Overview

The rooms high in the towers, off a narrow gallery: the count's bats, a stillroom of four glass vessels, a coven at its cauldron, Escher locked in his suite, and a ladder to the roofs.

A narrow gallery with doors on both sides. Somewhere a pot is boiling, somewhere bats are shifting, and behind one locked door somebody is humming.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [Escher's Petition](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/eschers-petition.md): Every night for nine years Escher has slid the same petition under the count's door: let me see the sun. Now an answer has come back.
- [The Heart of Sorrow](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-heart-of-sorrow.md): At the top of Castle Ravenloft's high tower hangs a crystal heart that takes Strahd's wounds before he does.
