## Overview

A hollow in the pines above the Village of Barovia where charcoal was burned once, with a ruined hut nobody wanted, until Agafia and her children came (The Oat Thief, docs/story/side_quests.md).

A clearing in the pines, black underfoot with old charcoal, a stream at the edge, and a little hut of logs and turf with its door patched shut from the inside.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

A Long Rest here makes camp, and 1 night in 6 is interrupted (never on Story difficulty). Then the forest night visitors for your level come out of the dark ([Random Encounters](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/world/random-encounters.md)):
