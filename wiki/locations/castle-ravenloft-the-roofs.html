    Castle Ravenloft, the Roofs

    ✦Castle Ravenloft · party level about 10

    The roofs of Castle Ravenloft: the south tower's battlements, a walkway to the slate roof of the keep, and a narrow bridge across the drop to the north tower peak, where stone guardians crouch on the parapet.

    Resting: no The wind up here would take a blanket and the sleeper in it.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    Wind, and nothing between you and the valley but a parapet. Slate roofs fall away on every side, and beyond them a long, long drop.
