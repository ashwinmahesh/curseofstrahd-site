    Krezk

    ✦Krezk · party level about 7

    The last village on the road west: a few dozen stone cottages on a mountain shelf behind a wall three men high, a gate that opens only for a reason, and the Pool of the White Sun at its northern end. The Abbey of St. Markovia looks down on it from the mountainside.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    Outside: the end of the road, the shut gate and its watchman, and a switchback track climbing toward the abbey. Inside: the burgomaster's house, the well and the square, goat pens, cottages, and the lane north to the shrine of the White Sun.
