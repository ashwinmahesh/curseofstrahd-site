## Overview

The tower's upper floor: the same bare adamantine, bedrolls laid along the walls, a stair down and a trapdoor up to the battlement.

**Resting:** Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

## Getting here

### Ways in

- **From [Daern's Instant Fortress, Ground Floor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/daerns-instant-fortress-ground-floor.md):** Stairs up
- **From [Daern's Instant Fortress, the Battlement](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/daerns-instant-fortress-the-battlement.md):** A hatch down to the second floor

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 5 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.
