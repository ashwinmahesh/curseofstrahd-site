    The Winery Cellars

    Vaulted cellars under the winery: racks of casks, a side vault of old vintages, and at the far end a wall the druids have broken through, where their blights are digging up the floor.

    The Winery

    Overview

    Cold, and the right kind of cold: the cellar air holds the smell of oak and a hundred years of wine. Somewhere at the far end, something is digging.

    Resting: no Not with the digging at the far end. Not with the dark this patient.

    Fights

    • The blights lie where they fell, dead brush in heaps around their holes. Only the druid with the lantern is…, in The Winery Cellars: Druid, Vine Blight, Needle Blight. Scales with your level.
    ✦By party level (the first line your party fits is the fight you get)
    • The Winery Cellars, when not cellar dig cleared and gulthias tree burned: Druid overseer (Druid) (450 XP)

    • The Winery Cellars, when not cellar dig cleared and level ≥ 6: Druid overseer (Druid), Vine Blight, 6 × Needle Blight (850 XP)
