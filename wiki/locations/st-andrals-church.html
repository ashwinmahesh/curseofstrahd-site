    St. Andral's Church

    Vallaki's church of the Morninglord, named for the saint whose bones lie in the crypt beneath it and keep the dead from its doors. Or did, until the reliquary was emptied.

    Overview

    A long nave of pews under a sunburst window, an altar on its step, a vestry and a narrow stair down to the crypt where St. Andral's sarcophagus waits.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Carry the Dawn: On the morning of her vows the Morninglord showed Liriel Dawnsong a valley where the sun had not risen in four hundred years, and told her…
    • Cold Prayers: Hedda Ironvow dreamed of a dying sun and a silver raven, and left her chapel before dawn to follow the dream. Since she crossed the mist,…
    • Ireena's Escort: Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
    • Lady Wachter's Book Club: Lady Fiona Wachter wants the Baron's chair, and she is not shy about who she would rule for.
    • Rosemary for Kolyan: Ireena asked us to lay a sprig of pressed rosemary on her father's grave in the Village of Barovia.
    • Sanctuary: Ismark wanted Ireena behind walls. Vallaki has walls. Whether it has anywhere safe inside them is another question.
    • The Attic Circle: The Baron's son Victor is trying to leave Barovia by magic, and it is not going well for the servants.
    • The Bones of St. Andral: Someone has stolen the bones of St. Andral from the crypt of Vallaki's church, and with them the only thing that kept the dead out of it.

    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.

    ❝Church register

    The register of St. Andral's, in Father Lucian's careful hand. Baptisms, few. Burials, many. A note on the inside cover: The saint lies below in the oak box in the sarcophagus; the gravedigger and I alone know the way of the lid. If I am taken, Milivoj will show my successor. Under it, a later line, pressed hard: May God forgive me for trusting a hungry boy.
