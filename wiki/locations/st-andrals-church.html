    St. Andral's Church

    ✦Vallaki · party level about 5

    Vallaki's church of the Morninglord, named for the saint whose bones lie in the crypt beneath it and keep the dead from its doors. Or did, until the reliquary was emptied.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim

    A long nave of pews under a sunburst window, an altar on its step, a vestry and a narrow stair down to the crypt where St. Andral's sarcophagus waits.

    Things to read

    ❝Church register

    The register of St. Andral's, in Father Lucian's careful hand. Baptisms, few. Burials, many. A note on the inside cover: The saint lies below in the oak box in the sarcophagus; the gravedigger and I alone know the way of the lid. If I am taken, Milivoj will show my successor. Under it, a later line, pressed hard: May God forgive me for trusting a hungry boy.
