    The Reed Isle

    A low island of reeds and shingle out on Lake Zarovich that nobody fishes near, where the Baron's boat leaves people who were unhappy too loudly (The Baron's Island, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    Overview

    A low island of reeds and shingle in the black water, a long row from the landing. Smoke, very faint, from somewhere in the middle, and fish drying on lines.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 8 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • The Baron's boatmen come up the shingle with clubs and a lantern, looking for the smoke, and the reeds behind…, in The Reed Isle: The Boatmaster (Bandit Captain, 90 HP), 2 × A Boatman (Warrior Veteran, 110 HP), 4 × A Boatman (Tough), Something in the Reeds (Giant Constrictor Snake, 100 HP). Part of The Baron's Island.

    Quests and lore

    • The Baron's Island: Some nights the Baron's boat rows out to the Reed Isle on Lake Zarovich with somebody tied up in it, and rows back with nobody.
