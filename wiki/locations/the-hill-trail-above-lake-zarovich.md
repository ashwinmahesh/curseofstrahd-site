## Overview

A stony trail through pines and boulders in the hills between Lake Zarovich, Lake Baratok and the wolves' den. Where the roads of this region turn into fights.

Pines, boulders, a trickle of meltwater across the path, and the lake glinting far below through the trees.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
