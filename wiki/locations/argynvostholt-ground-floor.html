    Argynvostholt, Ground Floor

    ✦Argynvostholt · party level about 8

    The ground floor of the dragon's house: the great hall where Vladimir Horngaard keeps court over a table of dead knights, the chapel with its dragon window, the armory, a ruined gallery and the servants' hall.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dark

    A burned entry hall with a broken statue at its heart; through tall doors north, a great hall where armoured figures sit along a long table; a chapel to the west under a window of coloured glass; an armory, a gallery of fallen banners, and the cold servants' hall.

    Things to read

    ❝The oath stone

    Letters cut deep into a slab of grey stone, their edges worn smooth by hands. THE OATH OF THE ORDER OF THE SILVER DRAGON. I will keep the watch while others sleep. I will shield the sleeper and the stranger alike. I will lift up the fallen and ask no price. I will take nothing that is not given. And when the light goes out, I will be the light.

    ❝The roll of honour

    A great book chained to a lectern, its pages swollen with damp. Names in a hundred hands, each with the year that knight took the oath. The last forty names share one date, written in a single hand that pressed so hard it tore the page.
