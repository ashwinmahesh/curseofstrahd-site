    The Amber Temple

    Overview

    A side vault off the stair down to the hall of vestiges. The wardens bricked its doorway up; long ago someone broke through from the stair, and the rubble lies inside among three more sarcophagi of amber: a storm, a star of ice, and one the eye keeps losing.

    Broken brick and dry mortar on the floor of a chamber the wardens meant to keep shut.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Nobody sleeps down here. The voices in the amber know your names now, and they take turns.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Quests and lore

    • The Amber Temple: High above the Tsolenka Pass, a temple of amber where dead powers talk and bargain, and where the lord of the valley once made his.
