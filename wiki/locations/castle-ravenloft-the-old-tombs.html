    Castle Ravenloft, the Old Tombs

    ✦Castle Ravenloft · party level about 10

    The oldest tombs under the castle: Sergei von Zarovich's white tomb, a hall of stone guardians, and the vault where King Barov and Queen Ravenovia lie side by side.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dark

    Below the crypts the stone changes: older, paler, cut with more care. Somebody loved the people buried here.
