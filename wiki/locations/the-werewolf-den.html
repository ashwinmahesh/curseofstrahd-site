    Overview

    Caves under the hill where Kiril Stoyanovich's pack sleeps: a great cave round a fire pit, the pack leader's lair, a shrine to Mother Night, the alcove where the old leader Emil hangs in silver chains, and a pen of stolen children.

    Warm, close and dark. Straw and furs, gnawed bones, the smell of animals that are sometimes people, and somewhere deeper in, small voices whispering.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not here. Every shadow in this cave has teeth, and some of them are pretending to sleep.
