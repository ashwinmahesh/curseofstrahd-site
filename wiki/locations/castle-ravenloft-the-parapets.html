    Castle Ravenloft, the Rooms of Weeping, Castle Ravenloft, the West Wing

    Overview

    A rain-slick walk round the outside of the Rooms of Weeping, a thousand feet above the valley, between the keep's wall and a low parapet. Strahd's empty armour keeps the watch. A window lets onto the count's bedroom.

    Freezing rain on a narrow stone walkway, high above the gulf of mist.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not out here, in the rain, with the armour walking.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • The empty armour pivots on its heel and raises a rain-slick gauntlet to strike., in Castle Ravenloft, the Parapets: 2 × Strahd's Animated Armor. Starts when you reach the parapets.
