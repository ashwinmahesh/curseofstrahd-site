    Overview

    A roadhouse on the Old Svalich Road between the Village of Barovia and the crossroads, burned out twenty winters ago, with a lantern in its upstairs window every night though it has no upstairs any more (The Lantern in the Roadhouse, docs/story/side_quests.md).

    A roadhouse by the road, burned down to its walls a long time ago. A painted goose still hangs from the sign-iron, grey with soot. Weeds in the taproom, and the hearth standing on its own like a tombstone.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 4 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    Fights

    • The nails squeal out of the trapdoor on their own, and what's been living in the cellar for twenty winters…, in The Grey Goose: Ghoul, Giant Rat, Rat. Scales with your level. Starts from a conversation. Part of The Lantern in the Roadhouse.
    ✦Every version (the first whose condition holds is the fight you get)
    • The Grey Goose, when level ≥ 3: The Innkeeper's Wife (Ghoul), 2 × A Son of the House (Ghoul), 4 × Giant Rat, Ghoul (900 XP)
    • The Grey Goose: The Innkeeper's Wife (Ghoul), A Son of the House (Ghoul), 4 × Giant Rat, 2 × Rat (520 XP)
