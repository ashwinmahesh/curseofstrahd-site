## Overview

The rutted lane between the Old Svalich Road and the windmill on the crag: pines, ditches, and the cart tracks of someone who comes this way every week. Where the lane's random fights and meetings play out.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
