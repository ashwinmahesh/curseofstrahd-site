## Overview

The abbey's church: a nave of plain pews, an altar on its step under a great gilded sunburst, the Abbot's bare cell on one side and the sacristy on the other. Where the Abbot works, prays and means to wake his bride.

**Resting:** no Not here. Not under that sunburst, with him lighting candles in the dark at the end of the nave.

## Quests and lore

- [Carry the Dawn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/carry-the-dawn.md): On the morning of her vows the Morninglord showed Liriel Dawnsong a valley where the sun had not risen in four hundred years, and told her…
- [The Abbot's Bride](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-abbots-bride.md): The Abbot of St. Markovia is stitching a bride for the lord of Barovia from Krezk's dead, and believes a wife will cure him.
- [The Burgomaster's Son](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-burgomasters-son.md): Ilya Krezkov, the burgomaster's last child, has been burning with fever for a month.
- [The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-holy-symbol-of-ravenkind.md): A holy symbol of great power against the undead, lost long ago in the valley.
- [The Sunsword](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-sunsword.md): A blade of sunlight that once belonged to the devil's brother, broken from its hilt and lost.
- [The Tome of Strahd](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-tome-of-strahd.md): A history of the devil, written in his own hand. Madam Eva says it holds the knowledge of his past.
