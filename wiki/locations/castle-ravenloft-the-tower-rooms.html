    Castle Ravenloft, the Tower Rooms

    The rooms high in the towers, off a narrow gallery: the count's bats, a stillroom of four glass vessels, a coven at its cauldron, Escher locked in his suite, and a ladder to the roofs.

    Overview

    A narrow gallery with doors on both sides. Somewhere a pot is boiling, somewhere bats are shifting, and behind one locked door somebody is humming.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

    Getting here

    Ways in

    • From Castle Ravenloft, the High Tower: The door to the tower rooms
    • From Castle Ravenloft, the Roofs: A hatch down to the tower rooms
    • From Castle Ravenloft, the Ravens' Roost: A narrow stair down to Escher's rooms

    Quests and lore

    • Escher's Petition: Every night for nine years Escher has slid the same petition under the count's door: let me see the sun. Now an answer has come back.
    • The Heart of Sorrow: At the top of Castle Ravenloft's high tower hangs a crystal heart that takes Strahd's wounds before he does.
