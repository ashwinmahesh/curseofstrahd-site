## Overview

A shingle landing on the south-west shore of Lake Zarovich: a rotten jetty, drying racks with no fish on them, empty huts, and Bluto Krogarov's shack, the last fisher on the lake.

Black water to the far shore and the wolf hills above it. The fishers left a year ago when the nets stopped filling; only Bluto stayed. His rowboat is tied at the jetty's foot.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [One Lantern Too Many](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/one-lantern-too-many.md): Every festival week the children of Vallaki carry paper lanterns round the square for the Baron. Thirty-one children. Thirty-two lanterns.
- [The Baron's Island](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-barons-island.md): Some nights the Baron's boat rows out to the Reed Isle on Lake Zarovich with somebody tied up in it, and rows back with nobody.
- [The Bell Under the Lake](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-bell-under-the-lake.md): Old Nistor, the last fisherman on Lake Zarovich, says the fish went deep forty years ago. He knows why, and he's never told anyone.
- [The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md): Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
- [The Hunters at the Inn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-hunters-at-the-inn.md): Two hunters in the corner of the Blue Water Inn sell wolf ears to the Baron at a copper apiece, and want help with the one wolf he pays…
