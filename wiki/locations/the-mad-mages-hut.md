## Overview

A stone hut colder inside than out: a dragonchess board with frost on the pieces, a mirror turned to the wall, shelves of blank books, and an old man in a magnificent ruin of a robe.

One room with a sleeping corner and a study divided off by a half wall. The hearth has never been lit; the cold doesn't seem to bother him.

**Resting:** Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.
