## Overview

A walled stone abbey on a shelf of the mountain above Krezk: a church wing, an east wing of old wards, a bell tower, a cart shed and a walled garden. Built by a saint for the sick and the motherless; kept now by the Abbot and the Belview family he has been improving.

The switchback track arrives at the abbey gate. Inside: the courtyard with its well and a statue of Markovia, the church doors to the north, the wards to the east and the garden door to the west.

**Resting:** Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.

## Quests and lore

- [Clovin's Audience](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/clovins-audience.md): Clovin Belview, the abbey's errand-runner, has written jokes for forty years and wants to tell them once to people who could choose not to…
- [Mother's Ninth Improvement](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/mothers-ninth-improvement.md): Mother, the Belviews' matriarch, hasn't left the abbey since she was carried up the mountain as a baby. Now that the Abbot is done…
- [The Abbot's Bride](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-abbots-bride.md): The Abbot of St. Markovia is stitching a bride for the lord of Barovia from Krezk's dead, and believes a wife will cure him.
- [The Destined Ally](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-destined-ally.md): Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
- [The Waystone](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-waystone.md): Every night somebody goes over the Abbey wall and down the Krezk road to light the candle at the old sunburst waystone.
