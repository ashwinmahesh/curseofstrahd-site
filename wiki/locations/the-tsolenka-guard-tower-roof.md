## Overview

The open roof of the tower in the cloud, with a gilded stone knight at each corner and the whole pass below.

A flat stone roof inside a battlement, reached by the hatch from the lookout. The book puts golden knight statues on top of the white tower; the rest of the roof is ours.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

## What to find

The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 9 the first time you come ([Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

## Fights

No fights of its own.
