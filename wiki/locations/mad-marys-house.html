    A narrow townhouse with three locks on the door and boards on every window, where Mad Mary weeps for her daughter.

    Quests and lore

    • Mary's Daughter: Mad Mary's daughter Gertruda, sixteen and never once allowed outside, slipped out of the house a few nights ago and hasn't come back.
    ❝Book of tales

    A child's book of tales, read until the spine gave up. Most pages are clean. One is soft with handling: a story about a girl in a tower and a prince in a black coat who sings under her window until she climbs down to him. In the margin, in a girl's hand: real?
