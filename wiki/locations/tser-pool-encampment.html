    Overview

    A Vistani camp beside a dark pool fed by the Tser Falls: painted wagons around a fire, horses, music, wine, and the great patched tent where Madam Eva reads the Tarokka.

    The Old Svalich Road runs past a clearing on the edge of the Svalich Woods. In it, painted wagons stand in a loose ring around a bonfire, horses doze in a rail pen by the water, and a tent the size of a small house glows from inside.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.
