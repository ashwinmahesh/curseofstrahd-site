## Overview

The Rooms of Weeping's west wing: a passage where two guards stand in alcoves before a steel door, the count's own dining hall with a wedding feast four centuries old, a closet of capes, the Hall of Heroes open to the storm, the maids' hall of eight lace beds, and the servants' rooms under the eaves, where a ladder runs down into the high tower.

Silver plates and crystal goblets under four centuries of grey dust.

**Resting:** Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
