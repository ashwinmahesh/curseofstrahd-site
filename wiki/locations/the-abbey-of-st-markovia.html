    Overview

    A walled stone abbey on a shelf of the mountain above Krezk: a church wing, an east wing of old wards, a bell tower, a cart shed and a walled garden. Built by a saint for the sick and the motherless; kept now by the Abbot and the Belview family he has been improving.

    The switchback track arrives at the abbey gate. Inside: the courtyard with its well and a statue of Markovia, the church doors to the north, the wards to the east and the garden door to the west.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Clovin's Audience: Clovin Belview, the abbey's errand-runner, has written jokes for forty years and wants to tell them once to people who could choose not to…
    • Mother's Ninth Improvement: Mother, the Belviews' matriarch, hasn't left the abbey since she was carried up the mountain as a baby. Now that the Abbot is done…
    • The Abbot's Bride: The Abbot of St. Markovia is stitching a bride for the lord of Barovia from Krezk's dead, and believes a wife will cure him.
    • The Destined Ally: Madam Eva's fourth card named the one who will stand with us against the devil.
    • The Waystone: Every night somebody goes over the Abbey wall and down the Krezk road to light the candle at the old sunburst waystone.
