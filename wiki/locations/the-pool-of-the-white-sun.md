## Overview

A round spring-fed pool at the north end of Krezk that never freezes, ringed by Krezk's graves, with an open pavilion shrine to the White Sun beside it. The sun lingers in its water a breath longer than in the sky.

**Resting:** Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.
