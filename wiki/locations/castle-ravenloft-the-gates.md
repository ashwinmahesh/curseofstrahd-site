## Overview

The road's end below Castle Ravenloft: a drawbridge over a black chasm, the gatehouse and its portcullis, the front courtyard before the keep's great doors, the servants' courtyard and the carriage house, and the walk along the wall to the overlook.

A chasm with no bottom you can see, a drawbridge already lowered, a gatehouse with its portcullis up. Beyond it a courtyard of cracked stone and weeds, and the keep rising over it with one window lit.

**Resting:** Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
