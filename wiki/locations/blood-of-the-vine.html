    Blood of the Vine

    The village's last tavern: a smoky common room, a bar with nearly empty casks, Ismark drinking alone and three Vistani women in the corner.

    Overview

    The only room in Barovia where people still talk, if quietly. The wine is Purple Grapemash No. 3, and there isn't much of it left.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • Count Off: Ilse Varga's mercenary company marched into the mists a year ago at the invitation of someone who signed himself S. She was meant to march…
    • Cut After Noon: The woodcutters of Barovia cut what they need by noon and go home. Grigore's son stayed late, and brought something back.
    • Ireena's Escort: Strahd has fed on Ireena Kolyana twice. Her brother Ismark believes she'll be safer behind the walls of Vallaki, to the west.
    • Six Feet: Old Mihail the sexton digs graves before anybody has died. There's always somebody by the end of the week.
    • The Burgomaster's Burial: Kolyan Indirovich, burgomaster of the village of Barovia, died three nights ago. His children want him buried in the churchyard, and no…
    • The Fourth Rumour: Vasile and Petre have traded the same three rumours since the mist came down. They might take a fourth, if somebody made one.
    • The Lantern in the Roadhouse: The Grey Goose roadhouse burned twenty winters ago, and every night a lantern burns in its upstairs window, though there's no upstairs any…
    • The Polite Caller: Widow Ileana's husband Teodor died three winters ago and still knocks at her shutter every night. He has never once asked to be let in.
    • The Wizard of Wines: Druids from Yester Hill have driven the Martikov family out of their winery and taken the magic gems, the family's three vine stones, that…
    ❝A scholar's note

    Third night in the village. The barkeep has said eleven words to me, four of them 'wine'. I have the castle's outline drawn from three sides now, and a list of the windows that are lit. One is lit every night: high in the east tower, a library or I am no judge of shelving. Tomorrow I go up the road. If anyone finds this, send it to the college at Mirabar, where they still have my good pen. Tell them I was right. Tell them he knows I am reading. A. M.
