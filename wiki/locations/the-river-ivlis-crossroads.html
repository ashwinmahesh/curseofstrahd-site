    The River Ivlis Crossroads

    Where the Old Svalich Road crosses the River Ivlis and meets the track to Tser Falls and the road climbing south toward the castle. A gallows stands at the junction, and it is not empty.

    Overview

    A stone bridge carries the road over the Ivlis. East lies the village of Barovia, west Tser Pool and Vallaki, north the river track to the falls; south, a steeper road climbs toward the castle on its cliff. A gallows stands beside the signpost, and behind it a field of unmarked mounds.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.
