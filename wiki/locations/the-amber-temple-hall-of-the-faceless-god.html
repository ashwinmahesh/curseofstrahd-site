    The Amber Temple: Hall of the Faceless God

    ✦The Amber Temple · party level about 10

    The temple's great hall, cut out of the mountain around a colossal robed statue whose face was never carved. East lies the ring of amber, where six sarcophagi talk; west, the frozen reading hall where something wails; north, the wardens' cells, the library passage and the stair down to the vaults.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dark

    Every sound you make comes back to you a moment later, slightly changed.
