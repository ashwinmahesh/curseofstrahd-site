    Castle Ravenloft, the Overlook

    A long stone balcony on the castle's flank, with a low parapet and then nothing for a thousand feet. The whole valley lies below it. Strahd comes here to look at what he owns.

    Castle Ravenloft, the Gates

    Overview

    A terrace of worn flagstones along the keep's wall. Past the parapet, the cliff falls away into mist, and the valley spreads out under it: the forest, the river, the lights of the village.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Nobody sleeps on a ledge over a thousand feet of nothing, under his windows.

    What to find

    The containers here also get random magic items and potions, rolled for a party of level 10 the first time you come (Random Treasure). Hidden finds need a Search (F) nearby; the party's best roll counts.

    • A tall figure stands at the parapet with his back to you, looking down at the valley. He lets you come quite…, in Castle Ravenloft, the Overlook: Strahd von Zarovich, Swarm of Bats, Vampire Spawn. Scales with your level.
