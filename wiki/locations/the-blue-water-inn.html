    The Blue Water Inn

    ✦Vallaki · party level about 5

    Vallaki's inn: a warm taproom, a careful kitchen, rooms upstairs, and in the stable yard a gaudy carnival wagon its owner lets nobody near.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.
    Light: dim

    A taproom with a long bar and a fire that is actually lit. Through the kitchen, the guest rooms; through the back door, the stable yard and a painted wagon.
