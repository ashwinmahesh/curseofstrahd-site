    The Blue Water Inn

    Vallaki's inn: a warm taproom, a careful kitchen, rooms upstairs, and in the stable yard a gaudy carnival wagon its owner lets nobody near.

    Overview

    A taproom with a long bar and a fire that is actually lit. Through the kitchen, the guest rooms; through the back door, the stable yard and a painted wagon.

    Resting: Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.

    ❝A battered journal

    A hunter's journal, in a small, exact hand. Lists of weaknesses: running water, a threshold not crossed without leave, sunlight, a stake through the heart. A page of notes on 'the lord of this valley' that stops halfway down. Inside the cover, a bookplate: from the library of Dr. R. van Richten. Below it, in fresher ink: Never again by that name. Never again.
