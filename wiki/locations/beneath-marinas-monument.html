    Beneath Marina's Monument

    ✦Berez · party level about 9

    A dry stone vault under the statue on the mound, where the girl Strahd loved in Berez lies on a bier as if asleep, with his candles burning at her head and his roses at her feet.

    Resting: no Not here. Not beside her.
    Light: dim

    A stair under the plinth comes down into one square room cut from the rock. The marsh is all around it and not a drop has got in.
