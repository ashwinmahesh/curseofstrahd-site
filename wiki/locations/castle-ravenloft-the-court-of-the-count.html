    Overview

    The castle's second floor, where the count keeps court: the long King's Hall, the audience hall and its throne, the study with a lady over the hearth, the trophy room, the accountant's office, the guardroom, and the stairs to the towers and to the rooms above.

    A long hall of black stone hung with old banners runs the width of the keep. Tall doors open on a throne room to the north; a study, a trophy room and a clerk's office open off it; the grand stairs come up from below.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    ❝Letters on a side table

    A stack of letters in one long, careful hand, every one addressed to the same woman, none of them sent. The newest ink is still glossy. The oldest pages have gone the colour of tea.

    ❝The count's ledger

    Columns in a small, perfect hand. Tithes from Vallaki, wine from the Martikovs, rent from Krezk. Under VAULT: carried behind the lady in the long hall. Under GUESTS: A. Karelova, gowns, account closed. G., sixteen, three gowns, pending.
