    Old Bonegrinder, the Loft

    ✦Old Bonegrinder · party level about 6

    The windmill's upper floor under the turning gear: the hags' straw nest, Bella's dressing table, Morgantha's rocking chair, and two cages hung from the rafters with children in them.

    Resting: no Not up here, under the cages, with the chair still rocking.
    Light: dim

    Low rafters, warm air rising from the oven below, and the great wooden gear of the sails overhead, locked still. It smells of straw, sugar and something kept too long.
