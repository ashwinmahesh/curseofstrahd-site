## Overview

The Martikovs' winery: a tasting room with a long bar, Davian's office, the fermenting hall with its great vats, a bottling room, and the stair down to the cellars. The druids have made it their barracks.

The winery smells of old wine gone to vinegar and of something else under it, green and wet, like a pond in summer. The floorboards have been pried up in rows, as if someone were looking for something.

**Resting:** Safe: a Long Rest here isn't interrupted.
