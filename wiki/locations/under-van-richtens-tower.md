## Overview

The undercroft the archmage Khazan cut under his tower, below the level of the tarn: a stair, a hall of shelved names, a crypt the lake has got into, and his study, where he waits to be remembered (The Name Over the Door, docs/story/side_quests.md).

A stair under the hearth goes down past the level of the lake. The walls sweat cold water. Every step down, the silence gets thicker, like the inside of a library that has never been opened.

**Resting:** no Every time you close your eyes, somebody whispers your name from the shelves. Nobody sleeps down here.
