    The Tsolenka Guard Tower

    The watch tower over the Tsolenka gate: a guardroom where dead soldiers still stand their posts, bunks nobody sleeps in, the winch that raises the portcullis, and the captain's room.

    Overview

    The door from the road opens into a vestibule and the guardroom. The gatehouse with its winch is to the west, the barracks in the middle, the captain's room to the east behind its own door.

    Resting: Risky: a Long Rest here makes camp, and the night can be interrupted.

    Quests and lore

    • The Frozen Pilgrims: Nine pilgrims from Vallaki went up the Tsolenka Pass forty years ago to ask the Amber Temple never to be cold again. Their lamp came back…
    • The Guide to the Temple: Since the Tsolenka gate opened, a man has led three parties up the goat path toward the Amber Temple and come down alone each time.
    • The Tsolenka Gate: The road to the Amber Temple crosses the Tsolenka Pass, through a guard tower whose portcullis has been down for centuries.
    • The Warm Snow: Sergeant Valcu's log has forty years of smoke on the north shoulder of Mount Ghakis, snow that melts in midwinter, and the count's cart…
    ❝The watch log

    The watch log of the Tsolenka tower, in a dozen hands, the last of them steady and square. Each entry is the same: date, weather, the watchword, and the words NOTHING CAME DOWN. Near the end: Two of the lads went up the road at night without orders. They came back talking about voices in the amber. Sent them down to the valley. Gate shut. Watchword unchanged: cold hearth, shut door. After that the dates stop, and the entries do not.
