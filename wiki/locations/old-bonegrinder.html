    Old Bonegrinder

    ✦Old Bonegrinder · party level about 6

    The windmill's ground floor: a bakery around a great brick oven, a millstone that grinds more than grain, a pantry of jars, and a stair up into the loft where the children are kept.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim

    Warm, floury and close. Everything is dusted white: the tables, the sacks, the floor, the millstone. The oven on the far wall breathes like something asleep.

    Things to read

    ❝A floury receipt book

    Morgantha's receipt book, in three hands. The first is old and spiky; the second large and round, with drawings of cakes; the third neat and bored. DREAM PASTRY: flour of the mill, honey, poppy-milk, a sweet sleep-root from the garden, one pinch of the special flour per dozen. The special flour has its own page. Under it someone has written, in the round hand: NOT THE LITTLE ONES YET, MOTHER SAYS. Further on, a ledger: households in the village and on the Vallaki road, how many pastries a week, and a column headed OWING.
