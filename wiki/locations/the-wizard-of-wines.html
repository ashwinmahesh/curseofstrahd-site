    The Wizard of Wines

    ✦The Wizard of Wines · party level about 6

    The Martikov family's winery in the hills south-west of Vallaki: a lane up through withered vines, a yard between the house, the winery and the press house, and the family's camp under the trees, where they have hidden since the druids came.

    Resting: Risky: a rest here can be interrupted.
    Light: dim, outdoors

    A painted sign over the yard gate shows a wizard in a starry hat pouring wine for a raven. Below it the vines stand in long rows, brown at the edges and curling, and nothing in the yard has moved in a while except the birds.
