    The Coffin Maker's Shop

    Henrik van der Voort's workshop: coffins on trestles, shavings on the floor, and a storeroom full of crates that are heavier than they should be.

    Overview

    A workshop that smells of pine and varnish. Henrik's rooms open off one side; the storeroom off the other, its door scored where the bolt has been forced and mended more than once.

    Resting: No sleeping here: a Short Rest only, out of sight of foes. Not among the coffins. Not with the storeroom so quiet.

    Quests and lore

    • The Bones of St. Andral: Someone has stolen the bones of St. Andral from the crypt of Vallaki's church, and with them the only thing that kept the dead out of it.
    ❝Order book

    Henrik's order book. Coffins, mostly child-sized this year. One entry stands apart, in a shaking hand: Bones, one set, from M. Paid 20 gold. Below it, a list headed DELIVERIES, with no names: six crates, to be kept in the storeroom, not to be opened, not to be moved, not to be mentioned.
