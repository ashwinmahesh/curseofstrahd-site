    Spell Scrolls

    Every one of the game's 390 spells can be had on a Spell Scroll. This page lists each spell's scroll by level: whose list it's on, who sells it and for how much, and every fixed place it's found. 94 are sold by a merchant and 14 lie somewhere in the world. Of the rest, 259 come only from random finds, and 32 cantrips only with a cheat code, since nothing random holds a cantrip.

    Reading a scroll

    • Right-click the scroll in the inventory and pick Cast. It works only if the spell is on the reader's class list, and needs no Material components.
    • A spell above the level the reader can cast needs a spellcasting ability check, DC 10 + the spell's level. The scroll is used up either way.
    • The scroll's own save DC and attack bonus are used, whoever reads it (the table below).
    • A Wizard can copy a scroll's spell into their spellbook from the inventory: 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level, for a level they can prepare (How Magic Works).

    The price is the guide price (Merchants explains it): a scroll in a merchant's magic stock costs exactly that, three at a time, while one on a merchant's own counter carries their markup. A friendly merchant or a won haggle takes a little off either.

    Fixed finds and gifts: chests, hiding places, skill checks and people that hand you a particular scroll, listed under each spell below.

    Random treasure: a place's containers roll random magic items the first time you come, for the level the region is built for, and some of those are spell scrolls. A random scroll holds any spell of a level its rarity allows: levels 2 and 3 for an uncommon one, 4 and 5 rare, 6 to 8 very rare, 9 legendary (party level 1–2: 100% uncommon; party level 3–4: 95% uncommon, 5% rare; party level 5–7: 75% uncommon, 25% rare; party level 8–10: 55% uncommon, 38% rare, 7% very rare; party level 11–20: 25% uncommon, 45% rare, 25% very rare, 5% legendary; Random Treasure).

    Fight spoils: a won road fight, or a story fight whose foes carry treasure, leaves one find, and 3 finds in 10 are a scroll of a random spell. Its level is half the party's level, rounded up, from 1 to 6 (a road meeting with no fight: a third of the party's level, at least 1).

    Scrolls of a random spell

    These scrolls say only their level. Each becomes a scroll of one spell of that level when you get it, picked from the spells of the level and different in each playthrough.

    • 2 × In a warden's locker, The Amber Temple: Hall of the Faceless God (The wardens' cells) (locked, DC 14): a level 1 spell
    • In the commander's strongbox, Argynvostholt, Upper Floor (The commander's room) (locked, DC 18): a level 1 spell
    • 2 × In a sealed reliquary with a slot in its lid, Castle Ravenloft, the Catacombs (The conjurer's crypt) (locked, DC 22; key: Arcane Focus (Wand)): a level 1 spell
    • In a chest under the straw, Old Bonegrinder, the Loft (The nest) (locked, DC 15): a level 1 spell
    • In Van Richten's strongbox, Van Richten's Tower (The lantern room): a level 1 spell
    • Given by Victor Vallakovich in The Burgomaster's Mansion (The Attic Circle): a level 1 spell
    • Given by the fallen doorway in Van Richten's Tower or the carved lintel and the door in Van Richten's Tower: a level 1 spell

    Scrolls the story asks for

    • Remove Curse: read in a conversation with The Billy Goat in Krezk or Little Petru in Krezk or Stella Wachter in Wachterhaus (The Cat in the Window, The One-Horned Billy, The Pack's Runt).
