# Spell Scrolls

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/spell-scrolls.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

Every one of the game's 390 spells can be had on a Spell Scroll. This page lists each spell's scroll by level: whose list it's on, who sells it and for how much, and every fixed place it's found. 358 are sold by a merchant and 14 lie somewhere in the world. The other 32 cantrips can only be had with a cheat code, since nothing random holds a cantrip.

## Reading a scroll

- Right-click the scroll in the inventory and pick **Cast**. It works only if the spell is on the reader's class list, and needs no Material components.
- A spell above the level the reader can cast needs a spellcasting ability check, DC 10 + the spell's level. The scroll is used up either way.
- The scroll's own save DC and attack bonus are used, whoever reads it (the table below).
- A Wizard can copy a scroll's spell into their spellbook from the inventory: 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level, for a level they can prepare ([How Magic Works](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/rules/how-magic-works.md)).

| Cantrip | common | 30 gp | 13 | +5 | 1 |
| 1 | common | 50 gp | 13 | +5 | 1 |

The price is the guide price ([Merchants](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/merchants.md) explains it): a scroll in a merchant's magic stock costs exactly that, three at a time, while one on a merchant's own counter carries their markup. A friendly merchant or a won haggle takes a little off either.

**Fixed finds and gifts:** chests, hiding places, skill checks and people that hand you a particular scroll, listed under each spell below.

**Random treasure:** a place's containers roll random magic items the first time you come, for the level the region is built for, and some of those are spell scrolls. A random scroll holds any spell of a level its rarity allows: levels 2 and 3 for an uncommon one, 4 and 5 rare, 6 to 8 very rare, 9 legendary (party level 1–2: 100% uncommon; party level 3–4: 95% uncommon, 5% rare; party level 5–7: 75% uncommon, 25% rare; party level 8–10: 55% uncommon, 38% rare, 7% very rare; party level 11–20: 25% uncommon, 45% rare, 25% very rare, 5% legendary; [Random Treasure](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/items/random-treasure.md)).

**Fight spoils:** a won road fight, or a story fight whose foes carry treasure, leaves one find, and 3 finds in 10 are a scroll of a random spell. Its level is half the party's level, rounded up, from 1 to 6 (a road meeting with no fight: a third of the party's level, at least 1).
