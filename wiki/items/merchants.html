    Merchants and Services

    How prices work

    • Each merchant marks up the book price of what they sell (their markup below) and pays a share of it for what they buy (their rate). Every price is rounded up to a whole gold piece.
    • A merchant's attitude to the party changes both: friendly takes 10% off and pays 10% more; hostile charges 25% more and pays 25% less.
    • Haggling: one Persuasion check by the hero at the counter, DC 15 unless the shop says otherwise. Win and that merchant gives you a further 10% both ways for good; lose and they never haggle with you again.
    • Some merchants close at night or until something happens; some stock only appears after a quest.

    Services

    Temples sell spells cast for the party, and inns sell a night's room.

    ServiceBase priceWhat it does
    Cure Wounds50 gpHeals one hero 2d8 plus the reader's Wisdom modifier.
    Lesser Restoration200 gpEnds one of these on a hero: Paralyzed, Blinded, Poisoned or Deafened.
    Remove Curse300 gpEnds every curse on a hero, and breaks their Attunement to a cursed item so they can put it down.
    Raise Dead2000 gpBrings back a hero who died within the last 10 days, with 1 Hit Point and -4 on D20 Tests for 10 days. The spell uses up a 500 gp diamond, which the church supplies.
    A room upstairs0.5 gp eachA bed each behind a door that locks. Sleep here, and everyone wakes with temporary Hit Points equal to their level.
    The best room, with a hot bath2 gp eachClean linen, a fire and a copper bath. Sleep here, and everyone wakes with temporary Hit Points equal to twice their level and one more level of Exhaustion gone.
