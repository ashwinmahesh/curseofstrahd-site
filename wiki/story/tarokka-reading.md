# The Tarokka Reading

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/story/tarokka-reading.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

Madam Eva's reading at Tser Pool decides where three treasures lie, who your destined ally is and where Strahd waits at the end. Each card drawn sets one of them. The reading is random per playthrough; the journal keeps her verses ([The Seer at Tser Pool](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/quests/the-seer-at-tser-pool.md)).
