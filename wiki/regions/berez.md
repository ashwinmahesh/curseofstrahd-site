# Berez

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/berez.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![castle glimpse](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/castle-glimpse.webp)

Built for a party of about level 9: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [The Barrow Moor](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-barrow-moor.md): Open moor in a cold fog: heather, black bog pools, and a long barrow ringed with standing stones beside the track. A random fight on the moor and marsh roads may play out here.
- [The Ruins of Berez](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-ruins-of-berez.md): A village the Luna drowned: broken walls standing in reeds and black water, a white statue on the only dry mound, and in the middle, on a stump wider than a house, a hut that breathes.
- [Baba Lysaga's Hut](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/baba-lysagas-hut.md): One crooked room on top of a giant stump, warmer than it should be: a hearth, a rocking chair, a cradle, jars that hum, and drawings of the same handsome boy pinned to every wall.
- [Beneath Marina's Monument](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/beneath-marinas-monument.md): A dry stone vault under the statue on the mound, where the girl Strahd loved in Berez lies on a bier as if asleep, with his candles burning at her head and his roses at her feet.
- [The Marsh Track](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-marsh-track.md): A raised track of old logs across the marsh between Vallaki's lake and the drowned village, with reeds higher than a man on both sides.
- [The Sunken Causeway](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-sunken-causeway.md): A causeway of sunken logs and piled stone wanders across the bog by the Luna, black water and sucking mire on both sides, a few firm islands of dead trees. A random fight on the marsh roads may play out here.
