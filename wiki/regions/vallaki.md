# Vallaki

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/vallaki.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![vallaki palisade](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/vallaki-palisade.webp)

Built for a party of about level 5: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [Vallaki](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/vallaki.md): A walled town on the shore of Lake Zarovich, kept cheerful by order of its Baron: a sharpened palisade, a church whose saint has gone missing, an inn of ravens, and a square where a great wicker sun waits to be burned.
- [Blinsky Toys](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/blinsky-toys.md): Gadof Blinsky's toyshop: shelves of wooden toys built by a man who understands children perfectly well and has decided to make toys for Barovia's instead.
- [The Blue Water Inn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-blue-water-inn.md): Vallaki's inn: a warm taproom, a careful kitchen, rooms upstairs, and in the stable yard a gaudy carnival wagon its owner lets nobody near.
- [The Burgomaster's Mansion](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-burgomasters-mansion.md): Baron Vallakovich's mansion: a hall hung with festival banners, a dining room laid for guests who never stay, Izek's bare room off the hall, and in the attic the Baron's son and his circle.
- [The Coffin Maker's Shop](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-coffin-makers-shop.md): Henrik van der Voort's workshop: coffins on trestles, shavings on the floor, and a storeroom full of crates that are heavier than they should be.
- [Haralamb's Studio](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/haralambs-studio.md): Haralamb the painter's house on Vallaki's east row: a front room of pots and rags, and up the back a long studio full of portraits of one woman, and the great canvas the count gave him to copy from (The Count's Portraitist, docs/story/side_quests.md).
- [St. Andral's Church](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/st-andrals-church.md): Vallaki's church of the Morninglord, named for the saint whose bones lie in the crypt beneath it and keep the dead from its doors. Or did, until the reliquary was emptied.
- [The Vistani Camp](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-vistani-camp.md): A ring of painted Vistani wagons outside Vallaki's west gate, between the road and the black water of Lake Zarovich. Music by night, horses by day, and one wagon with its lamp unlit.
- [Wachterhaus](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/wachterhaus.md): Lady Fiona Wachter's townhouse: a hall of family portraits, a parlour set for a book club, a locked bedroom that something scratches at from inside, and, behind the study bookcase, a stair down.
