# The Werewolf Den

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/the-werewolf-den.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![castle glimpse](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/castle-glimpse.webp)

Built for a party of about level 8: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [The Hills above Lake Zarovich](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-hills-above-lake-zarovich.md): A pine-dark slope in the hills above Lake Zarovich, climbing to a rock face with a cave in it. The ground around the cave mouth is bare from the traffic of many feet, not all of them on two legs.
- [The Werewolf Den](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-werewolf-den.md): Caves under the hill where Kiril Stoyanovich's pack sleeps: a great cave round a fire pit, the pack leader's lair, a shrine to Mother Night, the alcove where the old leader Emil hangs in silver chains, and a pen of stolen children.
