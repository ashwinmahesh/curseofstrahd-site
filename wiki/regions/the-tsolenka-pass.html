    The Tsolenka Pass

    Built for a party of about level 9: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

    Places

    • The Tsolenka Pass: The high road over the mountains to the Amber Temple: a cliff road, a stone bridge over a gorge where the roc of Mount Ghakis hunts, and a guard tower whose portcullis has been down for centuries.
    • The Tsolenka Guard Tower: The watch tower over the Tsolenka gate: a guardroom where dead soldiers still stand their posts, bunks nobody sleeps in, the winch that raises the portcullis, and the captain's room.
    • The Tsolenka Guard Tower, Roof: The open roof of the tower in the cloud, with a gilded stone knight at each corner and the whole pass below.
    • The Tsolenka Guard Tower, Lookout: The tower's upper floor: a bare lookout under the roof where the watch kept an eye on the road, with a spyglass at the slit window and a hatch to the roof.
