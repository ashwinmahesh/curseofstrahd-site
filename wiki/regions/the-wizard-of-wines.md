# The Wizard of Wines

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/the-wizard-of-wines.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![castle glimpse](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/castle-glimpse.webp)

Built for a party of about level 6: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [The Wizard of Wines](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-wizard-of-wines.md): The Martikov family's winery in the hills south-west of Vallaki: a lane up through withered vines, a yard between the house, the winery and the press house, and the family's camp under the trees, where they have hidden since the druids came.
- [The Winery Cellars](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-winery-cellars.md): Vaulted cellars under the winery: racks of casks, a side vault of old vintages, and at the far end a wall the druids have broken through, where their blights are digging up the floor.
- [The Press House](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-press-house.md): A tall barn of a building around the great wine press, where the Martikovs crush the harvest. A rack against the east wall holds a cask set aside every year for the wizard who gave the winery its name, and never came back to drink it.
- [The Winery](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-winery.md): The Martikovs' winery: a tasting room with a long bar, Davian's office, the fermenting hall with its great vats, a bottling room, and the stair down to the cellars. The druids have made it their barracks.
