    Daerns Fortress

    Places

    • Daern's Instant Fortress, Ground Floor: The tower's ground floor: bare adamantine walls pierced by arrow slits, one door that opens only at its owner's word, and a stair up along the east wall.
    • Daern's Instant Fortress, the Battlement: The roof: a square of flat adamantine inside a toothed parapet, thirty feet above the ground, with the trapdoor in one corner.
    • Daern's Instant Fortress, Second Floor: The tower's upper floor: the same bare adamantine, bedrolls laid along the walls, a stair down and a trapdoor up to the battlement.
