# Krezk

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/krezk.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![krezk wall](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/krezk-wall.webp)

Built for a party of about level 7: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [Krezk](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/krezk.md): The last village on the road west: a few dozen stone cottages on a mountain shelf behind a wall three men high, a gate that opens only for a reason, and the Pool of the White Sun at its northern end. The Abbey of St. Markovia looks down on it from the mountainside.
- [The Krezkovs' House](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-krezkovs-house.md): The burgomaster's house: a long hall with a hearth and a table older than the village, the room where Ilya lies burning with fever, Dmitri and Anna's bedroom, and a small room that was their eldest son's.
- [The Pool of the White Sun](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-pool-of-the-white-sun.md): A round spring-fed pool at the north end of Krezk that never freezes, ringed by Krezk's graves, with an open pavilion shrine to the White Sun beside it. The sun lingers in its water a breath longer than in the sky.
- [The Waystone](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-waystone.md): A bend of the Old Svalich Road on the way to Krezk, where an old waystone with a sunburst cut into it stands at the roadside with a candle burning at its foot every night (The Waystone, docs/story/side_quests.md).
