# Mount Ghakis

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/mount-ghakis.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![ghakis shoulder](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/ghakis-shoulder.webp)

Built for a party of about level 10: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [Under Mount Ghakis](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/under-mount-ghakis.md): The count's caves under the north shoulder of Mount Ghakis: the keepers' post, their larder, the anchor of a great chain, a gallery of fire-cracked rock and, at the bottom, the dragon chained on her hoard (The Warm Snow, docs/story/side_quests.md).
- [The Ghakis Quarry](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-ghakis-quarry.md): The count's quarry on the south face of Mount Ghakis, cut last spring: raw stone terraces, abandoned tools, a wagon with a glowing crate, and mountain folk chained to a ring in the floor (The Mountain's Heart, docs/story/side_quests.md).
- [The North Shoulder of Ghakis](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-north-shoulder-of-ghakis.md): A shelf of steaming black rock high on Mount Ghakis, where the snow won't lie, and an iron gate in the mountain with the count's crest on it (The Warm Snow, docs/story/side_quests.md).
