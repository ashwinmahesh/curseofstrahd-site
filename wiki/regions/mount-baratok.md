# Mount Baratok

> From the Curse of Strahd game wiki (it covers the whole game, spoilers included): https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/regions/mount-baratok.html. Unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy, not approved or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

![lightning ridge](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/img/cutscenes/lightning-ridge.webp)

Built for a party of about level 9: random treasure here is rolled for that level.

## Places

- [A Frozen Tarn](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/a-frozen-tarn.md): The track rounds a tarn frozen white in a bowl of the mountain: thin ice in the middle, drifts at its rim, a shepherd's fallen shelter and a crag over the north shore. A random fight on the mountain roads may play out here.
- [Mount Baratok](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/mount-baratok.md): A steep, pine-dark mountain above Van Richten's lake, with frost where frost shouldn't be, a ring of glass where lightning lands every evening, and a stone hut whose owner has forgotten who he is.
- [The Mad Mage's Hut](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/the-mad-mages-hut.md): A stone hut colder inside than out: a dragonchess board with frost on the pieces, a mirror turned to the wall, shelves of blank books, and an old man in a magnificent ruin of a robe.
- [A Mountain Ledge](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/a-mountain-ledge.md): A ledge road under pines and scree on the mountain roads north of Vallaki, with a drop on one side and a wall of rock on the other.
- [A Narrow Pass](https://curseofstrahd.app/wiki/locations/a-narrow-pass.md): The track squeezes between frost-cracked crags to a saddle in the snow: a ledge above it on the north side, a shelf of boulders on the south, and old cairns for the ones who didn't get over. A random fight on the mountain roads may play out here.
